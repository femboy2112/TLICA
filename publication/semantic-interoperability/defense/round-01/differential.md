- **Both-targeted (same anchor, meaningfully different pressure — preserved):**
  - **Understanding vs signal-echo:** ChatGPT **Q3** asks for a *forward, observable*
    criterion ("what response would convince you?"); Claude **Q8** asks for a

    ("what did understanding *have* that echo lacked?"). Different cognitive demand;
    both kept.

    *invariant* across differing minds; Claude **Q7** asks what the differing *dictionary
    is made of* and where it lives. The invariant vs the substrate; both kept.

  installing one approved mode).
- **Claude-only bearings:** **Q6** (why *demonstrate* multiple valid answers instead of
  defining — the phenomenological method); **Q7** (the *dictionary* construct's ontology);
  **Q9** (the *verify* act — what a listener concretely does).

  boundary (Q5); Claude anchors method (Q6), core-construct ontology (Q7), and the
  epistemic verify-act (Q9). The two overlap zones (understanding/echo, liberal education)
  receive **double, non-redundant** pressure from orthogonal angles.
