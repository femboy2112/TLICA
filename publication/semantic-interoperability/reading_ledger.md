## Differential questions to answer for each source (roadmap §12)

- Does it already model **shared conceptual repertoires**?
- Does it distinguish **transporting a perspective** from **transmitting a
  proposition**?
- Does it distinguish **understanding** from **agreement** and **truth**?
- Does it treat **education as social semantic infrastructure**?
- Does it discuss **compression cost** or **common conceptual coverage**?
- Does it explain **correlated blind spots** produced by a shared canon?
- What **prediction or distinction** would the present paper add on top of it?

A source that answers "yes" to enough of these is a prior-art collision to be
handled (cite, narrow, or drop — roadmap §38), not an inconvenience to be omitted.
