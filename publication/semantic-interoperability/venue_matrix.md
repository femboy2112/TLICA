## Selection rule (roadmap §9)

Choose the first venue to maximize **scope fit + policy compatibility +
anonymous object-level review**, not prestige. Before selecting:

1. Fill every `[ VERIFY ]` cell from the official page, dated.
2. Send the policy-inquiry email (roadmap §8.1) to any venue whose AI policy is
   silent or ambiguous — Ergo first.
3. Identify at least two scope-compatible venues with explicit permissive/disclosure
   policies.
4. Mark JCS incompatible unless its editor gives written clarification.

Do not wait until the manuscript is finished to discover a venue rejects the
workflow (roadmap §43).
