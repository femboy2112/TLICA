# When the Map Becomes a Mandate

[Applications overview](applications.md) · [Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [application paper v0.1.0](../applications/when_the_map_becomes_a_mandate_v0_1_0.md)

**First draft.** A TLICA application to attention, institutional abstraction, and moral authorization in the writings of Ted Kaczynski. It is not a diagnosis, an endorsement, or a validated explanation of his life. It uses the current v5.5.0 foundation and explicitly labeled application/research refinements; no foundation commitment is added.

## The question

How does “something is wrong” become “therefore I am entitled to do this to someone”?

The paper separates three jobs. An **explanation** says what is happening and why. A **practical evaluation** says which actions appear available and effective. An **authorization** says which actions the person takes themselves to be permitted to perform. These can interact, but succeeding at one does not settle the others.

A real constraint does not identify its cause. Identifying its cause does not prove that a proposed action will fix it. Predicting an effect does not establish permission to impose it on another person.

This is the paper's central application: keep those transitions visible rather than hiding them inside one compelling worldview.

## Three worked contrasts

### Experience is not its explanation

The historical material includes an institutional chronology, a public argument, retrospective interview material, prosecution-selected writing, and archival scholarship. They are different kinds of evidence.

The paper's first concrete result is narrow: an episode recalled as occurring in 1983 cannot precede the campaign onset dated to 1978. The episode may still matter as a later experience or narrative anchor. It cannot carry that particular origin story. The sources and their limits are specified in the paper, Section 5.

The newer source review also gives intellectual history its proper place. A worldview can develop through identifiable readings rather than simply emerging from isolation. Learning where an idea came from does not establish either its truth or its role in a later action.

### Rejected is not unavailable

An argument can explicitly consider ordinary publication and reject its expected impact. That is evidence of representation and evaluation. It is not, without more, evidence that the alternative was impossible to imagine or practically available at every relevant moment.

The distinction matters for agency. “I considered it inadequate,” “I could not execute it,” and “it never became a live possibility” are different statements. The paper does not diagnose capture from a retrospective text. It asks which statement the record actually supports.

### Knowing harm is not restraining harm

The [Cold Frame exposition](app-referent-routing.md) distinguishes modeling another person from affectively routing that model. This application adds an explicit question about action: even when the harm is known, does it constrain what is selected?

A wrong causal attribution, a poor model of another person, and a knowing permission to harm are distinct possibilities. Correcting a factual map does not automatically reverse an unacceptable priority. The paper rejects the earlier temptation to describe every moral failure as a source-map error.

## What the recent updates change

The v5.5.0 foundation places the actuator through the slow structure G and its effect on the field-reading f. In the stated baseline law, identity-weight ρ is not an extra force multiplying motion. The draft therefore does not tell a story in which “identity gets stronger” mathematically entails violence.

The developmental moving-machine refinement allows the apparatus doing the regulating to change over time. That blocks the assumption that one fixed adult response system can simply be projected backward across a life. It does not supply a developmental diagnosis of Kaczynski or transfer the TLICA author's history to him.

The [Moreish application](app-moreish-ontology.md) distinguishes a useful sensor from a global governor. Detecting one kind of harm can be informative without entitling that detector to choose the entire policy. Likewise, suffering under a policy may be relevant to its viability without constituting a logical refutation of every proposition associated with it.

The institutional v0.2 refinement, inspected at the paper's current-main snapshot, defines an institution through task-relative response equivalence. Two arrangements may behave identically for one institutional task while differing in other important respects. The draft gives the definition directly, because that research-tier source postdates this branch's base.

## The formal result about institutions

An abstraction preserves a new property only when that property is constant within each class the abstraction merges. This is a standard mathematical fact, not a new ethical theorem.

The paper's finite example contains eight arrangements and two task-equivalence classes. Its selected task response is preserved; an independently stipulated consent property is not. The point is not that a real institution was simulated. It is that a task-preserving model cannot silently be assumed to preserve everything needed for a different, ethical question.

Role substitutability does not make the people interchangeable in every respect.

## What was tested

The supplement contains sixteen software/control tests, rational-arithmetic comparisons of the baseline law, the institutional counterexample, and a declared source-dependency audit. The latter records which selected conclusions lose their cited support when a document family is removed. It is not a probability of truth or a blinded reanalysis of historical evidence.

No psychological coordinate was numerically estimated. No independent coder, fresh holdout, clinical assessment, or prevention intervention has run. Full-repository validation was unavailable in the drafting environment and remains an integration check.

## Where the paper lands

Attention-mediated foreclosure remains one live hypothesis. Revenge-first rationalization, reciprocal development, audience effects, substrate contributions, and knowing authorization remain alternatives or co-causes. A framework that can describe all of them still has to show which distinctions improve the explanation.

The paper's completed contribution is to expose the bridges that an argument must cross. Its causal explanation remains revisable. Its ethical stance against instrumental attacks on nonconsenting people is explicit rather than disguised as a consequence of the identity equations.
