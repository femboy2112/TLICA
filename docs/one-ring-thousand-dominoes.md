## The image: one ring, not a keyring of excuses

Most people carry a keyring of moralities — a work self, a family self, a Sunday self, a
tax self — and switch rings at each door, held together by weak excuses ("everyone does it
here," "that's just business"). The author rejects that. The aim is **one** stable normative
commitment whose *implementation* changes with context while its *worth* does not: you behave
differently in a hospital, a studio, and a courtroom, but the thing you are protecting is the
same thing in all three.

The candidate commitment the dialogue keeps returning to:

> No being's basic dignity should depend on productivity, rank, conformity, wealth, creed, or
> flawless behaviour; agency and accountability still matter — including accountability for
> avoidable harms and the effort to repair them.

Read the status carefully: this is **not** presented as proven, and **not** derived from the
cogito. It is a commitment offered up *to be examined, strengthened, and exposed to conflicts*.
It protects even an opponent's humanity without granting anyone a licence to persecute.

## The thousand dominoes: where responsibility lives

The other half of the title is about **causal reach**. If you tip the first domino in a long
chain, the first domino is not the moral boundary of what you did. Remote contributions,
distributed oversight, and cumulative small interactions are morally relevant. But — and the
dossier is careful here — responsibility stays **conditional** on knowledge, control,
foreseeability, choice, and the duties you actually hold. "Everything is connected" is not a
licence to assign infinite guilt, nor an excuse to assign none.

## The ten linked theses (arguments, not facts)

The executive synthesis is a chain of ten, each a claim to argue, not a result to cite:

1. **Consent is not inferred from mere survival** — being unable to leave an arrangement is not agreement to it.
2. **Accommodation does not establish legitimacy** — coping prudently with a threat doesn't make the threat fair or necessary.
3. **The first domino is not the causal boundary** — remote and cumulative effects count, *conditionally*.
4. **Institutions can be coherent locally and fail at their interfaces** — authority preserved while accountability fragments at role boundaries.
5. **Preserving a single ethic is a dynamical viability problem** — cheap to state, hard to hold under cost, reflex, and hostile incentives.
6. **Collective action is itself a collective-action problem** — building the thing that makes cooperation cheap often needs prior cooperation.
7. **Referent routing matters independently of factual recognition** — you can model someone's suffering accurately yet route its *relevance* away from your own self-evaluation (link to moral disengagement is **conjectural**).
8. **Population-level "progress" can be a selection artifact** — surviving-group averages can rise through exclusion or migration, not within-group improvement. Survival selects neither truth nor virtue automatically.
9. **Moral harm is not a GDP or liability ledger** — income, compliance, and legal wins don't by themselves make an arrangement legitimate.
10. **The design target is existential habitability** — how forgiving, intelligible, safe, and autonomy-preserving is a world for a newly arrived being who did not choose their starting conditions?

## The one piece that is more than conjecture

Most of the above is argued, not proved. One narrow technical point **is** separately
identifiable and machine-checked: the dossier's
[`narrative_causal_witness.py`](../research/one_ring_thousand_dominoes_2026-10-21/narrative_causal_witness.py)
runs **10 deterministic self-tests** on a small synthetic feedback model, used to keep four
things from being silently identified — ground-truth source **σ** versus *inferred*
attribution, identity-correlation **ρ** versus *causal participation*, and the indexed **I**
versus a distributed organisation. The lesson is a TLICA discipline, not a social measurement:
the world, its mediated representation, your narrative about it, and the causal process that
built that narrative are **four different things**.

## How it sits on existing TLICA

This is not a new theory of everything. It leans on coordinates TLICA already keeps apart —
contact **κ**, identity-correlation **ρ**, source map **σ** — and on the already-frozen
osmotic-imprinting picture (your substrate can encode a "lesson" the world never intended to
teach). The [`TLICA_RECONCILIATION.md`](../research/one_ring_thousand_dominoes_2026-10-21/TLICA_RECONCILIATION.md)
spells out the precise relationships; the normative proposal stays **application-/research-tier**,
not a foundation commitment.

## What's established vs. open

- **Identifiable:** the elementary logical distinctions (consent ≠ survival, accommodation ≠ legitimacy), the conditional algebra of distributed responsibility, and the 10-test non-identity witness.
- **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED:** the psychological and social *mechanisms* — referent routing as moral disengagement, the viability dynamics of a single ethic, the selection-artifact reading of moral "progress."
- **Explicitly not claimed:** a derived ethical invariant, a physics of society, or any diagnosis of a real person or institution.
