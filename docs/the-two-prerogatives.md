# The Two Prerogatives

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [File 2, §4.8](../foundation/2_access_and_development.md); [File 4, §9.5, §10.7](../foundation/4_derived_concepts_and_predictions.md)

---

Alongside the [three modes](modes-of-development.md), the architecture names two
**structural prerogatives** — fundamental features without which a lived self couldn't
function. A *prerogative* here just means a built-in tilt: not a rule you obey but a
direction the self leans by its very nature, before any choice. Neither is derivable from
the bare [cogito](the-cogito.md) (the bare "I think, therefore I am" anchor); both are
treated as features any operating lived self exhibits. Neither is metaphysically
inviolable — each can, under the right pressure, be bent or broken.

> **In plain terms —** every self comes with two standing tilts. One says *keep making
> sense to yourself*; the other says *keep yourself going*. You don't decide to have them
> any more than you decide to flinch — they're part of the equipment of being someone.

## The prerogative of consistency (PtCns)

**Your own frame is the implicit standard against which encounters are evaluated.**

**PtCns (the prerogative of consistency)** — in plain terms, the fact that *you are always
quietly grading the world by your own lights*. Whatever you already are is the yardstick;
everything you meet gets measured against it without your ever picking up the yardstick on
purpose.

When you meet something that conflicts with your frame, it registers as *inconsistent* —
and the registration *itself* is the privileging of your frame as the standard. You don't
deliberate "perhaps my frame should change"; the very sense that something is "off" is
your frame acting as the consistency-anchor.

> **From the inside —** someone says a thing that makes your stomach tighten and a "no"
> rise before you've thought a word. That flash of *wrong* isn't a verdict you reasoned
> your way to. It's your own frame, doing its job, registering a mismatch. The feeling of
> "off" *is* PtCns firing.

PtCns is what makes [Modes A and C](modes-of-development.md) possible at all. It's the
shared criterion underneath both:

- **Mode A** (drawing a line between you and not-you) is typically PtCns-*driven*: when
  integrating something would damage consistency, you differentiate from it — you push it
  out to stay coherent.
- **Mode C** (taking something in as part of you) is PtCns-*constrained*: positive
  identification can be driven by affinity, admiration, or aspiration, but integration only
  proceeds insofar as the profile can stay coherent. You let in what fits; you keep out
  what would wreck the fit.

> **In plain terms —** the same single instinct ("stay coherent") shows up two ways:
> sometimes it makes you reject a thing, sometimes it makes you absorb one. Push-away and
> pull-in are two faces of one drive to keep yourself making sense.

It is **not** specific to other people — it operates on any encounter (a misbehaving
gadget, a contradicting fact, a violated expectation). A printer that won't print, a fact
that won't square with what you knew, a friend who acts unlike themselves — each trips the
same "that's not right" register. What's special about meeting **another mind** is that
*two* PtCns structures operate at once, each treating the other as not-I from its own
frame, with no view-from-nowhere available to either. That's the structural form of
intersubjective disagreement — not "one of us is right," but two yardsticks, each measuring
the other, with no third yardstick above them. (See [Other Minds](other-minds.md).)

> **In plain terms —** when you and someone else clash, it isn't that one frame is the true
> one and the other false. It's that each of you is the standard *for yourself*, judging the
> other by it, and there's no neutral referee's chair anyone can sit in. That's why some
> disagreements feel unwinnable from the inside.

A subtlety: **indifference is still a relation.** Encountering something and filing it as
"not load-bearing for who I am" is *identity-neutral encapsulation* — a real, bounded,
low-propagation position in the network, not the absence of one. "Low-propagation" means
the thing is connected to you by such weak threads that it can't spread or reorganize
anything else about you; it's sealed off in a corner. And indifference can itself be
identity-bearing: a Stoic's *principled* indifference to insults is a high-**ρ** relation
to the whole *class* of insults, even as each individual insult gets encapsulated at low ρ.

> **ρ (rho, identity-correlation)** — in plain terms, how much of *you* is bound up in a
> thing; how much its loss would actually change who you are. High ρ = woven deep into you;
> low ρ = barely attached, easily shrugged off.

> **In plain terms —** not caring about something is itself a kind of caring-stance — you've
> placed it, you've decided it's a nobody in your world. And "I refuse, on principle, to be
> touched by insults" is the opposite of indifference at the deep level: the *policy* of not
> caring is one of the things you most are, even while each single insult bounces off.

## The prerogative of continued existence (PCE)

**The self structurally pulls toward the path that maximizes the continued existence of
the *maximally-self-defined I*.**

**PCE (the prerogative of continued existence)** — in plain terms, the part of you that
*wants to keep going*, that leans toward whatever path lets the self you actually are carry
on. Not bare survival of the body, but continuation of *you* as you currently count
yourself.

The "maximally-self-defined I" is not just the bare cogito — it's the action-relevant
configuration of the lived-I network whose preservation *counts*, under current
conditions, as you continuing. In other words: not just "a thinker exists" but "*this*
thinker, with *these* attachments and commitments, the one whose ending would be *your*
ending." PCE governs action **under existential pressure** — it's the tilt that shows up
when something is genuinely on the line. It operates always, and can be **defeated,
misdirected, or distorted** only under extreme, identifiable conditions.

PCE relies on a **preservation-ranking** (written **Π**, capital pi) over possible losses
and configurations. **Π is not ρ.** This separation is load-bearing — see below.

> **Π (Pi, the preservation-ranking)** — in plain terms, your in-the-moment ordering of
> *what you'd save first*. Faced with possible losses, something in you ranks them: this I
> could not bear to lose, this I could, this I'd give up to keep that. That ranking is Π. It
> may lean on ρ (how deep things are woven in), but it is a separate thing — a list of
> *what to protect*, not a measure of *how integrated* something is.

> **In plain terms —** ρ answers "how much is this part of me?" Π answers "if it came to it,
> what would I sacrifice for what?" Those are different questions, and keeping them apart is
> the key that unlocks the next section.

## Why ρ ≠ preservation-priority (the self-sacrifice case)

The clean test case: a parent dies pushing a child out of a car's path; a soldier covers
a grenade for comrades. On a naive reading — "the core I sits at ρ = 1 (you are, by
definition, maximally bound up in yourself), so it's preserved first" — these should be
impossible. They aren't. People do this; the theory has to fit it without flinching.

The resolution is that **ρ measures integration, not what you'd preserve.** ρ = 1 at the
cogito is a [mathematical anchor](identity-correlation-profile.md) — a normalization, the
way a thermometer fixes 0° at a chosen point, not a claim that you are the thing you'd save
above all. (Technically, ρ is computed as **max-flow** — the most "you-ness" that can flow
from your core to a thing through the network's connections — rooted at the cogito; the
core scores 1 simply because it's the source.) What drives action under pressure is **PCE
operating over the preservation-ranking Π**, not ρ-magnitude. A high-ρ *non-core* network
member — a person woven deep into you but who is not literally you — can drive action in
which the conditions for your own continued operation cease. And this is **consistent** with
the architecture, not a counterexample.

> **In plain terms —** the fact that you are, mathematically, "100% yourself" does not mean
> self-preservation always wins. Being maximally *part of you* (high ρ) is not the same as
> being first in line *to be saved* (high Π). A parent can be fully themselves and still,
> without contradiction, step in front of the car. The math never said otherwise.

The theory distinguishes (without ranking) at least three forms of such sacrifice:

- **Pre-reflective / substrate-routed** — the [third-order pathway](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)
  fires and the body moves before any deliberation. The hand shoots out, the body lunges, and
  only afterward (if ever) does the thinking self catch up to what happened.
- **Reflective** — Mode B (the self comparing imagined futures) compares *represented*
  future-states and prefers the one in which the network member continues. (From the inside,
  "valuing another's life over your own" is a comparison of represented futures — you never
  have direct access to the other's continuation; you are weighing two pictures *you* hold;
  see [Other Minds](other-minds.md).)
- **Mixed** — both at once, which is most real cases: the body already moving *and* the self
  endorsing it, with no clean line between them.

> **From the inside —** in the reflective version you are, in a flash, holding two imagined
> worlds: one where you're gone but they live, one where you live but they're gone. In the
> second, you picture carrying a them-shaped hole forward through every coming day. The
> choice for the first isn't heroism reasoned out — it's preferring the bearable picture.

> **In plain terms —** the architecture's claim here is modest and exact. It's a **negative
> result**: self-sacrifice does *not* require any non-core ρ to exceed 1 (nobody else has to
> become "more you than you"), and does *not* need a hidden valuation coordinate (no secret
> extra number bolted onto the theory). It explains why the case isn't a contradiction. It
> does *not* claim to predict who will jump. A full action-priority theory is named as
> [open work](predictions-and-exclusions.md), not delivered.

## How the two relate — and conflict

- **PtCns** governs **consistency-preservation in encounters** — keeping yourself *coherent*.
- **PCE** governs **action-priority under existential pressure** — keeping yourself *going*.

They're formally distinct but run in parallel, and they can **conflict** — most sharply
in **grief**. When someone woven deep into you (high ρ) dies, the two tilts pull opposite
ways: PtCns pushes the profile toward coherence with the loss (rebuild a self that fits a
world without them); PCE pushes to preserve the self that *included* the lost person at
high ρ (don't let that self go — keep them, keep being who-you-were-with-them). The
foundation distinguishes the two but **does not give a general rule for which wins** in a
given moment. "Prerogative arbitration" is listed as a genuine
[open problem](predictions-and-exclusions.md).

> **From the inside —** grief as a tug-of-war you can feel. One pull says *accept it, become
> someone who lives with this gone* — that's the drive to make sense again. The other says
> *no, I won't reorganize around their absence, because the me that's worth keeping is the
> me that had them*. Both are you. The theory is honest that it can't yet say which should
> win on a given day.

> **A hard case handled honestly.** In severe depression, a *functional* collapse of the
> partition between the I and its contents can route PCE through a distorted
> preservation-ranking, so that self-cessation is represented as completion or relief.
> The cogito is structurally untouched throughout; what fails is the I's *access* to it.
> This is covered under [Profile-Shape Disturbances](profile-shape-disturbances.md), and
> the foundation is explicit that the clinical work belongs to a dedicated application
> paper, not the foundation.

> **In plain terms —** PCE ("keep going") is not unbreakable. In severe depression the wall
> between *you* and *your darkest contents* can buckle, and the inner ranking (Π) gets
> bent so far that ending is mis-pictured as relief or as the only coherent path. The
> deep self is still there underneath — the failure is one of *reaching* it, not of its
> existence. The architecture flags this carefully and hands the actual clinical work to a
> dedicated paper; it is not a model of risk or treatment.
