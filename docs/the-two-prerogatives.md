# The Two Prerogatives

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [File 2, §4.8](../foundation/2_access_and_development.md); [File 4, §9.5, §10.7](../foundation/4_derived_concepts_and_predictions.md)

---

Alongside the [three modes](modes-of-development.md), the architecture names two
**structural prerogatives** — fundamental features without which a lived self couldn't
function. Neither is derivable from the bare [cogito](the-cogito.md); both are treated as
features any operating lived self exhibits. Neither is metaphysically inviolable.

## The prerogative of consistency (PtCns)

**Your own frame is the implicit standard against which encounters are evaluated.**

When you meet something that conflicts with your frame, it registers as *inconsistent* —
and the registration *itself* is the privileging of your frame as the standard. You don't
deliberate "perhaps my frame should change"; the very sense that something is "off" is
your frame acting as the consistency-anchor.

PtCns is what makes [Modes A and C](modes-of-development.md) possible at all. It's the
shared criterion underneath both:

- **Mode A** is typically PtCns-*driven*: when integrating something would damage
  consistency, you differentiate from it.
- **Mode C** is PtCns-*constrained*: positive identification can be driven by affinity,
  admiration, or aspiration, but integration only proceeds insofar as the profile can
  stay coherent.

It is **not** specific to other people — it operates on any encounter (a misbehaving
gadget, a contradicting fact, a violated expectation). What's special about meeting
**another mind** is that *two* PtCns structures operate at once, each treating the other
as not-I from its own frame, with no view-from-nowhere available to either. That's the
structural form of intersubjective disagreement. (See [Other Minds](other-minds.md).)

A subtlety: **indifference is still a relation.** Encountering something and filing it as
"not load-bearing for who I am" is *identity-neutral encapsulation* — a real, bounded,
low-propagation position in the network, not the absence of one. And indifference can
itself be identity-bearing: a Stoic's *principled* indifference to insults is a high-ρ
relation to the whole *class* of insults, even as each individual insult gets encapsulated
at low ρ.

## The prerogative of continued existence (PCE)

**The self structurally pulls toward the path that maximizes the continued existence of
the *maximally-self-defined I*.**

The "maximally-self-defined I" is not just the bare cogito — it's the action-relevant
configuration of the lived-I network whose preservation *counts*, under current
conditions, as you continuing. PCE governs action **under existential pressure**.
It operates always, and can be **defeated, misdirected, or distorted** only under extreme,
identifiable conditions.

PCE relies on a **preservation-ranking** (written Π) over possible losses and
configurations. **Π is not ρ.** This separation is load-bearing — see below.

## Why ρ ≠ preservation-priority (the self-sacrifice case)

The clean test case: a parent dies pushing a child out of a car's path; a soldier covers
a grenade for comrades. On a naive reading — "the core I sits at ρ = 1, so it's preserved
first" — these should be impossible. They aren't.

The resolution is that **ρ measures integration, not what you'd preserve.** ρ = 1 at the
cogito is a [mathematical anchor](identity-correlation-profile.md). What drives action
under pressure is **PCE operating over the preservation-ranking Π**, not ρ-magnitude. A
high-ρ *non-core* network member can drive action in which the conditions for your own
continued operation cease — and this is **consistent** with the architecture, not a
counterexample.

The theory distinguishes (without ranking) at least three forms of such sacrifice:

- **Pre-reflective / substrate-routed** — the [third-order pathway](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)
  fires and the body moves before any deliberation.
- **Reflective** — Mode B compares *represented* future-states and prefers the one in
  which the network member continues. (From the inside, "valuing another's life over your
  own" is a comparison of represented futures — you never have direct access to the
  other's continuation; see [Other Minds](other-minds.md).)
- **Mixed** — both at once, which is most real cases.

The architecture's claim here is the **negative result**: self-sacrifice does *not*
require any non-core ρ to exceed 1, and does *not* need a hidden valuation coordinate. A
full action-priority theory is named as [open work](predictions-and-exclusions.md), not
delivered.

## How the two relate — and conflict

- **PtCns** governs **consistency-preservation in encounters**.
- **PCE** governs **action-priority under existential pressure**.

They're formally distinct but run in parallel, and they can **conflict** — most sharply
in **grief** (PtCns pushes the profile toward coherence with the loss; PCE pushes to
preserve the self that included the lost person at high ρ). The foundation distinguishes
the two but **does not give a general rule for which wins** in a given moment.
"Prerogative arbitration" is listed as a genuine [open problem](predictions-and-exclusions.md).

> **A hard case handled honestly.** In severe depression, a *functional* collapse of the
> partition between the I and its contents can route PCE through a distorted
> preservation-ranking, so that self-cessation is represented as completion or relief.
> The cogito is structurally untouched throughout; what fails is the I's *access* to it.
> This is covered under [Profile-Shape Disturbances](profile-shape-disturbances.md), and
> the foundation is explicit that the clinical work belongs to a dedicated application
> paper, not the foundation.
