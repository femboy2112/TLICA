# Glossary

[← Wiki home](README.md)

Every term and symbol in one place. This page is the home of the **technical → everyday** mapping: each entry has a *formal one-liner* (what the theory says) and a separate ***Plain:*** rendering (what it means in ordinary language, ideally what it feels like from the inside). Links point to the page where each is developed.

> **In plain terms —** if you only ever read one page to "get" the vocabulary, read this one. The big words are mostly fancy names for things you already live: how much something is touching you, how undeniable it seems, and how much of *you* is wrapped up in it.

## Symbols

The theory uses a handful of Greek letters and a few Latin ones. Don't let them scare you — each is shorthand for a plain idea. Here is the whole alphabet in one place; fuller treatment is on the linked pages.

| Symbol | Name | Formal meaning | *Plain:* everyday rendering |
|---|---|---|---|
| **κ** (kappa) | Contact | How much a content is currently interacting with you — the world impinging on you right now. [→](three-coordinates.md) | *Plain:* how much a thing is **touching you this second**. A stubbed toe is high κ; a friend in another country is low κ. |
| **φ** (phi) | Truth-indistinguishability | How indistinguishable-from-true a content is under your reasoning. [→](three-coordinates.md) | *Plain:* how **undeniable** it feels. φ is high when you can't reason your way out of believing it; low when it's shaky or you can't test it. |
| **ρ** (rho) | Identity-correlation | How integrated a content is into you; formally, max-flow from the cogito through your lived network. [→](three-coordinates.md) | *Plain:* how much of **you** is bound up in a thing — the stuff whose loss would actually change who you are. Your child: high ρ. A stranger's name: near-zero ρ. |
| **δ** (delta) | The infinitesimal | The cost of *one perspectival step* from universal content to indexed (perspective-having) content. [→](the-cogito.md) | *Plain:* a vanishingly tiny gap — smaller than any real number. It's the hair's breadth between "x = x is just true" and "*I* am *I*," because the second one has a someone in it. |
| **Î** (iota-hat) | The cogito / core I | The indexed self; "I am I." Sits at φ = 1 − δ, ρ = 1. [→](the-cogito.md) | *Plain:* the bare fact that there is a **you** at the center of all this — the "I" that can't be denied without somebody there to do the denying. |
| **φ = 1 − δ** | (cogito's position) | The cogito's structural position: infinitesimally short of intrinsic truth. [→](the-cogito.md) | *Plain:* you are as close to rock-bottom certain as anything that has a perspective can get — one tiny step shy of pure logic itself. |
| **P** (or `P_{m,t}`) | The profile | The identity-correlation profile — the whole map of ρ across your field at a time; the theory's primary object. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md) | *Plain:* the **whole shape of your self** laid out as a map — not one number, but the full landscape of what's central, what's peripheral, what's not-you. |
| **Π** (Pi) | Preservation-ranking | What the self pulls to preserve under pressure (`Π_{m,t}`). Depends on ρ but is *not* ρ. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md) | *Plain:* in a crisis, **what you'd save first**. Related to how integrated things are, but not the same — a parent will die for a child without the child ever having ρ = 1. |
| **A** (or `A_{m,t}`) | Phenomenal availability | Whether a content is present-to-you right now (a status, 0 or 1, not a fourth coordinate). [→](two-layers.md) | *Plain:* simply whether something is **showing up in your awareness right now** or running silently in the background. |
| **n** (`n_{m,t}`) | Conditional depth | The number of indexed reasoning steps from universal truth down to a content, given your current tools. [→](three-coordinates.md) | *Plain:* **how many inference-steps away** from bedrock logic a belief sits, for you, with the tools you've got. More steps = a bit less certain. |
| **ε** (epsilon) | Empirical robustness uncertainty | The wobble in a content's truth-standing within a given depth, from probe-testing (`ε_{m,t}`). [→](three-coordinates.md) | *Plain:* the **extra slack** for "well, it could still be wrong" — how much poking-and-prodding shakes it. |
| **σ** (sigma) | Source map | What an item's contents trace back to in primal not-I (`σ_{m,t}`); used to define contact. Outside-perspective. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md) | *Plain:* **where a feeling or thought actually came from** — which you usually *can't* fully see from the inside. |
| **K / k** | Integration modes | The set of distinct ways something can be woven into you; ρ is computed per-mode then combined. [→](three-coordinates.md) | *Plain:* there's more than one **way to belong to you** (bodily, emotionally, by commitment…). ρ is figured out in each channel and blended. |
| **G** (`G^{m,k}_t`) | Integration graph | The weighted directed network, per mode, over which ρ's max-flow is computed. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md) | *Plain:* picture your self as a **web of connections** rooted at "I." How strong the path is from "I" to a thing is how much it's yours. |
| **C** (`C^{m,k}_t`) | Integration capacity | The raw max-flow value from cogito to a content, before being squashed into `[0, 1)`. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md) | *Plain:* the **raw strength of connection** before it's put on a 0-to-1 scale. ρ = C/(1+C), which is why nothing but the core ever quite reaches 1. |
| **M** (`M_m(t)`) | Focus capacity | The substrate-set bound on how many contents you can hold in focus at once. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md) | *Plain:* **how many balls you can keep in the air** — fixed by your hardware, not chosen by you. |
| **Foc** (`Foc^m_t`) | Focus | Your allocation/selection of attention across available contents at a time. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md) | *Plain:* **where your attention is pointed right now** — part grabbed by the world, part steered by you. |
| **D** (`D_{m,t}`) | Mediation depth | Which substrate pathway-order (1st/2nd/3rd) dominantly delivers a content. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md) | *Plain:* **how many filters a thing passed through** to reach you — a flash of light (few) vs. a wave of grief (many). |
| **Fz** (`Fz_{m,t}`) | Fuzziness | Low explicit information density / poor source-resolution from inside. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md) | *Plain:* **"I feel it but I can't put it into words."** Not weak, not less-conscious — just blurry about *what* and *why*. |
| **N** (`N_m`) | Primal not-I | The posited field of what exists independent of you. [→](two-layers.md) | *Plain:* **everything that isn't you** and was there before you — world, time, body, other people. |
| **T** (`T_m`) | Time | The posited time-order with a developmental origin 0. (Also re-used as **T** = truth-channel in the empathy paper — see below.) [→](two-layers.md) | *Plain:* **the clock you were born into** — not something the bare "I" can conjure; it had to be posited. |
| **δ-extended field** | Hyperreals (𝕂) | An ordered field extending ℝ that contains δ; the math home of the infinitesimal. [→](the-cogito.md) | *Plain:* a **bigger number-line** that has room for a quantity tinier than any ordinary fraction — that's where δ lives. |

> **In plain terms —** the three letters that do almost all the work are **κ, φ, ρ**: *is it touching me, does it feel undeniable, is it part of me.* The rest are bookkeeping for how those three are built and measured.

> **From the inside —** right now, your phone in your hand is high-κ (you feel it), high-φ (obviously real), and middling-ρ (losing it would annoy you, not unmake you). Your name, by contrast, is low-κ (you're not touching it), high-φ, and very high-ρ. Same field, three different readings.

## Terms

### The two layers and the intrinsic commitments

**Intrinsic layer** — mathematics, logic, and reason, treated as real *in re* (in the world,
mind-independent). Outside the φ-scale entirely. [→](two-layers.md)
*Plain:* the stuff that would be true even if nobody were around to think it — like "two stones are two stones" whether or not anyone counts. It's not "very certain"; it's the ruler everything else is measured against.

**Asymptotic layer** — everything else in your field, conscious or unconscious; it
approaches intrinsic truth without ever reaching it. The name is the analysis sense of
*asymptotic* (forever-approaching a limit, never arriving) — which is *why* the layer is so called:
its contents are known only in the limit, never with certainty. Keep two readings apart: the
asymptotic **stratum** (world-level, un-indexed — one of {intrinsic, asymptotic} in reality) versus
**`𝖠^m_t`**, *your own* indexed asymptotic content domain. [→](two-layers.md)
*Plain:* literally everything you actually experience — every thought, feeling, perception, memory. All of it gets *close* to certain but never lands on bedrock; that's why it's "asymptotic" (forever-approaching). Mind the two uses: the layer *as a feature of reality* vs. *your* particular contents.

> **In plain terms —** there's bedrock (math/logic, which just *holds*), and there's everything you live (which leans toward bedrock but never is it). You are always on the second floor, looking down.

**In re** — Latin "in the thing." The claim that mathematical/logical structure is in the
world's structure, not in a separate Platonic realm. [→](two-layers.md)
*Plain:* the math is **baked into reality itself**, not floating in some heaven of perfect forms. The river that nudges one stone beside another has *made two*, with no mathematician present.

**Naturalist-realism** — the architecture's specific, deliberately weak commitment about the
intrinsic layer: structure is real *in* the world, without the stronger Platonist claim of a separate realm of abstract objects.
[→](two-layers.md)
*Plain:* "the patterns are really out there in nature" — said in the most modest way that still does the job, so you don't have to buy into anything spooky.

**Cogito** — the single postulate: existence-as-an-I, whose form is self-identity (Î = Î). A
postulate, not a proof. [→](the-cogito.md)
*Plain:* the one free assumption the whole theory rests on — **"there is a me, and that me is itself."** Not derived from logic; just acknowledged, because any attempt to deny it needs a someone to do the denying.

> **In plain terms —** unlike Descartes ("I think, *therefore* I am"), this doesn't try to *prove* you exist. It just notices you already do, and reads off the shape of that fact: you = you.

### Access to intrinsic structure

**Verification tool** — an acquired recognition capacity for an intrinsic structure
(object permanence, cause-and-effect, modal reasoning, induction). What lets you assign φ. [→](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md)
*Plain:* a **mental skill for spotting a pattern** that you weren't born with but picked up — like the toddler's hard-won grasp that a toy still exists behind the couch. Each tool is a new way to test whether something's true.

**Three kinds of encounter** — how tools and structure are acquired: *direct*
world-encounter, *shadow* encounter (recognizing structure through its traces), and
*self-encounter* through meta-reasoning. [→](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md)
*Plain:* three ways the world teaches you its patterns — **bumping into it directly**, **noticing the footprints it leaves**, and **catching yourself reasoning and reasoning about that**.

**Developmental escalator** — tools imprint in an order set by temporal scale;
short-timescale tools first, each unlocking perception of slower structure. [→](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md)
*Plain:* you learn the **fast, obvious patterns first** (things fall, things persist), and only those let you eventually notice the **slow, sweeping ones** (seasons, consequences, lifetimes). You can't skip a step.

> **In plain terms —** nobody hands you "object permanence" or "cause and effect" at birth. You earn them by living, in a rough order, and each one is a lens that brings the next, slower pattern into focus.

### The modes of development

**Mode A — adversarial differentiation** — drawing the I/not-I boundary by registering
something as discrepant. Produces the boundary. [→](modes-of-development.md)
*Plain:* the **"that's not me" reflex.** Whenever something clashes with your sense of how things should be, the clash itself draws a line around you. This is how a "self" gets an edge in the first place.

**Mode C — positive identification** — folding something registered as continuous-with-you
inside the boundary. Populates the self. [→](modes-of-development.md)
*Plain:* the **"that's mine / that's me" pull.** When something feels like an extension of you, you absorb it. Most of who you are gets built this way — the baby who screams when the candy is taken has made the candy part of herself.

**Mode B — reflexive meta-reasoning** — turning attention on your own structure; refines
the whole. Can't run until there's something to reflect on. [→](modes-of-development.md)
*Plain:* **thinking about yourself thinking.** Standing back and examining your own mind. It can't start at birth (there's nothing yet to examine) but once you have some self built up, it sharpens everything.

> **In plain terms —** A draws your outline, C colors you in, B steps back and touches up the picture. The theory does *not* predict which one fires on a given day — only what each does when it does fire.

### The two prerogatives

**Prerogative of consistency (PtCns)** — your frame as the implicit standard against which
encounters are evaluated; underlies Modes A and C. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)
*Plain:* you treat **your own way of seeing as the default-correct one**, without ever deciding to. When something fits, you absorb it; when it jars, you push it away. You don't pause to wonder if *you're* the one who's wrong — the very feeling of "off" already assumes you're the ruler.

**Prerogative of continued existence (PCE)** — the structural pull toward preserving the
maximally-self-defined I. Governs action under existential pressure. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)
*Plain:* the deep **drive to keep being you.** Under real threat, you lean toward the path that keeps "you" going. It's always running — but, crucially, it can be misdirected or defeated (this is the theory's structural account of how suicidality is even possible).

**Maximally-self-defined I** — the action-relevant configuration of the lived self whose
preservation counts, under current conditions, as you continuing. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)
*Plain:* **"you" in the sense that matters for survival** — not just the bare core, but the whole working configuration of self you're actually trying to keep. Sometimes that includes people you'd die for.

**Identity-neutral encapsulation** — incorporating something as bounded / non-self-defining;
a real low-propagation relation in the graph, not the absence of one. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)
*Plain:* filing something under **"noted, but it's not about me."** Indifference isn't a blank — it's an active "this stays in its box and doesn't reshape who I am." (And being principled-ly indifferent to insults can itself be deeply you.)

> **In plain terms —** two engine-level pulls run your lived self: *keep my view coherent* (PtCns) and *keep existing* (PCE). Neither is sacred or unbreakable; both are just structurally there.

### Imprinting and the substrate

**Three orders of imprinting** — pathways by which contents reach you, each lossier than
the last: *first-order* (salience capture, sharp), *second-order* (thoughts, clear),
*third-order* (emotions/sensations, intense-but-fuzzy). [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)
*Plain:* things reach you through **filters, and the more filters, the more blurred.** A loud bang (one filter) arrives crisp; a thought (two filters) arrives structured; an emotion (three filters) arrives powerful but vague.

**Fuzziness** — low explicit information density / low source-resolution. *Not* low
intensity and *not* less-conscious. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)
*Plain:* **strong but shapeless.** A panic attack is screaming-loud yet says nothing more specific than "something is terribly wrong." Fully conscious, just blurry — not "almost unconscious."

**Osmotic imprinting** — substrate-level pattern formation by repeated ambient
co-occurrence; always operating; needs no focus or verification. Pavlov's dog is the pure
case. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)
*Plain:* the way things **soak into you just by being around** — no attention, no effort. The smell that suddenly makes you anxious; the accent you trust or distrust without knowing why. It's running all the time, even in sleep.

**Four kinds of imprinting** — content, contact, verification-tool, and mode-development
imprinting. (Osmotic imprinting runs transverse to all four.) [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)
*Plain:* the **four things that can get updated in you over time** — what's part of you, what you're in touch with, what skills you have for testing truth, and how your A/B/C machinery itself develops. Osmosis can sneak into any of them.

> **In plain terms —** most of what shapes you doesn't come through the front door of attention. It seeps in, gets compressed on the way, and shows up later as a feeling you can't explain.

### The profile and its parts

**Lived I** — the historically accumulated network of integrations rooted at the cogito;
the support of the profile where ρ > 0. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md)
*Plain:* **everything that has become "you" so far** — your body, memories, loves, commitments — as a connected web growing out from the bare "I." Newborns barely have one; it's built over a lifetime.

**Shells** — contour bands of the profile at ρ-thresholds; a visualization aid, not a
primitive. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md)
*Plain:* think of you as **onion rings of closeness** — innermost is the core, then body, then deep loves and commitments, out to passing strangers. Just a way to picture the profile, not a separate thing.

**Profile-shape disturbance** — a disturbance of the self understood as a change in the
*shape* of the profile, not its magnitude. [→](profile-shape-disturbances.md)
*Plain:* mental illness isn't "less self" or "more self" — it's the **map getting bent into a bad shape.** Depression isn't low-everything; it's the landscape collapsing and narrowing in a characteristic way.

**Developmental window** — the period (developmental *or* situational-overwhelm) where
conscious direction can't overcome substrate pressure; patterns rooted there aren't the
I's chosen errors. [→](profile-shape-disturbances.md)
*Plain:* times when **you couldn't have steered** — early childhood, or any moment so overwhelming (trauma) that your will simply wasn't in the driver's seat. What forms then isn't your fault.

**Four content classes** — by phenomenal availability × φ-status: *conscious-clear*,
*conscious-fuzzy / source-opaque*, *latent / retrievable*, *unconscious-operative*. [→](two-layers.md)
*Plain:* four states a thought or feeling can be in — **clear-and-present** (worked-out arithmetic), **present-but-murky** (dread you can't source), **filed-away-but-fetchable** (a fact you're not thinking of), and **silently-pulling-strings** (a bias you don't know you have).

> **In plain terms —** your profile is your self drawn as a map of closeness. Health and illness, on this theory, are about the *shape* of that map — and a lot of the worst shapes were drawn at times you couldn't hold the pen.

### Other minds and the form of the architecture

**Perspectival realism** — other minds are real but, from any one perspective, reached
only indirectly. Not solipsism. [→](other-minds.md)
*Plain:* **other people genuinely exist** — but you only ever reach them through your own model of them. You're not alone in the universe; you're just always looking through your own window.

**Form-invariance (conditional on existence)** — every mind builds the *same* architecture
for itself; the form is shared, the lived instances are not. [→](other-minds.md)
*Plain:* **everyone's inner machinery has the same blueprint**, even though everyone's actual life is different. The shape is universal; the content is yours alone.

**Primal not-I** — the posited field independent of the I: content multiplicity, time,
body, contact interface, optional other minds. [→](predictions-and-exclusions.md)
*Plain:* the **starter kit the theory has to assume** because the bare "I" can't conjure it: that there's more than one thing, that time passes, that you have a body, that the world can touch you, and maybe that other minds are out there.

### The word "field" — one word, a nested scope ladder

**Field** — TLICA reuses "field" for a *single nested structure seen at several scopes*, not
as unrelated homonyms, so every use must name its scope. The scopes nest *conceptually* (not by
strict set-inclusion): **(4) reality** — the whole system {intrinsic, asymptotic}; **(1) your
view of reality** — the accessible / phenomenal field `𝒰 ≈ 𝖠⁺`, a representation *of* (4) located
*within* (4); **(2) your available choices** — the filter/operator space `𝓕` you act from, developed
in [choice-as-filter](app-choice-as-filter.md). What is sometimes numbered **(3)** is not a fourth
field but a *claim about (2)*: choosing applies a filter over the choice-field rather than selecting
a point. Three uses sit *off* the ladder: the **situational field** `S` — the external
incentive/social environment an agent is tested against ("field-flattening"), which is a slice of
the primal not-I (`S ⊂ 𝖭 ⊂ 4`); the everyday **"field of X"** (a domain or area, carrying no
technical scope); and, fenced off entirely, the **algebraic field** (the ordered field `𝕂` /
surreals — the home of δ; genuine mathematics, not a scope; see *δ-extended field* above). The
load-bearing boundary is **(4)↔(1)**: what you hold in view is a representation *of* the not-I,
never the not-I itself — the information lives in `𝖭`, and your real-time picture only *corresponds
to* it across that wall. Collapsing that wall — mistaking the map for the territory — is the one
confusion this whole vocabulary exists to prevent. [→](two-layers.md)
*Plain:* one small word doing several jobs, and you always have to say which. **Reality** (everything
there is); **your view of it** (the world as it shows up to you); **your live options** (what you can
actually do from where you stand) — with the note that you *reweight* those options, you don't pick
them off a shelf one at a time; the **situation you're dropped into** (the incentives and audience
around you — part of the world, not part of you); plain **"a field of X"** (just an area, like "a
field of study"); and, kept firmly in its own box, the **math** meaning (a kind of number system).
The one mistake never to make: treating *the world* and *your picture of the world* as the same
thing. They line up — they are not identical.

## Application-paper terms

From the [application papers](applications.md). Foundation terms above; these are introduced
in specific applications. Each still gets a *formal* line and a ***Plain:*** line.

**Bandwidth-bounded integration** — fixed mental capacity, increasingly spent on
integrating a growing self, drives subjective time to accelerate with age. [→](app-temporal-phenomenology.md)
*Plain:* **why years feel faster as you get older.** Your mental "RAM" is fixed, but more and more of it goes to running the big self you've accumulated — so there's less left over to make time feel slow and full.

**Specious present** — the felt ~2–3 second "now," modeled as one span of focus holding
retention + the impressional moment + protention. [→](app-temporal-phenomenology.md)
*Plain:* the **"now" isn't an instant** — it's a couple of seconds wide, holding the just-gone, the right-here, and the about-to-be all at once. That's the width of a single grip of attention.

**Constitutive extension** — folding another into the self so deeply that their loss is
*damage to you*; the structural definition of **love** and the seed of the affect taxonomy.
[→](app-differentiated-affect.md)
*Plain:* **love, defined structurally** — letting someone so far into you that losing them would tear a piece *out of you*, not just sadden you. This is the root the theory grows its whole map of emotions from.

**Scope hierarchy (of self-evaluation)** — guilt → embarrassment → shame → self-loathing as the
same reflexive operation at widening scopes (act → presentation → identity-aspect → whole self). [→](app-differentiated-affect.md)
*Plain:* the **same self-judging move aimed at bigger and bigger targets.** Guilt: "I did a bad thing." Shame: "a part of me is bad." Self-loathing: "*I* am bad." One mechanism, zooming out.

**Six conditions of free choice** — live-option space, evaluative engagement, operator-bundle
selection, non-preemptive substrate coupling, non-confabulatory Mode B availability,
ownership-integration. [→](app-free-will.md)
*Plain:* the **six things that have to be in place for a choice to count as genuinely free** — roughly: real options exist, you actually weighed them, you picked, your body didn't decide first, you're not lying to yourself about why, and you own the result.

**Ownership vs. sourcehood** — owning a choice from inside and where it was sourced from outside
are dissociable; the crux of manipulation. [→](app-free-will.md)
*Plain:* **"I chose this" and "where this choice came from" are two different things.** A manipulated person fully owns a choice that was quietly planted in them — which is exactly why manipulation is so slippery.

**Orders of willing** — willing reaching outward: first (focus), second (thought), third (action),
and constructed orders 4+ (through people and institutions). [→](app-agency-architecture.md)
*Plain:* the **reach of your will, in widening rings** — you can steer your attention, then your thoughts, then your body, and finally (through other people and organizations) things far beyond your own arms.

**Attribution profile (ownership / responsibility / accountability)** — graded, profile-indexed,
never-zero-sum responsibility apparatus. [→](app-agency-architecture.md)
*Plain:* **blame and credit come in degrees and can be shared.** Responsibility isn't a single hot potato that lands on exactly one person — multiple people can each genuinely own a slice.

**Epistemic closure / "larger cave"** — escape from a cave is real but is always ascent to a
bigger cave, never an exit; Plato's allegory derived as closure dynamics. [→](app-out-of-the-cave.md)
*Plain:* **you can always learn more, but you never step outside knowing-from-a-viewpoint.** Every breakthrough is a move to a roomier cave, not the open air. There's no final exit.

**Source-map (σ) error** — being *correctly ordered and wrongly sourced*; the prisoners' real
mistake, available only because the theory separates truth from source. [→](app-out-of-the-cave.md)
*Plain:* getting the **pattern right but the origin wrong** — like correctly predicting the shadows' order while believing the shadows are the real things. You can be accurate and still deeply mistaken about *what you're looking at.*

**Truth-channel (T) / affect-routing channel (R) / λ** — the split of "empathy" into modeling
the other as real (T) and their state landing on you (R), coupled by λ. [→](app-referent-routing.md)
*Plain:* **empathy is two dials, not one.** **T** is "I know you're a real person with a real inner life." **R** is "your pain actually lands on me and moves me." **λ (lambda)** is how tightly the first dial is wired to the second.

**The cold frame** — treating another I as a non-routed instrument while modeling them fully;
reached by *referent-thinning*, not by lying. [→](app-referent-routing.md)
*Plain:* seeing someone **clearly but without letting them land on you** — high T, low R. Not achieved by denying they're real, but by quietly turning down how much their reality matters to you. This is how cruelty can be clear-eyed.

**Bindingness without desert** — morality can be authoritative even though where you land on it
is unearned. [→](app-referent-routing.md)
*Plain:* **the rules can really bind you even if you didn't earn your starting line.** That you happened to develop a conscience (or didn't) is unchosen luck — and morality still has a grip on you anyway.

> **In plain terms —** the application papers take the same κ/φ/ρ/profile machinery and point it at familiar human things — why time speeds up, what love is, when a choice is free, why empathy can switch off — and show the machinery already had the answers built in.

## Borrowed terms — scope pins

The archive is a philosophy built *using* mathematics, so it borrows math/physics-general words
and reuses them in specific technical senses. Each entry below states the TLICA sense(s) and
*fences* the off-the-shelf meaning, so no reader imports the wrong one. See **The word "field"**
above for the worked case — the same discipline applies to all of these.

**The governing move is dimensional analysis, not equality.** When TLICA reaches for a
math/physics word, the claim is that the thing described *has the same mathematical **shape*** as
the term — its structure, its dimensions — **not** that it literally obeys the term's law or
carries its units. That is exactly the framework's thesis that consciousness has a
*structurally-reasonable* aspect, so these borrows are **motivated**, not ornamental: "momentum"
asserts a directional inertia (a magnitude-and-direction that takes work to turn), not a conserved
`kg·m/s`; "field" asserts a domain-with-dynamics, not an algebraic field. So each entry states the
*shape* that transfers — load-bearing, and asserted — and fences the *literal* law or quantity,
which does **not** follow from the analogy. That fuller correspondence (the exact units,
specificities, and law) is a **separate** claim, to be *earned* by justification and never taken
for free: until earned it stays open, and where it would be a category error it is denied. When it
*is* earned — the grokking Fourier/gauge mathematics, the caves' Lagrange points — the borrow
graduates from analogy to genuine math, and that real-math sense is **named and kept**, not fenced
away.

**Resonance** — four distinct senses, not one. **Theological resonance**: a named
framework-translation scope-item — the architecture is *translatable into* theological framing
without deriving or requiring theology (translational, not felt). **Felt resonance / `R_i`**: the
experienced *fit* of a content to your self-model (the phenomenon, with scalar `R_i(c)`) —
explicitly **not** truth, not φ, not ρ (a resonant path can be false). **Substrate-mediated
resonance**: embodied empathic co-activation with a represented other's state. And the ordinary
**"has resonance with X"** (accords/echoes-with, non-technical). ρ is a max-flow coordinate, never
"resonance." [→](app-differentiated-affect.md)
*Plain:* one word, four jobs — a bridge to religious language; a *feeling that something fits you* (which can be flat wrong); your body catching someone else's pain; and plain "chimes with." None of them means "true," and none is how-much-it's-you.

**Projection** — the anchor is the Mode-B faculty that constructs/simulates possible **future or
counterfactual states** (the "projection-toolset"; threat-projection and PCE-over-projection build
on it). Fenced off: the **mathematical projection** of the profile onto a coordinate/threshold (a
coarse-graining — genuine linear algebra), and the ordinary "projected [book] series" / the fire
*casting* shadows in the cave. **Not** Freudian "projecting one's own traits onto others."
[→](app-differentiated-affect.md)
*Plain:* mostly it means **running the mental simulator** — picturing how things could go. Two off-to-the-side uses: the math one (squashing the profile onto one axis) and plain "casting"/"forecasting." It is *not* the pop-psych "seeing your own flaws in someone else."

**Projection (mathematical)** — the genuine linear-algebra sense fenced from the entry above: a
coarse-grained projection of the profile `P_{m,t}` onto a coordinate or threshold. Real math, used
correctly (foundation formal apparatus). [→](identity-correlation-profile.md)
*Plain:* the ordinary math meaning — reading off **one axis of a many-dimensional thing** (a single ρ-value is the profile projected onto one coordinate).

**Phase** — several senses. The foundation's numbered **developmental Phases 0–3** (a named
construct; Phases 0–3 ≠ paper "regimes"). **Phase-change**: a metastable affective state tipping
suddenly — the physics phase-*transition* used as an acknowledged metaphor, not a literal
thermodynamic claim. The genuine **oscillatory / Fourier phase** (signal-processing, in the grokking
analysis). And the ordinary "a phase/period." [→](app-differentiated-affect.md)
*Plain:* four jobs — the **numbered stages** a self grows through; a mood **flipping** all at once (borrowed from ice-to-water, but only as a picture); the real wave/signal meaning (only in the AI analysis); and plain "a phase you go through."

**Topology** — two technical senses. The anchor: the inside/outside **inclusion shape** of the
identity-correlation profile / constitutive extension ("topology preserved vs change"; love = the
topological inclusion of another) — point-set flavor, invariant while κ/φ vary. Distinct:
**graph/network topology** (connectivity, bridges, connected components, closure) of the
learning/evidence graph in the grokking work. [→](app-differentiated-affect.md)
*Plain:* two jobs — **who's inside vs outside "you"** (and love = pulling someone *inside*), which stays put even as feelings change; and, separately, the **shape of a network** (what's connected to what).

**Basis** — the anchor is the linear-algebra **basis** (representational/semantic basis vectors
spanning a space: "changing the basis," "basis enrichment," "ideological basis vectors"). Fenced
off: the ordinary "grounds/foundation" ("on the basis of," "X is the basis"). Pairs with **Vector**.
[→](app-shared-reality-divergent-maps.md)
*Plain:* mostly the math meaning — the **set of building-block directions** you represent things in (change the basis, you change what's easy to see). The everyday "on the basis of" is a different, non-technical word.

**Vector** — uniform: the linear-algebra **vector** (an ordered, directional, multi-component
quantity — a value-vector, state-vector, or basis-vector). Pairs with **Basis**.
[→](app-shared-reality-divergent-maps.md)
*Plain:* the math meaning throughout — a **little arrow of numbers** (e.g. a set of values moving together over time), not "a means of transmission."

**Geometry** — the anchor is the structural / relational **shape** (attention geometry, basin
geometry, the geometry of choosing — and the title *The Geometry of Actualization*). Fenced off:
literal **differential/analytic geometry** (the parabola and Lagrange points in the caves paper —
genuine math), and "geometric" as a reasoning-type in a list. [→](app-choice-as-filter.md)
*Plain:* mostly the **shape/layout of a thing** (how your options are arranged, how attention is laid out). The literal math meaning shows up only where there's an actual parabola; "geometric reasoning" in a list is just a category.

**Metric** — two senses, kept apart. The mathematical **distance function** (`d_X`, a world-space or
experienced metric; metric expansion) — genuine math. And the **measurement / yardstick** sense (a
"truth metric," "metric-distortion," "shared metric," "closure metric"). [→](app-out-of-the-cave.md)
*Plain:* two jobs — a real **distance-measure** (how far apart two things are in some space) and a plain **yardstick/score** (the standard you judge by, which can get distorted). Same word; don't fuse them.

**Amplitude** — uniform: the **magnitude/intensity** of affect or reactivity (reactivity-amplitude;
the amplitude oscillation of self-worth). The paper's own fence: "amplitude, not measurement." The
physics wave-amplitude is the disclaimed source. [→](app-self-applied-architecture.md)
*Plain:* **how big/loud a feeling is** — the volume knob on affect — not a measurement and not a literal wave.

**Gauge** — uniform: the physics **gauge-theory** borrow (gauge freedom, gauge fixing,
relative/global gauge, one gauge parameter per connection) used for the representational-alignment /
connected-overlap machinery in the grokking work. [→](grokking-toolkit-closure.md)
*Plain:* borrowed from physics — the idea that some **choices of "zero point" are free** and don't change the real content, only how it's described. Lives entirely in the AI analysis.

**Quotient** — uniform and genuine: the algebraic **quotient** by an equivalence relation —
discrete options recovered as quotient classes of filters (`𝒪 = 𝓕/∼`). Real mathematics, used
correctly; see **The word "field"** (scope 2). [→](app-choice-as-filter.md)
*Plain:* the math meaning — **grouping many fine-grained things into a few "same-for-our-purposes" buckets** (many filters that land on the same choice = one option).

**Fourier** — uniform and genuine: **Fourier** basis / phase / components in the grokking
transformer analysis — real signal-processing mathematics, used correctly. Not a literal
TLICA-framework mechanism. [→](grokking-toolkit-closure.md)
*Plain:* the real math meaning (breaking a signal into pure waves), and only ever about the **AI model** being analyzed — never a claim that a mind literally runs on it.

*The following are the control-theory / dynamical-systems family — motivated shape-borrows in the sense of the section intro.*

**Transport** — two senses. The anchor (*This Is Water*): the **epistemic-to-agential transport**
of a consideration from *availability* to *timely exercise* — the "availability-to-exercise gap,"
with online and compiled routes. It is an **architectural transport claim** about carrying a
content across a gap, explicitly *"not a theorem that self-knowledge produces truth, virtue, or the
uniquely right action."* Distinct genuine-math sense (grokking): a **declared transport `T_ij`** —
an operationally specified map between local charts/toolkits (change of coordinates, symbol
alignment, gauge choice) that must be *declared* before any cross-toolkit phase or linear-coherence
claim (*"no declared transport, no legitimate claim"*). Shape borrowed: *carrying a structure
across a boundary along a specified map*; not literal parallel transport of a physical vector unless
a common structure is supplied. Distinct from inter-mind **Transfer**. [→](app-this-is-water.md)
*Plain:* moving something across a gap along a stated route — inside one mind, a known consideration finally becoming *usable* right when it's needed; in the AI analysis, a spelled-out map between two coordinate systems. The carry-across-a-map *shape* is the point, not literal physics.

**Momentum** — uniform: **perceptual momentum**, the resistance of the current perceptual-substrate
trajectory to chosen reweighting (low momentum → a small filter-shift yields a large response-shift;
defined *toward a response-basin `B`*). Shape borrowed: a **directional inertia** — a
magnitude-and-direction that takes work to turn — explicitly *"not a new force,"* not a conserved
quantity, and *"not a scalar trait of the person"* (the error to avoid: a person-level willpower
score). Pairs with **Basin**. [→](app-choice-as-filter.md)
*Plain:* how hard your current way-of-perceiving is to swerve — it has a direction and a heft, so turning it takes work. The *shape* of momentum, not a real force and not a "how strong-willed you are" number.

**Slack** — uniform: the **residual room the unchosen parameters (`λ`, margin, field) leave open**,
on which individual willing operates — its *access* itself gated by the toolkit's self-model of the
will's own reach (hence "stuck but capable": the slack is present but the self-model never certifies
the attempt, so it sits unused and invisible). Shape borrowed: engineering/optimization **slack** —
the unused latitude in a constrained system — *"not libertarian free will, nor hard determinism"*;
the will is a bounded boundary-term working a genuine residual. [→](app-referent-routing.md)
*Plain:* the wiggle-room left after everything you didn't choose has had its say — where your will actually works. You can only use the slack your self-model lets you see, which is why you can be genuinely capable and still stuck.

**Reachability** — genuine control-theory borrow, uniform: the **attainability of a state or route
under the system's own dynamics**, always toolkit-/dynamics-relative — sharpened as
**κ-reachability** (a probe must physically contact beyond the current set) and as the grokking
"accessibility" (your current dynamics contain a *reachable route* to deploy a capability). Shape
borrowed: *which states the system can actually get to from here*; an unreached state is a
pathway/probe/access gap, **not** proven impossible. Pairs with **Traversability**. [→](app-out-of-the-cave.md)
*Plain:* what you can actually get to from where you are, given how you actually work — not what's abstractly possible. Can't reach it? That's a gap to explain, not proof it can't be done.

**Basin** — two senses, both the *shape* of a region states settle into. **Response basin**
(choice_as_filter): a class of total states converging on the same macroscopic bodily response
(remain still, withdraw, speak) — the target `B` perceptual momentum runs toward. **Developmental
basin** (born-good research): a broad/deep region of configuration space a developing self tends to
settle in (*"goodness as a basin, not a substance"*) — which can be *"shallow, domain-specific,
punctured by stress, or exited after cumulative load."* Shape borrowed: a **basin of attraction**;
not a claim of a fitted state-space with measured depth. Pairs with **Attractor**, **Momentum**. [→](app-choice-as-filter.md)
*Plain:* a low spot things roll into and tend to stay — either the bodily response you settle onto in a moment, or the broad character-region a person grows into. A basin can be shallow or escapable; it's the shape, not a measured landscape.

**Attractor** — two senses. **Developmental/dynamical attractor** (born-good research): a
configuration a system tends toward under supportive conditions (prosociality as *"a natural
developmental attractor... without being an innate moral essence"*) — flagged as *a dynamical
conjecture*, not inevitability, global stability, or essence. **Value/aspirational attractor**
(*This Is Water*, *Self-Applied*): an organizing end one is drawn toward, which *"can consume the
person precisely because its rule is not represented as a rule."* Shape borrowed: a state a
trajectory is *pulled toward*; not a proof of a formal state-transition model (the papers flag this
as open). Pairs with **Basin**. [→](app-this-is-water.md)
*Plain:* something a process gets pulled toward — a character a person tends to grow into, or a goal/value that quietly organizes a life. The *pull-toward* shape; calling it an attractor is a careful conjecture, not a proven mechanism.

**Metastability** — uniform (multi-scale): a **metastable** state is **closed under the operations
*currently deployed* but not under the toolkit's own closure** — stable for now, yet holding a
latent endogenous exit (the *metastable cave* C4b; reused for affective "metastable readiness"
tipping in a phase-change, and for orbitally metastable Lagrange points). Shape borrowed:
metastability — a locally-stable-but-not-globally-stable well with an escape — **not** a literal
thermodynamic/energy-landscape claim (see **Phase**, phase-change). [→](app-out-of-the-cave.md)
*Plain:* stuck-for-now but not stuck-for-good — a state that holds together under what you're currently doing while a way out quietly exists. The physics picture (a shallow well you can still escape) is the shape; there's no literal energy landscape.

**Oscillator** — two senses of *bounded cycling*. **Bounded correction-oscillation** (caves,
differentiated affect): *"the agent may oscillate — anger, grief, accommodation, renewed resistance
— while remaining within a bounded fidelity-preserving region"* (hope–despair cycling).
**Amplitude oscillation of self-worth** (*Self-Applied* C23): grandiosity ↔ help-ask paralysis as
up- and down-swings of *"the same oscillator"* — *"amplitude, not measurement."* Shape borrowed: an
**oscillation** — bounded cycling between poles — not a literal periodic signal with a fixed
frequency (the genuine signal-processing/Fourier oscillatory sense is reserved for the grokking
work; see **Phase**, **Amplitude**). [→](app-self-applied-architecture.md)
*Plain:* swinging back and forth within bounds — moods cycling inside a stable region, or self-worth pinballing between "I'm godlike" and "I'm worthless." The swing *shape*, not a real wave with a set frequency.
