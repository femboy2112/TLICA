## Symbols

| Symbol | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **κ** (kappa) | Contact | How much a content is interacting with you right now. [→](three-coordinates.md) |
| **φ** (phi) | Truth-indistinguishability | How indistinguishable-from-true a content is under reasoning. [→](three-coordinates.md) |
| **ρ** (rho) | Identity-correlation | How integrated a content is into you; max-flow from the cogito. [→](three-coordinates.md) |
| **δ** (delta) | The infinitesimal | The cost of *one perspectival step* from universal to indexed content. [→](the-cogito.md) |
| **Î** (iota-hat) | The cogito / core I | The indexed self; "I am I." Sits at φ = 1 − δ, ρ = 1. [→](the-cogito.md) |
| **φ = 1 − δ** | — | The cogito's structural position: infinitesimally short of intrinsic truth. [→](the-cogito.md) |
| **P** | The profile | The identity-correlation profile — the map of ρ across your whole field; the theory's primary object. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md) |
| **Π** (Pi) | Preservation-ranking | What the self pulls to preserve under pressure. Depends on ρ but is *not* ρ. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md) |
| **A** | Phenomenal availability | Whether a content is present-to-you right now (a status, not a fourth coordinate). [→](two-layers.md) |

## Terms

**Intrinsic layer** — mathematics, logic, reason, treated as real *in re* (in the world,
mind-independent). Outside the φ-scale. [→](two-layers.md)

**Asymptotic layer** — everything else in your field, conscious or unconscious;
approaches intrinsic truth without reaching it. [→](two-layers.md)

**In re** — "in the thing." The claim that mathematical/logical structure is in the
world's structure, not in a separate Platonic realm. [→](two-layers.md)

**Naturalist-realism** — the architecture's specific commitment about the intrinsic
layer: structure is real in the world (naturalist), deliberately weaker than Platonism.
[→](two-layers.md)

**Cogito** — the single postulate: existence-as-an-I, whose form is self-identity. A
postulate, not a proof. [→](the-cogito.md)

**Verification tool** — an acquired recognition capacity for an intrinsic structure
(object permanence, cause-and-effect, modal reasoning, induction). What lets you assign φ.
[→](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md)

**Three kinds of encounter** — how tools and structure are acquired: *direct*
world-encounter, *shadow* encounter (recognizing structure through its traces),
*self-encounter* through meta-reasoning. [→](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md)

**Developmental escalator** — tools imprint in an order set by temporal scale;
short-timescale tools first, each unlocking perception of slower structure.
[→](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md)

**Mode A — adversarial differentiation** — drawing the I/not-I boundary by registering
something as discrepant. Produces the boundary. [→](modes-of-development.md)

**Mode C — positive identification** — folding something registered as continuous-with-you
inside the boundary. Populates the self. [→](modes-of-development.md)

**Mode B — reflexive meta-reasoning** — turning attention on your own structure; refines
the whole. Can't run until there's something to reflect on. [→](modes-of-development.md)

**Prerogative of consistency (PtCns)** — your frame as the implicit standard against which
encounters are evaluated; underlies Modes A and C. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)

**Prerogative of continued existence (PCE)** — the structural pull toward preserving the
maximally-self-defined I. Governs action under existential pressure. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)

**Maximally-self-defined I** — the action-relevant configuration of the lived self whose
preservation counts, under current conditions, as you continuing. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)

**Identity-neutral encapsulation** — incorporating something as bounded / non-self-defining;
a real low-propagation relation, not the absence of one. [→](the-two-prerogatives.md)

**Three orders of imprinting** — pathways by which contents reach you, each lossier than
the last: *first-order* (salience capture, sharp), *second-order* (thoughts, clear),
*third-order* (emotions/sensations, intense-but-fuzzy). [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)

**Fuzziness** — low explicit information density / low source-resolution. *Not* low
intensity and *not* less-conscious. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)

**Osmotic imprinting** — substrate-level pattern formation by repeated ambient
co-occurrence; always operating; needs no focus or verification. Pavlov's dog is the pure
case. [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)

**Four kinds of imprinting** — content, contact, verification-tool, and mode-development
imprinting. (Osmotic imprinting runs transverse to all four.) [→](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)

**Lived I** — the historically accumulated network of integrations rooted at the cogito;
the support of the profile where ρ > 0. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md)

**Shells** — contour bands of the profile at ρ-thresholds; a visualization aid, not a
primitive. [→](identity-correlation-profile.md)

**Four content classes** — by phenomenal availability × φ-status: *conscious-clear*,
*conscious-fuzzy / source-opaque*, *latent / retrievable*, *unconscious-operative*.
[→](two-layers.md)

**Profile-shape disturbance** — a disturbance of the self understood as a change in the
*shape* of the profile, not its magnitude. [→](profile-shape-disturbances.md)

**Developmental window** — the period (developmental *or* situational-overwhelm) where
conscious direction can't overcome substrate pressure; patterns rooted there aren't the
I's chosen errors. [→](profile-shape-disturbances.md)

**Perspectival realism** — other minds are real but, from any one perspective, reached
only indirectly. Not solipsism. [→](other-minds.md)

**Form-invariance (conditional on existence)** — every mind builds the *same* architecture
for itself; the form is shared, the lived instances are not. [→](other-minds.md)

**Primal not-I** — the posited field independent of the I: content multiplicity, time,
body, contact interface, optional other minds. [→](predictions-and-exclusions.md)

## Application-paper terms

From the [application papers](applications.md). Foundation terms above; these are introduced
in specific applications.

**Bandwidth-bounded integration** — the idea that fixed mental capacity, increasingly spent on
integrating a growing self, drives subjective time to accelerate with age. [→](app-temporal-phenomenology.md)

**Specious present** — the felt ~2–3 second "now," modeled as one span of focus holding
retention + the impressional moment + protention. [→](app-temporal-phenomenology.md)

**Constitutive extension** — folding another into the self so deeply that their loss is
*damage to you*; the structural definition of **love** and the seed of the affect taxonomy.
[→](app-differentiated-affect.md)

**Scope hierarchy (of self-evaluation)** — guilt → embarrassment → shame → self-loathing as the
same reflexive operation at widening scopes (act → presentation → identity-aspect → whole self).
[→](app-differentiated-affect.md)

**Six conditions of free choice** — live-option space, evaluative engagement, operator-bundle
selection, non-preemptive substrate coupling, non-confabulatory Mode B availability,
ownership-integration. [→](app-free-will.md)

**Ownership vs. sourcehood** — owning a choice from inside and where it was sourced from outside
are dissociable; the crux of manipulation. [→](app-free-will.md)

**Orders of willing** — willing reaching outward: first (focus), second (thought), third (action),
and constructed orders 4+ (through people and institutions). [→](app-agency-architecture.md)

**Attribution profile (ownership / responsibility / accountability)** — graded, profile-indexed,
never-zero-sum responsibility apparatus. [→](app-agency-architecture.md)

**Epistemic closure / "larger cave"** — escape from a cave is real but is always ascent to a
bigger cave, never an exit; Plato's allegory derived as closure dynamics. [→](app-out-of-the-cave.md)

**Source-map (σ) error** — being *correctly ordered and wrongly sourced*; the prisoners' real
mistake, available only because the theory separates truth from source. [→](app-out-of-the-cave.md)

**Truth-channel (T) / affect-routing channel (R) / λ** — the split of "empathy" into modeling
the other as real (T) and their state landing on you (R), coupled by λ. [→](app-referent-routing.md)

**The cold frame** — treating another I as a non-routed instrument while modeling them fully;
reached by *referent-thinning*, not by lying. [→](app-referent-routing.md)

**Bindingness without desert** — morality can be authoritative even though where you land on it
is unearned. [→](app-referent-routing.md)
