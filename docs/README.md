# TLICA Wiki

A plain-language companion to **The Two-Layer Identity-Correlation Architecture**.

The [main README](../README.md) gives the whole theory in one pass. This wiki breaks it
into one page per concept — each readable on its own, each cross-linked, each pointing
back to the frozen foundation (Files 0–5) where the formal version lives. It also has a
page for every **application paper**, so you can follow the theory from its first
principles all the way out to time, emotion, free will, and beyond.

If you're new, the [main README](../README.md) is the place to start. Come here when you
want to go deeper on a single idea, or to read about one of the application papers. Each
page stands alone, so you can land anywhere and follow the links outward — but the order
below is the gentle slope: it walks from the puzzle that started everything, through the
small set of tools the theory uses, up to the things those tools turn out to explain.

> TLICA is one person's 20-year attempt to write the handbook for being a conscious being
> that nobody is handed at birth — developed first-hand, then formalized. That origin is
> worth keeping in mind: the abstractions are load-bearing because they were *lived* first.
> (See the author's note at the top of the [main README](../README.md).)

## Part 1 — The core theory

**Start here** — the motivating puzzle and the two foundational moves the rest is built on.
- [The Problem It Starts From](the-problem.md) — the dissociation cases that motivate everything: experiences where something is vividly present yet feels like it isn't *yours*, which is exactly what a one-dimensional picture of the self can't describe
- [The Two Layers](two-layers.md) — intrinsic vs. asymptotic, and why the split matters: the difference between what you simply *are* and what you keep reaching toward but never finish becoming
- [The Cogito](the-cogito.md) — the one thing taken for granted, and where it sits: "I am I," the single self-evident starting point everything else is allowed to lean on

**The core apparatus** — the actual instruments. Almost everything later is these few tools, reused.
- [The Three Coordinates](three-coordinates.md) — contact (**κ** — *how strongly something is in touch with you right now*), truth-indistinguishability (**φ** — *how undeniably real it seems, how impossible to doubt*), and identity-correlation (**ρ** — *how much of who-you-are is bound up in it*)
- [The Identity-Correlation Profile](identity-correlation-profile.md) — the theory's primary object: take every content of your experience, score it on those three coordinates, and the whole pattern *is* you, mapped

> **In plain terms —** the three coordinates are just three different questions you can ask about anything in your experience — *Is it here? Is it real? Is it me?* — and the theory's big claim is that these come apart, so they each deserve their own dial. The profile is what you get when you read all three dials for everything at once.

**How a self is built** — none of this is handed to you; it accrues. These pages are the assembly story.
- [Access to Intrinsic Structure](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md) — verification tools and the developmental escalator: the means by which you check what's real, and how each rung you climb makes the next one reachable
- [Modes of Development](modes-of-development.md) — how the lived self emerges (the A, C, B routes): the distinct paths a self can take as it forms
- [Substrate, Focus & Imprinting](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md) — capacity, pathways, and osmotic patterning: what you have to work with, where your attention runs, and how repeated exposure quietly leaves its shape on you the way water seeps into stone

**What it accounts for** — the payoff: ordinary and extraordinary facts that fall out of the apparatus.
- [The Two Prerogatives](the-two-prerogatives.md) — consistency, and continued existence: the two things a self is built to protect — staying coherent, and staying *here*
- [Profile-Shape Disturbances](profile-shape-disturbances.md) — dissociation and "mental illness" as shape, not magnitude: distress reframed as the *form* your profile takes, not how much of something you have
- [Other Minds](other-minds.md) — perspectival realism without solipsism: how other people can be fully real to you without your having to escape your own point of view to prove it

**Boundaries** — the honest edges: what the theory will and won't say.
- [Predictions & Exclusions](predictions-and-exclusions.md) — what it claims and, just as importantly, what it deliberately leaves out
- [Translations](translations.md) — how it maps onto (and clashes with) other frameworks, so you can locate it next to ideas you already know

**Reference**
- [Glossary](glossary.md) — every term and symbol (κ, φ, ρ, and the rest) in one place, for when a page assumes one you haven't met yet

## Part 2 — The application papers

The foundation is built to be *applied*. Each paper takes the same frozen apparatus and
turns it on a specific corner of human experience, holding to one discipline: the
phenomenon should **fall out of what's already there**, with no new architectural
commitments. Start with the overview, then dive into any paper.

> **In plain terms —** the rule the papers play by is "no new gadgets." If the theory is any good, big features of being human — time, feeling, choice — should emerge from the tools already on the table, not from something quietly bolted on to make the answer come out right.

  later moment it actually *takes over*. **Conjectured**; the frozen foundation is left untouched.
  (Sources: the [research paper](../research/grokking_as_toolkit_closure_2026-08-29.md) and its
  [experiment protocol](../research/grokking_toolkit_closure_experiment_protocol_2026-08-29.md),
  whose outcomes are **UNRUN**.)

> **In plain terms —** this one reaches sideways: from the theory of *you* toward how any
> learner, human or machine, crosses from "I've memorized a lot of examples" to "oh — I finally
> see the pattern they were all coming from." It comes with a real experiment attached, but
> nothing here has been run yet, and it says so.

## One-paragraph reminder

Classical accounts of the self blur three things that come apart in real experience: how
strongly something is *in contact* with you, how *undeniably real* it seems, and how much
it is *part of who you are*. TLICA makes these three independent coordinates and builds a
self from them — starting from one undeniable postulate ("I am I") and the claim that
logical and mathematical structure is real in the world independent of any mind. Everything
else about you is *acquired*. The result is a map of how deeply each content is bound into
the network that is you, and an account of how that map forms, shifts, and can be disturbed.

> **In plain terms —** the move at the heart of everything is splitting one feeling — "this is part of my experience" — into three you can rate separately: *here*, *real*, and *mine*. Pull them apart and a lot that used to look like a single mysterious thing turns out to be a pattern you can actually read.
