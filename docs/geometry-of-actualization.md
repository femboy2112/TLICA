# The Geometry of Actualization

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [`research/geometry_of_actualization_2026-09-07/MANUSCRIPT.md`](../research/geometry_of_actualization_2026-09-07/MANUSCRIPT.md) · **Research note · v0.1.0 · UNVERIFIED**

> **What this is.** A single page over a connected trio of early first-pass research drafts
> from 2026-09-07 to 2026-09-09: *Bataille, Dostoevsky, and Wallace: The Geometry of
> Actualization* (with its immersion and traversability extensions), *A Post-Mortem of the
> Four Horsemen*, and *Inside Out and Reflected* (the iDubbbz / Sam Hyde "depth without
> capture" strand). They share one worry worked from three angles: how a person can be fully
> *in* something — an activity, an argument, a judgment of another person — without the being-in
> quietly turning into being-lost, being-captured, or treating someone as a mere operator of a task.

> **Tier and status.** Research-tier, v0.1.0, all **UNVERIFIED**. These are working manuscripts,
> not registered applications, not validated psychology, not diagnoses of any person. They apply
> TLICA's vocabulary; they do **not** amend the frozen foundation, and they add **no new primitive**.
> Every human and ethical claim below is **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED** unless marked otherwise, and
> each paper keeps its own claim ledger — the hedges here are carried over from those ledgers, not softened.

> **What "geometry" refuses to mean.** Throughout, *geometry* means **relational organization** —
> which contents support which, in what role. It is **not** literal differential geometry. No metric,
> no manifold, no physical field, no Fourier basis, no phase, and no measured emotional amplitude is
> asserted; the sources disclaim each of these explicitly. Likewise "reflection", "momentum", and
> "resonance" are relational or phenomenological words here, not physics. See the [glossary](glossary.md)
> for how the theory keeps its everyday words separate from its technical ones.

## One question, three papers

The trio begins from an authorial correction. An assistant had suggested that a sovereign,
non-instrumental activity exposes a *missing faculty* in TLICA — some separate machinery for
"consummatory" action that mere instrumental labor doesn't use. Leah's reply is the hinge of the
whole set:

> TLICA handles this once you consider that **the field contains the self**.

Painting for eight hours and grinding through exhausting work to improve a résumé can run on the
*same coarse agency machinery* — encounter, affective and interpretive organization, a live
orientation, selection, implementation, feedback. What differs between them need not be two
mechanisms. It can be the **organization of the relations** through which an orientation becomes
significant: how presently operative contents link past, present, and future; self, not-self, and
identity-neutral context. This is an architectural proposal, **OBSERVED** as the author's stated
contribution (ledger G01), not a claim that every act issues from an explicit sentence or an
overwhelming feeling.

That single correction propagates. If the self is already inside the field, then the interesting
differences live in *relational structure*, and three further questions become askable with the
same tools:

- **Can you be absorbed in something without losing yourself in it?** (the Geometry paper and its
  immersion / traversability extensions)
- **Can your particularity survive being modeled by someone else?** (the iDubbbz / Hyde paper —
  "depth without capture")
- **When does a competence that genuinely works at its own task get credited with authority over a
  different task?** (the Four Horsemen post-mortem — "warrant substitution")

None of the three is a verdict. Each is a disciplined way of *representing* possibilities and a
program for testing which of them actually hold.

> **In plain terms —** Your sense of who-you-are isn't sitting *outside* your experience pulling
> levers; it's one of the things *in* the experience. Accept that, and you stop needing a special
> "authentic-self engine" to explain why one activity feels like living and another feels like a means
> to an end — the difference is in how the pieces are wired together this time. That one reframing is
> what lets the same question be asked about work, about argument, and about how we read other people.

## Inhabiting an activity without losing yourself

The Geometry paper's running pair is *painting for eight hours* versus *putting up with exhausting
work to improve a résumé*. The tempting move — call the first "consummatory", the second "instrumental",
and treat them as different kinds of agency — is declined. Either episode can hold a significant
encounter, a self-congruent orientation, stabilized attention, and sustained action; what differs is the
**support structure**. The paper makes that explicit with a *typed, context-annotated support graph*:
nodes for the relevant contents, and edges that are **not** one interchangeable weight but distinct
declared relations (remembered association, expected instrumental consequence, identity integration,
opposition, endorsement, opportunity, implementation constraint, the experienced significance of the
activity itself). A declared association is not automatically a cause; a normative reason is not
automatically an operative cause. This is the "geometry": relational, with **no metric and no physical
phase** — the disclaimer is load-bearing, not decorative.

Three writers supply three *non-exclusive questions*, not three personality types and not three
diagnoses (ledger G06, a **CONJECTURED** interpretation grounded in the inspected texts):

- **Bataille** asks whether an activity must draw all its significance from a useful future return.
  The paper keeps "present closure" (significance that survives removing the future payoff) only as a
  *conditional diagnostic lens*, and explicitly refuses to equate it with Bataillean sovereignty —
  that stronger reading is **UNVERIFIED and not adopted** (G07), because the sovereignty passage was
  reached only through a mediated excerpt, not a collated edition.
- **Dostoevsky's Underground Man** asks whether an assertion of independence can become *dependent on
  what it refuses*. A small binary toy model shows only that both compliance (`a = s`) and
  contrarianism (`a = 1 − s`) track the same external signal — negation doesn't remove dependence.
  That is **DISCLOSED within the model** (G05); it proves neither that real resistance is servile nor
  that principled refusal is unfree. Principled disagreement stays a live rival.
- **Wallace** asks whether knowledge of an alternative can become *operative in time* — live before a
  default interpretation closes the encounter — rather than merely recited afterward. Defaults are not
  the enemy; a practiced response can be efficient, owned, and humane, and reorientation must not
  license swapping an unsupported hostile story for an unsupported comforting one.

The nine-cell past/present/future × self/not-self/neutral table is offered as a **view, not an
ontology** — the columns deliberately overlap (another person can be both an independent other and
integrated into the lived-I; a neutral object can still matter causally). And every operative
representation is *present-indexed*: a represented future employer's approval can sustain effort now
even if that employer never exists. No retrocausality is implied.

The **immersion extension** ("Letting Go of the Underground") turns this toward the losing-yourself
worry. Its target is **correctable commitment**: an orientation can organize action while relevant
doubts stay honestly represented and routes to warranted revision stay usable. Leah's image is *sauce*
and *dish* — the sauce is the operative interpretive, affective, practical, and social medium; the dish
is the declared activity and the commitments and effects that matter. "To get lost in the sauce is to
partially let go of the underground, enough to give your frame momentum." The key move: the processes
that let an orientation become *lived* rather than merely contemplated are the same ones that can let it
become *self-insulating*. Flow is a motivating case, **not a certificate** of truth or goodness
(Csikszentmihalyi himself denies flow guarantees virtue), and the sharp result is logical, not clinical:

> A responsive loop can be highly effective at correcting the **wrong variable**.

Hence two scales of correction — *task correction* ("how should this move change?") versus *frame
correction* ("should this be the task, rule, or interpretation at all?"): a pianist adjusts timing
beautifully, a team passes its tests fast, while the tests encode the wrong requirement. Abundant
within-frame feedback can coexist with missing frame-challenging information. A stipulated counterexample
makes it concrete: if the only readable signal is `p(s,h) = s` (local success), then `p(1,0) = p(1,1) = 1`,
so observing success can't separate a clean outcome from one that also violated a wider constraint —
**local success is insufficient** to certify the whole declared task.

The **traversability extension** adds the other half: even when you *see* the problem you may be unable
to move. It keeps three steps apart — detect a mismatch **⇏** can reorient **⇏** can implement the
alternative — and insists that **context can close the exit without changing a single belief**: someone
who keeps a failing procedure to keep essential income has not thereby endorsed it, been fooled, or
failed to notice. Reachability is always *relative to constraints*; an absent route can be a pathway gap,
a probe gap, or an access gap, never an "absolute impossibility" without more evidence; the small proofs
concern stipulated finite systems only, and the four human probes designed are **explicitly unrun** (no
fitted dynamics, clinical scale, or human experiment is reported).

The practical principle the strand lands on:

> **Inhabit a frame without losing the conditions under which it can responsibly be reconsidered.**
> Let an orientation become operative without granting it immunity; preserve consequential information
> without requiring every question to stay in the foreground.

This connects to the theory's broader anti-finality discipline in [Out of the Cave](app-out-of-the-cave.md)
(every insight is a larger room, never an exit) and to the dynamical reading of commitment and the
self in [The Self in Motion](the-self-in-motion.md). The coordinates it keeps strictly separate —
κ/contact, φ/toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability, σ/source-map adequacy, ρ/identity
integration — are the [three coordinates](three-coordinates.md); **none is assigned a number here**,
and strong self-resonance does not resolve σ.

> **In plain terms —** Being completely absorbed isn't the failure. The failure is when the very thing
> that lets you *get into it* also seals it off from correction — so you get brilliantly good at fixing
> the small stuff while the big thing you're doing drifts wrong, and you never notice, because everything
> *inside* the task is going fine. The goal isn't to hover anxiously outside every activity; it's to be
> all the way in while keeping a real door open for when the whole premise needs rethinking — and to
> remember that a person who *has* seen the problem may still be unable to walk out, because the exit is
> blocked by their rent, not their beliefs.

## Particularity surviving being modeled (depth without capture)

*Inside Out and Reflected* starts from Leah's line: "I feel like Ian is the societal-normal form of
Hyde. Hyde is iDubbbz inside out and reflected." The paper treats the two creators — iDubbbz (Ian) and
Sam Hyde — **analytically and fairly, only as the public record frames them**: retrieved interviews and
reporting, plus video locators without complete footage. It makes no claim about either person's hidden
intent, entire politics, or ethics, and it is **not** a verdict on a feud or on who "won."

The proposed contrast is between two *non-exclusive practices*, offered as a **defined proposal**, not
a measured fact:

- **Norm-referenced adjudication** assembles someone's conduct into an assessable case under a benchmark
  (the conversation's association with the *Content Cop* format). The paper keeps three benchmarks apart
  — the target's *own* stated standard, the *critic's* defended standard, and the *audience's* presumed
  standard — and warns that exposing inconsistency under one does not establish the others. Its failure
  mode: the standard goes invisible and the *person* becomes the whole problem.
- **Protocol-disrupting inquiry** changes the conditions under which a social script runs (Hyde's 2013
  TEDx appearance is one bounded anchor) and reads the responses. Its failure mode: endless irony where
  no attribution can ever land.

Whether Ian predominantly does the first and Hyde the second is **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED** — it would
need a balanced, held-out corpus with explicit counterexamples. The "reflection" in the title is
**unsupported as a literal mathematical duality and not asserted**: no metric, parity operation, common
basis, or involution is supplied, and "writing a symbol for a reflection would add notation without
demonstrating a relation." The metaphor is kept because it usefully inverts *analytical roles*, not
because the math was done.

The paper's methodological spine is that **the intervention is part of the cause**. A schematic
bookkeeping line — `O = E(M(F(P,N,C,Q,J)))` — says the released artifact `O` is an *edit* `E` of a
*recording* `M` of a behavior produced jointly by the person `P`, the norm `N`, the setting `C`, the
observer's presence `Q`, and the intervention `J`. Observing `O` does not recover `P` or `J` without
extra assumptions. A finite counterexample nails it: two generators `F(a,n) = a` and `G(a,n) = a XOR n`
*agree* on the baseline `n = 0` and disagree at `n = 1`, so complete observation of the baseline cannot
determine the generator outside it. The upshot, carried as **Refuted** in the ledger: a provocative
response does **not** identify a stable person-trait — a humiliating setup can produce hostility without
disclosing any essence that existed independently of it.

The documentary encounter (Hyde's edit released January 8 2022; Ian's February 2 2022) is treated as a
**source-lineage problem, not a clean experiment**: two edits of shared footage are *not* two independent
replications of the event (**Refuted** where the footage derives from one event). And Ian's 2023 apology
and unlisting is **OBSERVED through reporting** as a public act of reassessment — which is exactly why it
functions as a counterexample: a model that insists Ian is *always* norm-enforcing and Hyde *always*
norm-subverting has made its labels too elastic to test.

Crucially, the paper links this to TLICA's own [Cold Frame / referent-routing](app-referent-routing.md)
distinction — and *repairs* an earlier conflation. **Modeling** (the application's `T`, building a model
of another) is **not the same** as **routing** (the application's `R`, the modeled other's affective
uptake), and *evidence attribution* — what a response actually tells you about the person versus the
intervention versus the edit — is a further, third task. The conversation had collapsed all of these
under the word "routing"; the paper separates them explicitly, and it borrows the conceptual connection
without adopting the Cold Frame's clinical claims as independently established.

> **In plain terms —** When one person puts another under a lens — auditing them, or poking at them to
> see what falls out — the picture that results was co-authored by the poke, the camera, and the edit, not
> just the person. So "look how they reacted" tells you far less about *who they really are* than it feels
> like it does. You can know exactly what someone said or did and still not own the inside of them — and a
> richer model is not a captured person. Particularity survives being modeled, because the model was never
> the person.

## The ethics of turning people into operators

The moral question underneath the iDubbbz / Hyde paper is the one that set the whole trio in motion:
Leah's worry about reducing communication to "clinical algorithmic steps" until another person becomes a
**useful operator** of a task. The paper sharpens it: *if an analyst learns which inputs produce
attention, embarrassment, compliance, or anger, what stops understanding from becoming operation?*

Its answer refuses two easy outs. First, **predictive accuracy cannot supply the ethics** (ledger:
"rich prediction establishes ethical permission" — **does not follow**). The very same accurate model can
back a respectful policy that accepts refusal *or* an exploitative one that uses the predicted refusal to
design better pressure; adding detail to the model does not choose between them. Second, an earlier idea
that tied personhood to *whatever escapes the model* ("person minus model") is judged **inadequate** — a
person does not lose standing by becoming predictable, and "person minus model" defines no meaningful
residual. The principle has to survive even an unusually accurate account of someone's behavior:

> Another's agency, privacy, relevant welfare, and refusal matter as **limits on action**, not merely as
> variables in the analyst's objective. The other must not disappear into the desired result.

And the paper holds *itself* to that rule: calling public figures "useful operators" would commit the
exact erasure it warns against, so its own labels must stay bounded and corrigible.

The customer-service case from the Geometry paper is the same ethics in miniature. An abstracted report —
an employee taking some pleasure in making customers wait, yet becoming genuinely helpful toward the
patient and kind — is used to expose **intervention-generated evidence**, not to diagnose anyone or to
license making strangers wait: if you create the delay and the customer gets irritated, the irritation is
partly a response to *your* conduct, not a clean measurement of their prior entitlement. The rival
readings (displaced frustration, role refusal, selective reciprocity, local power, interpersonal testing)
are each **UNVERIFIED**, and basic fairness must not be made conditional on passing an undisclosed
interpersonal test. The immersion strand adds the "jester" / role-bounded case: a bounded role can be
performed without being treated as a theory of reality, but a private disclaimer is **not** a moral
exemption — information about harm, dishonesty, and safety must still cross the role boundary.

> **In plain terms —** Getting good at predicting someone is not the same as being allowed to use them. The
> better your model, the easier it is to slide from *understanding* a person into *operating* one — and no
> amount of accuracy tells you which you should do; that's a separate moral choice. The guardrail isn't
> "stay ignorant of people." It's to treat their refusal, privacy, and welfare as walls you don't get to
> walk through, even when you could predict exactly how they'd fall if you did.

## Warrant substitution in public reason (the Four Horsemen post-mortem)

*A Post-Mortem of the Four Horsemen* is the public-reason version of "being in something without losing
yourself" — here, a capable reasoner inhabiting an argumentative frame. "Post-mortem" names a retrospective
look at an intellectual formation and a conversation about it; it announces no one's death and settles no
religious claim. The paper takes four named figures — Hitchens, Dennett, Dawkins, Harris — as *starting
points, not boxes the evidence must fill*, and it lets the evidence weaken, narrow, or reject the original
attributions. Its organizing term is **warrant substitution**, flagged as an *author-level analytic term,
not a new canonical TLICA coordinate and not an empirical law*:

> A result passes criterion A; that success is treated as if it also passed criterion B, without checking
> the bridge between them.

Four candidate sites (arena, procedure, self, model) organize the analysis; they are "neither exhaustive
categories nor mutually exclusive personality types." The paper notes that *immersion is not itself
failure* — a researcher can live inside a demanding project without becoming captive — and that a frame is
"captured" only *relative to a specified correction opportunity* that was repeatedly absorbed without an
adequate answer. The case verdicts stay hedged, and several cut against the tidy story:

- **Hitchens (the arena).** His 2007 account reports a false story *and* its retraction: error and public
  correction are **OBSERVED** in an authored record. That arena success *caused* the error, and that every
  later edition was fixed, is **UNVERIFIED / CONJECTURED**. The correction must stay in the portrait.
- **Dennett (procedure).** That accurate reconstruction of a view requires granting it equal credibility is
  **Refuted as an entailment** — the two are simply different criteria. But the charge that Dennett
  *habitually* grants undue equal standing is **UNVERIFIED** (he rejects theology he finds indefensible); a
  real dispute (Zahavi vs. Dennett on heterophenomenology) is substituted as the better object and labelled
  a **contested philosophical allegation, unresolved here** — not a diagnosis.
- **Dawkins (self).** His 2014 logic-and-emotion framing is **OBSERVED**; a correct logical distinction can
  still leave other objections open ("scope inflation"). The intellectual-identity-coupling story is
  **CONJECTURED and unmeasured** (ρ is not assigned by reputation), and his 2008 report of changing his mind
  after Grafen's model is an **OBSERVED** counterexample to any "never revises" portrait.
- **Harris (model).** The criticism developed is **unverified operational transport** — moving from a
  plausible explanatory concern to confidence in a particular way of sorting and acting on people — **not**
  an allegation of an inherent Muslim essence. Emphatically, the paper does **not** establish that "Islam is
  more violent in general" (unspecified as a measurement claim) and does **not** license that Muslims are
  inherently dangerous (**neither established**); Harris's own base-rate distinction is **Refuted** as a
  target because he explicitly states it, and a finite invented counterexample (two worlds, identical group
  association, opposite flag performance) **Refutes** the idea that group association determines a proxy's utility.
- **Baldwin (comparator).** His "Letter to My Nephew" models understanding-without-equalization, but calling
  him "the control," as the conversation did, is **Refuted** as a description of the design — no matching,
  randomization, or common measurement exists; admiration is not a comparative design.

The paper closes by auditing its own first overclaims and keeping competing explanations live (a format
constraint, a genuine philosophical disagreement, a legitimate unresolved tradeoff), and that whole four-site
account validating TLICA is itself **UNVERIFIED** — conceptual fit alone is insufficient. Its refrain:

> **Victory is not verification. Understanding is not equal credibility. Self-checking is not automatically
> independent checking. Causal relevance is not operational adequacy.**

> **In plain terms —** A smart person can win an argument, represent an opponent fairly, generate a slick
> rationale, or spot a real cause — and then quietly cash each of those *real* achievements in for a bigger
> claim it never actually bought. Winning the room isn't the same as being right; understanding a view isn't
> the same as it being as credible as its rival; checking your own work isn't the same as someone
> independent checking it; finding a cause isn't the same as having a policy that works. The failure isn't
> having commitments or confidence — it's letting an achievement collect authority outside the conditions
> that made it reliable.

## What is and isn't claimed

Across all three papers the recurring questions stay open:

- **Inhabiting without losing yourself** (immersion / traversability): absorption, passion, and routine
  labor are one family, distinguished by the *organization* of a committed orientation, not by immersion
  versus some detached consciousness. The aim is **correctable commitment**; the finding that a responsive
  loop can efficiently correct the *wrong* variable is a **logical** observation.
- **Particularity surviving modeling** (depth without capture): the two analytical modes are a **defined
  proposal**; the person-level mirror is **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED**; the literal "reflection" is
  **unsupported and not asserted**; a provocative response identifying a stable trait is **Refuted**.
- **The ethics of operators**: predictive accuracy **does not** confer permission to act; another's agency,
  welfare, privacy, and refusal are limits on action, not variables to optimize.
- **Public reason**: **warrant substitution** is an author-level analytic term, not a coordinate; the four
  portraits come out less symmetrical and more useful — one allegation weakly attributed, one narrowed.

**Claim-status labels preserved from the source ledgers:** OBSERVED (within stated access limits),
DISCLOSED / Disclosed (a logical result within explicit assumptions, e.g. the finite countermodels),
CONJECTURED, Refuted, UNVERIFIED, Contested, and "Not claimed" / "Does not follow." The coordinates
κ/contact, φ/toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability, σ/source-map adequacy, ρ/identity integration,
and μ/probe availability are kept strictly separate and **assigned no numerical value** anywhere in the
trio. The executed checks are logical, not empirical: 13 standard-library tests for the Geometry package
and 16 for the iDubbbz / Hyde structural checks (the latter shares its implementation with the broader
dossier and is **not an independent witness**); full-repository `make validate` was **not** run on a
complete checkout in those sessions.

**Foundation untouched.** All three are TLICA *applications*, not amendments. They introduce **no new
primitive**, assign no coordinate a value, and leave the frozen foundation unchanged (the Geometry package
records foundation v5.3.3 as frozen; the Four Horsemen package is an application of the vocabulary, not a
change to it). "Geometry" remains **relational organization** — no metric, no manifold, no physical field.
