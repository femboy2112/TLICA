# Substrate, Focus & Imprinting

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [File 3, §8](../foundation/3_formal_apparatus.md)

---

The [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md) doesn't form in a vacuum. It's shaped by a
**substrate** you didn't choose, a finite **focus** you allocate within hard limits, and
several **imprinting** mechanisms that write contents into the network. This page covers
the machinery.

## Substrate-bound focus

Your **focus capacity** — how much you can hold in attention at once — is set by your
substrate and **you cannot choose it**. You can *allocate* focus among available contents,
but only within that inherited bound.

Focus allocation has two drivers that shift in balance over development:

- **Contact-driven** — salient things in the world grab focus from outside. Dominant
  early, when there's little internal structure to direct from.
- **Self-directed** — accumulated lived-I structure directs focus from inside. Grows as
  the self grows. The shift is gradual and **never complete**.

## The three orders of imprinting

Contents reach you through pathways of increasing **mediation** — and every stage is
**lossy** (compresses the signal). The "order" of a content is its *dominant* pathway
(most real contents mix all three):

- **First-order — salience capture.** World → salience-gating → focus. A loud bang grabs
  your attention. Minimally mediated, so **sharp**. (Not *unmediated* — the signal still
  passes through substrate; it's just the shortest path.)
- **Second-order — cognitive mediation.** World → cognitive substrate → I. **Thoughts.**
  These carry propositional structure ("that person insulted me"), which makes them
  **clear** and easy to run reason-tests on — well-suited for φ-placement.
- **Third-order — somatic-then-cognitive mediation.** World → body → cognitive substrate
  → I. **Emotions and sensations.** Two compression stages have run by the time they
  reach you, so they tend to be **intense but fuzzy**.

> **Intensity is not clarity.** A panic attack is maximal intensity with near-zero
> information density — "something is terribly wrong" without saying what. It is fully
> conscious, high κ, source-opaque, undefined φ. Fuzziness means *low explicit
> information density / low source-resolution*, not low intensity, and not "less
> conscious." (Trained interoception, expert somatic awareness, and contemplative
> practice can make third-order content unusually sharp — the rule is a *tendency*, not a
> law.)

A useful corollary: **thoughts can trigger emotions.** A second-order thought about a
frightening possibility activates the body; the body's response is compressed back into
affect that reaches you via the third-order pathway — which is why anticipatory anxiety
or grief arrives forceful but with its original reasoning no longer attached.

## Osmotic imprinting

Underneath all the focus-driven machinery runs a different mechanism entirely:
**osmotic imprinting** — substrate-level pattern formation by sheer **repeated
co-occurrence in ambient experience**. It requires **no focus, no verification, no mode
operation**. **Pavlov's dog** is the pure case: bell-and-food co-occur, and the bell
alone later triggers the food-response.

Key properties:

- **Always operating.** The substrate is continuously patterned by what surrounds you —
  a constant tug-of-war between focus and ambient exposure. High focus narrows ambient
  intake but never seals it; rest, mind-wandering, and sleep raise it. ("Always
  operating" means the mechanism stays *online* — not that every moment produces a
  durable update.)
- **Imprinting vs. activation are distinct.** *Imprinting* is the substrate forming the
  association; *activation* is an imprinted pattern firing later (a smell triggers
  anxiety). Activation can be conscious or entirely pre-conscious, and doesn't require you
  to recognize what's being triggered.
- **It's the engine of "I don't know why I feel this way about X."** Implicit attitudes,
  attachment patterns, trauma-cue responses, aesthetic dispositions, absorbed cultural and
  linguistic patterns — largely osmotic. Many become
  [unconscious-operative or conscious-fuzzy](two-layers.md) contents.
- **It seeds verification tools too** — populating the substrate with proto-patterns that
  [later become explicit tools](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md).

Osmotic imprinting is **transverse**: not a fifth kind of imprinting, but a substrate-level
process running *underneath* the four kinds below.

## The four kinds of imprinting

All governed by the focus-and-contact dynamics above:

1. **Content imprinting** — a content's ρ rising as it integrates into the network.
2. **Contact imprinting** — changes in contact relationships (κ).
3. **Verification-tool imprinting** — acquiring reasoning tools through encounter.
4. **Mode-development imprinting** — the actualization of Modes A, B, and C.

## Why this isn't "anything goes"

The architecture leaves several functions open (exactly how substrate sets capacity, how
focus shapes updates). But the **dependencies are fixed**: substrate determines capacity;
focus shapes probing and updating; contact shapes weighting and imprinting. The specific
empirical content is application-level work — the *structure* of the dependencies is the
commitment.
