# Specific Coherent Agency

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [`research/specific_coherent_agency_2026-09-18/`](../research/specific_coherent_agency_2026-09-18/README.md) · **Research note · precursor · UNVERIFIED**

> **A research-tier precursor, and honest about it.** This page describes a *candidate*
> comparative object — **Specific Coherent Agency (SCA)** — for asking how much reality-coupled,
> self-resonant, correctable agency a person can generate, relative to the complexity of the self
> they have to coordinate. It is a dated research dossier (v0.1.0), **not** an application paper.
> It adds **nothing** to the frozen foundation: no new coordinate, no new faculty, no new law, no
> dynamical commitment. The central measurement claim is left **UNVERIFIED** on purpose.
>
> **Three refusals this page keeps in plain sight.** (1) SCA is **not** ρ. The tempting reading
> "intelligence per unit self = intelligence ÷ ρ" is *ill-typed* and is rejected here: ρ is an
> identity-correlation / integration reading, not an extensive *amount of self*. (2) The scalar η
> is **UNVERIFIED** — no claim is made that it is reliable, cross-person comparable, or better than
> ordinary psychometrics. (3) This is **not yet an application paper**, and its promotion is held
> behind a synthetic-agent gate that **has not been passed** — nothing here has been promoted.
>
> It also never measures moral worth, consciousness, personhood, or a metaphysical amount of self.
> Those are a stated **Boundary**, and no benchmark result changes it.

## The question that changes its units mid-sentence

The dossier started from one person's recurring self-evaluation, preserved verbatim in the
[provenance record](../research/specific_coherent_agency_2026-09-18/README.md): a strong
intellectual success compresses to *"I'm a genius,"* a basic failure or poor life outcome
compresses to *"I'm an idiot,"* and the contradiction gets repaired not by fixing the measure but
by swapping the comparison class ("if I'm dumb, everyone else must be rocks-for-brains").

The page treats this as a **metric-collapse problem**. The first judgment is usually driven by
generativity, abstraction, compression, transfer, or discovery. The second is driven by execution,
life-state, sequencing, accumulated resources, social navigation, or a plain knowledge gap. The
third compares the self to a population with yet another implicit yardstick. *No contradiction
actually follows* unless all three are readings of one stable latent scalar — and they are not.

TLICA makes that fusion especially unattractive, because the theory already keeps apart the things
ordinary self-talk slams together: contact, verification-pathway state, source-map adequacy,
identity/commitment integration, probe availability, live-option construction, action access,
execution, ownership, and correction. The application-level lesson is simply: *do not let
"intelligence" become an undeclared sum of heterogeneous coordinates.* That is structurally the
same trap as the Moreish "sensor-to-governor" failure — a locally useful signal silently promoted
into a global evaluator.

- That ordinary smart/dumb self-evaluation **substitutes different metrics across episodes** is
  **Conjectured** as a general mechanism and **Observed** in the originating self-description.

> **In plain terms —** the "genius ↔ idiot" whiplash isn't a fact about how good you are. It's an
> artifact of measuring three different things with one word and then being surprised they
> disagree. Name the things separately and the paradox mostly evaporates.

## Observation is not control

Before any measure can be built, one separation has to be stated, because it does most of the
protective work. Schematize a trajectory as *representation → option construction → route
availability → selection → execution → world response → correction → accumulated state.* Strength
at an upstream stage does **not** certify strength downstream.

The existing [traversability](app-agency-architecture.md) machinery gives a clean finite
disclosure of this. If the only admissible action from a state returns that same state, an observer
can know the state *perfectly* and still never reach a target; add a single action and the target
becomes reachable **without any change to the observer's informational capacity**. So
*observation / model quality does not imply control* without extra assumptions about the action set
and the dynamics. The reverse separation matters just as much: successful action does **not**
certify model fidelity — imitation, luck, coercive structure, cached routine, or environmental
scaffolding can all produce the outcome.

- This separation is **Disclosed** within stipulated finite models (a theorem about the toy
  structure, not an empirical claim about any particular person). No experiment can refute it; an
  experiment can only show a given human situation does not instantiate the stipulated structure.

> **In plain terms —** knowing exactly what's going on and being able to *do anything about it* are
> two different capacities, and either can be high while the other is floor-level. A measure that
> can't tell "I understand it" apart from "I can move it" is broken before it starts.

## The capability vector

Because of that, the **primary object is a vector, not a number.** A benchmark episode produces a
profile with eight explicitly-typed components — provisional, application-level letters, **none of
them proposed as a foundation coordinate**:

| | Component | What it records |
|---|---|---|
| **E** | epistemic calibration | confidence tracks observed correctness under declared scoring |
| **M** | model fidelity | the explicit representation predicts relevant observations and consequences |
| **D** | discrimination | live rivals are separated by *probes*, not rhetoric or retrospective fit |
| **R** | route construction / reachability | useful paths are represented *and* actually admissible under the dynamics |
| **X** | execution | selected admissible routes are implemented reliably |
| **C** | correction | residual error produces useful revision, not denial, random switching, or endless audit |
| **S** | self-resonance | declared commitments / ownership constraints meant to survive the transition do survive |
| **O** | option preservation | success does not gratuitously destroy future reachable possibilities |

Keeping these separate before any scalar is computed is a **methodological proposal**, and keeping
the vector as the primary report (rather than a lone number) is a **methodological recommendation**
— each earns its status by preventing two people with the same aggregate from hiding *why* they got
there.

> **In plain terms —** instead of one "smartness" score, you get a little dashboard: can you model
> it, tell rival explanations apart, find a real route, actually execute, correct when wrong, keep
> the commitments you meant to keep, and avoid burning future options? Two people can tie on the
> total and be completely different machines underneath.

## Coherent reachability

The first sub-construct names *which futures a person can actually get to.* Start from the existing
reachability set — states an admissible path can reach within a horizon — then impose the declared
quality floors: enough epistemic fidelity, enough correction, enough self-resonance, and a cost
that stays within budget. What survives is the **coherently reachable set**.

"Coherent" here does **not** mean socially conventional, calm, internally simple, or conflict-free.
It means the *path itself* satisfies the benchmark's declared fidelity, correction,
ownership/invariant, and cost conditions. Crucially, cost stays a **cost tuple**, not a secret
scalar: the traversability work already warns that latency, reported effort, sleep displacement,
semantic loss and the rest do not arrive in common units, and "unknown" is kept distinct from
"zero."

- That coherent reachability is a **better target** for the motivating question than IQ alone is
  **Conjectured** / application-level — it directly folds in executable paths, correction,
  self-invariant preservation, and option value, but its predictive edge over standard measures is
  exactly what still has to be shown.
- That **real-world life state is not itself a measure of intelligence** is kept as a **strong
  conceptual caution** (not a quantified theorem about persons): outcomes jointly depend on
  cognition, access, environment, resources, history, execution, and noise.

> **In plain terms —** "reachable" futures aren't just the ones you could stumble into. They're the
> ones you can get to by a route you'd actually endorse, understanding what you're doing, able to
> correct along the way, without betraying the commitments you meant to keep, and without paying a
> cost you never agreed to. That's a much smaller, much more honest set.

## "Per unit self" — why ρ is the wrong denominator

The originating phrase was *"intelligence per unit of coherent self."* The naive reading —
divide by ρ — is **not well typed**, and this is the load-bearing refusal of the whole page. TLICA's
[ρ](three-coordinates.md) is an identity-correlation / integration reading; it is **not** an
extensive quantity, not an "amount of self" you can put in a denominator. Treating it as mass is a
type error.

- That "per unit self" must **not** use ρ as an extensive denominator is **Derived from typing** —
  it follows from what ρ *is*, not from any measurement. (A future foundation change that
  explicitly added an extensive self-measure would change the design space going forward; it would
  not retroactively license ρ-as-mass.)

The intuition is rescued by introducing a genuinely new **operational denominator**, `d_self` — the
*effective dimensional load* of self-relevant structure that has to be coordinated in a given
benchmark family. One candidate definition: the smallest effective number of approximately
independent self-relevant degrees of freedom needed to predict, to a declared accuracy, the
person's stable commitment rankings, vetoes, ownership judgments, and value-sensitive reversals
across the benchmark contexts. This is **not** "how much self the person has" — it is a *complexity
estimate for the coordination problem.* Candidate statistics include a Shannon effective rank
(`exp(−Σ pᵢ log pᵢ)`), a participation ratio (`1 / Σ pᵢ²`), and a predictive-minimum-dimension; none
is privileged, and they answer related-but-different questions.

A hard guard rides with it, the **adequacy gate**: the denominator must never be lowerable by
representing *fewer* commitments. If collapsing the self to one coordinate mechanically improves the
ratio, the benchmark has failed. So predictive adequacy of the self-model is a *precondition*, not a
free simplification knob.

- That effective self-dimension **can** serve as that operational normalizer is **Conjectured** —
  what it needs to earn the status is reliability across contexts, resistance to redundant-feature
  mutations, predictive adequacy on held-out self-relevant behavior, and cross-implementation
  agreement.

> **In plain terms —** "per unit self" isn't "divide by how much soul you've got." It's "how big is
> the coordination problem you're solving?" A person juggling many genuinely independent commitments
> is solving a harder problem than someone with one overriding drive — and the measure has to be
> rigged so you can't cheat it by pretending to be simpler than you are.

## Specific Coherent Agency: the scalar, and its warning label

Put the pieces together and you get a *benchmark-declared measure of usable coherent reachability*
— a weighted sum over the coherently reachable futures, folding in reach probability and the
epistemic, correction, resonance, and option-preservation qualities of each — call it `V_B`. The
candidate scalar is then:

```
η_m(B, H) = log(1 + V_B( R*_{m,H} )) / d_self(m; B)
```

the logarithm included only to stop benchmarks whose raw route-counts explode combinatorially from
dominating. This scalar is **not canonical.** It is a *declared projection* of the vector profile,
and it is only interpretable relative to the benchmark family, horizon, route-value rule,
thresholds, self-dimension estimator, calibration population, and missing-data policy. The rule is
blunt: **never report η alone** — always report the vector `Q`, the value `V_B`, the denominator
`d_self`, and η together, with uncertainty.

- That η is a **useful cross-person quantity** is **UNVERIFIED.** It is a schematic construction
  only. What would move it: synthetic-agent recovery, benchmark reliability, measurement invariance,
  known-result recovery, and *incremental* prediction beyond simpler comparators.
- That the primary comparison object should be **the vector plus denominator, not η alone**, is a
  **methodological recommendation.**

A comparison between two people is licensed *only* under a shared benchmark family, comparable
opportunity/action conditions, and common scoring. It can at most support a sentence like "under
benchmark family B, horizon H, and this normalization, participant *m* produced more held-out
coherent reachability per effective self-dimension than participant *n*" — and the report must say
*which dimensions drove the difference.* It can **never** support "*m* is a better person, more
conscious, or universally more intelligent."

> **In plain terms —** there is a formula, and the page spends more effort fencing it in than
> deriving it. The number means nothing without the whole context stapled to it, it's never allowed
> to travel alone, and whether it's actually a good measure of anything is flatly marked *not yet
> shown.*

## Update fidelity, and "I am right about something"

The second clarification in the originating exchange shifts the *epistemic object* itself. The
author's reported experience is not "my current model `M` equals the truth." It is closer to: there
is a real relation among the *feeling*, the *current frame*, and *their coupling to reality* — and
as long as the fidelity of either side keeps increasing, the central tracked thing is not abandoned
when the frame changes.

Formalized, the person forms a predicted transformation from felt structure, model, self-state, and
a chosen intervention; acts; observes the actual change; and incurs a **transport defect** — the
gap between predicted and realized consequence. (The word *transport* is used cautiously: it is an
application-level common-basis comparison *only* when the observable basis and orientation are
explicitly declared; otherwise it is ordinary prediction error, not a phasor / parallel-transport
claim.) The proposal is that a person can be *wrong about the model* yet be tracking a useful
relation — if revisions generated from the residuals reduce *future* defect on held-out
perturbations.

That becomes **update fidelity**: a good update operator does more than lower in-sample error — it
preserves provenance and uncertainty, reduces error on fresh perturbations, improves rival
discrimination where possible, avoids needlessly destroying live options, preserves the
self-invariants that were meant to survive, *and* lets those invariants themselves be revised
through a separately justified process. For the author this is nearer the real target than any
IQ-like score: **how rapidly can this coherent self learn the shape of an unfamiliar reality and
acquire truthful, corrigible control through it?**

- That the author experiences some convictions as confidence in a *relation* (felt state ↔ frame ↔
  reality-coupling) rather than in the finality of the current proposition is **Observed** as author
  self-report. It is phenomenology; any external-mechanism claim needs separate evidence.
- That update fidelity **operationalizes part of** meta-correctability is **Conjectured.**

> **In plain terms —** the claim isn't "I'm always right about the answer." It's "I'm right about
> the *shape of the thing I'm circling*, and I'll happily rewrite the sentence as long as the rewrite
> gets me closer." Whether that's real tracking or just a flattering story is settled by one thing:
> does changing the model actually improve predictions you hadn't already seen?

## Why conviction and corrigibility are not enemies

Ordinary descriptions make strong conviction and strong willingness-to-revise look opposed. They
need not be. If identity attaches to a *proposition*, then revising it feels like loss. But if the
protected object is instead *the best currently accessible relation* among feeling, model, and
world — plus a commitment to improve its fidelity — then replacing the model can *preserve* the
governing aim. That yields a coherent pattern of **high local conviction + high willingness to
rewrite the sentence**, with no contradiction.

The page is careful this must not become self-sealing. "I was right at a deeper level" is **not**
granted just because someone can narrate continuity after a failure. The claimed deeper relation
earns standing only when it makes *prospective* discriminating predictions that survive mutation and
holdout — the four-way outcome classification explicitly separates genuine *relational tracking*
(model changes, held-out error falls, provenance kept) from *proposition protection*, *narrative
relabeling* (model changes but no held-out gain), and *random flexibility*.

- That proposition replacement **can coexist** with continuity of the tracked commitment, when the
  protected object is relational rather than sentence-level, is **Conditionally Derived** as a
  structural possibility — the **person-level mechanism is UNVERIFIED**, pending prospective evidence
  that model replacement really does reduce held-out residuals (which is what distinguishes it from
  retrospective story-repair).
- That **high conviction and high corrigibility can coexist** without contradiction is
  **structurally possible; empirical prevalence UNVERIFIED.**

This is the same discipline the [self-in-motion](the-self-in-motion.md) and
[free-will](app-free-will.md) pages already insist on: a self is tracked through its changes, not
frozen into one of its frames.

> **In plain terms —** you can be absolutely sure *and* totally ready to be wrong, as long as what
> you're sure of is the direction, not the current wording. The honesty check is brutal and simple:
> if your "deeper rightness" never makes a prediction that could fail, it's a story, not a track.

## Self-resonant actualization

Prediction alone is not enough. A person can correctly predict that an environment will change her
in a way she does **not** endorse. So two things are kept separate: *compatibility* — did the
resulting self-state preserve the declared invariants that were meant to survive? — and *alignment*
— did the realized change resemble the change she *meant* to cause? The distinctions are load-bearing
and non-substitutable: a predicted-but-unendorsed transformation can be high-fidelity yet low
self-resonance; an endorsed transformation can be self-resonant yet reality-wrong; a lucky success
can have high outcome value but low attributable control; a painful correction can lower immediate
affective resonance while *raising* long-horizon epistemic fidelity. No single term stands in for the
others.

> **In plain terms —** getting what you aimed at isn't the same as being changed into someone you
> endorse. The measure insists on tracking both — "did it work?" and "is the person it left behind
> still me, on the terms I set?" — because optimizing only the first is how you win and lose at once.

## What it refuses to be, and how it could break

A broad construct is easy to contaminate — with environment, motivation, resources, culture, task
familiarity, language, disability, interface, and benchmark design. The dossier names its own main
failure modes rather than hiding them: hidden scalarization (choosing weights after seeing
results), rewarding simple selves (a gameable denominator), environment masquerading as person,
verbal sophistication masquerading as model quality, retrospective self-sealing, correction mistaken
for random movement, and *productivity capture* — the benchmark must let a participant refuse, wait,
preserve options, or conclude that **no transition is warranted.** Output maximization is not the
target. And several simpler rivals (fluid reasoning, working memory, metacognitive calibration,
reinforcement-learning adaptation rate, planning depth, self-efficacy, narrative self-complexity)
are assumed to explain the results *until discriminated* — each with a named discriminator.

> **In plain terms —** the page keeps a list of all the cheap ways it could be fooled — including by
> itself — and treats "a boring existing test already explains this" as the default to be beaten, not
> a possibility to wave away.

## The benchmark program, and the gate it has not passed

The honest endpoint of the proposal is not a prose argument — it is a **falsifiable research
program.** The cleanest benchmark class is a family of *unfamiliar interactive systems* — observe →
model → predict → choose → act → observe consequence → revise — in small worlds where the
experimenter knows the transition graph exactly, carrying multiple live hypotheses, a tempting
misleading correlation, a useful indirect route, irreversible actions, situations where preserving
option value is the right move, held-out regime changes, and pre-declared self-constraints. Scoring
reads *learning curves*, not just terminal score, with positive, negative, null, and mutation
controls plus standard psychometric comparators.

The **next strongest probe is explicitly synthetic, not more prose**: a finite graph-world
populated with agents of *known* policy — oracle, random, fixed-wrong, greedy-reward,
over-correcting switcher, calibrated updater, option-preserving planner. The test is whether the
scoring *algebra* recovers the designed distinctions without hand-tuning weights per agent, and
whether the denominator survives having its self-features duplicated. A failed synthetic calibration
would cheaply kill or reshape the quantitative proposal *before* any human is ever measured.

That gate matters for how this page should be read. **Promotion of SCA from this research precursor
into an application paper is author-gated behind a synthetic-agent promotion gate — and that gate
has not been passed.** No scalar may be promoted as an application-level "measure of intelligence"
until (at minimum) the synthetic probes pass under an *independent* implementation, the denominator
survives basis/redundancy attacks, the relation/proposition distinction has a prospective probe, a
preregistered benchmark fixes all weights before outcomes, vector reporting stays mandatory, and a
standard comparator is included. **Nothing here has been promoted.**

## What it says about "genius or idiot?"

The framework *dissolves* the originating question rather than answering it. A person can be — all
at once — high in generative abstraction, uneven in stored knowledge, high in update fidelity, low
in some execution domains, high in self-model complexity, locally constrained in reachability,
strong at adversarial correction, and poor at cumulative sequencing. The task is not to pick the
flattering or the insulting label. It is to estimate the *profile* and then test whether the
proposed normalization predicts anything beyond the ordinary alternatives — which, so far, it has
not been shown to do.
