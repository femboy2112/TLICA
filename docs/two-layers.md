# The Two Layers

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [File 1, §2.1–2.6](../foundation/1_foundations.md)

---

The architecture divides everything into two layers. The name of the whole theory —
*Two-Layer* Identity-Correlation Architecture — comes from this split.

## The intrinsic layer

The intrinsic layer contains **mathematics, logic, and reason**. The theory treats these
as *in re* — real *in the structure of the world*, holding whether or not any mind is
present to verify them.

The diagnostic image is the **river and the stones**: when a river pushes one stone next
to another, there are now two stones. The arithmetic `1 + 1 = 2` is realized in the
situation itself, without anyone needing to count. The *cognized* instance — a mind
picking up the stones and reasoning "that's two" — is a separate event that depends on a
mind existing. The structure is in the world; the access to it is the mind's.

This is a **naturalist-realist** commitment, deliberately weaker than Platonism. The
theory does **not** claim mathematical objects live in a separate abstract realm. It
claims only that the world the I is embedded in *contains pervasive structure a mind can
encounter* — which is what the empirical sciences find at every scale. (See
[Access to Intrinsic Structure](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md) for how a mind reaches
it.)

Intrinsic items are **outside the φ-scale entirely** (see [Three Coordinates](three-coordinates.md)).
They aren't "maximally true things measured on the same ruler as everything else" — they
are the ruler's reference point, the ontological standard the rest is measured *against*.

## The asymptotic layer

The asymptotic layer contains **everything else**: every content in the I's field that
doesn't hold by reason alone — perceptions, thoughts, emotions, memories, relationships,
bodily sensations — **conscious or unconscious**.

"Asymptotic" because these contents approach intrinsic truth without reaching it. The
nearest any of them gets is the [cogito](the-cogito.md), which sits one infinitesimal
step short.

A key subtlety the theory is careful about: the asymptotic layer is *not* the same as
"the conscious mind." It contains four classes of content, sorted by two independent
questions — *is it present to you right now?* (phenomenal availability) and *can you
construct a verification pathway to it?* (φ-status):

- **Conscious-clear** — present, with a traceable pathway (worked arithmetic, explicit
  reasoning).
- **Conscious-fuzzy / source-opaque** — present, but you can't say *why* (panic, dread,
  raw affect). Fully conscious, just opaque — *not* "almost unconscious."
- **Latent / retrievable** — not present now, but reachable (a stored fact, a dormant
  skill).
- **Unconscious-operative** — not present and not traceable, yet still shaping you
  (implicit bias, repressed content, osmotic dispositions).

This is why the theory can speak of identity-correlation (ρ) for a repressed memory you
can't access: ρ and contact (κ) are defined across the *whole* asymptotic layer, while
φ applies only where a pathway is constructible.

## The boundary between them

The two layers meet at exactly one point: the [cogito](the-cogito.md). "I am I" is the
single indexed instance of universal reflexivity ("x = x") — one perspectival step out
of the intrinsic layer and into a particular point of view. That step is what the symbol
**φ = 1 − δ** measures.
