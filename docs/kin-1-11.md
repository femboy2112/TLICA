# KIN-1-11 — The Egalitarian Spiritual Soldier

[← Wiki home](README.md) · [Doctrinal edition v0.2](../applications/kin_1_11_doctrine_edition_v0_2.md) · [Original v0.1](../applications/kin_1_11_egalitarian_spiritual_field_manual_v0_1.md) · [Pocket card](../applications/kin_1_11_pocket_card_v0_1.md) · **Normative field-doctrine draft v0.2.0 · 2026-10-22 · author review pending**

> **No thrones. No masters. No disposable souls.** This is a voluntary, nonviolent, decentralized ethical practice—not a military formation, recruitment system, church, ideology test, hierarchy, or permit to harm.

## Why this manual exists

The [Anarchist's Cookbook for Operating a Mind](app-anarchists-cookbook.md) gives a single person tools for examining their own mind and preserving cognitive independence. KIN-1-11 asks the next question: **how should independent people relate, work, disagree, protect one another, and exercise temporary authority without turning belonging into domination?**

Its answer is not an army with a command structure. It is a pattern that any person or small, independently accountable community may practice, criticize, fork, or refuse.

**KIN** means *Kinship, Interdependence, Non-Domination*. The **1** stands for an irreducible, accountable self. **11** depicts two individual "ones" standing beside one another rather than one above another; it also counts the eleven Articles of Standing.

## What it borrows from ADP 6-22

The U.S. Army's [Leader Development Guide](https://usacac.army.mil/Organizations/CAC-Transformation-Integration-Directorate/Center-for-the-Army-Profession-and-Leadership-CAPL/Developing-Leaders/Interactive-Leader-Development-Guide?PageID=4&PgrID=1124) separates leadership attributes—character, presence, intellect—from competencies such as leading, developing, and achieving. KIN takes that *practical organization*, not military command: **BE** (stance and integrity), **SEE** (a correctable reading of events), **DO** (service, protection, coordination), and **REVISE** (feedback, correction, accountability). The v0.2 Doctrine Edition also adapts chapter-and-paragraph numbering, definitions, logical mapping, mission/end-state language, and doctrinal vignette structure. Its civilian satire is original: the narration stays clinically serious while ordinary institutional failures convict themselves.

The manual's "spiritual soldier" is a civilian moral metaphor: the practice is about resilience under pressure, not physical combat. No affiliation with the Army is asserted.

## Eleven standing articles

1. Dignity is not awarded.
2. Conscience cannot be outsourced.
3. Truth outranks the tribe.
4. Assistance must not become ownership.
5. Freedom meets the equal freedom of others.
6. Responsibility includes consequences.
7. Bear burdens without manufacturing martyrs.
8. Conflict is not permission to dehumanize.
9. Authority is a duty with an expiration condition.
10. Build systems that can be corrected.
11. The next generation is not collateral.

The **v0.1** manual contains a six-step **KIN-SITREP**, four field exercises, a bidirectional **Iroh leadership** practice, an anti-capture constitution, failure modes, a multiobjective evaluation table, a deliberately limited mathematical sketch, and an explicit claim register. **v0.2** retains those principles and reorganizes them into **10 numbered chapters**, **11 Articles**, **seven civilian field vignettes** (the meeting that achieves readiness, the worker who becomes the procedure, the benefits application, the charitable bargain, neighbors and a shared wall, the equal-dignity fallacy, and the dissenting member), a doctrinal logic map, and three appendices.

## The TLICA boundary

This is a **normative application**, not an inference that dignity or political legitimacy follows from an identity equation. It uses TLICA distinctions among contact (κ), toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability (φ), source-map adequacy (σ), identity-correlation (ρ), preservation ranking (Π), and accessible probes (μ). None is a human-value score. The core foundation is unchanged.

Its nearby sources include [Moreish](app-moreish-ontology.md) (a good sensor becomes dangerous when promoted to a universal governor), [When the Map Becomes a Mandate](app-map-becomes-mandate.md) (explanation, evaluation and authorization are separate), [Cultural Interoperability](cultural-interoperability.md) (different lives can coordinate without being reduced to one worldview), and [Distributed Institutional Realization](distributed-institutional-realization.md) (a functioning institution is not a collective human mind).

The practical claim that KIN-1-11 *improves outcomes* is **UNVERIFIED**. It needs tests that can demonstrate increased dependence, disguised burden-shifting, arbitrary leadership, harms ignored in the name of unity, or diminished exit options.

## The ethical asymmetry that makes it work

**Equal dignity does not imply equal innocence, authority, or permission to harm.** A victim and aggressor need not receive identical practical treatment. Protecting vulnerable people and enforcing rights by lawful, proportionate means is compatible with refusing to strip any person of intrinsic worth.

Its "fair playing field" is therefore *not* procedural neutrality in the presence of active coercion. It means building material, epistemic, civic, and institutional conditions under which choice can actually be exercised.
