# Authority-Compatible Frame Dynamics

> **A candidate explanation, honest about being one.** This is a research-tier working
> dossier — a *paper seed*, not a finished application paper — about a family of behaviors
> colloquially lumped together as "bootlicking." Its careful name for that family is
> **authority-compatible frame dynamics**. The analytic distinctions it draws (what follows from
> what, logically) are secure. The *psychological and social mechanisms* it proposes — the
> interesting part, the part about how a cage becomes common sense — are **CONJECTURED /
> UNVERIFIED**. No human data have been run; the one synthetic witness the dossier describes has
> **not yet been built**.
>
> **What this page refuses to do.** It will not read anyone's insides off their outward
> compliance: identical obedient behavior can come from survival, habit, incentive, belief, or
> identification, and the page keeps those apart on principle. It is **not** a diagnosis, a moral
> score, or a claim that complying with a hierarchy makes you its author. It does not add a single
> new coordinate to the theory of the self. **The foundation is untouched.** Everything reaching
> past a plain logical point carries its hedge in plain sight.

## One word doing too many jobs

Start with the colloquial word, because it is where the confusion lives. "Bootlicking" is used for a
worker who follows an absurd rule because rent is due, for one who sincerely believes rule-breaking
reveals a defect of character, for someone deferring to real expertise, for someone obeying an
emergency command because coordination matters, and for someone who has wrapped their whole sense of
self around the institution giving the orders. These are not one thing. Outward behavior badly
underdetermines what is happening inside.

So the dossier refuses, from the first page, to classify compliance by its surface. It keeps three
limiting cases apart and never lets them blur:

- **Compartmentalized survival emulation** — the person *runs* a model of what authority expects
  to reduce danger or preserve scarce margin, while privately holding that the frame is contingent
  or unjust. The authority model is being executed, not believed.
- **Internalized authority compatibility** — the frame outlives the immediate coercion and starts
  organizing judgment: what is *enforced* quietly becomes what is "realistic," "responsible," or
  deserved.
- **Positive self-resonant reproduction** — perceiving, inhabiting, and reproducing the hierarchy
  themselves start to *feel good*, and the frame becomes a cross-context attractor that rewards the
  agent for seeing the world its way.

These are candidate mechanisms, not natural kinds, and not mutually exclusive. The whole project is
to make them *distinguishable* rather than collapse them into a single vice.

> **In plain terms —** the same person bending to a boss might be terrified, cynical, convinced, or
> true-believing, and you cannot tell which from the bending alone. This page is about keeping those
> apart — especially about *not* sneering at the person who bends because they have a kid to feed.

## The hinge: a cage you can model is not a cage that must exist

Here is the load-bearing distinction, the one the rest of the page is built on. There is a world of
difference between **a frame-compliant move** — "given how things are right now, this is the
sensible thing to do" — and **a constraint illicitly reified into a necessity** — "therefore this
is how reality *is*." The first is prudence. The second is a smuggled upgrade, and it is where the
dossier locates the real phenomenon.

The originating objection, in the author's own words, was against a chain that looks airtight and
isn't: *I can model this; I foresee the outcome; if I go along, minimal friction; if I don't, bad
things for me; therefore not going along is on me, because it was foreseeable.* That sequence
performs several illicit conversions at once, and the dossier separates four non-equivalences that
the chain runs together:

- **predictability is not invariance** — a reliable model of the *present* regime is not a law of
  the system across all the states it could reach;
- **foreseeability is not authorship** — knowing that someone with discretion will punish you does
  not erase them from the causal story and make you the author of your own punishment;
- **local infeasibility is not global impossibility** — "I cannot presently afford this action" is
  not "this action could not matter";
- **enforcement is not legitimacy** — the capacity to impose a rule is not evidence that the rule
  is warranted.

Each non-equivalence is **Disclosed** as a logical point (the first; and "compliance is not
internalization," and "resonance is not truth," are Disclosed too). Several carry an **UNVERIFIED**
tail the moment they become claims about *how ordinary people actually judge* — foreseeability
versus authorship is Disclosed at the causal-structure level but UNVERIFIED as a model of real
responsibility judgments; local-versus-global is Disclosed as a distinction but its empirical
prevalence is UNVERIFIED.

> **In plain terms —** "that's just how it works" can be doing honest double duty. It might mean
> "here, now, this is what happens" — true and useful. Or it might have quietly become "and it
> couldn't be otherwise" — which does not follow. The slide from the first meaning to the second
> is the thing under study.

## Six conversions

From those distinctions the dossier names six candidate errors or dynamics — the specific ways a
constraint gets reified into a necessity. Every one of these, as a claim about human psychology, is
**Conjectured / UNVERIFIED** unless noted.

- **Constraint reification** (predictability → invariance). A model of the current regime is treated
  as a law of the system. But institutions are not passive environments — their response-functions
  depend on policy, incentive, staffing, coordination, and the actions of others — and a regime can
  be stable *precisely because* people treat its current response-function as fixed.
- **Foreseeability laundering** (foreseeability → responsibility). "You knew what would happen"
  slides into "therefore this is on you." The first can be true while the second stays unsupported:
  a mugger can make your decision environment perfectly predictable without that converting the
  threatened act into a force of nature, or into your authorship. The structure of the inference is
  the object — not a claim that every workplace rule is a mugging.
- **Local constraint → global impossibility.** "My survivable action set is narrow" becomes "there
  exists no effective action" — but a small feasible set is a fact about the agent's constraint
  surface, not a proof that all resistance is causally null.
- **Resistance-visibility error** (C-004). Resistance gets judged by one coarse variable — did the
  institution *visibly* lose? — when an intervention's real effect is a vector: macrostate, but also
  enforcement cost, information revealed or recorded, coordination structure, and future
  reachability. An unchanged macrostate does not mean nothing changed. (Falsifier: if observers
  *reliably* integrate the non-macro effects once they are made explicit, this error does no work.)
- **Captured fairness** (C-006). The fairness faculty keeps working but its *reference frame* gets
  captured: people judge fairness *among participants under a fixed rule* instead of asking whether
  the rule should exist, so someone who escapes an arbitrary cost looks like they got an unfair
  exemption. (Rivals kept live: ordinary norm enforcement, material incentives, selfish-defection
  detection, status defense.)
- **Retrospective necessity and sunk suffering** (C-008, C-009). Having paid a cost can raise the
  tendency to read it as *necessary* — so a successful resister becomes a counterexample to one's
  own autobiography, and punishing them protects the necessity story. This is distinguished from
  plain sunk-cost reasoning as a stronger candidate — **sunk suffering as an epistemic commitment** —
  and offered as a falsifiable rival among several (effort justification, fairness, envy, status
  threat), not as *the* explanation for hazing or gatekeeping.

A related piece, the **resister-as-cheater** frame, turns on **information collapse** (C-007): if
the live representation no longer distinguishes *principled resistance* from *self-serving
defection* — both compressed into "noncompliance" — the resister can only appear as someone chasing
benefits without paying the price. Whether that information was actively rejected, eroded by
repeated conditioning, or never represented at sufficient resolution is kept as three separate
possibilities.

> **In plain terms —** six different ways a true, local observation ("break this rule here and now
> and you get hurt") hardens into a false global one ("so resistance is pointless, and anyone who
> got away with it cheated"). Each is a specific hinge where a fact about *your* situation gets
> rewritten as a fact about *reality*.

## Slack, the edge, and why compliance is not cowardice

The dossier is emphatic on one point, and it is a moral one: it refuses the cheap reading that every
act of compliance is cowardice. "Nothing I do will change anything" usually mixes two different
questions — *is there any action that could perturb the system?* and *is there an action whose cost
I can presently absorb?* These come apart.

The variable that matters is **slack**: not just money, but whatever margin remains before an
unacceptable boundary is crossed — and it includes obligations to dependents and other coupled
people. As slack goes to zero, the feasible action set can collapse, and that collapse is morally
and strategically real. A person supporting children, dependent on medication, or facing
deportation, prison, or violence may have very little usable resistance capacity. **Local
infeasibility is frequently overgeneralized into global impossibility** — but the overgeneralization
is the error, not the compliance.

This is why **compartmentalized survival emulation is a protected rival** (C-017): local authority
emulation can be strategically rational under low slack while the agent still epistemically and
normatively rejects the frame. One can, in the dossier's phrase, *bend without declaring gravity* —
comply in behavior while withholding both normative assent and epistemic reification. The theory is
interested in the *conversion* of coercion into a moral law one then enforces on others, not in the
bending itself.

The repository's existing work on reflective access and slack — the resources that *can* permit
intervention on accumulated structure — lives in [the-self-in-motion](the-self-in-motion.md). A
frame can be osmotically formed, source-opaque, identity-integrated, and costly to inspect; low
slack can shrink the reachable alternatives without ever converting the frame into truth or
necessity. That keeps the distinction between **formation** and **endorsement** load-bearing.

> **In plain terms —** "I can't fight this" and "fighting this can't work" are different sentences,
> and most of the time the first is honest and the second is the smuggled upgrade. Someone close to
> the edge who complies to protect the people depending on them has done nothing the page condemns.

## Three regimes, and a vector instead of a score

To keep "bootlicking" from becoming a single moral number, the dossier proposes four *separable*
application-level axes — a vector, deliberately not collapsed into a scalar:

- **scope** — how many contexts activate the authority-compatible frame;
- **internalization** — how far the authority model is treated as the agent's *own* normative
  world-model rather than a local emulator that collapses when the threat is removed;
- **positive self-resonance** — how far frame-consistent states carry positive self-valence above
  matched alternatives;
- **externalization** — how far the agent imposes or propagates the frame onto peers and newcomers.

The axes are kept uncollapsed on purpose: high externalization with low resonance might just be
material incentive to enforce; high resonance with low externalization might be private
identification with no enforcement. Two people can behave identically under threat while sitting in
completely different regions of this space — a survival emulator low on every axis, a cross-context
attractor high on some or all. The clean discriminator for the first two regimes is what happens
when the threat or context is removed: if endorsement and peer-enforcement collapse with the
coercion, survival emulation remains the strong explanation; if they persist — or if the same
authority-compatible relation reappears under a *different* authority — internalization or a
**cross-context authority attractor** (C-015, **UNVERIFIED**) is in play. The decomposition itself
(C-011) is **Conjectured as useful; UNVERIFIED empirically**. None of it is a diagnosis or a
person-level measurement instrument.

## Positive self-resonance: when inhabiting the frame feels good

This is the dossier's central generative insight, and its central conjecture (C-018, the paper
thesis): an authority-compatible frame becomes especially durable when it does not merely *explain*
the world but causes the organism to positively *value* perceiving, inhabiting, and reproducing the
world in that frame. The status of that thesis is stated flatly: **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED**.

The key move is to make "resonance" broad. **Positive self-resonance is not conscious pleasure**
(C-012, **Conjectured** at this level): a self-relevant representation can carry positive
substrate-mediated valence that presents as relief, certainty, familiarity, righteousness,
belonging, safety, pride, or a plain "this is how things are supposed to be" — with no reflective "I
enjoy this" anywhere in sight. Valence can attach to the *act of inhabiting the frame itself* ("I am
responsible," "I understand how the world works," "rule-breakers get what they deserve," "order
restored"), so that criticism of the hierarchy imposes an identity-level cost even when no material
sanction follows.

The dynamical worry is **valence-weighted sampling** (C-013, **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED**). If a
frame feels good, it can bias what gets noticed, retained, and interpreted — so explicit reasoning
operates on a *systematically selected* evidence surface, and the agent may reason perfectly
coherently over a distorted sample. This is stronger and more unsettling than ordinary conscious
motivated reasoning, which is why the dossier insists in boldface that **coherence within sampled
evidence does not imply adequate sampling of the world** — with its own strong falsifier: if valence
adds no incremental effect on sampling once threat, incentives, and prior belief are controlled, the
mechanism adds little.

All of this rests on the repository's standing firewall, which this dossier extends rather than
invents (C-016, **Disclosed**): **resonance is not truth**. The separation of self-resonance from
truth-indistinguishability (φ) and source-map adequacy (σ) is set out in
[three-coordinates](three-coordinates.md) and pinned in the [glossary](glossary.md). The paper's
claim is emphatically *not* "people are wrong because they feel good." It is a candidate account of
how valence can shape sampling and persistence *while remaining epistemically non-warranting*:
positive self-resonance supplies no warrant that the resonant frame is accurate or legitimate.

> **In plain terms —** the strongest version of the story isn't "they're paid to agree" or "they're
> scared to disagree." It's that seeing the world the hierarchy's way has started to *feel* like
> relief, or rightness, or safety — and so the frame quietly steers what they even notice, and then
> they reason flawlessly about a stacked deck. Feeling right about it is not the same as being
> right, and this page never lets those two touch.

## How the cage replicates itself

Perception is only half the loop; agents also act. If a frame leads someone to reward compliant
peers, punish challengers, choose authority-affirming company, repeat the institution's
explanations, and avoid dissonant environments, the frame *modifies its own future input
distribution*. The world seen later is not merely selected — it is partly **constructed** by earlier
frame-consistent action. This is **ecological self-reinforcement** (C-014, **Conjectured** as a
mechanism family; population importance **UNVERIFIED**).

Zoom out and the candidate population-level loop is a **self-confirming domination equilibrium**
(C-005, **Conjectured**; the finite synthetic witness described in the probe plan has **not yet been
built**): *expect persistence → adapt to persistence → reduce perturbations → persistence increases
→ which confirms the expectation.* The name is deliberately narrower than "self-fulfilling
prophecy," and the boundary is stated: the institution already has material powers, so **belief is
not claimed to create arbitrary reality**. The conjecture is only that agents' adaptation can become
*one of the causal inputs* preserving the state they correctly predict — a belief locally accurate
and dynamically constitutive at once.

Crucially, **no group mind is required**. The hierarchy does not need to enforce every relation
directly once some enforcement is performed by agents for whom doing so is itself identity-consistent
or positively resonant. This leans on the repository's existing
[distributed institutional realization](distributed-institutional-realization.md): distributed
people, relations, records, and incentives can realize a stable macro-pattern without any
super-agent that believes or wants. "The institution internalizes itself" is allowed only as
shorthand for *local agents acquiring dispositions that reproduce parts of the institutional response
function without continuous direct command*.

## What it borrows, and what stays untouched

Nothing here is a new foundation primitive. The dossier composes existing machinery: substrate
mediation and the third-order compression of input into intensity, valence, and action-tendency, and
osmotic imprinting by repeated co-occurrence — both set out in
[substrate-focus-and-imprinting](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md); the formation-versus-activation
split; identity-correlation and self-relevant integration; reflective (Mode-B) access and slack; the
resonance-is-not-truth firewall; the agency work separating structural responsibility inputs from
normative sufficiency; and the institutional work treating social structure as distributed
realization rather than a group mind. It does **not** derive authority preference from that
machinery — it uses it only to make one candidate mechanism *architecturally admissible*: repeated
authority-compatible experience *could, in principle*, form substrate-mediated dispositions whose
later activation changes salience, valence, and action readiness. Whether it *does*, in the social
cases of interest, is empirical and **UNVERIFIED**.

The genuinely new objects — authority-compatible frame, constraint reification, foreseeability
laundering, captured fairness, the resistance-visibility vector, and the
scope/internalization/resonance/externalization profile — all stay at research/application level. κ,
φ, σ, ρ, μ, the modes, slack, substrate mediation, osmotic imprinting, and institutional realization
are left exactly as they were. **Foundation untouched.** If later data force a new primitive, the
dossier insists that be argued separately rather than smuggled in through this paper.

## What has — and has not — been shown

The conceptual non-equivalences are secure *as logical distinctions*. The proposed psychological and
social mechanisms are **not** yet established. The dossier states plainly that it has **not** shown
that authority-compatible self-resonance is common; that it explains any particular person's
behavior; that it out-predicts established rivals (system justification, cognitive dissonance and
effort justification, just-world reasoning, authoritarian submission, social dominance, normative
conformity, learned helplessness, identity-protective cognition, and more — a **prior-art search
queue, not a literature claim**; no novelty earned, C-019, **Disclosed**); that successful resistance
generally improves outcomes; that compliers are therefore internalized or culpable (C-010, C-020,
**Disclosed** — no person-level diagnosis); that "bootlicking" is a single trait; or that the
proposed attractor exists as a measurable human dynamical object.

**Claim-status summary.** The logical non-implications — predictability ≠ invariance, foreseeability
≠ authorship (at the causal-structure level), enforcement ≠ legitimacy, compliance ≠ internalization,
resonance ≠ truth, no novelty claimed, no person-level diagnosis — are **Disclosed**. The
psychological and social mechanisms — resistance-visibility error, self-confirming domination
equilibrium, captured fairness, information collapse, retrospective necessity, sunk suffering as
epistemic commitment, the heterogeneity decomposition, broad positive self-resonance,
valence-weighted sampling, ecological self-reinforcement — are **Conjectured**, most with an explicit
**UNVERIFIED** empirical tail and a predeclared falsifier. The cross-context authority attractor and
the central paper thesis are **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED**. No human data have been run; the synthetic
witness is **not yet built**; prior-art novelty is unaudited. The ledger keeps **Observed**,
**Corroborated**, **Refuted**, and **Dark** available in its status vocabulary, but no load-bearing
claim in the dossier currently carries them. The conclusion inherits the weakest load-bearing
premise, so the whole package reads as **UNVERIFIED**. **The foundation is untouched.**
