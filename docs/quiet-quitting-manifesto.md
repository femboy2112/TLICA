> **A recovered archive, not a theory result.** This page surfaces a self-contained

> safety, honesty, restraint, and proportionality are argued morally; no TLICA number derives
> them, and none certifies that its mechanisms are true of any real workplace.

## The object: *individual quiet quitting proper*

The ordinary public phrase "quiet quitting" is a bag of very different acts and motives. The
manifesto is about **one narrow thing inside it**, and most of its care goes into keeping that
thing distinct from everything nearby.

Its worker still cares about doing the job properly. But the institution has quietly folded
**exceptional, uncompensated effort into its definition of normal performance**: the worker
keeps rescuing missing staffing, incompatible priorities, and inadequate tools; the rescue
becomes the evidence that capacity was fine all along; the next demand assumes the rescue will
recur. A wage-supported life outside work gets consumed by an arrangement that describes the
consumption as *character*.

The proposed response is precise, and its precision is the whole point:

> Keep competent, safe, honest performance of the defensible role. **Withdraw the discretionary,
> unowned, unsustainable excess** — but only after a *material, persistent* mismatch, and only
> when reasonable correction has been tried and has failed, or is genuinely unavailable, unsafe,
> futile, or available only as *assimilation* (become the missing capacity yourself).

Or, in the line the manifesto keeps returning to:

> We withdraw the subsidy, not the integrity.

Three things it deliberately is **not**, each kept as a separate question:

- **Not ordinary boundary-setting.** Simply declining an optional gift is fine and does not need
  this whole apparatus; the construct is narrower.
- **Not collective pressure.** Coordinated withdrawal is a different use of worker power and a
  different ethics.
- **Not a verdict on outright refusal.** Whether flat refusal to work can be justified is left
  **open**; the narrow individual-boundary definition is explicitly forbidden from legislating
  all labor action.

## The moving parts, in plain language

- **The hidden-input problem.** An account of output can silently omit a real input — the
  worker's private heroic compensation. The manifesto uses a schematic sketch (output as a
  function of demand, staffing, equipment, and hidden effort) purely to ask *"has the story left
  an input out?"* — **not** as a fitted production law. "Ethical de-buffering" means turning that
  hidden contribution *down* while keeping safety, honesty, and competent baseline work — you
  stop privately erasing a deficit, you do not manufacture one to make a point.
- **Pathway closure, felt vs. actual.** Effort, voice, and repair attempts can stop leading
  anywhere — but a person can also *feel* a route is closed when it isn't. The manifesto keeps
  both, and refuses to treat the feeling as proof.
- **The exit fallacy.** A vacancy existing somewhere is not the same as a transition this worker
  can actually make now — money, time, schedule, benefits, transport, and a survivable pay gap
  all gate it. "Just leave" does not establish reachability; feeling trapped does not prove every
  route is shut.
- **Grind and identity wages.** Demanding work can be genuinely meaningful when the relation
  among person, task, autonomy, tangible return, and a self-directed future actually holds. The
  claim is only that an institution **cannot treat personal passion as an indefinitely available
  labor input**. (The term *identity wages* is attributed in the source ledger to Alison R. Buck
  and adapted; that attribution is preserved from the historical source, not independently
  re-verified here.)
- **The "fuck-it factor" — acute triage.** Under a hard deadline with incompatible demands, a
  worker can face *no* action that satisfies every live constraint in time and still be forced to
  act. The manifesto coins a blunt name for that felt transition into triage, and is careful
  about it: it names a *felt* moment, **not** a proven scientific construct or an excuse, and
  later drafts explicitly keep safe-stop, slow-down, escalate, and ask-for-priorities as real
  actions the model may not delete just to manufacture an empty choice-set. (Earlier drafts'
  stronger claims — a generic extra error-rate, a single scalar "threshold" recurrence — did
  **not** survive the later audit in that form and were downgraded to conditional proposals.)

## The distinction the whole work is built to protect

**Equal human worth does not make workplace power or repair duties equal.** The employer controls
most of the pay, staffing, scheduling, classification, discipline, and institutional design; the
worker's remaining control over their own discretionary effort is limited but real. Equal
personhood does **not** imply equal information, equal choice, or equal responsibility — and so
the larger investigation-and-repair burden sits with those holding greater authority.

This asymmetry is the load-bearing beam, and the editorial history proves it: a preliminary
rewrite that made worker and employer look pleasingly *symmetric* — contract-like reliance
duties, a heavier burden on the worker to *prove* institutional causation — was **rejected**
precisely because the tidier balance damaged the argument. At the same time, a structural
complaint is **not** a moral blank cheque: no label converts deception, retaliation, manufactured
failure, or deliberate neglect into ethical action. Each actor owns what they knowingly choose
within what they can reasonably know and control.

## Why the version numbers do not sort themselves

A later filename is **not** automatically the preferred text. The recovered lineage is a *branch*,
not a ladder:

- **v1.4 — the voice-preserving parent.** The literary and moral master; the full pledge, the
  direct address, the grind declaration, the American-Dream section, the manager warning, the
  deliberately forceful "cake" analogy.
- **v2.0 — a rejected direction, preserved.** A heavily audited rewrite that made real precision
  repairs but compressed the rhetoric and dropped the pledge and cake analogy. It was **rejected
  as the canonical direction** — *not* promoted by its larger number. It is kept as history.
- **A preliminary v1.5 — a separate wrong turn.** The false-symmetry draft described above; kept
  as a labeled rejected draft.
- **Final v1.5 (+ .1 → .4) — the accepted rebase onto v1.4.** Restores the parent's voice while
  keeping the useful corrections: separating experienced from actual closure, treating output
  changes as diagnostic rather than uniquely causal, and removing unsupported numeric claims. The
  patches add the refusal boundary (1.5.1), a structural reorganization (1.5.2), and non-perfection
  standards (1.5.3).
- **v1.5.3 — an identifiable checkpoint.** Not a freeze that erases what came after.
- **v1.5.4 — the latest *recovered* revision.** Adds three autobiographical paragraphs in §1
  (an admission that the author's own earlier conduct under the broad label would not necessarily
  meet the mature ethic; that a persistent intuition was a *clue requiring testing*, not a
  verdict; and that this disclosure is **not** a confession other workers owe). "Latest recovered"
  does **not** mean "newly approved release," and the mature framework is explicitly barred from
  **retroactively certifying** earlier behavior.

## What is recovered, and what is honestly missing

The **file** recovery is substantial: 86 source paths (85 distinct byte payloads), eleven
numbered manuscripts, six release ZIPs and all 43 of their members, and the complete uploaded
nineteen-section editorial handoff — all preserved unchanged and checkable by SHA-256.

The **conversation** recovery is **partial**, and the archive refuses to paper over the gap.
A complete original chat export was not available; the full v1.4→v1.5 decision thread, the exact
prompt behind v1.5.4's disclosure, and the complete August 27 quantitative-companion reply
remain **missing**. What survives is kept in strict fidelity classes — byte-preserved artifacts,
a byte-identical uploaded instruction file, role-labeled retrieved messages (one visibly
truncated), and weaker retrieval summaries — and **nothing was invented to fill the gap**. A
retrieval summary is never promoted into a verbatim user message.

## The TLICA bridge — a translation, not a validation

TLICA motivated parts of the thinking, so the dossier includes a **retrospective** map from the
manifesto's distinctions to the current corpus — and hedges it hard. The bridge is a *September
reading of an August text*, not evidence that the manuscripts were derived from the theory. Its
own boundaries, all preserved:

- **The two "slacks" are different quantities.** The manifesto's operational margin (capacity
  plus hidden effort minus workload) and the foundation's reflexive budget (§8.11) are **not one
  measurable reservoir**; sharing the word *slack* does not make a deadline, a financial reserve,
  and a unit of attention one fuel. The zero-threshold prediction stays **UNVERIFIED**.
- **A changed identity weight is not the v5.5.0 actuator.** Under the repaired baseline law,
  changing the metric weight ρ alone does not move the baseline; only a rewrite of the slow
  structure *G* (Posit G) can. This is a conditional formal account, not a measured equation for
  anyone.
- **Explanation does not generate the ethics.** TLICA can organize evidence about constraints,
  options, contact, and control; it does **not** derive the manifesto's duties from any coordinate
  value. An empirically adequate structural account could still describe an *unethical* response.

Read the full crosswalk and its non-erasable distinctions in the
[TLICA bridge](../research/quiet_quitting_manifesto_2026-09-16/TLICA_BRIDGE.md), and every claim's
status — definitional, conjectured, or explicitly *not licensed* — in the
[claim ledger](../research/quiet_quitting_manifesto_2026-09-16/CLAIM_LEDGER.md). The bridge lists
five discriminating probes; **all of them are unrun.** Any future application must keep an
ordinary staffing / scheduling / communication / incentives account as a live competitor and ask
what TLICA adds — because *renaming* an ordinary explanation is not evidence of a better one.

> **In plain terms —** this is a real piece of moral writing about work, recovered with its whole
> messy edit history intact and its gaps left showing. The theory can *describe* its distinctions;
> it does not *prove* them, and the manifesto never needed it to.
