# Applications — Overview

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [`applications/`](../applications/)

---

The [foundation](../foundation/) is deliberately a *foundation*: it sets up the
[coordinates](three-coordinates.md), the [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md), the
[developmental machinery](modes-of-development.md), and the [two prerogatives](the-two-prerogatives.md),
but it intentionally leaves most concrete phenomena to **application papers**. This is
where the theory earns its keep. The foundation tells you what an *I* is made of; the
applications take a familiar piece of human life — feeling time speed up, loving someone,
choosing freely, knowing or failing to know, treating a person as a thing — and show that
it is already implied by those parts, without bolting anything new on.

> **In plain terms —** the foundation is the toolbox. The application papers are the
> theory walking up to ordinary experiences you already have and saying "here is exactly
> which tools, already on the table, produce that."

## The discipline

Every application paper holds itself to one rule: the phenomenon should **fall out of the
apparatus already in the foundation**, introducing *no new architectural commitments*. In
practice that means a paper is forbidden from inventing a new coordinate, a new faculty, or
a new force to explain its target; it must reach for things the foundation already defines —
the three coordinates ([κ](three-coordinates.md) contact, [φ](three-coordinates.md)
truth-status, [ρ](three-coordinates.md) identity-correlation), the
[modes of development](modes-of-development.md), the [prerogatives](the-two-prerogatives.md) —
and reassemble them. When something *resists* clean derivation, that resistance is treated
as informative rather than swept aside: it is read as a sign that either the foundation needs
extending, the phenomenon is a genuine **compound** of simpler ones that should be taken
apart, or it was misclassified to begin with. So the papers are tests of the foundation as
much as they are extensions of it — each one is an experiment that could in principle have
failed, and the places where the apparatus strains are recorded, not hidden.

> **In plain terms —** the rule is "no cheating." You are not allowed to invent a new gadget
> to explain love or free will; you have to build it out of parts already on the table. If
> you can't, that failure is itself a finding worth keeping.

## The method

A recurring **method** runs through the mature papers: pick the *canonical case* — the single
configuration where the structure is most legible, where all the moving parts sit in their
clearest arrangement — define it precisely, and then derive everything else as a variation,
loss, threat, or failure of it. Instead of cataloguing fifty phenomena side by side, you
nail down the one clean specimen and read the rest as what happens when that specimen is bent,
broken, or only partly present.

- *Differentiated Affect* defines **love** first — as the clean case where the self has genuinely
  extended to include another and stayed coherent — then derives grief, fear, jealousy, pride,
  shame, anger, and the rest as that same structure under loss, threat, or distortion.
- *Free Will* / *Agency Architecture* define **free choice** first — the fully-conditions-met case —
  then derive akrasia, compulsion, addiction, manipulation, and flow as named structural
  modifications of it, places where one or another condition is bent or missing.

> **In plain terms —** rather than describe every emotion or every kind of choice one by
> one, each paper finds the cleanest example, gets it exactly right, and then explains all
> the messier cases as that example going partly wrong.

## The "layer" numbering

The foundation is Files 0–5. Application papers continue the count as **layers**, each
building on the frozen base — "frozen" meaning the foundation's commitments are fixed and not
quietly revised to make a later paper work; a layer may *lean on* earlier layers, but it
inherits the foundation unchanged:

| 8B | [Agency Architecture](app-agency-architecture.md) | Draft (v0.3.0) |
| — | [Out of the Cave](app-out-of-the-cave.md) | Early draft (v0.1.2) |
| — | [The Cold Frame and Its Sources](app-referent-routing.md) | Draft prose (v0.4.3) |

Layer 8 is split into two companion papers — *Free Will* (the philosophical core: what free
choice *is* and what it would take to undo it) and *Agency Architecture* (the structural and
empirical core: how willing, trying, and responsibility are actually built) — because the
unified draft grew past 200 pages. They are meant to be read together; each cross-references
apparatus the other defines.

The two newest papers (*Out of the Cave*, *The Cold Frame*) carry no layer number yet: they
are still finding their place in the stack.

## How maturity is marked

The papers sit at very different stages, and the wiki pages say so plainly rather than
dressing an early sketch up as a finished result:

- **Complete** — *Temporal Phenomenology*, *Differentiated Affect*. Worked all the way
  through, with committed empirical predictions and explicit exclusions (the things the paper
  says it deliberately does *not* explain).
- **Draft** — *Free Will*, *Agency Architecture*, and *The Cold Frame and Its Sources*. The
  architecture is in place and written in prose; some apparatus is cross-referenced between
  companions, and some detail (weighting, operational tests, or empirical anchoring) is
  explicitly unfinished and flagged as such. *(The Cold Frame reached fully closed prose at
  v0.4.0 — no skeleton blocks remain — and carries a signed author's note on moral stance.)*
- **Early draft** — *Out of the Cave*. Claims are fully *stated* and tagged with grades, but
  prose, citations, and some derivations are still pending; read it as the theory thinking out
  loud, not as a settled result.

> **In plain terms —** the labels are honest. "Complete" means worked through and willing to
> make falsifiable bets; "draft" means the bones are set but some flesh is missing; "early
> draft" means the ideas are stated but not yet defended in full.

## Reading order

If you're working through the applications, a natural path runs from the cleanest demonstration
to the most exploratory:

1. [Temporal Phenomenology](app-temporal-phenomenology.md) — the cleanest demonstration that
   the foundation has reach. Nothing new is added, yet real predictions come out: why subjective
   time *speeds up* with age, what the felt "now" is made of, and why memory and anticipation
   feel structurally different. Best read first because it shows the whole game in miniature.
2. [Differentiated Affect](app-differentiated-affect.md) — the richest complete paper; the
   "canonical case → derive the rest" method at full power. Defines love structurally, then
   builds five clusters of emotion (connection, self, threat, boundary, embodied) and their
   characteristic pathologies out of it, closing with fourteen discriminating predictions.
3. [Free Will](app-free-will.md) + [Agency Architecture](app-agency-architecture.md) — the
   two-paper treatment of choice, will, and responsibility: what free choice requires (six
   conditions), how manipulation differs from ordinary sourcing, and a full taxonomy of the
   ways agency can be modified or contracted.
4. [Out of the Cave](app-out-of-the-cave.md) — the theory turned on knowledge itself. It reads
   Plato's cave not as a metaphor to decorate but as a structure to *derive*, re-diagnosing the
   prisoners' condition as systematic source-error rather than false belief — and, in its coda,
   turns the same apparatus on the theory's own status.
5. [The Cold Frame and Its Sources](app-referent-routing.md) — the newest and most exploratory.
   It splits empathy into two operations — *modeling* another as a subject and *routing* that
   model into your own feeling — and shows that the same cold treatment of a person can arise
   from several structurally distinct causes, ending in a meta-ethics of "bindingness without
   desert."
