The [foundation](../foundation/) is deliberately a *foundation*: it sets up the
[coordinates](three-coordinates.md), the [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md), the
[developmental machinery](modes-of-development.md), and the [two prerogatives](the-two-prerogatives.md),
but it intentionally leaves most concrete phenomena to **application papers**. This is
where the theory earns its keep.

## The discipline

Every application paper holds itself to one rule: the phenomenon should **fall out of the
apparatus already in the foundation**, introducing *no new architectural commitments*. When
something resists clean derivation, that resistance is treated as informative — a sign that
either the foundation needs extending, the phenomenon is a genuine compound of simpler ones,
or it was misclassified. The papers are tests of the foundation as much as they are
extensions of it.

A recurring **method** runs through the mature papers: pick the *canonical case* — the
configuration where the structure is most legible — define it precisely, then derive
everything else as a variation, loss, threat, or failure of it.

- *Differentiated Affect* defines **love** first, then derives the other emotions from it.
- *Free Will* / *Agency Architecture* define **free choice** first, then derive akrasia,
  compulsion, addiction, manipulation, flow, and the rest as structural modifications.

## How maturity is marked

The papers sit at very different stages, and the wiki pages say so plainly:

- **Complete** — *Temporal Phenomenology*, *Differentiated Affect*. Worked through, with
  predictions and exclusions.
- **Draft** — *Free Will*, *Agency Architecture*. The architecture is in place; some
  apparatus is cross-referenced between the two companions, and some weighting/operational
  detail is explicitly unfinished.
- **Early draft / working skeleton** — *Out of the Cave*, *Referent Routing*. Claims are
  fully *stated* and tagged, but prose, citations, and some derivations are still pending.
  These should be read as the theory thinking out loud, not as settled results.

## Reading order

If you're working through the applications, a natural path:

1. [Temporal Phenomenology](app-temporal-phenomenology.md) — the cleanest demonstration that
   the foundation has reach; nothing new is added, yet real predictions come out.
2. [Differentiated Affect](app-differentiated-affect.md) — the richest complete paper; the
   "canonical case → derive the rest" method at full power.
3. [Free Will](app-free-will.md) + [Agency Architecture](app-agency-architecture.md) — the
   two-paper treatment of choice, will, and responsibility.
4. [Out of the Cave](app-out-of-the-cave.md) — the theory turned on knowledge itself (and,
   in its coda, on the theory's own status).
5. [Referent Routing](app-referent-routing.md) — the newest and most exploratory; empathy,
   the cold frame, and a meta-ethics of "bindingness without desert."
