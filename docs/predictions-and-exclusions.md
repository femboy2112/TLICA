A theory is defined as much by what it **refuses to claim** as by what it asserts. This
page collects both edges.

## Permitted configurations

The coordinate independence (see [Three Coordinates](three-coordinates.md)) predicts which
combinations of κ, φ, ρ can occur. A sample of the permitted table:

| Case | κ | φ | ρ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable embodied content | high | high | high |
| Vivid hallucination | low | high | low |
| Anesthetized bodily ownership | low | low | high |
| Alien clear content | varies | high | low |
| Phantom limb | low | varies | high |
| Repressed memory | varies | undefined | high |
| Automatic motor pattern | high | undefined | high |
| Intrusive thought | high | low/undefined | low |
| Insight (the moment of) | varies | newly defined | growing |
| Mode-C-merged content (early dev) | high | undefined | very high |

## What the architecture rules out

The foundation lists 20 explicit exclusions. The load-bearing ones:

- **No rich self from the cogito alone.** The [cogito](the-cogito.md) licenses *only*
  self-identity — no body, no toolkit, no developed self. (Exclusions 1–3, 5, 11, 15.)
- **Nothing non-core reaches ρ = 1.** Only the core I does; and ρ = 1 there is a
  [mathematical anchor](identity-correlation-profile.md), not preservation-primacy.
  (Exclusions 7, 19.)
- **No intrinsic item gets a φ value.** Logic and math are *outside* the φ-scale, not
  maximal points on it. (Exclusions 6, 12.)
- **The asymptotic field is not conscious-only.** It contains unconscious contents too.
  (Exclusion 13.)
- **No uniform reasoning across minds.** Different minds have different toolkits.
  (Exclusion 14.)
- **No per-encounter prediction of which mode fires.** The theory predicts what a
  [mode](modes-of-development.md) *does*, not which one operates for a given encounter.
  (Exclusion 17.)
- **Consciousness is not reducible to identity-correlation.** ρ does **not** measure "how
  conscious" something is; conscious beings can't be ranked by overall ρ-strength.
  Identity-correlation is a relation *within* a presupposed conscious field. (Exclusion
  18 — the most important one.)
- **ρ is not valuation, preservation-priority, or action-priority.** (Exclusion 19; see
  the [self-sacrifice case](the-two-prerogatives.md).)
- **The prerogative of continued existence is not metaphysically inviolable.** It can be
  defeated or misdirected under extreme, identifiable conditions; completed suicide in
  severe [profile disturbance](profile-shape-disturbances.md) is not a simple
  counterexample. (Exclusion 20.)

## Honest limits — out of scope *by design*

These aren't gaps to be filled; they're outside what a single-perspective theory can
address:

- **Whether other minds actually exist** — unverifiable from inside; assumed, not proven.
  See [Other Minds](other-minds.md).
- **The metaphysics of the I** — what the self *is* (substance, process, pattern). Out of
  scope; only the structural fact of self-identity is claimed.
- **The world independent of the I**, and the **physics-level direction of time**.
- **Physical implementation** — what realizes the substrate (brains, bodies, anything
  else) is an empirical question the formal theory doesn't settle.

## Working assumptions — named, not hidden

Things the foundation *rests on* without deriving: the [intrinsic layer](two-layers.md)
(naturalist-realism), the [cogito](the-cogito.md) as postulate, the primal not-I posits
(content multiplicity, time, body, contact, optional other minds), a
**profile-preservation principle**, and an **agency / action-space** assumption. The
[two prerogatives](the-two-prerogatives.md) are treated as theorem-candidates relative to
these — derivable in principle, but the full derivation is application-level work.

## Genuine open problems (in scope)

Things the theory *could* address with more development but currently doesn't — e.g. the
exact set of integration modes and their weights; whether max-flow is the right
ρ-aggregation; the per-encounter mode-selection rule; **prerogative arbitration** (which
of PtCns/PCE wins in grief, sacrifice, trauma); action-priority dynamics under pressure;
the distributional shape of [Mode B onset](modes-of-development.md); and a list of
phenomena left for application papers (the felt stream of experience, dreams and altered
states, narrative self, specific affects, empathy, aesthetics, and more).

> The honesty about edges is itself a design commitment. Where critiques surfaced real
> problems, earlier versions fixed them; where critiques surfaced genuine philosophical
> commitments (rationalism about intrinsic structure, perspectival anchoring, structural
> rather than predictive scope), the architecture **stands on them** rather than evading.
