# Predictions & Exclusions

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [File 4, §11](../foundation/4_derived_concepts_and_predictions.md); [File 5, §13](../foundation/5_translations_open_problems_conclusion.md)

---

A theory is defined as much by what it **refuses to claim** as by what it asserts. This
page collects both edges: the configurations the architecture *allows*, the ones it
*forbids*, the questions it deliberately leaves alone, the assumptions it leans on without
proving, and the honest open problems it hasn't yet solved.

This is the "what would falsify it / what it does not claim" page. So before the lists, a
quick translation of the three letters that run through all of them:

- **κ (contact)** — how much a thing is *touching your experience right now*. The warmth of
  the mug in your hand has high κ; a friend in another city has low κ even though they
  matter to you.
- **φ (truth-indistinguishability / verification)** — how much a content *checks out as
  real* through some path you can actually run. A solved equation has high φ; a vivid dream
  felt high-φ while you were in it, then collapsed on waking.
- **ρ (identity-correlation)** — how much of *you* is bound up in a thing; the stuff whose
  loss would actually change who you are. Your child has high ρ; a stranger's umbrella has
  ρ near zero.

> **In plain terms —** these three are different dials, not one. Something can be right in
> your face (high κ), or feel true (high φ), or be part of who you are (high ρ), in any
> combination. Most of this page is about keeping those dials from getting mashed together.

## Permitted configurations

The coordinate independence (see [Three Coordinates](three-coordinates.md)) predicts which
combinations of κ, φ, ρ can occur — because the three dials are genuinely separate, the
theory expects to find every "shape" of experience where they're turned to different
settings. The table below is a sample of those permitted combinations; each row is a kind
of experience you've probably had, decoded into the three dials.

| Case | κ | φ | ρ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable embodied content | high | high | high |
| Vivid hallucination | low | high | low |
| Anesthetized bodily ownership | low | low | high |
| Alien clear content | varies | high | low |
| Phantom limb | low | varies | high |
| Repressed memory | varies | undefined | high |
| Automatic motor pattern | high | undefined | high |
| Intrusive thought | high | low/undefined | low |
| Insight (the moment of) | varies | newly defined | growing |
| Mode-C-merged content (early dev) | high | undefined | very high |

A few rows, unpacked so the abstraction lands:

- **Stable embodied content (high/high/high)** — your own hand, looked at calmly. It's in
  contact (you can feel it), it checks out (it's really there), and it's yours (losing it
  would change you). All three dials up. This is the ordinary case, the baseline.
- **Vivid hallucination (low/high/low)** — something *seems* completely real (φ high) but
  there's nothing actually touching you (κ low) and it isn't part of who you are (ρ low).
  The felt-realness and the actual-contact have come apart.
- **Anesthetized bodily ownership (low/low/high)** — your arm under local anesthetic. You
  can't feel it (κ low) and can't currently verify much through it (φ low), yet it's still
  *your arm* (ρ high) — the years of it being yours don't switch off because the nerve
  block did.
- **Phantom limb (low/varies/high)** — the canonical case of identity without contact. The
  limb is gone, so κ is low, but its integration into you (ρ) was built up over a lifetime
  and persists. You still feel where it *should* be.
- **Mode-C-merged content, early development (high/undefined/very-high)** — an infant before
  the self/not-self line has hardened. A thing is right there (high κ) and felt as utterly
  continuous-with-self (very high ρ), with no verification machinery yet to ask "is this me
  or not?" (φ undefined). This is what "no boundary yet" looks like in the three dials.

> **In plain terms —** every row is a real, recognizable state, and what makes each one
> *that* state is which dials are up and which are down. The theory's bet is that no normal
> human experience falls outside this kind of breakdown — and if you found one that did,
> you'd have found a problem.

> **From the inside —** the phantom-limb row is the easiest to feel: "I know it's not there.
> I can see it's not there. It is still, unmistakably, *mine*, and it itches." Contact gone,
> ownership intact.

## What the architecture rules out

The foundation lists 20 explicit exclusions — places where it slams the door on a claim it
will *not* make. These are the theory's load-bearing "no"s. Getting one of them wrong would
break it; affirming one of these forbidden claims is exactly what would falsify it. The
load-bearing ones:

- **No rich self from the cogito alone.** The [cogito](the-cogito.md) — the bare "I am, I
  exist," the one thing that can't be doubted because doubting it already needs an *I* to do
  the doubting — licenses *only* self-identity. It does **not** hand you a body, a toolkit of
  verification skills, or a developed personality for free. All of that has to be built
  through encounter and time. (Exclusions 1–3, 5, 11, 15.)
- **Nothing non-core reaches ρ = 1.** Only the core I sits at **ρ = 1** (total
  identity-correlation, the maximum on the scale). Your child, your body, your dearest
  memory — all sit *below* 1, however high. And even the core's ρ = 1 is a
  [mathematical anchor](identity-correlation-profile.md) — the fixed point the whole scale is
  measured against — *not* a claim that the core I is what you'd save first. (Exclusions 7,
  19.)
- **No intrinsic item gets a φ value.** Logic and math don't sit at the *top* of the
  verification scale — they're *off the scale entirely* (the theory calls this "Tier 1,"
  outside the φ-domain). The φ-dial measures how well something checks out *for you, through
  your tools*; a logical truth isn't a thing you verify that way, it's part of the
  machinery you verify *with*. (Exclusions 6, 12.)
- **The asymptotic field is not conscious-only.** The "asymptotic field" — the whole sprawl
  of contents in your mental field that aren't the bare core I — contains *unconscious*
  contents too, not just the ones you're currently aware of. (Exclusion 13.)
- **No uniform reasoning across minds.** Different minds have different toolkits — the
  verification skills you've built aren't the ones someone else built. There's no single
  universal reasoner everyone shares. (Exclusion 14.)
- **No per-encounter prediction of which mode fires.** The theory tells you what a
  [mode](modes-of-development.md) of development *does* when it operates — it does *not*
  predict which one will fire for any particular moment of your day. Which mode runs depends
  on substrate, history, environment, and chance, which the theory doesn't formalize.
  (Exclusion 17.)
- **Consciousness is not reducible to identity-correlation.** This is the most important one.
  ρ does **not** measure "how conscious" something is, and conscious beings can't be ranked
  by overall ρ-strength — a person with a "bigger" self isn't more conscious. Consciousness
  is *presupposed*: it's the fact that there's an I with a field at all. Identity-correlation
  is a relation taxonomized *within* that already-given conscious field. The theory sorts the
  furniture in the room; it does not claim to explain the lights being on. (Exclusion 18.)
- **ρ is not valuation, preservation-priority, or action-priority.** How much a thing is
  *part of you* (ρ) is a different question from how much you *value* it, how much you'd act
  to *preserve* it, or what you'd reach for *first under pressure*. (Exclusion 19; see the
  [self-sacrifice case](the-two-prerogatives.md).)
- **The prerogative of continued existence is not metaphysically inviolable.** The pull to
  keep existing (PCE) operates *always* as a structural feature — but it is not an
  unbreakable law. Under extreme, identifiable conditions it can be defeated, misdirected, or
  routed through distorted projections. A completed suicide in severe
  [profile disturbance](profile-shape-disturbances.md) is **not** a simple counterexample to
  the theory; it's exactly the kind of extreme, identifiable distortion the exclusion names.
  (Exclusion 20.)

> **In plain terms —** the door-slams cluster around three confusions the theory refuses to
> make: don't conflate the bare "I exist" with a whole fleshed-out person; don't conflate
> *how much something is you* with *how much you'd fight to keep it*; and don't conflate
> *being conscious* with *being a self*. Affirm any of those and you've contradicted the
> architecture — which is precisely how you'd know it had been falsified.

> **From the inside —** Exclusion 19 is the one with teeth: a parent dives in front of a car
> for their child, and the surface story "they valued the child more than themselves"
> dissolves. The child has high ρ but not ρ = 1; the core I still anchors at 1 the whole
> time. What moved the body wasn't a ρ-ranking — it was a separate drive to preserve a
> particular *configuration* of who they are, one that includes the child surviving. (See
> [the two prerogatives](the-two-prerogatives.md).)

### Common misreadings

- *"ρ = 1 at the core means the core is what you most want to protect."* No — it's a
  **mathematical anchor**, the fixed reference point the rest of the scale is measured from.
  Protection-priority is a different mechanism entirely (PCE, the prerogative of continued
  existence). This is the trap Exclusions 7 and 19 exist to block.
- *"Higher ρ means more conscious."* No — Exclusion 18. ρ ranks how *bound up with you*
  things are, *inside* a conscious field that's already assumed. It says nothing about the
  degree or presence of consciousness.
- *"Math and logic are maximally-verified contents."* No — Exclusions 6 and 12. They're not
  high points on the φ-scale; they're off it, in the intrinsic tier you reason *with*.

## Honest limits — out of scope *by design*

These aren't gaps to be filled; they're outside what a single-perspective theory — a theory
written strictly from inside *one* I's point of view — can address at all. Asking it to
settle them would be asking it to step outside the one perspective it's built on.

- **Whether other minds actually exist** — unverifiable from inside; you can model another
  person in rich detail, but you can never confirm from within your own apparatus that
  there's genuinely "someone home" in them. The theory *assumes* they're real (this is
  "perspectival realism," not solipsism) but admits it can't *prove* it from inside. See
  [Other Minds](other-minds.md).
- **The metaphysics of the I** — what the self *is* (a substance? a process? a pattern? a
  fixed-point of computation? an illusion?). Out of scope; only the structural fact of
  self-identity is claimed. What the index "I" *points at*, metaphysically, is a different
  paper.
- **The world independent of the I**, and the **physics-level direction of time** — why
  entropy increases, why causation runs one way. Those are physics problems; the theory only
  treats the *felt* asymmetry of time, not its physical source.
- **Physical implementation** — what actually realizes the substrate (brains, bodies,
  something else) is an empirical question the formal theory doesn't settle. The form could
  in principle be implemented in more than one kind of stuff.

> **In plain terms —** there are some questions a one-person-view theory simply *can't* be
> the right tool for — like proving other people are conscious, or saying what the self is
> made of "from outside." The theory marks these as out of bounds *on purpose*, so you don't
> mistake a deliberate silence for a failure.

## Working assumptions — named, not hidden

These are the things the foundation *rests on* without deriving them — the posts holding up
the tent. The theory's honesty move is to name them out loud rather than smuggle them in as
if they fell out of the cogito:

- The [intrinsic layer](two-layers.md) — naturalist-realism, the bet that real mathematical
  structure pervades the system you're embedded in. Motivated (rivers really do have
  geometry), not proven.
- The [cogito](the-cogito.md) as a postulate — taken, not derived. The "any denial of the I
  needs an I to deny it" argument backs it up, but it's still treated as a starting point.
- The **primal not-I posits** — the basic givens that aren't the I: content multiplicity
  (there's more than one thing), time, body, contact interface, and *optionally* other
  minds. None of these is squeezed out of the bare cogito; each is named as a working posit.
- A **profile-preservation principle** — the operating self tends to preserve or restore a
  coherent continuation of its own structure under encounter and projected action. In plain
  terms: you tend to stay *you* through change instead of dissolving into random content.
- An **agency / action-space assumption** — you operate not just as a field of contents but
  as an *agent* among possible actions and projected futures. The bare cogito gives you "I
  am"; it does not give you "I can act toward a future" — that's a separate posit.

The [two prerogatives](the-two-prerogatives.md) — the pull toward consistency (**PtCns**,
treating your own frame as the standard against which the world is checked) and the pull
toward continued existence (**PCE**, the drive to keep the maximally-self-defined you going)
— are treated as *theorem-candidates* relative to these assumptions: derivable in principle
from the operating self plus the principles above, but the full derivation is application-
level work, not yet done in the foundation.

> **In plain terms —** every theory leans on something it doesn't prove. This one writes its
> lean down: real structure out there, the bare "I exist," a few basic givens, the tendency
> to stay yourself, and the fact that you act. The two big drives (stay consistent, stay
> alive) are promised as *derivable* from these — IOUs, honestly labeled as IOUs.

## Genuine open problems (in scope)

Unlike the out-of-scope limits above, these are questions the theory *could* answer with
more development but currently doesn't. They're live work, not closed doors:

- **The exact set of integration modes and their weights** — the precise menu of ways
  contents get integrated into the lived-I network, and how each is weighted, is
  underspecified; different choices yield different applied predictions.
- **Whether max-flow is the right ρ-aggregation.** ρ is computed as **max-flow** — picture
  your self as a network of pipes rooted at the core I, and a thing's ρ as *how much can flow
  to it through the strongest set of connections* (so a thing held by one thin pipe is
  fragile; a thing held by many is robust). This matches the felt fragility of identity well,
  but it isn't strictly *forced* — other aggregation rules are compatible with the
  foundation.
- **The per-encounter mode-selection rule** — *which* mode fires for a given moment. The
  theory commits to what each mode does, not to which one runs when.
- **Prerogative arbitration** — when the two drives *conflict*, which wins? In grief, your
  consistency-drive (PtCns) pushes you to update toward "they're gone" while your
  continuation-drive (PCE) pushes to preserve the version of you that *included* them at high
  ρ. The same tug-of-war appears in self-sacrifice, trauma, and depressive distortion. The
  foundation distinguishes the two prerogatives but gives **no general rule** for which one
  dominates in a given case — dominance is treated as substrate-, history-, and
  context-sensitive.
- **Action-priority dynamics under pressure** — what actually determines your choices in a
  split-second crisis. The theory names *three contributing mechanisms* — a gut-level
  protective reflex (third-order pathway activation, the body that moves before you think); a
  deliberate imagining of post-action futures (future-state projection, weighing "the world
  with me gone but them saved" against "me here but them lost"); and a substrate-level
  urgency surge (threat recalibrating what grabs your attention) — but does **not** formalize
  how they combine or trade off when they pull in different directions. ρ is *not* the thing
  doing the deciding here; ρ is structural integration, not action-priority.
- **The distributional shape of [Mode B onset](modes-of-development.md)** — *when*, across
  people, reflexive self-aware reasoning comes online. The theory commits to the structural
  constraints (zero at the start, non-zero later, varying across individuals) but does *not*
  pin a specific curve; a bell shape is intuitive but not forced.
- **A list of phenomena left for application papers** — the felt continuous stream of
  experience, dreams and altered states, the narrative self, specific affects (shame vs guilt
  vs embarrassment), empathy and emotional contagion, aesthetics, and more. Not denied — just
  not yet derived here.

> **In plain terms —** the loudest open problem is *referee-ing your own drives*. When
> "stay consistent with reality" and "preserve who you are" point opposite ways — which is
> exactly what grief, sacrifice, and crisis feel like — the theory honestly admits it can't
> yet tell you which one will win. It names the wrestlers; it doesn't call the match.

> **From the inside —** action-priority under pressure is the one you've felt without words:
> the body that yanks you back from a curb before "danger" has finished forming as a thought.
> Three things were pulling at once — the reflex, the imagined future, the adrenaline-surge —
> and the theory can name all three but not yet write the equation for how they add up.

> The honesty about edges is itself a design commitment. Where critiques surfaced real
> problems, earlier versions fixed them; where critiques surfaced genuine philosophical
> commitments (rationalism about intrinsic structure, perspectival anchoring, structural
> rather than predictive scope), the architecture **stands on them** rather than evading.
