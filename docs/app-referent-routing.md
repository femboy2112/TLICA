## The one structural move

Ordinary talk fuses "empathy" into a single faculty. This paper **splits it into two
operations**, both already living inside the existing apparatus:

- **The modeling channel (T)** — in plain terms, the part of you that *builds an accurate
  picture of another person*: the [verification toolkit](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md)
  registering another as a real I, with their own
  [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md), [preservation prerogative](the-two-prerogatives.md),
  and field of experience. T is *reading the other correctly* — seeing that there's a someone
  in there, and seeing them clearly.
- **The routing channel (R)** — in plain terms, the part where that pictured other *gets in
  and moves you*: the modeled person is taken up as an input to *your own*
  profile, so their being hurt **lands** as a disturbance in your own feeling.
  R is *the other mattering* to you — not knowing they hurt, but the hurt reaching you.

The two are coupled by a developed parameter **λ (lambda, the coupling)** — in plain terms,
*how reliably seeing-someone turns into feeling-them*. The default is coupling — to recognize
a subject is, by default, to feel them — but the channels are **dissociable**, and that
dissociation is the whole paper. λ is *not chosen*, *not a fourth
[coordinate](three-coordinates.md)*, and *plastic over a life*.

> **In plain terms —** there are two separate jobs hiding inside "caring about someone":
> *getting them right* (T) and *being moved by them* (R). Usually the first triggers the
> second automatically. λ is just the strength of that "automatically." Everything dark in
> this paper happens when the two come apart.

## The cold frame, and its three sources

The **cold frame** is the configuration in which another figures as a means or an obstacle
rather than as a center of experience — the machinery that would make them *land* is not
engaged. Its unsettling property: it looks identical from outside however it was produced,
and it is produced **three structurally different ways** that ordinary moral vocabulary
collapses into one. They share an output and nothing else — which is why the **repairs are
not interchangeable**:

| Path | The agent | Modeling (T) | Routing (R) | What produces the coldness | What repairs it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Psychopathy** | born or driven to low λ | intact, often sharp | severed on the other-class | the channels are decoupled | barely repairable |
| **Power-decoupling** | high λ, *instrumental* golden rule | intact | available | a flattened field removes the incentive | **restore the field** (accountability) |
| **Atrocity-path** | high λ, *constitutive*, fully moral | **exogenously thinned** on the target class | intact, faithful | the model was hollowed out at the cultural source | **restore the model** (re-humanize) |

Cross-applying a repair is why interventions misfire: accountability does little for the
atrocity-path agent (who already feels fully accountable to an intact morality), and
re-humanization does little for the psychopath (whose model was never thinned).

> **In plain terms —** three very different people — a manipulator, a decent boss who turns
> cruel once no one can check him, and an ordinary moral person caught up in an atrocity —
> can do the identical cold thing for completely different inner reasons. Treating them as
> one kind of person means handing each the wrong fix.

## Psychopathy is not the absence of feeling

Affect in psychopathy is intact — the pleasure of winning, of power, contempt — and the
modeling channel is frequently *sharp* (manipulation requires an accurate model). What's
specifically attenuated is **routing on the other-subject class**: the accurate model is
simply not routed. That is *more* dangerous than emptiness, because it pairs accurate reading
with absent feeling — the exact combination that makes another legible *as an instrument*.

> **In plain terms —** the psychopath isn't numb. They feel plenty — for themselves. What's
> missing is the wire that carries *your* pain across to them. They read you perfectly and
> aren't moved at all, which is precisely what lets them use you: they know exactly what hurts
> and feel nothing about pressing it.

## Entering the cold frame without a lie

You might expect that treating a recognized subject as a non-subject requires self-deception.
For a tightly-coupled agent ("truth-as-affect" — someone for whom T can't output *true*
without R firing, so honesty and feeling are the same event) that route is blocked. Yet they
can still go cold — through **referent-thinning** (in plain terms, *quietly letting the other
go blurry in your mind* until there's barely a person there to feel): attenuating the *model*
of the other until routing has almost nothing to take up. No falsehood is ever asserted; the
other just becomes less present, and the affect, with less to route, fires less. The crucial
consequence is the **phenomenology** (in plain terms, *what it feels like from the inside*):
because nothing false was claimed, the thinned state doesn't present as deception — it presents
as **clarity**. The agent feels not *"I am failing to feel a person"* but *"I am accurately
perceiving that there is less here to feel."* The cold frame arrives wearing the face of
insight. *This is the single most important fact for understanding how decent people do
terrible things.*

> **From the inside —** you never tell yourself a lie. The other person just gets fainter,
> less vivid, easier to look past — and because you never said anything false, it doesn't feel
> like you're shutting down. It feels like you're finally *seeing straight*. That counterfeit
> clarity is the trapdoor.

## Driven, not built — a distribution, not a verdict

λ is not fixed hardware sorting people into warm and cold. It's developed and **distributed**
across the population (in plain terms, *people are spread out along it, not split into two
bins*): some begin *past* the threshold (coldness is their baseline), some hold a high coupling
that only accumulated load could lower (reachable only by being *driven* there), and some sit
so far inside that no realistic load would reach it. The driven route is a **state-to-trait
conversion** (in plain terms, *a temporary state hardening into who you just are now*) with
the same shape as addiction (the sibling *Substrate Override States* paper). So the author's
own anchoring report — *"I cannot lie to myself"* — is a true description of a *current*
high-coupling regime, **not a law of their nature**.

"Jadedness," on this account, is *not* the corrosion of the capacity to feel; it is the
installation of a **threat-model** (in plain terms, *a learned filter for who's safe to open
up to*) that prices routing correctly — gating *whom* to route by measured profile rather than
by default. The naïve configuration was routing *without* a threat-model; the mature one is
routing *plus* one. *(The fine margin-structure of the born-low region is held in author-gated
skeleton.)*

> **In plain terms —** nobody is simply "a cold person." Some people start cold, some can only
> become cold if life beats it into them, and some basically never will. And "getting jaded"
> isn't your heart hardening — it's your heart learning *who to spend itself on*. You didn't
> lose the feeling; you installed a doorman.

## Occupied per-relation, not held per-person

The cold frame is not a standing stance toward a person; it's occupied per *relation* and
reassigns in **real time** as the salient relation shifts. The paper's domestic "cat case"
makes the point: a pet that interrupts you is an unrouted impediment; when a second pet
misbehaves, you may visibly lavish affection on the *first* — but that affection is an action
aimed at *correcting the second*, routed *through* the first's body, and it only works because
the second is modeled as a subject who can read the display and be stung. So in one instant:
one animal is an unrouted medium, the other a fully-modeled subject acted on instrumentally.

Two consequences. **Behavior does not reveal λ** — kindness can run over an unrouted referent
and instrumentality over a routed one. And the *genuinely* cold operation isn't thinning (which
at least removes the subject from view) but its limit: **full modeling, available routing, and
the golden rule simply never recruited** — the subject stays in full view and is declined
anyway.

> **In plain terms —** you flip in and out of the cold frame constantly, all day, depending on
> who matters *to the moment*. So you can't read someone's heart off a single act: petting one
> cat to spite the other looks like love and isn't; the coldest move of all is seeing a person
> completely, being perfectly able to care — and just choosing not to.

## The golden rule is a placement, not a faculty

Having routed another, the further moral step is the golden rule: back-propagating *your own*
[preservation-ranking](the-two-prerogatives.md) onto them (in plain terms, *treating what
you'd want done to you as the cue for what to do to them*) as the signal for right action. The
paper's reorganizing claim: this isn't a faculty you have or lack — it's a **content held at a
position in your [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md)** (in plain terms, *a rule stored
somewhere in you — and how deep it's stored is everything*), and *where* it's held decides
everything:

- Held **constitutively** (in plain terms, *built into who you are*) — enforced from *inside*;
  defecting is a [consistency](the-two-prerogatives.md) violation, a partial self-loss.
  **Field-independent**: removing accountability doesn't remove the cost.
- Held **instrumentally** (in plain terms, *kept on as a handy tool*) — enforced from *outside*
  by the social field. **Field-contingent**: flatten the field and it's dropped.

At baseline the two are **behaviorally identical** — both do well by others. They diverge
*only* under **field-flattening** (in plain terms, *taking away anyone's power to catch you,
fault you, or punish you*), which is the paper's strongest operational handle: the everyday
catastrophe of power isn't a faculty breaking, it's a flattened field acting on an
instrumentally-placed rule. (The rule also carries a **projection error**: back-propagation is
valid only under profile-similarity; naïve good faith assumes the other wants what you'd want
and is exploited where that fails — which is what the threat-model corrects.)

> **In plain terms —** "be good to others" can be wired in two ways that look the same on a
> normal day: either it's part of your bones, or it's a tool you keep because it pays. You only
> find out which when the consequences vanish — when no one can touch you. The decent boss who
> turns monstrous with power didn't break; the leash that was doing the work just got cut.

## The will, and its reflexive bound

Where does choice enter? Placement is **derived, not chosen** — set by λ, by margin (in plain
terms, *how far past the line you started*), by the fields you've lived in, and by your
toolkit's own model of what your will can do. None of those is chosen, so making placement a
*freely-willed* knob would contradict the desert-removal below. The will is real but
**bounded**: it works the *slack* those parameters leave — and its access to the slack is
itself gated, because **you can attempt only what your toolkit certifies the will is capable
of.** This explains the agent who is *stuck while capable*: the slack is there, but the
self-model never certifies the attempt as a live option, so it sits unused and invisible.

> **From the inside —** your will is real, but it only gets to push on the bit of you that's
> actually loose — and worse, you can only even *try* what some inner voice has already marked
> "possible for me." So you can be perfectly able to change and never reach for it, because the
> option was never lit up as a thing you could do.

A sharp epistemic corollary the paper develops carefully: whether a self-model (or anyone's
virtue) is *accurate* is **not readable from a single outcome** — calibration (in plain terms,
*whether your self-estimate actually matches reality*) is a property of the *pattern across
instances*. And even a long record can be manufactured three ways, each needing a different
control: a **fixed estimate** that happens to cross the truth (check whether it *moves with
conditions*), **selection on output** where failures were filtered before you looked (sample
*independently of outcome*), and a **field-contingent** producer that tracks perfectly *while
the field holds* (vary the field). To read genuine structure off conduct you need multiple
instances, across varied conditions, sampled independently of outcome.

> **In plain terms —** you can't tell whether someone (or you) is really good from one good
> deed, or even a long clean streak. A broken clock is right twice a day; a record can look
> spotless because the failures got quietly filtered out; and a fair-weather decency tracks
> perfectly right up until the weather turns. To trust the pattern you have to watch it under
> changed conditions you didn't get to cherry-pick.

## §6A — How ordinary moral agents produce atrocity

The highest-stakes path locates the coldness **not in the agent** but in the *target*. Mass
atrocity is overwhelmingly committed by *ordinary moral agents* — intact, wide, constitutively-held
golden rules; people who love their families and feel guilt over ordinary wrongs. Agent-side
parameters can't explain it, because by those measures these people aren't cold. The mechanism:
**referent-thinning performed exogenously** (in plain terms, *the blurring done to the victim
group by the culture, not by the individual*) — on a whole *class* of others, at the level of
the **cultural-I** (in plain terms, *the shared, distributed mind-set of a whole people — a
pattern, not a person*), and inherited by the individual as an already-corrupted input to their
modeling channel. Propaganda, the standing-stripping legal category, the slur, the
pseudo-scientific apparatus — these are *thinning instruments*.

What such an agent runs is *not* psychopathy (accurate model, severed routing). It is **faithful
routing over a model thinned at the source** — the routing works perfectly and finds almost
nothing to route. And because (per the no-lie mechanism) the thinned model never registers as a
lie, the agent feels not cruel but **correct**. *"The horror is not malfunction; it is correct
operation over corrupted input."*

> **In plain terms —** most people who do monstrous things aren't monsters. Their caring works
> fine — it's just been handed a pre-blurred picture of the victims by their whole society, so
> when they look there's "no one much there" to feel for. Their machinery is healthy; the input
> was poisoned upstream, and that's exactly why they feel righteous instead of guilty.

The paper's most load-bearing discipline guards this section: **the cultural-I is not
anthropomorphized.** It is a distributed pattern, not a mind; it *thins* but carries no intent.
Because it is *emergent over* its individuals (in plain terms, *it only exists as the sum of the
people, with nothing extra behind them*), any predicate pinned to it **distributes back down**
onto every correlated individual — so the discipline is bidirectional:

- Attribute *intent* upward to "the culture" → you've laundered an unearned guilt onto the
  uninvolved (manufactured collective guilt).
- Refuse to attribute the wrong *downward* to actual participants → atrocity becomes nobody's
  deed, a fog of "forces."

> **In plain terms —** "the culture" can't *want* anything; only people do. Blame the culture
> as if it had a will, and that blame quietly splashes onto every innocent who happens to belong
> to it. Refuse to blame any actual person, and the horror becomes nobody's fault at all. Both
> are the same mistake — putting intent where there's only a pattern.

Intent lives **only** at the individual and institutional layer. The population under a thinning
apparatus therefore resolves into five attributable positions, scored *by production path, not by
deed alone*: **architects/operators** (built the corrupted model — full intent), **compliant**
(acted on it without reaching for available counter-evidence — mitigated, not erased), **genuine
resisters** (held their model intact *against* the thinning — the expected high-coupling,
intact-model tail), **performative resisters** (instrumental placement in an unflattened field
where resistance *pays* — identical output, contingent on the field), and **merely correlated
non-participants** (the cultural correlation, *no* intent — the position the no-anthropomorphism
rule exists to protect). The **capacity layer** (λ, margin, placement) and the **attribution
layer** are orthogonal and cross — which is what stops the whole analysis collapsing back into
"good people and bad people."

> **In plain terms —** under an atrocity there are at least five different kinds of people: the
> ones who built the lie, the ones who went along without looking for the truth they could have
> found, the ones who saw through it and refused, the ones who "resisted" only because resisting
> happened to pay, and the ones merely born into the group who did nothing. *How* you got to
> your behavior matters as much as the behavior — and notice it cuts both ways: even some heroes
> were only heroes while it was rewarded.

## Bindingness without desert

The meta-ethical core, and the part the author flags as the highest overreach risk. The
placements that decide your moral character are, *with respect to desert*, **arbitrary** (in
plain terms, *with respect to whether you've earned praise or blame for them* — born or driven,
never authored). Run honestly, the symmetry **cuts both ways**: the arbitrariness that disarms
*blame* for a low coupling equally disarms *credit* for a high one. But this isn't the
dissolution of morality, because **bindingness and desert come apart** (in plain terms,
*"you're truly held to this" and "you earned being the kind who's held" are two different
things*): a constraint can be fully authoritative over you without being authored by you (most
real constraints are). You keep *"this is right and I am held to it"* and give up *"and I deserve
credit for being the sort who is held."*

The sharpest form: **incorruptibility under power** — the most admired property in the catalogue —
is, on this account, an *unauthored placement*. The incorruptible person really is more to be
relied on **and merits no credit for the placement that makes them so**. *"The better agent is
really better, and deserves nothing for it."* This is explicitly **not nihilism** — it removes
*desert*, not *value* or *bindingness*. And it compounds with the epistemic limit: since observers
can't even recover placement from conduct, praise aimed from outside at observed deeds can't
reliably track which agent it's looking at.

> **In plain terms —** the things that make you a good person — that you can't be bought, that
> you don't curdle under power — you didn't earn. They were handed to you by a lottery you didn't
> run. That doesn't make right-and-wrong fake, and it doesn't let you off the hook: you're still
> truly bound to do right. You just don't get to feel *superior* for being the lucky one who is.
> The good person is genuinely better and is owed nothing for it.

## Discriminators, and what it is *not*

The paper stakes **six independent, falsifiable core discriminators** (in plain terms, *six
risky predictions that could turn out false and would sink the theory if they did* — each still
needing the measurement-hardening the *Substrate Override* predictions received): T/R dissociation
(and behavior ≠ λ); field-flattening separating constitutive from instrumental placement (for
both cruelty *and* virtue); affect-restoration failing to transfer to standing-restoration;
non-interchangeability of the three repairs; the multi-instance/varied-condition/outcome-independent
specification for reading structure off conduct; and the recursive bound on the will.

> **In plain terms —** the theory sticks its neck out six times in ways you could actually test:
> that reading-someone and feeling-them can be pried apart; that taking away accountability sorts
> the truly-good from the merely-careful (and the true heroes from the show-offs); that making
> people *feel warmer* toward a group won't by itself restore that group's *standing*; that the
> three cold-frame fixes don't substitute for each other; that you need varied, un-cherry-picked
> evidence to judge real virtue; and that changing your character needs both room to move and a
> self-image that says "I can."

> **The paper's boundary disciplines (quoted in spirit):** it is *not* "psychopathy is a choice,"
> not a moralization, not a theory of good vs. bad people, not a diagnostic tool, not an
> anthropomorphizing of culture, not proven moral realism, not nihilism, not libertarian free will
> nor hard determinism, **not an adjudication of any contested present-day conflict**, and **not an
> excuse for atrocity** — *"explaining how a wrong was produced leaves the wrong wrong and its
> authors answerable. Mechanism is not exculpation."*

**Open problems** include keeping λ a *relation between two operations* (never a fourth
coordinate), placing these phenomena within the [Agency Architecture](app-agency-architecture.md)
taxonomy, and the two author-gated forks — how endogenous and exogenous thinning interact (OP8),
and whether restoring a thinned model automatically re-recruits routing or leaves a residual (OP9).
