## The one structural move

Ordinary talk fuses "empathy" into a single faculty. This paper **splits it into two
operations**, both already living inside the existing apparatus:

- **The modeling channel (T)** — in plain terms, the part of you that *builds a picture of
  another person*: the [verification toolkit](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md) registering
  another as a real I, with their own [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md),
  [preservation prerogative](the-two-prerogatives.md), and field of experience. Run well, T
  *reads the other correctly* — seeing that there's a someone in there, and seeing them
  clearly. But T is not guaranteed accurate: when its source is corrupted (see thinning,
  below) it delivers a confident *wrong* picture instead.
- **The routing channel (R)** — in plain terms, the part where that pictured other *gets in
  and moves you*: the modeled person is taken up as an input to *your own* profile, so their
  being hurt **lands** as a disturbance in your own feeling. R is *the other mattering* to you
  — not knowing they hurt, but the hurt reaching you.

The two are coupled by a developed parameter **λ (lambda, the coupling)** — in plain terms,
*how reliably seeing-someone turns into feeling-them*. The default is coupling — to recognize
a subject is, by default, to feel them — but the channels are **dissociable**, and that
dissociation is the whole paper. λ is *not chosen*, *not a fourth
[coordinate](three-coordinates.md)*, and *plastic over a life*.

> **In plain terms —** there are two separate jobs hiding inside "caring about someone":
> *getting them right* (T) and *being moved by them* (R). Usually the first triggers the
> second automatically. λ is just the strength of that "automatically." Everything dark in
> this paper happens when the two come apart.

> **In plain terms — where this paper is headed:** first it splits "caring about someone" into
> two separate jobs — seeing them clearly, and being moved by what you see. Then it shows three
> completely different reasons a person can go cold toward someone else. Then it asks the hardest
> question in the paper: how do ordinarily decent people end up producing atrocity — and the
> answer isn't that they turned into monsters. It's that their caring worked perfectly, on a
> picture that had already been quietly hollowed out.

### The φ-gap

One fact colours everything below. The modeling channel never delivers *the other* — it
delivers a **model** of the other, carried at the architecture's
[truth-indistinguishability coordinate φ](three-coordinates.md) (in plain terms, *how
true-it-seems, which is not how true-it-is*). Routing then takes up that model, not the actual
person. So the whole T→R pipeline runs on a φ-graded picture, and wherever the picture and the
person come apart, what's felt and done faithfully tracks the *picture*. And here is the sting:
where the picture is **high-φ** and no higher-order suspicion about its *source* is active, the
response presents to the agent as **correct rather than as error**. (The paper is careful here
— this is "presents as correct," not "never wrong"; the agent *can* come to doubt the source,
and that doubt is the only handle.) The paper calls this the **φ-gap**, and it is not a flaw to
be patched but the medium the architecture works in. It resurfaces at four points below: the
thinning that feels like *clarity* not a lie (§3), the driven route that runs on *apparent*
wrong not verified wrong (§4), the dual golden rule that misfires into confident paternalism
(§6.1), and the ordinary atrocity-path agent who *feels correct* (§6A).

> **In plain terms —** you never act on people; you act on your *picture* of them. And a
> convincing picture feels accurate from the inside — that's just what having a point of view
> is like. So when the picture is wrong but *seems* solid, you don't feel mistaken; you feel
> *right*. The only way out is to grow suspicious of where the picture came from. Every dark
> turn in this paper is a clean machine running on a quietly corrupted picture.

## The cold frame, and its three sources

The **cold frame** is the configuration in which another figures as a means or an obstacle
rather than as a center of experience — the machinery that would make them *land* is not
engaged. Its unsettling property: it looks identical from outside however it was produced,
and it is produced **three structurally different ways** that ordinary moral vocabulary
collapses into one. They share an output and nothing else — which is why the **repairs are
not interchangeable**:

| Path | The agent | Modeling (T) | Routing (R) | What produces the coldness | What repairs it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Psychopathy** | born or driven to low λ | adequate, sometimes sharp | severed on the other-class | the channels are decoupled | barely repairable |
| **Power-decoupling** | high λ, *instrumental* golden rule | intact | available | a flattened field removes the incentive | **restore the field** (accountability) |
| **Atrocity-path** | high λ, *constitutive*; morally ordinary | **thinned** on the target class | intact, faithful | the model was hollowed out at the source | **restore the model** (re-humanize) |

Cross-applying a repair is why interventions misfire: accountability does little for the
atrocity-path agent (who already feels fully accountable to an intact morality), and
re-humanization does little for the psychopath (whose model was never thinned).

> **In plain terms —** three very different people — a manipulator, a decent boss who turns
> cruel once no one can check him, and an ordinary moral person caught up in an atrocity —
> can do the identical cold thing for completely different inner reasons. Treating them as
> one kind of person means handing each the wrong fix.

## Psychopathy is not the absence of feeling

Affect in psychopathy is intact — the pleasure of winning, of power, contempt. What's
specifically attenuated is **routing on the other-subject class**: the model, whatever its
fidelity, is simply not routed. Where the configuration is manipulative, the modeling channel
is at least adequate and sometimes sharp (manipulation needs a workable model of what moves
the target). That pairing — workable reading, absent feeling — is *more* dangerous than
emptiness, because it is exactly the combination that makes another legible *as an instrument*.

A careful hedge the paper insists on: the claim is **dissociation, not enhancement**. It does
*not* say psychopaths have superior or uniformly intact theory of mind — the mentalizing
evidence is mixed. It says only that the deficit sits on the *routing* side, on a *specific
referent class*, rather than being a global flattening of affect. The diagnosis is a routing
failure, not low feeling and not primarily a modeling deficit.

> **In plain terms —** the psychopath isn't numb. They feel plenty — for themselves. What's
> missing is the wire that carries *your* pain across to them. They can read you well enough to
> use you and feel nothing about pressing where it hurts. The point isn't that they're
> brilliant mind-readers; it's that the reading and the feeling have come unhooked.

## Entering the cold frame without a lie

You might expect that treating a recognized subject as a non-subject requires self-deception.
For a tightly-coupled agent ("truth-as-affect" — someone for whom T can't output *true*
without R firing, so honesty and feeling are the same event) that route is blocked. Yet they
can still go cold — through **referent-thinning** (in plain terms, *quietly letting the other
go blurry in your mind* until there's barely a person there to feel): attenuating the *model*
of the other until routing has almost nothing to take up. No falsehood is ever asserted; the
other just becomes less present, and the affect, with less to route, fires less. The crucial
consequence is the **phenomenology** (in plain terms, *what it feels like from the inside*):
because nothing false was claimed, the thinned state doesn't present as deception — it presents
as **clarity**. The agent feels not *"I am failing to feel a person"* but *"I am accurately
perceiving that there is less here to feel."* The cold frame arrives wearing the face of
insight. *This is the single most important fact for understanding how decent people do
terrible things.*

> **From the inside —** you never tell yourself a lie. The other person just gets fainter,
> less vivid, easier to look past — and because you never said anything false, it doesn't feel
> like you're shutting down. It feels like you're finally *seeing straight*. That counterfeit
> clarity is the trapdoor.

### Why an agent thins — the driver (new in v0.4.3)

The mechanism above says *how* self-thinning works, but not *what moves an agent to do it*.
v0.4.3 supplies the driver, and it is ordinary: **a painful, complex truth is itself a thinning
instrument.** An accurate model is not always simple. It can be internally contradictory —
*frame-decoherent*: the person who wronged me is *also* a victim; the threat *also* contains
the innocent; this warrants my anger *and* my grief, at once. Holding such a model is costly,
because it sits crosswise to your frame and generates the standing tension of an unresolved
incoherence — a [consistency](the-two-prerogatives.md) (PtCns) load. That tension has two
repairs. One is to **update**: enlarge the frame until it can hold the contradiction — slow,
effortful, routing-dependent (this is the warm corrective of §4.5). The other is to **thin**:
collapse the model until it stops generating the tension — fast, coherence-restoring, and (per
above) presenting as clarity, not evasion. Under acute threat, grief, or load the gradient
tilts toward thinning, because thinning is the cheaper, more immediately stabilizing repair.

This is the most ordinary road into the cold frame: not malice and not a lie, but **the refusal
of a truth too costly to hold, traded for a model simple enough to live with.** It needs no
propaganda to install — though, as §6A shows, propaganda everywhere *exploits* it by supplying
the simplified model ready-made.

> **In plain terms —** the quickest way to stop feeling for someone isn't to decide they don't
> matter — it's to stop holding the *complicated* truth about them. "He hurt me and he's also
> hurting" is exhausting to keep in your head; "he's just bad" is a relief. Under stress you
> reach for the relief, and it doesn't feel like giving up on them — it feels like finally
> seeing them clearly. That relief is the trap, and it's the same lever propaganda pulls.

## Driven, not built — a distribution, not a verdict

λ is not fixed hardware sorting people into warm and cold. It's developed and **distributed**
across the population (in plain terms, *people are spread out along it, not split into two
bins*): some begin *past* the threshold (coldness is their baseline), some hold a high coupling
that only accumulated load could lower (reachable only by being *driven* there), and some sit
so far inside that no realistic load would reach it. The driven route is a **state-to-trait
conversion** (in plain terms, *a temporary state hardening into who you just are now*): repeated
load durably lowers the coupling by the foundation's own [osmotic-imprinting](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md)
dynamics (File 3, §8.7) — the same machinery that forms any disposition, here recalibrating the
substrate that carries routing, with a *formation* layer (the lowered coupling becomes the new
baseline) and an *activation* layer (the recalibrated substrate fires attenuated routing on that
class whenever it's met).

Crucially, the load is **apparent**, not verified — *apparent* betrayal, *apparent*
exploitation, the cost *as registered* (this is the φ-gap again: φ is
truth-*indistinguishability*, not truth). A registration carries its felt cost whether or not
it was real, so the conversion can be driven by a **miscalibrated threat-model** just as well as
by genuine mistreatment: an agent who comes to register a *benign* class as exploitative has its
routing toward that class lowered all the same — and the cold frame so reached need not have been
earned by anyone's actual wrong. So the author's own anchoring report — *"I cannot lie to
myself"* — is a true description of a *current* high-coupling regime, **not a law of their
nature**.

"Jadedness," on this account, is *not* the corrosion of the capacity to feel; it is the
installation of a **threat-model** (in plain terms, *a learned filter for who's safe to open
up to*) that prices routing correctly — gating *whom* to route by measured profile rather than
by default. The naïve configuration was routing *without* a threat-model; the mature one is
routing *plus* one.

> **In plain terms —** nobody is simply "a cold person." Some people start cold, some can only
> become cold if life beats it into them, and some basically never will. And "getting jaded"
> isn't your heart hardening — it's your heart learning *who to spend itself on*. You didn't
> lose the feeling; you installed a doorman. (And the doorman can be wrong: a bad experience
> can make you wary of a whole group that never did anything to you.)

## Margin, and the principled cold frame

The born-past-threshold region — the people who start cold — subdivides by **margin**, *how far
past the line they began*, and margin has a surprising inverse relation to what kind of morality
they can acquire:

- **Low margin** (just past the line) — a sliver of routing-baseline survives, so the golden
  rule can **integrate constitutively** around it: a genuine, internally-enforced morality that
  *doesn't* revert when accountability is removed. The catch resolved in v0.4.0: this pattern is
  narrow **in activation, not in content**. It's a *full* principle that's simply rarely
  triggered — its reach set by which others the person's history happened to imprint as live
  referents. So the scope isn't fixed; it can **widen** as new referents are imprinted (the
  re-thickening dynamics below, run on the agent's own circle).
- **High margin** (far past the line) — no routing-baseline survives to integrate around, so the
  *systematic* route gives only **toolkit morality**, which reverts under field-flattening.

But "high margin → toolkit only" is a *tendency, not a law*. Because osmotic imprinting is always
running, a **historical accident** — an upbringing that wove a specific ethical pattern into the
self before any threat-model could price it as a mere tool — can leave even a high-margin agent
with an observer-free ethical pattern that is genuinely *part of the self*. Once it's
constitutive, defecting from it perturbs [self-consistency (PtCns)](the-two-prerogatives.md) and
is enforced from inside — **exactly like constitutive morality, but with no feeling underneath
it.** The conduct survives field-flattening; the warmth was never there. This is the **principled
cold frame**: reliably good, and cold. It's the **unfelt twin** of the truth-as-affect agent of
§3 — both treat defection as self-incoherence and are field-independent for it; they differ only
in whether routing rides along. And it earns its holder no credit (see *Bindingness without
desert*): the installing accident was wholly unauthored.

> **In plain terms —** there's a person who'll never sell you out and never feels a thing for
> you either. Not cold in the cruel sense — just *good by structure, not by warmth*. The right
> upbringing happened to build "do right" into their bones before life could teach them it was
> only a tool, so they hold to it the way the warm-hearted do — except there's no inner heat,
> only an inner *consistency* that would crack if they broke it. From the outside you can't tell
> them from someone who genuinely feels for you; you'd have to look for the feeling itself.

### The vice on the back of that virtue

Read as a virtue, the principled cold frame is flattening-proof and reliable. But the same
structure has a **vice**, and it's important. PtCns enforces consistency to *whatever* frame is
held — the enforcement is **content-neutral**. So a consistency-backed cold agent holds their
operative frame as steadily when it has **gone wrong or out of date** as when it's right — and,
lacking routing, they have *no felt signal* that it's gone wrong. A warm, routing-backed agent
*does* have that signal: the other's actual condition routes in as affect, and where the held
frame and that condition diverge, the affect builds a tension against the frame and eventually
flips it. So the two profiles **invert** depending on whether the frame is correct. Where the
frame is good, consistency is the reliable thing and routing the wavering thing. Where the frame
**needs updating** — the φ-gap widening underfoot — it reverses: the cold agent stays locked to
the outdated frame, while the warm agent's wavering routing is the *only* thing in the room
tracking that the model now needs to change.

The paper's anchor case is an interrogation. Two interrogators — A (principled cold) and B
(warm) — and a prisoner who won't break. A escalates, consistent with the mandate and unchecked,
because the prisoner isn't routed and there's no felt cost to weigh against the frame. B routes
the prisoner; the mounting suffering registers as affect, builds a tension against B's own
participation, and erodes B's adherence until B **flips** — drawing the line at torture exactly
where A still sees fit to proceed. A's consistency, the very thing that would make A incorruptible
*if the frame were good*, is here what locks A into the escalation. Neither disposition was
authored, so the desert-removal falls on the *dispositions* symmetrically — and (per the boundary
note) it does **not** touch the *acts*: A's escalation stays as wrong and answerable as ever.

> **In plain terms —** "reliable" cuts both ways. The person who can't be swayed is exactly the
> person who can't be *corrected* — when the rule they're loyal to is good, that's a virtue;
> when it's gone bad, their steadiness is the problem and the "weak," feeling person who can't
> stomach it is the only one still tracking the truth. Warmth makes you manipulable, and it's
> also sometimes the only brake in the room.

## Occupied per-relation, not held per-person

The cold frame is not a standing stance toward a person; it's occupied per *relation* and
reassigns in **real time** as the salient relation shifts. The paper's domestic "cat case"
makes the point: a pet that interrupts you is an unrouted impediment; when a second pet
misbehaves, you may visibly lavish affection on the *first* — but that affection is an action
aimed at *correcting the second*, routed *through* the first's body, and it only works because
the second is modeled as a subject who can read the display and be stung. So in one instant:
one animal is an unrouted medium, the other a fully-modeled subject acted on instrumentally.

Two consequences. **Behavior does not reveal λ** — kindness can run over an unrouted referent
and instrumentality over a routed one. And the *genuinely* cold operation isn't thinning (which
at least removes the subject from view) but its limit: **full modeling, available routing, and
the golden rule simply never recruited** — the subject stays in full view and is declined
anyway.

> **In plain terms —** you flip in and out of the cold frame constantly, all day, depending on
> who matters *to the moment*. So you can't read someone's heart off a single act: petting one
> cat to spite the other looks like love and isn't; the coldest move of all is seeing a person
> completely, being perfectly able to care — and just choosing not to.

## The golden rule is a placement, not a faculty

Having routed another, the further moral step is the golden rule: back-propagating *your own*
[preservation-ranking](the-two-prerogatives.md) onto them (in plain terms, *treating what
you'd want done to you as the cue for what to do to them*) as the signal for right action. The
paper's reorganizing claim: this isn't a faculty you have or lack — it's a **content held at a
position in your [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md)** (in plain terms, *a rule stored
somewhere in you — and how deep it's stored is everything*), and *where* it's held decides
everything:

- Held **constitutively** (in plain terms, *built into who you are*) — enforced from *inside*;
  defecting is a [consistency](the-two-prerogatives.md) violation, a partial self-loss.
  **Field-independent**: removing accountability doesn't remove the cost.
- Held **instrumentally** (in plain terms, *kept on as a handy tool*) — enforced from *outside*
  by the social field. **Field-contingent**: flatten the field and it's dropped.

At baseline the two are **behaviorally identical** — both do well by others. They diverge
*only* under **field-flattening** (in plain terms, *taking away anyone's power to catch you,
fault you, or punish you*), which is the paper's strongest operational handle: the everyday
catastrophe of power isn't a faculty breaking, it's a flattened field acting on an
instrumentally-placed rule.

**What flattening actually separates** (the refinement the cold region forces): not *warm from
cold*, and not even *constitutive from instrumental*, but **field-independent from
field-contingent** enforcement — and field-independence does *not* require routing. The warm
constitutive holder is field-independent-and-routed; the principled cold frame above is
field-independent-and-*un*routed. Both survive flattening, so flattening groups them together.
To tell them apart you need a **second probe**: does routing or affect accompany the maintained
conduct? Remove the field and you learn whether a morality is internally or externally floored;
add the routing-probe and you learn, among the internally-floored, whether the floor is *felt*
or *merely consistent*.

### The projection inside it, and its dual

The rule carries a **projection error**: back-propagation is valid only under profile-similarity.
Naïve good faith assumes the other wants what you'd want and is exploited where that fails — which
is what the threat-model corrects. There's also a **dual** form that sharpens the point. The bare
rule fills its counterfactual with *your own* ranking — *what would I want done to me* — and gives
the other that. Its dual substitutes the *other's perceived* ranking — *given I had their ranking,
how would I want to be treated* (the folk "platinum rule"). The two fail in complementary places:
the self-form fails under **dissimilarity** (your ranking isn't theirs); the dual form removes
that but inherits a **modeling** failure — it acts on the other's ranking *as perceived*, high-φ
content that needn't be true, so a mis-modeled ranking yields confident, symmetric-feeling,
*wrong* treatment (the structure of paternalism and well-meant harm). Two repairs for one
projection problem, trading a *similarity* requirement for an *accuracy* one — and the mature rule
uses both.

> **In plain terms —** "be good to others" can be wired two ways that look the same on a normal
> day: part of your bones, or a tool you keep because it pays. You only find out which when the
> consequences vanish — when no one can touch you. The decent boss who turns monstrous with power
> didn't break; the leash that was doing the work just got cut. And "treat others as you'd want"
> has a sister rule — "treat them as *they'd* want" — that fixes the wrong-when-they're-not-like-you
> problem but breaks when you've *misread* them, and never feels wrong while it does.

### Why one cold output is harder to fix than another — the bottleneck (OP8, resolved)

The three things this paper has separated — **model-thickness** (is the picture intact or
thinned?), **coupling** (does the picture route into feeling?), and an **enforcement floor**
(is the rule held constitutively or only by the field?) — are not additive but **serial**. Warm
moral output toward a class requires *all three*, and if any one is near zero the output is cold
whatever the others do. This is the bottleneck that resolves the old open problem OP8 (how the
thinning sources and the agent-side parameters interact). When **one** gate is degraded — a
thinned model, a low coupling, an instrumental floor in a flattened field — the cold frame
produced is the relatively *repairable* kind: restore that one gate and the output returns. When
**several** are degraded at once, the result isn't "more" cold in any linear sense; it's cold
that is more **stable**, more **portable across fields**, and **less repairable by any single
intervention**, because each repair opens only its own gate while the others still block. This is
why the three repairs stay non-interchangeable, and why the *rate* at which a society's members
participate in atrocity tracks the **joint** degradation of its gates rather than any one alone.

> **In plain terms —** warmth has to pass through three doors in a row — *see them clearly*,
> *feel them*, *be the kind who'd act on it even unwatched* — and one shut door stops it,
> whichever it is. Shut one and the cold is fixable: open that door. Shut several and you get a
> cold that's deep, travels anywhere, and shrugs off any single fix, because you keep opening
> the wrong door.

## The will, and its reflexive bound

Where does choice enter? Placement is **derived, not chosen** — set by λ, by margin (in plain
terms, *how far past the line you started*), by the fields you've lived in, and by your
toolkit's own model of what your will can do. None of those is chosen, so making placement a
*freely-willed* knob would contradict the desert-removal below. The will is real but
**bounded**: it works the *slack* those parameters leave — and its access to the slack is
itself gated, because **you can attempt only what your toolkit certifies the will is capable
of.** This explains the agent who is *stuck while capable*: the slack is there, but the
self-model never certifies the attempt as a live option, so it sits unused and invisible.

> **From the inside —** your will is real, but it only gets to push on the bit of you that's
> actually loose — and worse, you can only even *try* what some inner voice has already marked
> "possible for me." So you can be perfectly able to change and never reach for it, because the
> option was never lit up as a thing you could do.

A sharp epistemic corollary the paper develops carefully: whether a self-model (or anyone's
virtue) is *accurate* is **not readable from a single outcome** — calibration (in plain terms,
*whether your self-estimate actually matches reality*) is a property of the *pattern across
instances*. And even a long record can be manufactured three ways, each needing a different
control: a **fixed estimate** that happens to cross the truth (check whether it *moves with
conditions*), **selection on output** where failures were filtered before you looked (sample
*independently of outcome*), and a **field-contingent** producer that tracks perfectly *while
the field holds* (vary the field). To read genuine structure off conduct you need multiple
instances, across varied conditions, sampled independently of outcome.

> **In plain terms —** you can't tell whether someone (or you) is really good from one good
> deed, or even a long clean streak. A broken clock is right twice a day; a record can look
> spotless because the failures got quietly filtered out; and a fair-weather decency tracks
> perfectly right up until the weather turns. To trust the pattern you have to watch it under
> changed conditions you didn't get to cherry-pick.

## §6A — How ordinary moral agents produce atrocity

The highest-stakes path locates the coldness **not in the agent** but in the *target*. Mass
atrocity is, in the main, *not* the work of psychopaths. Many perpetrators were ordinarily
socialized people with **intact moral life in local domains** — family, neighbors, comrades,
duty, promise — who loved their families, kept their word, and felt guilt over ordinary wrongs,
*while the targeted class was routed through a culturally damaged model.* (The paper is careful
here not to overclaim a "wide, constitutive golden rule" for such agents — only an intact moral
life in those local domains.) Agent-side parameters can't explain the coldness, because by those
measures these people weren't cold.

The mechanism: **referent-thinning performed exogenously** (in plain terms, *the blurring done
to the victim group by the culture, not by the individual*) — on a whole *class* of others, at
the level of the **cultural-I** (in plain terms, *the shared, distributed mind-set of a whole
people — a pattern, not a person*), and inherited by the individual as an already-corrupted input
to their modeling channel. Propaganda, the standing-stripping legal category, the slur, the
pseudo-scientific apparatus — these are *thinning instruments*. And they bite **partly because
they don't work against the grain**: they hand over, ready-made, exactly the simplified model
that an agent under the load of a complex or painful truth (§3) is already inclined to reach for.
Exogenous thinning meets the endogenous tendency halfway.

What such an agent runs is *not* psychopathy (accurate model, severed routing). It is **faithful
routing over a model thinned at the source** — the routing works perfectly and finds almost
nothing to route. And because (per the no-lie mechanism) the thinned model never registers as a
lie, the agent feels not cruel but **correct** — the φ-gap at population scale. The horror is not
malfunction; it is *correct operation over corrupted input.*

> **In plain terms —** most people who do monstrous things aren't monsters. Their caring works
> fine — it's just been handed a pre-blurred picture of the victims by their whole society, so
> when they look there's "no one much there" to feel for. Their machinery is healthy; the input
> was poisoned upstream, and that's exactly why they feel righteous instead of guilty.

### Upstream, downstream, or both

The temporal order isn't fixed. Sometimes the model is thinned **upstream**, by culture, before
the agent ever acts. Sometimes the violence itself thins it **downstream**: repeated
participation is repeated co-occurrence, and by the same osmotic dynamics that drive §3's
endogenous thinning, it dulls the victim-class's affective salience and reclassifies it, over
time, as routine — so an agent who *began* with a fuller model can have it thinned **by their own
conduct**. The point isn't that propaganda always precedes the violence; it's that the routing
channel takes whatever model is available — thinned upstream by culture, downstream by habituated
participation, or both. No single historical case should be read as pure pre-thinning.

### Thinning a whole population down to its worst part (the H ⊂ P result)

A particular thinning recurs and admits a precise result. Often the class that gets thinned is
**not** the actual source of threat but a larger population that merely *contains* it: a
threatening subgroup **H** sits inside a much larger class **P** (so `H ⊂ P`), and the thinning
collapses *P* to "*= H*" — the whole population modeled as the threat its subgroup poses. The
accurate model is exactly the complex, painful one §3's driver flees: *this population contains
those who threaten me and a far larger number who do not.* Thin that distinction away, and the
consequence is structural, not rhetorical: a response motivated by a real threat from *H* but
**aimed through the model "P = H"** falls on `P \ H` — the non-threatening remainder — in
proportion to `|P \ H| / |P|`. The larger the host population relative to the dangerous subgroup,
the larger the share of any force that lands, *by the model's own lights*, on people who never
matched the threat. This is derived from the thinning operation alone, independent of any case —
and it's the same part-to-whole error, run at the level of the class, that the no-anthropomorphism
rule (below) forbids at the level of intent.

> **In plain terms —** "those people are dangerous" almost always starts from a real but *small*
> dangerous subset and then quietly swallows the whole group. Once the group is modeled as
> nothing-but-the-threat, any "defensive" force lands mostly on the innocent majority — not by
> accident, but as a mathematical consequence of having erased the distinction. The bigger the
> group relative to the actual threat, the more of the harm falls on people who were never it.

### The cultural-I does the thinning — and is not a mind

The paper's most load-bearing discipline guards this section: **the cultural-I is not
anthropomorphized.** It is a distributed pattern, not a mind; it *thins* but carries no intent.
Because it is *emergent over* its individuals (in plain terms, *it only exists as the sum of the
people, with nothing extra behind them*), any predicate pinned to it **distributes back down**
onto every correlated individual — so the discipline is bidirectional:

- Attribute *intent* upward to "the culture" → you've laundered an unearned guilt onto the
  uninvolved (manufactured collective guilt).
- Refuse to attribute the wrong *downward* to actual participants → atrocity becomes nobody's
  deed, a fog of "forces."

> **In plain terms —** "the culture" can't *want* anything; only people do. Blame the culture
> as if it had a will, and that blame quietly splashes onto every innocent who happens to belong
> to it. Refuse to blame any actual person, and the horror becomes nobody's fault at all. Both
> are the same mistake — putting intent where there's only a pattern.

Intent lives **only** at the individual and institutional layer. The population under a thinning
apparatus therefore resolves into five attributable positions, scored *by production path, not by
deed alone*: **architects/operators** (built the corrupted model — full intent), **compliant**
(acted on it without reaching for available counter-evidence — mitigated, not erased), **genuine
resisters** (held their model intact *against* the thinning — the expected high-coupling,
intact-model tail), **performative resisters** (instrumental placement in an unflattened field
where resistance *pays* — identical output, contingent on the field), and **merely correlated
non-participants** (the cultural correlation, *no* intent — the position the no-anthropomorphism
rule exists to protect). The **capacity layer** (λ, margin, placement) and the **attribution
layer** are orthogonal and cross — which is what stops the whole analysis collapsing back into
"good people and bad people."

> **In plain terms —** under an atrocity there are at least five kinds of people: the ones who
> built the lie, the ones who went along without looking for the truth they could have found,
> the ones who saw through it and refused, the ones who "resisted" only because resisting
> happened to pay, and the ones merely born into the group who did nothing. *How* you got to
> your behavior matters as much as the behavior — and it cuts both ways: even some heroes were
> only heroes while it was rewarded.

### What observation can't recover

Because genuine and performative resistance are behaviorally identical in an unflattened field,
**observable right action is not evidence of an intact model, recruited routing, or a
constitutive placement.** "The rescuers were the ones who could feel" is not licensed: some
rescuers were genuine, some were calculators in a field that rewarded rescue, and the deeds alone
can't tell them apart. This isn't nihilism — the genuine resister really is more reliably good —
but it's a hard limit on what an outside observer can read off conduct, and it's the exact
channel through which reputations get gamed by the instrumentally-placed.

### Re-thickening: does restoring the model bring the feeling back? (OP9, resolved)

The atrocity-path repair is "restore the model — re-humanize the other." But does an accurate
model automatically pull routing back with it, or can a group be seen correctly and *still* not
felt? Both happen, and **how** you re-thicken decides which. Refill the model with **information
alone** — being told, accurately, that the class are full people — and you rebuild the modeling
channel (T) while leaving routing (R) untouched: an accurate picture that still doesn't land.
Refill it through **lived** contact — sustained, direct, shared experience of the class as
subjects — and you rebuild R too, because that experience *just is* the repeated affective
co-occurrence that [osmotically forms](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md) a routing disposition
(File 3, §8.7). It's the §4 driven thinning run *forward* instead of back. Lived re-thickening
usually rebuilds routing; instruction alone usually doesn't.

And the rebuild is **latent until something tests it**. By the same formation/activation split as
§4, lived contact accumulates routing strength silently (formation) below the threshold at which
it fires (activation); the feeling stays invisible through all the ordinary cooperation and shows
only when a triggering event — the class's safety actually in question — arrives *after* the
threshold is crossed. So the prediction is a **discontinuity, not a gradient**: not steadily
warming conduct, but a sudden engagement at the first real test. The canonical case: two people
from mutually-thinned groups forced into long, often hierarchical, cooperation; A's degraded model
of B quietly fills in, routing rebuilds unseen — and then A witnesses B in danger and *moves to
defend them*, where before there'd have been nothing there, *for A*, to be moved by.

> **In plain terms —** telling someone "those people are just as human as you" fixes their
> *facts* and often leaves their *heart* cold. What rebuilds the heart is *living alongside* the
> group, not being lectured about them — and even then you won't see it coming, because the
> feeling builds up silently and only shows the day something actually threatens them.

**The quieter signature — the bounded "leak" (new in v0.4.2).** There's a smaller, commoner sign
that shows up *before* any grave trigger. Re-thickening runs **per individual, not per class**
(§5): sustained closeness to one particular *B* re-thickens *that* model while the class stays
thinned. Because the atrocity-path agent's routing channel was intact all along, that individuated
routing now has somewhere to go — but it can't go far, because the agent is still inside a frame
(ideological, institutional, or just the surrounding field) that prices open defection too high to
pay. So the routing leaks out in the only register frame-coherence leaves cheap: a **minimal but
meaningful relaxation** of the cruelties aimed at the class, applied to *B* in particular — small
considerations extended, a blind eye turned. *B* becomes, within the constraints, more human-like
*to the agent* — not by revising the class-model or breaking the frame, but by rebuilt routing
leaking wherever leaking is cheap and deniable. The historical texture is familiar: the enslaved
person given house privileges that vanish the instant an outsider appears; the camp guard who
declines to notice food hidden under a favored prisoner's bunk.

Read carefully, the leak **discriminates** — and along the field-axis, not off the surface.
Special treatment of a *useful* individual could be pure instrumentality; so the diagnostic
instances are the **unobserved and uncompensated** ones — consideration extended where there's no
payoff and no audience, which is what routing explains and calculation does not. So read, the leak
marks the atrocity-path specifically: intact routing meeting a locally re-thickened model. The
psychopath produces no such leak (no routing to re-engage; any favoritism collapses the moment the
person stops being useful). And the boundary note binds at full strength: **the leak does not
soften the structure it occurs inside.** The privileged position is still bondage; the favored
prisoner is still imprisoned and still at the guard's discretion. The bounded mercy isn't evidence
of a humane frame — it's *the measure of how much intact routing the frame was throttling, read
off the little that escaped.*

> **In plain terms —** the same person enforcing a brutal system will sometimes quietly soften it
> for the one victim they've come to know — slip them food, look away, spare them a cruelty — *and
> do it precisely when no one's watching and there's nothing in it for them.* That's the tell that
> their capacity to care was intact the whole time; the system was strangling it. It's also not
> redemption: the kindness measures the cage, it doesn't open it.

### Boundary note

The architecture explains the *general structure* by which ordinary moral agents produce the cold
frame at scale, through cultural-level corruption of their model of a targeted class. It does
**not** adjudicate contested present-day cases, assign perpetrator and victim roles in disputed
conflicts, or rule on who is thinning whom now. The historical anchors it uses — the Shoah,
chattel slavery and its racial pseudo-science, apartheid, the Armenian genocide — are the
established *explanandum*, the thing to be explained, not rhetoric. Any extension to a present,
contested case is a matter for evidence and judgment outside the paper's remit: the model supplies
the **mechanism, not the verdict.** And a second boundary, as load-bearing: **explaining the
mechanism is not excusing the deed.** That the atrocity-path agent's architecture was healthy and
their model arrived pre-thinned clarifies *how* the wrong was produced; it does not make the wrong
less wrong, and it leaves the compliant answerable for the counter-evidence they declined and the
architects answerable for everything. *Mechanism is not exculpation.*

## The author's note on moral stance

v0.4.1 added something the rest of the paper deliberately withholds: a **signed, first-person
moral verdict**, set apart from the analytic voice and placed inside the §6A.5 boundary note. The
reason is itself drawn from the architecture. A treatise written *only* in the neutral, unrouted
register would itself be an instance of the **principled cold frame** — reliable, but heir to its
vice: consistency without routing holds a frame as steadily when it's wrong as when it's right,
with no felt signal of the error. So rather than let the unrouted register stand in for the whole
of moral perception, the author states, *in her own voice and as moral principle rather than as a
theorem of the formalism*, the verdict her own routing delivers — one routed instance of the very
architecture the paper describes.

The note's content, represented faithfully and without editorial addition:

- The deliberate or reckless mass killing and terrorizing of civilians is **wrong wherever it
  occurs**, and routing one set of victims does not require un-routing another.
- Within the φ-graded constraints of her own model (the same standard the paper applies to every
  agent it treats, including its author), she judges that **Jews as a people face a resurgent
  hatred and violence directed at them as Jews**, and that in Israel specifically the **torture,
  murder, and terrorizing of Jewish and other Israeli civilians by Hamas is a moral atrocity**.
- She also judges that **Palestinians are being subjected to systematic, targeted action aimed at
  their destruction as a people** — *genocide* on the definition she holds: the systematic and
  targeted action to annihilate a group, intended or not. On her framing the verdict attaches to
  the destruction itself rather than waiting on a finding of intent.
- **Both are wrong; neither cancels the other.** She presents this as the paper's own thesis lived
  out — that an intact agent can, and must, route *both*, and that the cold frame, by whichever of
  its three sources, is exactly the failure to.

She marks these explicitly as her **model's outputs, not claims to model-independent certainty** —
the paper's own epistemics applied to its author — and notes that **the architecture's formal
claims stand or fall independently of the note.** It is included here because it is part of the
document; the wiki represents it, and takes no position of its own on the underlying events.

> **In plain terms —** the author breaks the fourth wall once, on purpose. Her own theory warns
> that writing about atrocity in a perfectly cool, detached voice is *itself* the cold frame in
> disguise — so she refuses to hide behind it, and says plainly where she stands: that violence
> against Jewish civilians and the destruction of Palestinians as a people are *both* atrocities,
> that caring about one doesn't require dropping the other, and that she's stating this as her own
> moral conviction, not as something the math proves. The formal theory doesn't depend on it.

## Bindingness without desert

The meta-ethical core, and the part the author flags as the highest overreach risk. The
placements that decide your moral character are, *with respect to desert*, **arbitrary** (in
plain terms, *with respect to whether you've earned praise or blame for them* — born or driven,
never authored). Run honestly, the symmetry **cuts both ways**: the arbitrariness that disarms
*blame* for a low coupling equally disarms *credit* for a high one. But this isn't the
dissolution of morality, because **bindingness and desert come apart** (in plain terms,
*"you're truly held to this" and "you earned being the kind who's held" are two different
things*): a constraint can be fully authoritative over you without being authored by you (most
real constraints are). You keep *"this is right and I am held to it"* and give up *"and I deserve
credit for being the sort who is held."*

The sharpest form: **incorruptibility under power** — the most admired property in the catalogue —
is, on this account, an *unauthored placement*. The incorruptible person really is more to be
relied on **and merits no credit for the placement that makes them so**. *"The better agent is
really better, and deserves nothing for it."* This is explicitly **not nihilism** — it removes
*desert*, not *value* or *bindingness*. And it compounds with the epistemic limit: since observers
can't even recover placement from conduct, praise aimed from outside at observed deeds can't
reliably track which agent it's looking at.

> **In plain terms —** the things that make you a good person — that you can't be bought, that
> you don't curdle under power — you didn't earn. They were handed to you by a lottery you didn't
> run. That doesn't make right-and-wrong fake, and it doesn't let you off the hook: you're still
> truly bound to do right. You just don't get to feel *superior* for being the lucky one who is.
> The good person is genuinely better and is owed nothing for it.

## Discriminators, and what it is *not*

The paper stakes **six independent, falsifiable core discriminators** (in plain terms, *six
risky predictions that could turn out false and would sink the theory if they did*). As of
v0.3.1/v0.4.0 each is now **paired with a measurement handle and a set of excluded alternatives**
in a closing table — the operational hardening the earlier drafts only promised:

1. **T/R dissociation** (and behavior ≠ λ) — the load-bearing one; if the channels don't
   dissociate, the whole distinction is false.
2. **Field-flattening separates placement, for cruelty *and* virtue** — and, precisely, it
   separates *field-independent from field-contingent* enforcement (not warm from cold), so a
   **second R-probe** is needed to tell routed constitution from the principled cold frame.
3. **Affect-restoration ≠ standing-restoration** — making people *feel* warmer toward a thinned
   group won't, by itself, restore that group's *standing*; that needs work on the model.
4. **Non-interchangeability of the three repairs** — the strongest applied claim: misdiagnose
   the path and the intervention fails.
5. **Reading structure off conduct needs varied conditions, sampled independently of outcome** —
   a methodological constraint as much as a prediction.
6. **The recursive bound on the will** — placement-change needs *both* the capacity and a
   self-model that certifies the attempt as a live option.

> **In plain terms —** the theory sticks its neck out six times in ways you could actually test:
> that reading-someone and feeling-them can be pried apart; that taking away accountability sorts
> the truly-good from the merely-careful (and the true heroes from the show-offs); that making
> people *feel warmer* toward a group won't by itself restore that group's *standing*; that the
> three cold-frame fixes don't substitute for each other; that you need varied, un-cherry-picked
> evidence to judge real virtue; and that changing your character needs both room to move and a
> self-image that says "I can."

> **The paper's boundary disciplines (quoted in spirit):** it is *not* "psychopathy is a choice,"
> not a moralization, not a theory of good vs. bad people, not a diagnostic tool, not an
> anthropomorphizing of culture, not proven moral realism, not nihilism, not libertarian free will
> nor hard determinism, **not an adjudication of any contested present-day conflict** (its analytic
> voice; the signed author's note above is explicitly separate), and **not an excuse for atrocity**
> — *"explaining how a wrong was produced leaves the wrong wrong and its authors answerable.
> Mechanism is not exculpation."*

**Open problems.** Both former gating problems are now **resolved** — OP8 (how the thinning
sources and agent-side parameters interact) by the *bottleneck* model above, and OP9
(re-thickening) by the *lived-vs-informational* split above. What remains live: keeping λ a
*relation between two operations* (never a fourth coordinate), placing these phenomena within the
[Agency Architecture](app-agency-architecture.md) taxonomy, the clinical-population mapping (held
as hypothesis, reclassifying no diagnostic category), and empirical anchoring of the §2, §6A, and
field-flattening claims — the meta-ethics of *Bindingness without desert* being flagged as the
highest overreach risk and kept indexed to the verification-architecture that grounds it.
