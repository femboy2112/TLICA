## The one structural move

Ordinary talk fuses "empathy" into a single faculty. This paper **splits it into two
channels**:

- **The truth-channel (T)** — modeling another as a real I, with their own
  [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md), [preservation-ranking](the-two-prerogatives.md),
  and inner states. T is *accuracy* about the other.
- **The affect-routing channel (R)** — taking the other's modeled states up as inputs to
  *your own* [ρ and Π](three-coordinates.md), so their pain actually **lands** on you. R is
  *the other mattering* to you.

The two are coupled by a variable **λ** (how strongly T feeds R). The default is coupling.
But the channels are **dissociable** — and that dissociation is the whole paper.

## The "cold frame"

The **cold frame** (treating another I as a non-routed instrument-or-obstacle) is, at its
purest, *full T + instrumental action + no recruitment of the golden rule's symmetry* — you
model the person completely and still decline to let them land. The paper notes this is
**colder than de-animation**: it keeps the person in full view and routes them as a thing.

From this single split, three otherwise-unrelated phenomena get one structure:

- **Psychopathy is not an absence of feeling.** Affect is intact (including the pleasure of
  power); what's severed is *routing* toward other-I referents. Accurate modeling plus severed
  routing is, the paper argues, *more* dangerous than emptiness, because the other is fully
  legible as an instrument.
- **The exit requires no lie.** For someone whose T and R are tightly coupled ("truth-as-affect"
  — they can't quite lie to themselves), the cold frame isn't reached by asserting a
  falsehood. It's reached by **referent-thinning**: thinning your model of the other until
  there's nothing left for R to route — *without asserting anything false*. That's why it can
  present as clarity or relief rather than as deception, and why it's a trap.
- **Atrocity by ordinary moral agents.** Most mass atrocity is committed by people with
  *intact* empathy who love their families and keep their promises. The paper's account:
  **faithful routing over a model that culture has already thinned.** The agent routes R
  correctly — over a referent-class whose T-status was degraded at the cultural source. *"The
  horror is not malfunction; it is correct operation over corrupted input."*

## A distribution, not a verdict

A discipline the paper insists on: the cold frame is reached by **different routes in
different people**, and where you sit is set by things you didn't choose — a congenital
baseline (λ already low), a *driven* lowering of λ under accumulated load (a state-to-trait
conversion, the mirror of addiction), or a placement so far inside the threshold no realistic
load would reach it. So it is careful never to read off a person's inner structure from their
behavior: *kindness can run on an unrouted referent and instrumentality on a routed one —
"behavior does not reveal λ."*

It also splits the **golden rule** into two placements: held *constitutively* (high
[ρ](three-coordinates.md) — defecting feels like self-betrayal, and holds *regardless of who's
watching*) or held as a *toolkit* heuristic (instrumental — holds only while the social field
rewards it). The two look identical day-to-day and diverge only under **field-flattening**
(when accountability is removed). This is also how it distinguishes genuine moral resisters
from performative ones.

## The hard ending: bindingness without desert

The meta-ethical payoff, flagged by the author as the highest-risk part: morality can be
**genuinely binding without being deserved.** Where you land — your λ, your placement — is
*arbitrary with respect to desert*, yet the resulting moral constraint is still authoritative
(a constraint can bind you without being authored by you). The sting cuts both ways: it gives
no *credit* for field-independent virtue either — *"the most admired moral property in the
catalogue — incorruptibility under power — is, on this account, an unauthored placement."*
This is explicitly **not** moral nihilism: it removes *desert*, not *value* or *bindingness*.

## Status

A pre-prose skeleton with its spine fully stated: the T/R split, the three production paths of
the cold frame (psychopathy / power-decoupling / culturally-thinned atrocity, each needing a
*different* repair), the per-relation real-time reassignment of the frame (worked through a
small everyday "cat case"), the two golden-rule placements, and the desert-free meta-ethics.
Still to do: prose, literature anchoring, hardening the discriminators, and a few flagged
inferences awaiting the author's confirmation. It builds on the [affect paper](app-differentiated-affect.md),
the [agency papers](app-agency-architecture.md), and a sibling *Substrate Override States* paper
not yet in this archive.

> **The paper's own boundary discipline (quoted in spirit):** it is *not* "psychopathy is a
> choice," not a moralization, not a diagnostic tool, not an anthropomorphizing of culture,
> not a proof of moral realism, not nihilism, not an adjudication of any contested present-day
> conflict, and **not** a claim that atrocity-path perpetrators are blameless — *"mechanism ≠
> excuse."* It aims to explain *structure*, never to deliver a verdict.
