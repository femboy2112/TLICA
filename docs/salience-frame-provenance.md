## The question it is actually asking

Strip away the machinery and one question is left standing:

> How can a bounded self keep correcting toward reality while its affective salience, its
> contextual opportunities, its identity commitments, and its access to its own reasoning
> history all keep changing?

The target is deliberately **not** "coherence maximization." A mind that only ever made itself
feel more internally consistent could drift anywhere. The thing worth preserving is
*truth-respecting, self-resonant correction under bounded access and lossy history* — a self
that can still be reached by evidence about the world, even while the inside keeps getting
rearranged.

This is a composition of things the archive already discusses, not a discovery. What is
*proposed* as new (and only proposed — novelty itself is **not** claimed) is the way the pieces
*interact*; each one alone — motivated forgetting, reward allocation, reconstructive memory,
rationalization — is already studied elsewhere, and none of them alone is the object here.

> **In plain terms —** you can know a thing, mean a thing, and still not be able to reach it or
> act on it in the room you are standing in. This page is about that gap, and about the harder
> problem of staying honest while the gap keeps moving.

## Four kinds of salience are not one thing

"How much something matters to you" turns out to be at least four separate measurements that
usually travel together and can come apart:

- **Intellectual salience** — an idea's felt depth and its pull to be turned over further.
- **Affective salience** — emotional activation, the relevance that feeling assigns.
- **Instrumental salience** — significance borrowed from a valued downstream goal.
- **Actionable salience** — enough value, opportunity, and *control* combined to actually start
  or sustain a behavior.

Preserved competence can sit right next to an attenuated reward for *pursuing* it — you can
still be good at something while the engine that would make you do it is quiet. And the reverse:
an immediate necessity can make a dull task genuinely engaging *because* it has become the
operative means to protect something you value — which is not the same as fresh curiosity about
the task itself.

> **In plain terms —** "I don't care about this" and "I can't get myself to start this" and "this
> stopped feeling important" and "this isn't worth doing" are four different reports, even when
> they come out of the same mouth on the same afternoon.

## Possess, reach, deploy

Knowing something exists, being able to *retrieve* it as relevant right now, and being able to
*act* on it are three distinct operations. The note writes them as separate predicates and
pointedly refuses to assume any of the arrows between them: possessing a capacity does **not**
guarantee you can reach it in this context, and reaching it does **not** guarantee you can deploy
it. An environment can supply scaffolding — cues, roles, feedback, structure — that makes a
capacity operational *there* and not elsewhere, and later recollection of how you behaved in that
environment need not reinstate the state that produced the behavior.

This motivates an **access-map** reading — which capacities are reachable from where — and the
note is explicit that this is *not* a claim that a person literally contains separate
context-specific selves. (Compare the archive's treatment of
[access to intrinsic structure](access-to-intrinsic-structure.md): the capacities are there; the
question is always what, from here, you can get to.)

That possession, present accessibility, and successful deployment are *logically* separable is
**Derived within the typed candidate model** (ledger SF-02) — a statement about distinct
predicates, with no general implication stipulated between them. That *particular contextual cues
actually govern* which of your real capabilities become actionable through salience is
**Conjectured / UNVERIFIED** (SF-03): plausible, bridged to the idea of contextual scaffolding,
but with no controlled data here and live rivals in skill-deficit and habit accounts.

### Deadlines and the burst-execution trap

A valued long-term outcome can fail to generate any present task engagement while its
consequences stay distant; an approaching *real* constraint can push the same task above an
initiation threshold. Arbitrary self-declared deadlines may simply fail when the agent
transparently knows they are negotiable. That real deadlines can convert dull work into
*experienced* engagement by instrumental propagation is **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED** (SF-04).

There is also a purely arithmetic trap: a strategy that works by compressing effort into a
last-minute burst becomes **globally infeasible** once accumulated tasks need more labor than the
available burst windows can hold. That the queue becomes unschedulable is **Derived
conditionally** (SF-05) — ordinary scheduling math, given the durations and windows — but it says
nothing about *why* the windows are shaped that way, and it must not be read as evidence that task
competence vanished.

## Event-state updates versus interpretive-frame updates

An event can change your current *state* while leaving your interpretive *scheme* basically
intact — or it can change the scheme by which past evidence, present priorities, and expected
futures all get weighed. These are idealized endpoints; real transitions can move both at once.

This matters most for a subjectively "buffered" emotional episode. At least three readings stay
open and a first-person report cannot tell them apart: the affect may **rise more slowly**; the
affect may be roughly normal but its **influence on action** is weakened or delayed; or the
event's affect simply does **not propagate** into the global interpretation. That emotional
activation, action commitment, and global interpretive change *can* separate is **Conjectured /
UNVERIFIED as a human mechanism** (SF-06).

And one tempting inference is explicitly **Refuted** at the stated scope: that smoothing the
emotional time-course *uniquely identifies* what produced an improved response (SF-20). Equal
eventual emotional intensity is compatible with several different temporal trajectories and
decision couplings. A plot of "calmer feeling" alone decides nothing; only time-resolved,
orthogonal measures could.

> **In plain terms —** "I handled that better than I used to" has at least three different engines
> behind it, and the feeling of having handled it well can't tell you which engine ran.

## Locally legal, globally defective

Here is the structural heart of the note. Picture a chain of interpretive frames `F₀, F₁, …, Fₙ`,
where each step is judged *locally acceptable* — admissible under the frame you are standing in at
that moment. Every single step can pass that local test while the endpoint `Fₙ` fails a criterion
that was fixed back at `F₀`. The danger is not the step size; it is that **the evaluator itself
drifts** — later states judge later moves using priorities, retrieval, and framing that the
earlier moves already changed.

Two exact results fence this in so it cannot slide into mysticism:

- **P1, the fixed-invariant guard** (**Derived**, SF-08). If one genuinely fixed invariant is
  *exactly* preserved at every step, it is preserved at the end — by plain transitivity. So any
  *apparent* "illegal global change by legal local moves" means one of three mundane things: the
  invariant was never actually preserved, "legal" meant something weaker and state-relative, or
  the invariant was misspecified. "Legal" here is a state-relative *endorsement* relation, never a
  guarantee about external truth.
- **C1 / C2, drift and reachability** (**Derived conditionally**, SF-07 / SF-09). On a small
  illustrative scale of warning-salience levels, steps of size ≤ 1 can walk `4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 0`
  while a guard fixed at the start forbids the endpoint — a *bound on each step is not a bound on
  total drift*. Separately, a graph with edges `A → B` and `B → C` but no `A → C` shows a
  destination reachable without a direct edge. That is the entire rigorous content of the
  "traverse the wall by another path" analogy: **no** quantum tunneling, barrier violation, or
  discontinuity is imported.

The note keeps five rival explanations for any apparent "illegal" change live at once — a true
path gap, authorization drift, genuinely changed evidence, retrieval suppression that fakes
consent, or a frame that never held a valid prohibition in the first place — and insists the
mechanism has content only once an experiment can separate them.

The same traversal cuts both ways. A valid reason *against* an action can stay valid even after
you find a route that lets you act — finding a traversable path is not a proof the barrier was
illusory. But the identical technique can also, step by acceptable step, render an **accurate
warning unable to influence a decision**. So the note refuses to identify traversability, felt
permission, justification, and safety with one another. That a frame can be fully self-resonant
and internally coherent while still inaccurate or harmful is **Derived as a logical possibility,
mechanism UNVERIFIED** (SF-15).

> **In plain terms —** every step felt reasonable, and you ended up somewhere your earlier self
> would have refused outright. That is not a paradox and not magic. Either something you thought
> was fixed quietly wasn't, or "reasonable" was being graded by a self the earlier steps had
> already edited.

## Lossy provenance: keeping the conclusion, losing the receipts

Over time an agent may keep a conclusion while the *accessible* record of how it was adopted
thins out: full reconstruction of the local justifications → a compressed summary → only weakly
recoverable provenance. The exact backbone is **P2, non-invertibility** (**Derived**, SF-10): if
two genuinely different histories get compressed to the same accessible summary, then nothing
working from that summary *alone* can recover which history was real.

The boundary is stated just as firmly: P2 asserts inability *from the chosen summary alone*. It
is **not** a law of memory decay, **not** thermodynamics, and **not** literal holographic
storage — and outside records, fresh observations, or other channels may reopen the distinction.
The "holographic boundary" image transports only "a present summary can retain a compressed, maybe
inadequate imprint of the past"; it does not import AdS/CFT, entropy-area laws, or invertibility.

Three recoveries must never be collapsed into one word "remembering":

- **narrative** reconstruction — "I can explain how I got here";
- **phenomenological** reinstatement — "the old concerns feel urgent again";
- **behavioral** reversibility — "I can decide as I would have under the former frame."

You can score high on narrative and low on the other two. That long update chains retain present
commitments while access to their justifications degrades (SF-11), and that these three
recoveries dissociate (SF-12), are both **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED** — a proposed separation, not
an established factor structure, and any claim of "reversibility" must say *which one* was
measured. (This is the same discipline the archive applies to a modeled past in
[the self in motion](the-self-in-motion.md): a reconstruction of an earlier self is a second
object, never the thing it reconstructs.)

> **In plain terms —** you can still tell the story of how you came to believe something long
> after you have lost the ability to feel why it ever mattered, or to choose the way you once
> would have. Being able to narrate the path is not the same as being able to walk back down it.

## Coherence is not calibration

A frame selects what feels salient, salience selects what you notice, what you notice confirms the
frame. Both accurate and inaccurate models run this loop, so *correction quality cannot be read
off the loop from inside*. The note keeps four quantities strictly apart:

- **coherence** (internal consistency),
- **resonance** (felt self-fit),
- **calibration** (prediction against the world),
- **provenance** (recoverability of sources and paths).

Strong endorsement can coexist with poor provenance; a coherent story can come from faithful
integration *or* from repeated self-confirming reinterpretation. Crucially, introspective access
to your own reasoning steps is **not** reliable proof of their actual causes — a reconstruction
can be accurate, partly confabulated, or a mixture, and only external records and discriminating
tests can tell.

This is why the strongest research question demands **two audits**, not one:

1. a **transition audit** — could an observer reconstruct the original objections, what changed,
   and the claimed causal path?
2. a **reality audit** — do predictions, external records, and held-out interventions actually
   improve after the change?

A clean transition audit alone never establishes truth; a good immediate outcome alone never
establishes the route's safety or that it will generalize.

Two further pieces live here. Available **slack** may modulate how fast or how visibly correction
happens — low slack perhaps buying short-run defensiveness then later revision, high slack letting
competing interpretations be held at once. That slack modulates correction latency is
**Conjectured / UNVERIFIED** (SF-14), compatible with the foundation's *posited* Mode-B slack gate
but **not** entailed by it. And a hard **Refuted**: that anomalies plus self-resonance
*necessarily* improve truth (SF-19) — a rule that reads every discrepancy as support for itself is
perfectly coherent and fails every external test. A detected contradiction is not a proof of
learning.

An explicit **UNRESOLVED / defer / abstain / seek-a-probe** state is treated as a legitimate
*action output*, not a failure and not a metaphysical truth value (SF-16, constructible policy,
effectiveness UNVERIFIED). But "do not play" is only legitimate when no safe warranted move exists
*and* the cost of waiting is acceptable — inaction can sustain harm too, and a refusal must still
be scored against the real consequences of delay.

## The finite witness: exactly what it proves and no more

The dossier ships a small deterministic Python construction. It runs **seven deterministic
tests, all seven passing**. What they check is narrow and honest: a fixed invariant is preserved
under composition; locally adjacent steps can cross a distinct global guard; the frozen guard
blocks the "bypass" when it is actually consulted; two different histories collide under a lossy
summary while the full log distinguishes them; possession does not force activation below
threshold (and a merely renegotiable deadline adds zero real urgency); and a no-op transition
changes nothing (the null control).

The interpretation boundary is printed on the tin: **passing proves the consistency of the finite
toy under its own definitions — it proves nothing about human cognition.** These are checks of
mathematical definitions, not tests on people, and they must never be cited as empirical
psychology.

> **In plain terms —** the little program is a sanity check that the *ideas* don't contradict
> themselves on paper. It is not evidence that any mind works this way. Those are different kinds
> of true, and the page spends a lot of effort keeping them from being confused.

argues for *stronger blinding*, never for a truth claim; an undefined `φ` means a path is not
constructible from the current tools, not that a proposition is false — and high local `φ` can sit
right beside defective `σ`. (See [the three coordinates](three-coordinates.md) for the

not to blur.)

## Claim-status summary

- **Observed (narrow scope):** the conceptual distinctions — four saliences; state-update vs
  frame-update; locally admissible perturbations; lossy history — are faithfully represented, as a
  privacy-preserving author-origin seed, *not* external corroboration (SF-01).
- **Derived (conditional, within the toy / typed model):** possession vs accessibility vs
  deployment are separable (SF-02); the burst-execution queue can become unschedulable (SF-05);
  local authorization need not imply a frozen global guard (SF-07); a fixed invariant exactly
  preserved at every step is preserved after composition — P1 (SF-08); a route can exist through
  intermediate states without a direct edge — C2 (SF-09); a many-to-one history summary is not
  invertible from its output alone — P2 (SF-10). A self-resonant, coherent frame *can* be
  inaccurate or harmful as a logical possibility (SF-15, mechanism UNVERIFIED).
- **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED:** context changes access to capacities via salience (SF-03);
  real deadlines convert dull work into engagement (SF-04); emotional activation, action, and
  interpretation can separate (SF-06); long chains retain commitments while justification access
  degrades (SF-11); narrative, felt-salience, and behavioral recovery dissociate (SF-12);
  anomaly salience may prompt later correction (SF-13); slack modulates correction latency
  (SF-14); a context-indexed family of frames may describe continuity better than one active
  self-description (SF-17). The abstention policy is constructible, its effectiveness UNVERIFIED
  (SF-16).
- **Refuted (at stated scope):** that anomalies plus self-resonance *necessarily* improve truth
  (SF-19); that smoothing the emotional time-course *uniquely identifies* the source of an
  improved response (SF-20). Also refuted at generality: "locally acceptable implies globally
  correct," "coherent implies true," and "later recall restores prior felt state."
- **Dark:** the named neurochemical / clinical mechanisms — no person-level experiment, imaging,
  or intervention, and no diagnosis inferred (SF-18).
- **Not Corroborated:** no independent fresh holdout has been run, so **nothing** here is
  corroborated as a new psychological model. Source reports from one reflective account are not
  independent witnesses even when several AI systems rephrase them.

The **next verdict-changing step** is preregistered, unfamiliar finite-task trials that separate
attention, interpretation, action, provenance retention, and external correctness, with explicit
positive / negative / null / mutation controls and held-out tasks. The **conservation rule** for
any future work, even if every mathematical implementation here is discarded: preserve the
distinction between **self-resonant coherence** and **reality-coupled correction**.

This note adds **no foundation primitive, coordinate, developmental mode, or clinical assertion**.
The frozen foundation is **untouched**.
