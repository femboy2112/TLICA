# Profile-Shape Disturbances

[← Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [File 4, §10.5](../foundation/4_derived_concepts_and_predictions.md)

---

Because the theory's primary object is the [profile](identity-correlation-profile.md) —
the *shape* of integration across your field — disturbances of the self are **shape
disturbances**, not "too little self" or "too much." This is one of the architecture's
sharpest commitments: conditions often classed as mental illness are **not** scalar
ρ-deficits ("depression = low ρ"). The same average ρ can correspond to completely
different conditions; what matters is the **structure**.

## Descriptive, not prescriptive

The architecture is **descriptive and non-moralizing**. It commits to a **developmental
window** — boundaries ill-defined but real — during which conscious direction is
insufficient to overcome substrate and environment. Patterns rooted there are **not the
I's chosen errors**.

The window is not only chronological. Two classes share the architecturally-relevant
feature ("conscious direction couldn't overcome the substrate response at formation"):

- **Developmental conditions** — early life, before self-direction exists. Childhood-onset
  PTSD, attachment disturbances, developmental trauma.
- **Situational-overwhelm conditions** — substrate response so intense that mature
  self-direction is overwhelmed *regardless of age*. Adult-onset PTSD from combat,
  assault, accident, sudden loss.

Outside the window, in mature operation, patterns *may* carry contributions from the I's
own choices — but substrate constraints still limit how much can be changed. The theory
**doesn't assert universal innocence**; it says the window protects against responsibility
for patterns rooted there, substrate limits modification throughout life, and the share of
mature self-direction in any case is an empirical question it doesn't pre-judge.

> **Extended self is baseline-normal, not a disturbance.** A self whose high-ρ
> integration extends broadly into family, community, land, lineage, or sacred objects is
> normal across many cultures and traditions. The theory **rejects** the
> Western-individualist reflex to pathologize this as enmeshment. Extension is
> structurally neutral; pathology arises only when it *combines* with other shape-features.

## The disturbance vocabulary

Disturbances are described as configurations of the apparatus you've already met:

- **Mode A disturbances** — weak boundary (fluid identity, self/other confusion) or
  rigid boundary (nearly all not-I rejected); **hypertrophied PtCns** (others' frames
  treated as illegitimate) or **atrophied PtCns** (one's own frame deprivileged, deferring
  to others as authoritative).
- **Mode B disturbances** — atrophied (concrete thinking, lack of insight) or
  hypertrophied (obsessive self-monitoring, dissociative self-observation).
- **Mode C disturbances** — atrophied (can't integrate experience as one's own) or
  hypertrophied (merger, loss-of-self in relationships).
- **Coordinate partitions** — dissociation as the profile **splitting**: some contents
  keep ρ-integration but lose φ-access, or vice versa. The partition *itself* is the
  diagnostic feature.

Many disturbances have a heavy **osmotic** component (trauma-cue dynamics, attachment
patterns, implicit-attitude dispositions) — which is why they persist and resist purely
cognitive engagement: the pattern lives at the substrate level. (See
[Substrate, Focus & Imprinting](substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md).)

## Worked example: severe depression

The foundation's most developed case shows the vocabulary in action:

- **Basic level** — *collapsed support*: previously high-ρ contents lose integration; the
  network **narrows**. The signature is structural narrowing, not low-ρ-everywhere. The
  third-order region (anhedonia, somatic flattening) is hit first and hardest.
- **Severe level** — a deeper disturbance: **functional collapse of the cogito/thought
  partition**. Normally there's a gap between the cogito (the I *itself*) and thoughts
  (contents the I *has*). In severe depression, depressive thoughts — "I am worthless,"
  "things will never get better" — acquire ρ approaching 1 and get treated as
  *constitutive of who the I is* rather than as contents the I holds. **Depression
  becomes functional proto-self** ("I am not myself, I am the depression").

  Structurally, the cogito is **untouched** — it can't fail, since any denial presupposes
  it. What collapses is the I's *functional access* to the partition. Within this
  disturbed profile, *represented* death-content can attain high ρ as the "natural
  completion" of the depressive proto-self, and the
  [prerogative of continued existence](the-two-prerogatives.md) can be **misdirected** —
  routed through a distorted preservation-ranking so that self-cessation is represented as
  relief. (Note: *actual* cessation is never integration; only its *representation* can
  carry ρ.)

- **Recovery** is structurally specifiable: functional access to the partition reasserts;
  depressive thoughts fall back to ordinary positions in the field; the depression-as-self
  dissolves. This matches the phenomenology of "getting myself back" — *the cogito was
  always there; what returns is access to it.*

  A downstream implication: rational arguments against suicide often fail in severe
  depression **not because they're unsound** but because they're addressed to the I as
  separate from the depressive content — and that separation is exactly what has
  functionally collapsed. It's a failure of *address*, not of reasoning.

The architecture is explicit that this is a **structural** account. A clinical risk model,
treatment model, or normative claim belongs to a dedicated application paper that must
engage existing suicidology — *not* the foundation.
