# Differentiated Affect

[← Applications overview](applications.md) · [Wiki home](README.md) · Source: [`applications/differentiated_affect_v1_0_2.md`](../applications/differentiated_affect_v1_0_2.md) · **Layer 7 · v1.0.2 · Complete**

---

The richest of the complete papers. Its claim: **emotions are configurations of the self
and its operators** — extension, loss, threat, reflexive evaluation, boundary operation,
frame-expansion, substrate perturbation. Name an emotion and the paper asks: *what is this
doing to the [maximally-self-defined I](the-two-prerogatives.md), and through which
operator?* The payoff is that affect becomes classifiable **by structural operators rather
than by its ordinary labels.**

## The method: start from love

Rather than enumerate emotions, the paper defines **one** precisely and derives the rest as
variations of it. That one is **love**, defined structurally by three conditions:

1. The self has [extended](modes-of-development.md) (via Mode C) to include another, **X**,
   as a *constitutive extension* — X is *inside* the self, so that losing X is represented
   as damage to the self. (High [ρ](three-coordinates.md) is necessary but not enough; the
   load-bearing feature is *topological* — X is part of you, not merely strongly linked to
   you.)
2. The [prerogative of consistency](the-two-prerogatives.md) has held the self's frame
   together across the extension (you remain "the one doing the loving," not dissolved into X).
3. X stays in the constitutive range under ordinary fluctuation of mood, contact, and
   certainty.

A lot follows immediately: self-sacrifice for a loved one stops being paradoxical (the child
*is* part of you); "you complete me" stops being metaphor; love can survive the beloved
changing, and can be one-sided, or directed at a country or a craft. And love can end **only
three ways** — active [Mode A](modes-of-development.md) differentiation (the real "falling
out of love," usually an accumulation of tiny differentiating updates), a wholesale
reorganization of the self, or death. Ordinary *fading* doesn't dissolve it.

> When the structure shifts before you can explain why, you get **recognition-lag** — the
> profile changes first, the conscious story catches up later. It's the architecture's
> account of "everyone could see it but you."

## The five clusters

Real affect is then organized into five families, treated as overlapping *projections* (an
actual feeling is usually a compound):

- **Connection** (extension affects) — **grief** (loss of a constitutive extension; "the
  shaking is literal" as the self reorganizes around the hole), fear-for-the-beloved,
  devotion, **jealousy** (threat to an extension), joy in another's flourishing. Plus the
  **dissolution** pathologies — obsession, codependency, cultic merger — where Mode C runs
  past the point the frame can survive: *not deeper love, but loss of the self that loved.*
- **Self** (frame affects) — the standout structural contribution. **Guilt, embarrassment,
  shame, and self-loathing are the same operation at different scopes** of reflexive
  self-evaluation: an *act* (guilt) → a *presentation* (embarrassment) → an *identity-aspect*
  (shame) → the *whole self* (self-loathing). Globalizing up that ladder is structurally
  easier than climbing back down — which is exactly why toxic shame is so hard to treat.
  Pride is the expansive (positive-valence) version. Shame also runs through a **witness**
  construct (actual, imagined, internalized, or retrospective), explaining shame inherited
  from standards you've consciously rejected.
- **Threat** (preservation affects) — **fear** (focused projected damage with an action
  available) vs. **anxiety** (diffuse projection across many futures) vs. **dread** (likely
  damage with the action-space *foreclosed*); **hope** and **despair** as the presence or
  absence of a viable projected path. *Learned helplessness* is reclassified: not an emotion
  but a damaged *projection apparatus* — the self perceives the world as having no paths, so
  belief alone can't fix it; only scaffolded successful action rebuilds the tool.
- **Boundary** (encounter affects) — **anger** (a frame-violation, met by Mode A to restore
  coherence), **disgust** (content too intolerable to integrate, expelled — and *physical
  and moral disgust are the same form*, whose ugliest failure mode is disgust aimed at
  *persons*, i.e. dehumanization), **awe** (encounter with something too vast for the
  current frame), boredom, and **hate** (sustained Mode A where *every* resolution pathway
  has failed — pathological by construction).
- **Embodied** (somatic affects) — pain, pleasure, trauma-cue activation, mood. Organized by
  *pathway* rather than by relation, so this cluster sits crosswise to the other four.

## Pathology as structure, not deficit

A throughline: severe states are **apparatus-level failures**, not just strong feelings. The
paper names a set — dissolution (frame), severe depression (the [cogito/thought partition
collapse](profile-shape-disturbances.md)), learned helplessness (projection apparatus),
chronic boredom (engagement-finding), anhedonia (pleasure-response) — each with distinct
damaged equipment and distinct recovery dynamics. **Panic** is the recurring teaching case:
maximal intensity, near-zero information, fully conscious but *source-opaque* — "fully
conscious, not almost unconscious."

## Status and stance

Complete, with **14 discriminating predictions** and a careful set of **translations** to
basic-emotion theory, appraisal theory, constructionism, affective neuroscience,
phenomenology, predictive coding, and attachment theory — "in dialogue with each," not
replacing them. The author is explicit that it is a **methodological exemplar, not an
exhaustive catalogue**, and that some of its evidence is her own first-person access to
depression, trauma activation, and suicidality — used as load-bearing data rather than
bracketed.
