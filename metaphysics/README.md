# metaphysics/ — spiritual and metaphysical companion material

This folder holds the project's **spiritual / metaphysical companion material**: work that
stands *beside* the formal identity architecture rather than being derived from it. It is kept
separate on purpose. These pieces are **explicitly voluntary and non-foundational** — nothing in
the frozen [`foundation/`](../foundation/) depends on them, and they introduce no new foundation
commitment.

If you are looking for the theory, start at the root [`README.md`](../README.md) or the
[`docs/` wiki](../docs/README.md). The formal, citable work lives in
[`foundation/`](../foundation/) and [`applications/`](../applications/); the research tier lives
in [`research/`](../research/).

## KIN-1-11 — The Egalitarian Spiritual Soldier

*Kinship · Interdependence · Non-Domination — "carry a conscience, not a chain of command."* A
companion manual for how independent people can act together: an explicitly voluntary, nonviolent,
decentralized field doctrine for dignity, practical agency, non-domination, bounded service, and
mutual accountability. It is not a military formation, recruitment system, church, ideology test,
hierarchy, or permit to harm.
