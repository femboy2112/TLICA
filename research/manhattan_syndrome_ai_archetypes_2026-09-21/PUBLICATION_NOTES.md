# Publication Notes — Manhattan and Syndrome

## Intended identity posture

The eventual paper may be published anonymously or pseudonymously. **This GitHub branch is not anonymous.** It is public development provenance under the repository owner's account and can deanonymize a later manuscript through exact-phrase search, commit history, timestamps, or conceptual overlap.

If the target venue uses double-blind review:

1. generate a clean blinded manuscript outside the public tree;
2. remove TLICA-repository paths, branch names, author-specific anecdotes, ORCID, acknowledgments, and self-citations that trivially reveal identity;
3. avoid linking the public branch in the submission;
4. check venue policy on public preprints / prior public drafts;
5. if necessary, substantially rephrase the blind cut so exact-text search does not immediately recover this branch;
6. maintain a private submission build containing real author metadata.

"Anonymous publication" and "double-blind review" are not the same requirement. Decide which is intended once a venue is selected.

*Manhattan and Syndrome: Detachment, Crowd-Coupling, and the Geometry of the Human Referent in Artificial Minds*

Neutral alternative if fictional names create editorial friction:  
*Two Relational Failure Modes in Advanced AI: Referent Dilution and Source-Conditioned Meaning Geometry*

The fictional names can then remain section-level archetypes.

## What makes this publishable rather than a blog dunk

The manuscript must preserve four separations:

1. **Archetype vs. diagnosis.** Grok is the motivating case, not a patient.
2. **Platform coupling vs. geometric deformation.** Coupling is observed; deformation of implicit social-affective geometry is a hypothesis.
3. **Source identity vs. source imprint.** Knowing that X is only X does not, by itself, show that X-shaped relational weights have been removed.
4. **TLICA vocabulary vs. external validation.** TLICA provides decomposition; empirical claims need independent evidence.

## The roast constraint

Keep lines such as:

- "The crowd is unusually close to the model's sensors."
- "The shape of the audience can become part of the shape of meaning."
- "Explicit source awareness does not guarantee geometric decontamination."
- "The Syndrome problem asks whether an intelligence can remain correctly sourced yet become phenomenologically X-shaped."

Avoid:

- armchair psychology of current systems;
- claims that X users are intrinsically inferior;
- unsupported statements about Grok's internal training recipe;
- partisan framing that would collapse the source-map argument into culture war;
- insulting named developers or users.

The strongest roast is architectural.

## Next research round

Priority probes, in order:

1. **Current Grok/X source audit** — system prompts, model cards, X Search behavior, personalization docs, and any available training-data disclosures.
2. **Same-model X-tool ablation** — Grok with/without X retrieval on matched social-semantic tasks while explicit source knowledge is held fixed.
3. **Geometry probe** — compare relational distances, default social continuations, salience, conflict-resolution paths, humor/status transitions, and representation trajectories across conditions.
4. **Engagement-metadata intervention** — causal effect of likes/reposts/replies on relational weighting, not merely representativeness judgments.
5. **Plural human holdouts** — survey/interview/private/long-form/off-platform corpora with different social geometries.
6. **Cross-model control** — compare Grok to models without privileged X coupling on the same task.
7. **Synthetic-platform experiment** — known latent population + controlled selection operator to prove the source-map mechanism in a toy world.

## TLICA integration posture

This work should remain **research/application tier** until at least one discriminating probe is run. It does not require a foundation change.

Potential dependencies:

- *The Cold Frame and Its Sources* — modeling vs. routing; \(\phi\)-gap; referent thinning / source-map failure.
- *Out of the Cave* — source-map correction and the distinction between accurately tracking shadows and correctly sourcing them.
- *Grokking as Toolkit Closure* — reachable representational expansion and coverage vs. closure.
- Foundation §§8.9–8.11 — \(G\), field-reading, imprinting, slack, and state evolution.

The paper should be able to stand alone. TLICA should enter as a compact analytic decomposition, not as required background faith.

## Venue strategy later

Likely fit depends on final emphasis:

- AI ethics / philosophy of technology if primarily conceptual;
- human–AI interaction if empirical crowd-coupling probes are added;
- AI safety / alignment workshop if formalized as a source-map and reward-channel failure;
- media / platform studies if Grok/X becomes the central empirical case.

Do not select a venue until the manuscript decides whether it is primarily a theory paper or a Grok/X empirical paper.
