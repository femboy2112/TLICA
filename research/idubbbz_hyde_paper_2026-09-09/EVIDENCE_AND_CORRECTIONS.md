## Corrections to the conversational version

The earlier answer called the comparison a clean duality, assumed a fixed person under Hyde's changing norm, and treated Ian's documentary as an extension of a stable Content Cop apparatus. All three moves were too quick. The paper preserves the author's intuition while replacing those claims with an episode-level hypothesis.

The earlier identification of societal normality with prosocial correction was also too simple. A norm can be socially legible and unjust; a norm can change without a mechanism changing, or a mechanism can change while a recognizable format remains. No single current worldview is used as the unquestioned baseline.

The conversational analyses of Hyde and Zappa sometimes described provocation as a decisive experiment. It is not decisive without rival predictions, intervention controls, source adequacy, and an observation that actually distinguishes the rivals. No such controlled human study occurred here.

The prior claim that Hyde attacks outward whereas Peart revises inward, or that Baldwin guarantees correct attribution, is not used as fact. These were analogical lenses, not stable psychological properties. The same limitation applies to declaring all sincerity unavailable under irony: conduct and effect can remain attributable even when belief is uncertain.

Unverified 2026 podcast, guest, season, and production claims from earlier turns are not used to classify either creator. No serious allegation is inferred from an unnamed source, an edited meme, or a printed citation without content inspection. Critiquing harmful rhetoric remains compatible with examining a method; neither task supplies a global character verdict.

## Publication boundary

The manuscript is complete prose, but full primary-video collation and comparative validation are not complete research activities. They are explicitly unrun rather than hidden behind a confident essay. This is an adequate basis for author review and a research handoff, not for presenting the comparative hypothesis as an established empirical result.
