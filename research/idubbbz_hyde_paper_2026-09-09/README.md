**Leah — assistant-assisted paper development — 2026-09-09**

**Status:** complete conceptual working manuscript with an evidence ledger, counterarguments, formal limitations, and an unrun comparative research protocol. This is not a completed empirical creator study, a diagnosis, a verdict on either person's entire politics or ethics, or a claim of mathematical duality.

The author's originating hypothesis is: 'I feel like Ian is the societal-normal form of Hyde. Hyde is Iddubz inside out and reflected.' The paper preserves that insight while asking exactly what could be reflected: the allocation of authority between a person, an operative norm, a staged encounter, and the editor of its record.

The strongest proposed contrast is between **norm-referenced adjudication** and **protocol-disrupting inquiry**. These are nonexclusive practices. Both creators may judge, provoke, perform, edit, change norms, or preserve ambiguity. The paper's comparative claim must remain capable of losing to those facts.

## Research claim

The question is not which creator is smarter, more authentic, socially normal, or the winner of a feud. It is: **what does a critical performance treat as its standard, what does it make available for revision, and who gets to decide what the resulting evidence means?**

A person may be evaluated against a norm; a norm may be exposed by disturbing the situation in which people enact it. But edited footage cannot automatically isolate person, norm, intervention, or audience effects. Nor does accurate social prediction establish ethical permission to produce a response.
