# Distributed Institutional Realization

**Status:** research-program bootstrap / application-paper seed

**Branch:** `research/distributed-institutional-realization-2026-09-15`

**Date:** 2026-09-15

## Core question

How can TLICA describe socially real structures such as governments, courts, firms, norms, offices, currencies, procedures, and other institutions without either:

1. reducing them to documents or individual beliefs, or
2. reifying them into unindexed group minds?

The motivating intuition is that an institution is physically realized through many indexed people, their role-conditioned profiles, their relations to one another, and shared artifacts embedded in primal not-I. A local observer can therefore see behavior that appears abrupt or discontinuous even though the causal support for that behavior is continuously distributed across a larger relational structure.

The program proposes an application-level **distributed institutional realization** scaffold:

\[
\mathcal I_t = \mathfrak R_t(\mathbf P_t,\mathbf R_t,\mathbf D_t,\mathbf S_t),
\]

where:

- `P_t` is the indexed family of agent profiles,
- `R_t` is the role/authority/communication/obligation relation structure,
- `D_t` is the family of external artifacts and records,
- `S_t` is the relevant situational field / material environment,
- `R_t` (fraktur R in the displayed equation) is an application-level realization map, not a new foundation coordinate,
- `I_t` is the realized institutional configuration at time `t`, not an agent and not a group mind.

The corresponding action/effect map is schematically:

\[
\mathscr A_t : \mathcal I_t \longrightarrow \Delta \mathcal W_t,
\]

where `W_t` is the relevant state of primal not-I. This makes explicit how a distributed social structure can have ordinary physical consequences without becoming an additional supernatural or mental entity.

## Central phenomenon

A local projection can make distributed causation look discontinuous:

\[
\pi_L(\mathcal W_{t^-}) \to \text{apparently abrupt action} \to \pi_L(\mathcal W_{t^+}),
\]

while the larger relational state contains a continuous causal path through documents, agents, role activation, communication, and institutional relations.

The working slogan is:

> **Apparent local discontinuity can be the projection of distributed causal structure onto an insufficient local frame.**

This is not claimed as the explanation of every abrupt event. It is a candidate structural class that TLICA currently has the ingredients to represent but does not yet canonically package.

## Existing TLICA machinery being reused

This program deliberately builds from existing architecture rather than inventing a new ontology prematurely:

- indexed modeling Is rather than unindexed group subjects;
- identity-correlation profiles and graph structure;
- role-indexed profiles and relational cascades in the agency applications;
- the `cultural-I` as an emergent distributed pattern rather than an intending agent;
- primal not-I as the home of external artifacts, bodies, buildings, records, networks, and other implementation substrate;
- situational fields as external incentive/social environments;
- formation/activation separation and always-on osmotic imprinting;
- source-pathway sensitivity and anti-anthropomorphism discipline.

The current foundation is v5.5.0. This research program is **application-level unless and until it survives comparison, counterexample search, formal pressure, and empirical translation.**

## Non-negotiable constraints

1. **No group-mind reification.** An institution may be causally real as a distributed pattern without being a subject.
2. **No vector-space cosplay.** The vector metaphor is useful only if relations, local sections, and implementation structure are retained. A plain coefficient vector over persons is insufficient.
3. **No causality by naming.** Writing `institution -> outcome` is shorthand until an indexed path through agents/artifacts/relations is supplied.
4. **No foundation promotion by elegance.** `\mathfrak R_t`, `\mathcal I_t`, and `\mathscr A_t` are application objects unless independent work shows they are required more generally.
5. **No metaphysical inflation.** Socially real does not mean ontologically primitive; emergent does not mean fictional; distributed does not mean conscious.
6. **Local discontinuity is a projection claim, not a universal excuse.** The model must identify the hidden path and predict what interventions would break it.

## Target application paper

Working title:

**Distributed Institutional Realization: Local Discontinuity, Relational Causation, and Social Structure Without a Group Mind**

The paper should eventually answer four questions cleanly:

1. What exactly is realized when an institution exists *now*?
2. Where is that structure physically carried?
3. How does it produce local action without becoming an additional agent?
4. What observations discriminate this account from simpler alternatives such as document-only, belief-sum, role-script, or ordinary network descriptions?
