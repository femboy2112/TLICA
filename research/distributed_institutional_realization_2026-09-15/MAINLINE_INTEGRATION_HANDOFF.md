## Core object to recover

The proposal starts from the application-level scaffold

\[
\mathcal I_t
=
\mathfrak R_t(\mathbf P_t,\mathbf R_t,\mathbf D_t,\mathbf S_t),
\]

where:

- `P_t`: indexed agent profiles;
- `R_t`: typed relational / role / authority / communication / obligation structure;
- `D_t`: material-symbolic artifacts and records in primal not-I;
- `S_t`: relevant situational/material field;
- `I_t`: a distributed institutional configuration, **not an agent**;
- `fraktur R_t`: an application-level realization map, not currently a foundation primitive.

The corresponding shorthand action map is

\[
\mathscr A_t:\mathcal I_t\to\Delta\mathcal W_t,
\]

but every such arrow must be expandable into indexed paths through actual agents/artifacts/communication/activation. No magical downward causation is permitted.

The central phenomenological/system claim is:

> **Apparent local discontinuity can be the projection of distributed causal structure onto an insufficient local frame.**

This is only useful if the model predicts which omitted support variables matter and which interventions sever the path. Do not let it collapse into the generic truism that hidden causes exist.

## Architectural constraints

Preserve these unless a decisive contradiction is found.

### 1. No group-mind reification

Do not introduce an unindexed collective phenomenal subject.

Institutional shorthand such as "the court decided" may remain ordinary-language compression, but formal causation and attribution must be recoverable through indexed roles/procedures/agents/artifacts.

### 2. No plain-vector reduction

The motivating "people as a basis" image is not the final mathematics. A coefficient vector over persons loses typed relational structure. Treat vector language as intuition only unless a precise construction earns more.

### 3. Keep cultural-I distinct until proven otherwise

Audit all current uses. Working hypothesis:

- cultural-I = distributed cultural encoding/pattern;
- institutional realization = broader operational structure including profiles, typed roles/relations, artifacts, records, and material pathways.

Do not silently redefine `cultural-I`.

### 4. Keep situational field distinct

Institution and field may couple bidirectionally. Do not absorb all external conditions into `I_t`.

### 5. Keep identity-correlation separate from causal participation

An agent can be high-rho toward an institution but causally peripheral, or low-rho but operationally central. Preserve this dissociation.

### 6. Formation is not activation

Use existing foundation machinery rather than inventing a duplicate role-state primitive where possible.

### 7. Source path matters

Identical content can propagate differently under distinct provenance/authority paths. Reuse or extend existing source-status machinery rather than creating an unrelated authority scalar.

### 8. Application first

Default assumption: the new objects remain application-level derived structures. Foundation promotion requires repeated cross-application necessity.

## Discriminating probes

Prioritize probes that can kill the preferred interpretation.

### Probe A — local support visibility

Compare the same terminal event under:

- local-only context;
- correct distributed causal support;
- equal-length irrelevant context;
- plausible but false support.

Measure prediction/intervention accuracy, not only subjective coherence.

### Probe B — global similarity versus interface compatibility

Full 2x2:

- high global similarity / high interface compatibility;
- high global similarity / low interface compatibility;
- low global similarity / high interface compatibility;
- low global similarity / low interface compatibility.

Test whether interface compatibility is the stronger predictor of successful coordination.

### Probe C — source-path replay

Replay identical content through authorized, unauthorized, forged, unknown, and delegated automated paths.

### Probe D — codified versus lived

Use a bounded, low-risk workflow and compare formal procedure graph against observed action graph.

### Probe E — persistence under turnover

Compare role-preserving person replacement against person-preserving edge destruction.
