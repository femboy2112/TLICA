# Author seed — authority-compatible frame dynamics

**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Status:** author-originated phenomenology / conceptual seed  
**Use:** provenance only; not independent evidence

This file preserves the load-bearing intuitions that motivated the dossier. The language is intentionally close to the originating conversation rather than publication-polished.

## Seed 1 — predictability is not necessity

> "I can model this fuckshit and work around it" => it's a structural invariant you must submit to.
>
> I can model it.  
> I foresee this outcome.  
> If I act in accordance with fuckshit, minimal friction in the implicitly assumed static system.  
> If I don't act in accordance with fuckshit, bad things for me.  
> Therefore, to not act in accordance with fuckshit shifts the blame of the bad things on myself because it was strictly foreseeable.

The originating objection is that this sequence performs multiple illicit conversions:

1. current predictive success becomes structural invariance;
2. foreseeability of retaliation becomes authorship of retaliation;
3. prudential adaptation becomes normative assent.

## Seed 2 — slack and the edge

> "Nothing I do will change it" is never actually true; it's more a matter of what you are willing to lose, and how close to the edge you are.

Refinement preserved in this dossier: the relevant variable is not only willingness but **ability to absorb cost**, including obligations to dependents and other coupled agents. The claim to test is therefore not that every person always has a practically usable resistance path, but that **local infeasibility is frequently overgeneralized into global impossibility**.

## Seed 3 — the resister as cheater

> Phenomenologically, it's because in the bootlicker frame, the information that would make distinguishing bona fide resistance from "bad guy" has been thrown away, either on purpose or through environmental dynamics.
>
> There is also this feeling that, I had to suffer therefore they have to as well, otherwise mine was meaningless / I was wrong / etc.
>
> The bootlicker sees the resister as a cheater.

This motivates the distinctions among:

- information loss or coarse classification of resistance;
- fairness conditional on a rule versus evaluation of the rule itself;
- retrospective necessity;
- sunk suffering as an epistemic or identity commitment;
- counterexample suppression when successful resistance threatens an autobiographical justification.

## Seed 4 — heterogeneous bootlicking

> Some people compartmentalize bootlicker frames to survive. Some people fucking are bootlickers. Some people are bootlickers because it's a positive self-resonance feedback loop.

The dossier does **not** interpret "are bootlickers" as an immutable essence. It treats this as a hypothesis about a **stable cross-context attractor**: a relational policy can become sufficiently integrated and self-reinforcing that it reappears under changed authorities and contexts.

## Seed 5 — positive self-resonance

> Having your self perception, your model of reality, your way of being, your thoughts, the things you notice, your priorities, literally anything in reality that when it is presented to you the natural physical reaction of your substrate is to send "GOOD THING" signals.

This is the central generative insight of the present package.

Positive self-resonance is not restricted to conscious pleasure or verbal endorsement. The candidate object is broad **positive valence attached to self-relevant representations or relations**, potentially expressed as relief, familiarity, righteousness, belonging, certainty, pride, safety, salience, motivational pull, or other substrate-mediated "good/approach/keep" signals.

The central conjecture is that an authority-compatible frame becomes especially durable when it does not merely explain the world but helps organize which parts of the world are noticed, retained, preferred, acted on, and environmentally reproduced.

## Authorial normative stance

The originating stance is strongly anti-submission. The research package separates that stance from the empirical mechanism claims. The analysis is intended to explain a family of authority-compatible dynamics without treating ordinary survival under coercion as endorsement and without converting the author's moral disgust into empirical evidence.
