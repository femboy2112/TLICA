# Claim ledger

**Status vocabulary:** Disclosed / Corroborated / Observed / Conjectured / UNVERIFIED / Dark / Refuted.

The ledger follows the repository rule that conclusions inherit the weakest load-bearing premise.

## C-001 — Current-regime predictability does not entail structural invariance

**Claim.** From \(P(H\mid a,S_t)\approx1\) one cannot infer \(\forall S\,P(H\mid a,S)\approx1\) without additional premises.

**Status:** **Disclosed** as a logical non-implication.

**Truth debt:** none beyond defining the state family correctly.

---

## C-002 — Foreseeability does not by itself establish authorship

**Claim.** A target's ability to foresee another actor's discretionary response does not, by itself, erase the responding actor from causal attribution.

**Status:** **Disclosed** at the causal-structure level; **UNVERIFIED** as a model of ordinary responsibility judgments.

**Truth debt:** distinguish causal contribution from normative responsibility in future writing.

---

## C-003 — Local infeasibility does not entail global causal impotence

**Claim.** A person's feasible action set can be narrow because of low slack even when actions outside that set would perturb the system.

**Status:** **Disclosed** in the proposed formal distinction; empirical prevalence **UNVERIFIED**.

---

## C-004 — Resistance-visibility error

**Claim.** People sometimes infer "nothing changed" from unchanged macrostate while ignoring changes to enforcement cost, information, coordination, or future reachability.

**Status:** **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED.**

**Falsifier:** observers reliably integrate non-macro effects when those effects are made explicit.

---

## C-005 — Self-confirming domination equilibrium

**Claim.** Expectations of institutional persistence can increase low-perturbation adaptation, which can itself contribute to persistence and thereby confirm the expectation.

**Status:** **Conjectured** as a social mechanism; finite synthetic witness not yet built.

**Boundary:** existing material power remains causal; belief is not claimed to create arbitrary reality.

---

## C-006 — Captured fairness

**Claim.** Some agents evaluate fairness among participants while holding the rule fixed, producing resentment toward those who escape a cost even when the rule itself is contestable.

**Status:** **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED.**

**Rivals:** ordinary norm enforcement, material incentives, selfish-defection detection, status defense.

---

## C-007 — Resistance / defection information collapse

**Claim.** In some authority-compatible frames, information sufficient to distinguish principled resistance from selfish defection is absent, ignored, or compressed away, causing both to map to "noncompliance."

**Status:** **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED.**

**Probe:** restore motive and legitimacy information while holding outward behavior fixed.

---

## C-008 — Retrospective necessity

**Claim.** Having paid a cost can increase the tendency to interpret that cost as necessary, such that a successful resister becomes a counterexample to the agent's autobiographical model.

**Status:** **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED.**

**Rivals:** effort justification, sunk-cost reasoning, fairness, envy, status threat.

---

## C-009 — Sunk suffering as epistemic commitment

**Claim.** Past suffering can become coupled to a necessity narrative strongly enough that preserving the narrative becomes a motive for imposing the cost on later entrants.

**Status:** **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED.**

**Boundary:** not every "I had to do it" norm has this mechanism.

---

## C-010 — Compliance is not internalization

**Claim.** Identical compliant behavior can arise from survival emulation, normative endorsement, material incentives, habit, or self-resonant identification.

**Status:** **Disclosed** as underdetermination of internal state by behavior alone.

## C-011 — Authority-compatible frame dynamics are heterogeneous

**Claim.** The colloquial category "bootlicking" should be decomposed into at least context scope, internalization, positive self-resonance, and externalization rather than treated as a unitary trait.

**Status:** **Conjectured as a useful decomposition; UNVERIFIED empirically.**

## C-012 — Positive self-resonance is broader than conscious pleasure

**Claim.** A self-relevant representation may carry positive substrate-mediated valence expressed as relief, certainty, familiarity, righteousness, belonging, safety, pride, salience, or approach tendency without a reflective judgment "I enjoy this."

**Status:** **Conjectured at this application level**, compatible with existing TLICA affect machinery.

**Boundary:** examples are phenomenological possibilities, not a validated exhaustive taxonomy.

## C-013 — Valence-weighted sampling

**Claim.** Positive self-valence can bias salience, retention, or interpretation such that explicit reasoning operates on a systematically selected evidence surface.

**Status:** **Conjectured / UNVERIFIED.**

**Strong falsifier:** valence adds no incremental effect on sampling after threat, incentives, and prior belief are controlled.

---

## C-014 — Ecological self-reinforcement

**Claim.** Frame-consistent actions can alter the future social environment in ways that increase exposure to frame-consistent inputs.

**Status:** **Conjectured** as a mechanism family; obvious construction-level examples are possible, population importance **UNVERIFIED**.

## C-015 — Cross-context authority attractor

**Claim.** For some agents, authority-compatible organization may be stable enough to reappear under changed authority objects and contexts after immediate threat is removed.

**Status:** **UNVERIFIED.**

**Boundary:** this is not an immutable essence or diagnosis.

## C-016 — Resonance is not truth
