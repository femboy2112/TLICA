# Authority-Compatible Frame Dynamics

## Constraint reification, foreseeability laundering, captured fairness, and positive self-resonance

**Status:** research-tier working dossier  
**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Foundation:** untouched  
**Empirical status:** central mechanisms **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED**  
**Publication status:** paper seed, not a finished application paper  
**Colloquial target:** "bootlicking"  
**Technical object:** heterogeneous **authority-compatible frame dynamics**

This dossier develops a candidate explanation for a family of behaviors colloquially grouped as "bootlicking": not mere obedience, deference, prudential submission, or respect for expertise, but processes by which a locally predictive model of power can become reified, normatively endorsed, identity-integrated, and eventually reproduced by the people subject to it.

The central warning is simple:

> **Predicting domination does not make domination necessary; foreseeing punishment does not make the punished person its author; being unable to afford resistance does not make resistance causally impossible; and enforcement does not by itself confer legitimacy.**

The dossier then adds a TLICA-compatible dynamical conjecture: an authority-compatible frame may become self-sustaining when the substrate attaches positive valence — "GOOD THING" signals in the author's phenomenological shorthand — to the self-perception, interpretations, priorities, attention patterns, and actions that keep the frame coherent. At that point the frame can bias what is sampled from reality before explicit reasoning begins and can help construct an environment that feeds confirming inputs back into the same frame.

## Load-bearing distinctions

This package refuses to classify outward compliance by itself.

Three limiting cases are kept separate:

1. **Compartmentalized survival emulation.** The person models an authority frame locally because doing so reduces immediate danger or preserves scarce slack, while retaining the judgment that the frame is contingent or unjust.
2. **Internalized authority compatibility.** The frame persists beyond the immediate coercive context and begins to organize normative judgment: what is enforced becomes what is "realistic," "responsible," or deserved.
3. **Positive self-resonant reproduction.** Authority-compatible perception and behavior themselves acquire positive self-valence and can become a cross-context attractor that rewards the agent for perceiving, inhabiting, and reproducing the hierarchy.

These are candidate mechanisms, not mutually exclusive natural kinds.

## Core transformations under study

The manuscript seed develops six candidate errors or dynamics:

- **predictability → invariance**: a reliable model of a present regime is mistaken for a law of the system;
- **foreseeability → responsibility**: knowing retaliation will occur is treated as authorship of the retaliation;
- **local constraint → global impossibility**: "I cannot presently afford this action" becomes "this action cannot matter";
- **enforcement → legitimacy**: the capacity to impose a rule becomes evidence that the rule is warranted;
- **within-rule fairness → rule legitimacy**: equal payment of an arbitrary cost is substituted for evaluation of whether the cost should exist;
- **past suffering → retrospective necessity**: having paid a cost becomes evidence that the cost had to be paid, making successful resisters appear to have "cheated."

The candidate population-level loop is a **self-confirming domination equilibrium**:

\[
\text{expect persistence}
\rightarrow
\text{adapt to persistence}
\rightarrow
\text{reduce perturbations}
\rightarrow
\text{increase persistence}
\rightarrow
\text{evidence for expected persistence}.
\]

The positive self-resonance extension adds:

\[
\text{authority-compatible frame}
\rightarrow
\text{positive valence for frame-consistent states}
\rightarrow
\text{biased salience / retention / action}
\rightarrow
\text{environmental reproduction}
\rightarrow
\text{new frame-consistent inputs}.
\]

## Current verdict

The conceptual non-equivalences are secure as logical distinctions. The proposed psychological and social mechanisms are **not** yet established.

In particular, this package has **not** shown that:

- authority-compatible self-resonance is common;
- it explains any particular person's behavior;
- it is more predictive than established rival constructs;
- successful resistance generally improves outcomes;
- people who comply are therefore internalized or morally culpable;
- "bootlicking" is a single trait;
- the proposed attractor exists as a measurable human dynamical object.

Those are the debts future work has to pay.
