## 2. Substrate and affect

The strongest bridge is to:

- docs/substrate-focus-and-imprinting.md
- foundation/1_foundations.md
- foundation/3_formal_apparatus.md

Current TLICA already states that third-order pathways compress input through the body and cognitive substrate into intensity / valence / action-tendency, and that osmotic imprinting can alter later salience and somatic-affective association through repeated co-occurrence without requiring focus or verification.

The present dossier does **not** derive authority preference from that machinery.

It uses the machinery to make one candidate mechanism architecturally admissible:

> repeated authority-compatible experience could, in principle, form substrate-mediated dispositions whose later activation changes salience, valence, and action readiness.

Whether this actually occurs in the proposed social cases is empirical and remains UNVERIFIED.

## 3. Resonance firewall

Relevant precedent:

- research/specific_coherent_agency_2026-09-18/
- research/moreish_ontology_2026-09-13/
- docs/glossary.md

The repository already distinguishes self-resonance from truth.

This dossier extends that firewall:

\[
\text{positive self-resonance}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{truth},
\]

\[
\text{positive self-resonance}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{legitimacy},
\]

\[
\text{coherent reasoning over sampled inputs}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{adequate sampling}.
\]

This is essential. The paper is not "people are wrong because they feel good." It is a candidate account of how valence can alter sampling and persistence while remaining epistemically non-warranting.

## 4. Mode B and slack

Existing TLICA treats reflective access and slack as resources for self-directed intervention on accumulated structure.

The present paper should therefore avoid claiming that an internalized authority frame is simply chosen.

A frame can be:

- osmotically formed;
- source-opaque;
- high-ρ / identity-integrated;
- costly to inspect;
- locally adaptive under real threat.

Low slack can reduce reachable alternatives without converting the frame into truth or necessity.

This makes the distinction between **formation** and **endorsement** load-bearing.

## 5. Agency and responsibility

Relevant precedent:

- applications/free_will_v0_3_0.md
- docs/app-agency-architecture.md

Existing work explicitly separates structural inputs to responsibility attribution from normative sufficiency and warns that wholehearted endorsement can exist under manipulation or source compromise.

The new concept **foreseeability laundering** therefore remains modest:

- it identifies an invalid inference if foreseeability alone is used to erase the imposing actor;
- it does not supply a complete responsibility theory;
- it does not claim the target bears zero responsibility in every foreseeable-risk case.

## 6. Institutions

Relevant precedent:

- research/distributed_institutional_realization_2026-09-15/

The authority-frame dossier requires no literal group mind.

A hierarchy can be reproduced through indexed agents, rules, records, incentives, and peer enforcement. "The institution internalizes itself" is acceptable only as shorthand for:

> local agents acquire dispositions that reproduce parts of the institutional response function without continuous direct command.

Keep the indexed implementation visible in any publication draft.

## 7. Moreish relation

Relevant precedent:

- applications/moreish_ontology_v1_0.md
- research/moreish_ontology_2026-09-13/

Moreish already studies a self-reinforcing failure mode in which present resonance can be mistaken for sufficient policy evidence.

The current dossier generalizes a structurally related warning in another domain:

\[
\text{frame resonance}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{frame warrant}.
\]

But do not claim identity between the mechanisms.

Moreish concerns a greedy temporal policy and future causal discounting. Authority-compatible frame dynamics concern coercion, rule reification, social attribution, and valence-weighted replication.

They may share a higher-level "resonance protects current frame" motif while remaining distinct applications.

## 8. Quiet-quitting / labor material

Relevant precedent:

- research/quiet_quitting_manifesto_2026-09-16/

The motivating examples often concern workplaces because they make asymmetry, dependence, and low slack concrete.

The eventual paper should not become a workplace-only theory unless deliberately scoped that way.

The authority object could be familial, bureaucratic, educational, military, religious, corporate, cultural, or interpersonal. Any extension requires domain-specific evidence.

## 9. Terminology boundary

"Bootlicker" is retained in the author seed because it is the originating phenomenon label.

Publication-facing prose should prefer:

- authority-compatible frame;
- survival emulator;
- internalized authority frame;
- self-resonant authority attractor;
- peer-enforcement / externalization.

This is not euphemism. It is needed to prevent the conclusion from being encoded in the label.

## 10. Foundation verdict

**Foundation untouched.**

The present work is a composition and extension at research/application level. If later data force a new primitive, that should be argued separately rather than smuggled into the theory through this paper.
