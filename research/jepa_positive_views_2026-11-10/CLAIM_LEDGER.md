Their code, paper, and model reports share provenance. **Paper publication and author code are not independent replications.**

The bridge from source-reported JEPA alignment to human TLICA substrate learning inherits the status **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED** regardless of how strong the theorem is under its separate assumptions. Spectral coherence and tool-closure diagnostics are not truth probabilities.
