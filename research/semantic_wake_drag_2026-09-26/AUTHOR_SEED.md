## Scope note

The dossier that grew from these remarks treats the aerodynamic / wave / Cherenkov language as an **analogy generator**, not as a literal physical claim. The target is a social-control and communication problem:

1. a high-resolution internal model produces rapid, locally coherent interventions;
2. surrounding people or institutions observe only projections of those interventions;
3. the model that made the interventions coherent is not transmitted at equal bandwidth;
4. observers reconstruct a lower-resolution or lagged model;
5. their reactions become part of the next field the author must act through.

The durable labels introduced by the analysis are:

- **semantic wake drag** — accumulated resistance generated by the social consequences of lossy or lagged reconstruction;
- **semantic Cherenkov radiation** — the deliberately comic limiting-case metaphor for visible disturbance when externally legible action changes faster than the surrounding medium can update its interpretation;
- **behavioral compatibility without representational interoperability** — output-level coordination or compliance can occur even when the generating frame is not shared or reconstructed.

None of these labels is a new TLICA coordinate or foundation primitive.
