## 0. Question

Does the semantic-wake proposal require a new TLICA primitive?

**No.**

The phenomenon can be expressed as a composition of machinery already present across the self-applied, semantic-interoperability, agency, and distributed-institutional layers.

The genuinely new content is:

1. the **timescale-mismatch synthesis**;
2. the **resolution-mismatch construction** applied to the author's action stream;
3. the accumulated **semantic wake** / **wake drag** construct;
4. the proposed **semantic header** intervention;
5. the relation to malicious compliance under the broader label **behavioral compatibility without representational interoperability**;
6. the intentionally comic **semantic Cherenkov radiation** mnemonic.

Items 1–6 remain application/research-level until tested.

## 3. Dependencies that must remain separate

### Truth vs. legibility

\[
\text{well reconstructed} \not\Rightarrow \text{true}.
\]

### Legibility vs. agreement

\[
\text{understood} \not\Rightarrow \text{accepted}.
\]

### Precision vs. completeness

\[
\text{high internal resolution} \not\Rightarrow \text{all relevant variables modeled}.
\]

### Friction vs. misunderstanding

\[
\text{resistance} \not\Rightarrow \text{observer lag}.
\]

### Institution vs. observer mind

Persistent wake may be distributed across artifacts, roles, and procedures. It need not live in one person's memory.

These separations are the main anti-self-confirmation firewalls.

## 4. TLICA diagnostic vector

This dossier does **not** collapse TLICA diagnostics into one score.

Where relevant:

- \(\kappa\): whether the relevant person/institution is presently coupled to the action stream;
- \(\phi\): toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability of claims, not how well someone understands the actor;
- \(\sigma\): source-map adequacy may matter when interpreting why a move occurred;
- \(\rho\): identity/commitment coupling may affect stakes but is not evidence that an interpretation is correct;
- \(\mu\): probe availability is directly relevant to whether rival explanations can be distinguished.

The proposed \(\chi\) timescale ratio is **not** a replacement or aggregate for any of these.

## 5. Why this stays research-tier

The repository already supports the **language** needed to formulate the problem, but not the empirical claim that this mechanism explains the author's recurrent friction.

Moving directly into the foundation would commit the exact error the repository's methodology warns against:

> phenomenological fit → analogy → immediate ontology.

The correct path is:

\[
\text{phenomenology}
\rightarrow
\text{candidate model}
\rightarrow
\text{discriminating probe}
\rightarrow
\text{update}.
\]

The present branch therefore:

- preserves the author seed;
- states the model;
- declares the analogy boundary;
- carries a claim ledger;
- specifies falsifiers;
- leaves the foundation untouched.

## 6. Promotion criteria

A future application-level promotion would require at minimum:

1. event-level evidence separating misunderstanding from substantive disagreement and actor error;
2. a calibrated semantic-header or resolution probe;
3. evidence that the proposed variables add predictive value over generic workload/change-cost explanations;
4. at least one hostile or unflattering holdout where the framework correctly classifies the author as wrong rather than under-translated;
5. a decision about whether the malicious-compliance relation is genuinely one shared formal object or merely an illuminating analogy.

Until then:

> **Semantic Wake Drag is a promising self-applied research construct, not a foundation result.**
