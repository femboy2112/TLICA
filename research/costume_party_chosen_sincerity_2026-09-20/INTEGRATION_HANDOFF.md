The section should remain short in the front-door paper. The research note can carry the full autobiography.

## Candidate compressed insertion prose

> There was also a social-developmental asymmetry underneath the compiler bug. I took many declared norms seriously enough to pay their internal cost. Later I discovered that social life often contains more performance, selective enforcement, negotiated commitment, and frame-management than my earlier model allowed. The discovery did not merely produce skepticism. It produced embarrassment: I had sometimes shown up with what felt like my real face and discovered that the room was partly a costume party.
>
> That matters because skepticism then pays in more than epistemic accuracy. It can also protect against renewed humiliation. If earnest participation made me feel exploitable, ironic distance becomes a way of refusing the jurisdiction of the frame itself. Underground Super Hans therefore does not only say, "models are not reality." He can also say, "you are not making a mug out of me again."
>
> This does not vindicate the later policy. It explains why the policy was rewarding enough to compile. A scoped truth — some norms are performative, some authorities are wrong, some scripts deserve refusal — can become a global governor: never let sincere commitment expose you again.
>
> The eventual correction is not a return to innocence. It is **chosen sincerity**: retaining the ability to inspect, reject, or laugh at a frame while still being able to say, with eyes open, "I choose to care anyway."

## Candidate later insertion near §8 ("Cosmic comedy")

The *Falling Down* resonance belongs better near the retrospective-integration section than near the sensor introduction.

Compressed form:

> The delayed punchline can also be morally disorienting. A sequence of locally intelligible refusals can alter the state from which the next refusal is made until the accumulated trajectory no longer resembles the invariant that originally justified resistance. The resulting question is not necessarily "When did I decide to become this?" There may have been no such decision. It is closer to the bewilderment captured by *Falling Down*: "I'm the bad guy? How the hell did that happen?" Structurally, this is a path-dependence problem. The controller inherits its own output.

Do not overuse the film comparison. It should remain a structural reference, not a moral equivalence between the author and the character.

## Candidate conclusion addition

Near the existing conclusion:

> The answer is therefore not to restore childhood sincerity by deleting skepticism. Innocence cannot be recovered by pretending the costume party was never there. The stronger endpoint is chosen sincerity: commitment after exposure to contingency, irony, and refusal. The frame has been inspected; the exit remains visible; the value is endorsed anyway.

## Canon / epistemic guardrails

If integrated:

- Keep the autobiographical mapping explicitly author-derived.
- Do not generalize from this case to children, neurodivergence, trauma, or any diagnostic class without evidence.
- Preserve "automatic is not the same as myopic."
- Preserve the current Moreish distinction between a true sensor and a bad governor.
- Do not let "costume party" become a global claim that people are fake.
- Do not let chosen sincerity become another global governor; it is a proposed authority relation, not a universal life rule.
- Preserve the existing warning against redemptive over-coherence.
