# Claim Ledger — Cinema as Self-Coupled Meaning-Space Reconstruction

**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Scope:** Research-tier conceptual reconstruction. No foundation change.

| ID | Claim | Status | Basis | Verdict-changing probe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | A person's operative representation of a world-state is not identical to the world-state itself. | **Foundation-compatible / inherited** | TLICA already separates primal not-I from the I's represented contents and treats substrate-mediated access as lossy/toolkit-relative. | A successful architecture-level argument that the relevant representation map is literally identity on the declared domain would remove the need for this distinction. |
| C2 | A perceptual “quotient” is mathematically legitimate only after a task-relative equivalence relation is declared. | **Disclosed / mathematical** | Standard quotient construction plus TLICA glossary discipline. | Refuted only by changing the mathematical meaning of “quotient”; otherwise definitional. |
| C3 | For declared \(i,c,\tau\), \(w_1\sim_{i,c,\tau}w_2\) may be defined by equivalence of their task-relevant decoded representations, yielding \(\mathcal W/\!\sim_{i,c,\tau}\) when the relation satisfies reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity. | **Disclosed / conditional construction** | Direct definition. | Failure of the proposed relation to be an equivalence relation on the chosen domain forces a different object (e.g. tolerance relation, pseudometric neighborhood, cover, clustering). |
| C4 | Acting can usefully be modeled as selecting/constructing a physical representative intended to preserve an experienced form under intersubjective transport. | **CONJECTURED structural model** | Author intuition + compatibility with TLICA art/reconstruction machinery. | If a simpler surface-cue model predicts acting success/failure equally well or better without representative/transport structure, this model loses explanatory value. |
| C5 | \(P_v\circ G_a\approx\mathrm{id}\) is generally too strong because actor and viewer do not share a guaranteed common meaning-space; \(P_v(G_a(m))\approx T_{a\to v}(m)\) is the safer form. | **Disclosed as modeling constraint** | Follows from nonidentity of private representational bases unless a common basis is independently established. | A demonstrated shared basis for a bounded task could justify identity on that restricted subspace. |
| C6 | Acting resembles an approximate local section/representative-selection problem over a restricted semantic region. | **CONJECTURED analogy with formal bridge** | Quotient projection + constrained generative realization. | If section-like invariants add no predictive or diagnostic power across performances, retain only the weaker generative-map model. |
| C7 | Physical fidelity and semantic fidelity can dissociate; a physically nonliteral performance may preserve the target relation better for viewers. | **UNVERIFIED empirical claim** | Derived as a prediction of the model, not yet tested here. | P1: compare literal-copy vs structure-preserving performances with predeclared semantic recovery measures. |
| C8 | Cinema adds an engineered operator \(K:\mathcal W'\to\mathcal F\) that can alter which distinctions are preserved, amplified, suppressed, or newly related. | **Observed/descriptive at the operator level; quantitative form UNVERIFIED** | Framing, focus, sound, montage, temporal ordering, and omission plainly change the presented signal; the stronger “discrimination geometry” interpretation remains model-level. | P2: hold performance fixed and ablate one cinematic operator at a time while measuring recovered distinctions. |
| C9 | Editing can synthesize a presented relational object from fragments that were not one continuous physical event during production. | **Observed/descriptive** | Basic property of montage and temporal assembly. | The stronger claim about which semantic relations viewers infer requires controlled measurement. |
| C10 | Cinema is “self-coupled” in the bounded sense that reality-trained representational machinery generates signals for other reality-trained representational machinery. | **CONJECTURED conceptual characterization** | Composition \(\mathcal W\to\mathcal M_c\to\mathcal W'\to\mathcal F\to\mathcal M_v\). | Weakens if the intermediate representational description fails to improve prediction or decomposition versus a simpler stimulus-response account. |
| C11 | Controlled representation may increase task-relative semantic legibility while decreasing world-state completeness. | **UNVERIFIED empirical claim** | Follows as a candidate consequence of nuisance suppression and salience engineering. | P4: compare naturalistic vs controlled/stylized carriers, measuring target recovery and unrelated factual recall separately. |
| C12 | Viewer history/toolkit \(B_v\) should modulate reconstruction of the same film artifact. | **CONJECTURED; strongly compatible with existing TLICA** | Toolkit-relative discrimination is already part of the architecture; this is its cinema-specific prediction. | P3: predeclared viewer-basis groups with the same artifact; null group differences under adequate power would weaken the application claim. |
| C13 | “Art is perception becoming generative” is a compact slogan for the reversal \(\mathcal W\to\mathcal M\) then \(\mathcal M\to\mathcal W'\), not a claim that perception and generation are literal inverses. | **Interpretive summary** | Formal pipeline in README. | Must be abandoned if used to imply identity, invertibility, or direct mind-to-mind transfer. |
| C14 | The present model is empirically established or novel relative to film theory, cognitive science, acting theory, semiotics, or aesthetics. | **NOT CLAIMED** | No external literature tomography or human experiment was performed in this note. | Requires dedicated prior-art review and controlled probes. |

## Firewalls

The following distinctions are load-bearing:

\[
\text{world} \neq \text{world-model}
\]

\[
\text{representation} \neq \text{quotient unless an equivalence relation is declared}
\]

\[
\text{actor meaning-space} \neq \text{viewer meaning-space}
\]

\[
\text{physical fidelity} \neq \text{semantic fidelity}
\]

\[
\text{semantic legibility} \neq \text{truth}
\]

\[
\text{resonance} \neq \text{epistemic correspondence}
\]

\[
\text{successful reconstruction} \neq \text{identical phenomenal content}
\]

\[
\text{formal analogy} \neq \text{empirical mechanism}
\]

Any future integration into an application paper should preserve these separations explicitly.
