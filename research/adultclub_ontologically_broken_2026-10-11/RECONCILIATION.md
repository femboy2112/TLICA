## Coordinates and categorical hygiene

- \(\kappa\): contact, not social conformity.
- \(\phi\): truth indistinguishability **toolkit-relative** and pathway state; not "how true the boundary feels" as an absolute fact.
- \(\sigma\): source-map adequacy. The observer's inability to reconstruct where a boundary response came from is a possible \(\sigma\) deficit, not established by the response alone.
- \(\rho\): identity correlation, not emotional intensity itself.
- \(\mu\): probe availability; restricted meaningful probes do not imply that a proposition is false.
- Mode B: potential reflective revision, not a mandate that a low-slack person ought to overcome coercion by trying harder.
- "Gluing," "atlas," "transport," "attractor," "curvature," or "sheaf" are **mathematical analogies until typed and operationalized**. No merely verbal allusion proves a mathematical or empirical result.

## Differentiation from existing dossier

Earlier target: "Why can a person subject to authority internalize/reproduce it?"  
New target: "Why can individually plausible local models be bound together by unexamined authority-derived inference rules, particularly when independently constructing the bridge is costly?"

Existing internalization and positive valence can strengthen or result from boundary defaults, but are not necessary for boundary-defaulted inference and must not be assumed as premises. A perfectly rational, non-endorsing survival emulator must remain a genuine rival.

## 2026-10-11 transport correction

The paper's original *boundary-versus-within-compartment* contrast is insufficient. **Authority can appear on a frame-compliant rerouting path**, so authority mediation is not an exclusive signature of a high-affect conflict. The proposed distinctive event is an *experienced challenge to the warrantedness of bridges supporting core commitments*. This may involve authority or not, and may occur within a domain. TLICA \(\rho\) is about self-relevant represented contents, not inherently graph-theoretic edge centrality or emotional intensity. See [ROUTING_AND_ROOT_IMPLICATION.md](ROUTING_AND_ROOT_IMPLICATION.md).

## Authorship and normative boundary

The author opposes coercive demands for assent. The manuscript does **not** infer that every disliked rule is illegitimate, that parents are unreliable, that all distress is indoctrination, or that a person capable of questioning must do so despite catastrophic consequences.

The principle "comply under material necessity while preserving dissent" is central. The social field can causally restrict available actions; observer error is not the only or even the principal cause of unjust conditions.
