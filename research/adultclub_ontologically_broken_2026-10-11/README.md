# The AdultClub™️ Is Ontologically Broken

**Subtitle:** Low slack, truncated models, authority-mediated boundary transport, and the developmental reproduction of apparent necessity

**Origin:** Leah, discussion of 2026-10-11; mathematical/editorial development assisted by ChatGPT (GPT-6).
**Status:** new research-tier paper project, manuscript seed v0.1; **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED** empirical mechanisms.
**Scope:** social epistemology, developmental learning, phenomenology, distributed institutional dynamics.
**Foundation:** existing TLICA foundation untouched; this is a proposed application/composition, not a theorem about society.
**Provenance:** first-person author statements, assistant hypotheses, and checked primary publications are differentiated, not interchangeable.

## Thesis

A society can reproduce its current authority structure without requiring everyone to believe an explicit, coherent ideology. Under constrained practical and cognitive slack, people can maintain accurate but limited local models while relying on inherited, authority-compatible *transition rules* to connect them. Those rules can be learned early, later acquire disproportionate affective weight, and become culturally reproduced. What began as a locally useful response to power may be mistakenly treated as a necessary structure of reality.

The essential new location of the proposed defect is **the glue between compartments, not necessarily the compartments themselves**.

> **A person may have to comply with a constraint without acquiring an epistemic or moral obligation to treat that constraint as legitimate or ontologically necessary.**

The paper title is the author's provocation and research program, not an empirical verdict about all adults or all institutions. "Ontologically broken" means, more precisely, a suspected *illicit promotion of contingent, context-dependent rules into necessities at the interfaces between local models*.

## Core chain (hypothesis, not established law)

1. Material and developmental circumstances may reduce usable *slack*: distinct margins of time, safety, attention, emotional flexibility, resources, and permission.
2. Under those conditions, available inquiry routes and feasible cross-context model building may be truncated, without implying lower intelligence or wholesale false beliefs.
3. Locally effective compartments become connected by inexpensive, culturally supplied defaults.
4. At some boundaries, the default treats authority as the missing warrant: ability to punish is taken as ability to know; ability to enforce is treated as legitimacy.
5. Early learning with high self-relative load may make some boundary transitions highly affective ("hitting a nerve"), with explicit propositional revision and somatic response changing at different rates.
6. Agents acting through these learned rules reproduce parts of an institutional response pattern, which becomes the next generation's learning environment.

Every arrow beyond definitional implications is a separate causal question; no universal deterministic sequence is claimed.
