## H. Consolidated discussion trace (assistant-assisted, not author verbatim)

1. Separate descriptive necessity, individual prudence, normative legitimacy, and social commands to stop questioning.
2. Separate authority's control over consequences from its epistemic authority; aggregate scope may omit local information.
3. Separate involuntary survival compliance from endorsement and peer-enforced internalization.
4. Correct the explanatory locus: local compartments may be accurate while an *unjustified* authority-compatible transition across compartments carries the questionable conclusion.
5. Formulate missing/cheap boundary transport, rather than assuming the whole global worldview is incoherent or that every authority bridge is invalid.
6. Treat childhood punishment as a social observation sometimes mistakenly used to calibrate epistemic correctness.
7. Distinguish learning a boundary, later somatic activation, explicit propositional revision, and behavioral choice.
8. Interpret "hitting a nerve" as a possible high-gain transition and demand controls for genuine injury, danger, and disagreement.
9. Introduce age of consolidation and *self-relative*, not merely objective, load as candidate modulators of later response.
10. Close the individual/social loop: distributed behavior can reproduce institutions and their future developmental environment, without postulating a social group mind.
