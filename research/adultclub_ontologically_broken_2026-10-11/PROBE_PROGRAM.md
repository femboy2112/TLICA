# Discriminating probes — AdultClub boundary-transition hypothesis

**Status:** preregistration-ready *design outline*, not a registered, approved, run, or empirically validated study. No participants recruited, no experiments executed, no measured effect sizes.

## 1. Research question

When observers reroute between contexts, **when does authority function as an ordinary, frame-compliant intermediary, and when does challenging a bridge appear to undermine the observer's root-organizing commitments**? Distinguish these from generic deference, justified expertise, material coercion, cognitive difficulty, and other identity threats.

**2026-10-11 correction:** an authority-mediated cross-domain route is *not* necessarily a threatening conflict. The first factorial below remains a useful preliminary design for inference defaulting, but cannot alone identify an existentially weighted boundary.

## 2. Four-way core contrast (complete factorial before interactions)

Manipulate independently:

1. **Probe type:** within one domain vs. between two domains.
2. **Slack proxy:** unpressured time/safety vs. time-limited *nonpunitive* task. This tests the local experimental manipulation only; it is **not** equivalent to poverty or chronic vulnerability.
3. **Authority cue:** neutral statement vs. status-bearing institutional speaker, with content otherwise matched.
4. **Bridge warrant:** explicit valid cross-context justification vs. unjustified status/necessity inference.

There are \(2^4=16\) design cells. Randomize scenario versions and counterbalance content. Avoid treating unobserved cells as if they were implicitly zero. Predefine the model, outcome units, multiple-testing correction, exclusions, and contrasts before data collection.

### Outcomes

Measure separately: inference-validity choices, explanatory justification, confidence, source attribution, agreement with rule legitimacy, voluntary peer enforcement in a harmless vignette, reaction time, and optional self-reported affect. Do **not** infer a subject's childhood mechanism from any single response or facial expression.

### Predictions and vetoes (declared before looking at results)

- **P1 — boundary specificity:** at matched substantive evidence, unjustified status-based inference should increase more for between-domain than within-domain probes under scarce experimental slack.
- **P2 — bridge repair:** providing an actually valid, inspectable bridge should reduce *unwarranted* authority defaulting; it need not reduce legitimate deference when the expert provides real evidence.
- **P3 — non-ideological dissociation:** some participants will reject authority's claim in private while reporting affective discomfort during boundary challenge. This dissociation is not unique to the present theory; source controls are required.
- **Failure:** equal authority dependence in within- and between-domain cases after matching difficulty, or no boundary-specific benefit from repairing bridge warrant, weakens the new contribution. A global status effect alone supports simpler conformity/deference models.
- **Ambiguous:** tasks differ in perceived stakes, moral framing, or difficulty, or time pressure induces guessing without selective cross-domain behavior.

The four-way mixed term may be considered only after all cells are observed and measurement invariance and lower-order effects are checked. The theory does not *require* that particular four-way interaction; planned simpler boundary × warrant and boundary × slack contrasts are more interpretable.

## 2A. Primary correction: reroute × root implication × authority (2026-10-11)

The original 16-cell matrix crosses within-/between-domain, time pressure, source status, and bridge warrant. **It does not establish the difference between ordinary deference and root-threatening transport**, because the two are not disjoint classes.

Use a second complete \(2^3=8\) design or equivalent predeclared matched contrasts: (a) an independently intelligible alternative route is available vs. the disputed bridge must be confronted; (b) the disputed bridge supports independently elicited core commitments vs. peripheral commitments; and (c) an appropriately identified authority-supplied route vs. a source-free route. Keep route validity distinct from authority prestige. Do not infer "core" from the very emotional response being explained.

Predeclare:

- **Rerouting hypothesis:** authority may be used in safe, frame-compliant resolution, with low affective cost; this is not a counterexample to authority-mediated *transport*, only to the assumption that all authority transport is existentially threatening.
- **Root-implication hypothesis:** perceived pressure to revise central commitments predicts affective/inferential disruption beyond bare domain crossing or authority cues, after controlling relevant current threats.
- **Potential falsifiers:** no route-selection differences under availability manipulation; root relevance contributes no predictive validity under independent measurement; status alone explains all effects; or centrality was only post-hoc inferred from distress.
- **Boundary:** justified intense reactions and calmly welcomed root revisions exist; no necessary reaction is posited.

Detailed formalism, constructed examples and counterexamples: [ROUTING_AND_ROOT_IMPLICATION.md](ROUTING_AND_ROOT_IMPLICATION.md).

## 2B. Polarity/translation extension — do not confuse comprehension with approval

The 2026-10-11 signed-field proposal distinguishes a person's semantic decoding from their self-relative valence and response. Add independent dependent variables for literal comprehension, actual inferential warrant, context-specific valence, attentional capture, route selection, and final behavioral decision. Do not substitute an inferred attitude for comprehension.

**Prediction to test:** with the same prevalidated, externally grounded proposition, context entrance and independently elicited self-relevance may change affective sign or attention without a corresponding change in correct comprehension. A negatively valenced representation may prompt vigilance or analysis rather than always producing rejection.

**Controls:** paraphrase equivalence, pragmatic implication, complexity, novelty, current threat, arousal, status, and demand characteristics; establish semantic equivalence before interpreting any sign mismatch. Hold out independent prompts and participant groups. A route inferred only from an answer is not an independent causal variable.

**Strong rival/failure:** all apparent sign flips are accounted for by changed propositional meaning or explicit disagreement, or route differences disappear once common-referent content is held constant. No literal electric-field, attractor or chaos interpretation is earned from questionnaire contrasts.

See [FIELD_POLARITY_AND_TRANSPORT.md](FIELD_POLARITY_AND_TRANSPORT.md).

## 3. Calibration controls

**Positive controls:** examples with indisputably valid bridges that depend on relevant demonstrated expertise, and examples with manifestly invalid inference where all local propositions are true but the global conclusion is false.

**Negative controls:** two-domain questions with an explicit sound translation, and within-domain reasoning under equal status prompts.

**Null controls:** socially neutral, matched cognitive-load questions that do not invoke sanction or rank.

**Mutation controls:** swap the status label while leaving evidence unchanged; invert the valence of the outcome without altering logical validity; replace a relevant expert with an irrelevant prestigious role; make an institutional rule explicitly optional.

**Independent rivals:** simple fear of punishment, belief that the authority knows more, just-world beliefs, post-hoc effort justification, status-seeking, baseline conformity, and cognitive load causing indiscriminate reliance on heuristics. Measure each or create a contrast that makes rival predictions diverge.

## 4. Developmental boundary-formation study

The proposed age/load law demands a **separate** design. A retrospective adult questionnaire can generate hypotheses, but memories of exact age of "solidification" are weak evidence of childhood causal timing.

Prefer ethically approved longitudinal, multi-informant observation of naturally occurring educational questioning environments; observe supportiveness/correction **without creating punitive exposures**. Treat perceived load, objective consequence, baseline sensitivity, relationship quality, frequency, and time since learning as distinct variables. Where feasible, test later matched authority probes and independently measure capacity, conflict history, and metacognition.

Compare candidates:

- inverse-age monotonic effect;
- developmental windows/nonmonotonic sensitivity;
- relative-load-only model;
- repeated-exposure model;
- current-context/identity-coupling-only model;
- null age and null load effects.

**Strong falsification of the strict author law:** age coefficient is zero or reversed across robust predeclared outcomes after credible measurement; dependence differs by domain or sensitive window; load effect vanishes or reverses. Do not rescue a failed law by redefining "solidification" after inspecting the result.

**Causal boundary:** observational age estimates plus adult distress do not establish that childhood punishment installed an authority-transition rule. Cross-lagged or within-family designs may reduce some confounding but do not eliminate all competing causes.

## 5. Institutional feedback tests

In observational workplaces or educational settings, compare environments with different actual costs of questioning and different norms of explanatory justification. Predefine individual-level and institution-level measurements and time ordering. Test whether changing real rules (sanctions, time, access) and changing explicit bridge practices predict distinct outcomes.

For an agent-based toy model, implement **at least**: reluctant survival emulator, sincere authority internalizer, independent dissenter, valid-expertise follower, and random/noisy policy; vary interactions and environmental enforcement. Stability in the simulated model is **construction-dependent**, not evidence of real-world cultural attraction.

## 6. Ethical, epistemic, and reproducibility conditions

- Participants can decline and withdraw; do not provoke trauma, parental scolding, economic jeopardy, or real sanctions as experimental treatments.
- Define emotionally salient or punitive content narrowly, pilot for safety, anonymize reports, and maintain safeguarding appropriate to the population and jurisdiction.
- No diagnostic interpretation of a particular individual's intensity or family history.
- Blind coders to conditions; preregister primary endpoints and minimum detectable effects; hold out a fresh sample and replicate with independent teams and different domain materials.
- Preserve raw responses, task versions, attrition, transformations, analysis scripts, confidence intervals, null results, and qualitative failure cases.
- Never count ChatGPT agreement, repository coherence, or mathematical notation as empirical triangulation.

## 7. Verdict-changing lamp

The stronger discriminator is **alternative-route availability × independently assessed root-commitment relevance × authority mediation**. A within-/between-domain contrast alone cannot distinguish ordinary authority-rerouting from a felt threat to foundational commitments. If independent root relevance and rerouting produce no separable explanatory value beyond status preference, cognitive difficulty, present threats and general identity-protective reasoning, revise or abandon the paper's distinctive existential-transport mechanism rather than rescuing it through post-hoc emotion labels.
