## 2. The single-ethic problem is not an extra epistemic coordinate

The user called for one underlying normative invariant whose implementation survives transport between scale and context. A moral rule cannot be deduced from "I am I" merely by adjusting \(\varphi\), \(\rho\), or \(\sigma\).

Three layers must not be conflated:
1. **Moral commitment:** e.g. dignity not contingent on wealth/productivity/status.
2. **Epistemic access:** what agents know, how well the referents are represented, and which external tests exist.
3. **Dynamical viability:** whether feasible actions and disturbances allow the commitment to be enacted, or at least violations to be detected and repaired.

Strong moral feeling is not external validation, and unable-to-enact is not falsity of the commitment. See [FORMALISM.md](FORMALISM.md).

## 3. Three failure interfaces, no blanket diagnosis

### Interface A: Source → representation

The person's model \(T(v)\) of another or of an institution may be missing evidence, trained on stereotypes, or have poor source adequacy \(\sigma\). \(\varphi\) may nevertheless be high relative to the current toolkit. Remedies: better sources, discriminating probes, independent records.

### Interface B: Representation → affective/evaluative routing

The model may be accurate yet fail to influence conduct because the referent is routed through "irrelevant stranger," "enemy," "institutional cost," "not my jurisdiction," or a defended self-role. The Bandura connection is **a candidate**. Remedies to test: framing counterfactuals, role reversals, field changes, restitution, explicit accountability.

### Interface C: Evaluation → feasible action

The actor may see accurately and care sincerely but lack safe feasible actions, money, time, access, physical protection, or coordination. Remedy: change constraints and protective infrastructure, not merely demand more empathy or blame the actor for inaction.

**These interfaces yield similar surface behavior but distinct mechanisms. No observation of conformity or coldness uniquely identifies one.** In particular, an actor who refrains from costly resistance may be afraid, pragmatically adapting, endorsing the authority, honestly uncertain, or lacking a valid legal route. That cannot be inferred from one act.

## 4. AdultClub's specific mechanism and today's extension

Existing dossier:
\[
\text{low slack}\to\text{local model use}\to
\text{authority-compatible boundary shortcut}\to
\text{possible promotion to necessity}.
\]

Today's extension adds two coupled loops, neither established as a universal empirical phenomenon.

**Normalization loop:**
\[
\text{burden imposed}\to\text{survival accommodation}
\to\text{new benchmark}\to\text{moral expectation}
\to\text{possible intensified burden}.
\]

**Authority/credibility loop:**
\[
\text{resource power}\to\text{gatekeeper access}
\to\text{institutional endorsement}\to
\text{more resource/access power}.
\]

The loops could reinforce each other: the successful become trusted by virtue of visible success, while the less powerful are evaluated primarily against compliance requirements. The existence of real counterexamples and competing explanations is acknowledged.

### Important distinction

The claim that *an institutional system can implement an objectionable rule while no participant fully models the total consequences* fits distributed realization and causal networks. It does **not** establish that the institution possesses consciousness, intent, memory, or culpability exactly as a person does.

## 5. Referent routing and Bandura without premature merger

Bandura-style mechanisms discussed: moral justification, euphemistic labeling, displacement or diffusion of responsibility, minimizing effects, victim-blaming, and dehumanization.

Potential TLICA operational mapping:
- *model-thinning*—the subject of concern is misrepresented (source/representation issue);
- *routing attenuation*—the subject is represented but does not land as a constraint on self-action;
- *professional-duty bypass*—the subject is represented and felt, but role labels alter the explicit action criterion;
- *incentive dominance*—adequate modeling and routing coexist with unacceptable but materially motivated conduct.

Moral disengagement may include any mix. The bridge earns warrant only if it predicts **which** mechanism is operative on independent data. No psychopathy, personality pathology, clinical feature, political affiliation, or moral rank is assigned to the author or named public persons here.

## 6. Downstream dominoes as an institution-level probe

Existing institutional response models are task-relative. Let a task class ask "what action can this hierarchy cause?" It may yield a large authority surface. Let a second ask "which records, warnings, and responsibilities are recognized when harms occur?" This may produce a smaller attributed surface.

Candidate discrepancy:
\[
\text{scope of authorized interventions}
\quad\not\subseteq\quad
\text{scope of effective oversight/remedy}.
\]

This is **not** a formal theorem about any actual company or government. It points to an organizational audit: trace real communication paths, obligations, warnings, response times, ignored escalation channels, and control levers. An institutional failure is not reducible to a mere mismatch of metaphors.

## 7. The population-selection extension must not become moral Darwinism

The Price decomposition for a group metric is a mathematical identity; converting population growth/survival into positive *moral fitness* is an additional unlicensed premise.

The author's catastrophe scenario warns that average observed morality can improve because those who suffered disappear. This can be treated as a **selection and measurement hazard** within the proposed existential-habitability application, not as a claim that extinction is ethically salutary.

Keep observed/selected metrics jointly with total exposure, mortality, exclusion, forced migration, resource distribution, and per-agent welfare; else a regime can obtain a higher score by denying membership or removing the least fortunate.

## 8. Where the word "gauge" stops

The session used "gauge bullshit" for rhetorically shifting institutional descriptions to evade responsibility. That is an evocative phrase. It is **not** a demonstration of a mathematically declared gauge group or gauge-invariant social observable.

To earn formal gauge language, we would need:
- the space of equivalent institutional representations;
- transformations between representations;
- a declared group/groupoid action with identity/composition laws;
- an operational invariant under those changes;
- evidence the chosen transformations preserve the claimed object.

Until then, use **frame substitution**, **responsibility-routing shift**, **role-based segmentation**, or **relabeling** rather than claiming a physical gauge theory.

Likewise, "entropy" is not a physical societal quantity until states, microstates, measure, closure, and conservation/monotonicity assumptions are declared.

## 9. A candidate research-level invariant and its repair protocol

One possible *normative* clause, not mandated by TLICA:

> Each being's dignity remains morally relevant independent of economic utility, reputation, rank, prior suffering, ideological identification, or perfection; restrictions on harmful behavior must themselves be justified through appropriate evidence, necessity, and proportionality.

This clause is not an objective numerical theorem, and conflicts between obligations may be underdetermined. A practical implementation should record:
- what was believed and on which sources (\(\sigma\));
- how much was actually contact-observed (\(\kappa\));
- what was constructibly testable and genuinely tested (\(\mu\), tool closure);
- what felt settled under current probes (\(\varphi\));
- what self/identity commitments made re-evaluation difficult (\(\rho\));
- whether an alternative path was both known and materially feasible;
- what remediation followed a known failure.

**Do not collapse that vector into a moral score.**

## 10. Stable continuation priorities

1. Create an explicitly typed, minimal model demonstrating that two locally compliant institutional subsystems can jointly cause an undesirable outcome (synthetic; no unwarranted social validation claim).
2. Distinguish policy-role routing from loss of harm comprehension in a preregistered vignette study or public case-data analysis.
3. Probe documented positive cases where institutions kept warnings alive and actually prevented escalation; otherwise all examples are selected failures.
4. Build a non-exclusionary habitability metric sensitive to distribution, death, social exit, rule navigability, and durable dignity; compare to established capability/welfare approaches.
5. Recover real cooperative bootstraps where first-mover costs were tolerable; examine failed near-matches rather than fitting only successes.
6. Use historical source-family ablations and strong negative controls before attributing motive or blame to organizations or individuals.

**Boundary:** This entire package belongs in research. Neither a foundation patch nor an application paper should be produced merely because the arguments are rhetorically persuasive.
