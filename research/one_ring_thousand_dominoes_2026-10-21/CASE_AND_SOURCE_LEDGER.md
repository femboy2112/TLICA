# Case and source ledger — evidence classes and provenance debt

**Purpose:** preserve the complete *case inventory* from the 2026-10-21 dialogue, with durable starting links and explicit evidentiary limits. This is not an encyclopedia, a legal opinion, an accusation that all named persons acted in concert, or a claim that every source mentioned by the assistant was independently audited.

**Method:** Source families are distinguished: (P) primary historical/legal/institutional record, (R) peer-reviewed research, (J) accountable journalism, (A) ancient/literary narrative, (M) satire or metaphor, (U) unverified claim / unexamined provenance. Some primary public URLs below were freshly located during preparation on 2026-10-21; the rest are research leads to recheck. A reported fact from an adversarial court filing is not equivalent to an adjudicated finding; a settlement is not a blanket factual admission.

### Epstein research discriminators

Event-level questions to pursue without speculative insinuation: Which warning was received by whom on what date? What was the recipient's actual authority to act? Which protective or profitable relationship did non-intervention preserve? Was the response consistent with contemporaneous institutional policies? What direct archival artifacts or audited records prove the particular edge?

Avoid treating "his connections enabled access" as a complete mechanism without determining which interactions actually conferred access and whether warnings were known at those times.

## G. Source-map weaknesses and follow-up

1. **Source families:** dozens of assistant-provided transient web citations existed in chat. This archive cannot establish that those results were independent or properly interpreted.
2. **Timing:** legislation, humanitarian status, monastery policies, and economic statistics are time-dependent. Do not transport 2026 facts to another time without date filtering.
3. **Attribution:** direct author statements remain quotations even when inaccurate; polished assistant generalizations are not author propositions.
4. **Causal claims:** documented temporal adjacency/proximity never suffices for planning, guilt, or conspiracy. Prove *who did what*, not only *who met whom*.
5. **Negative controls:** look for equally connected people who did *not* become abusers, organizations that received and acted on comparable warnings, and policies where donors failed to obtain desired outcomes.
6. **Privacy:** do not publish raw personal/case material without separate informed permission; this repository is public.
