## B. Probe 1: moral referent routing × institutional language

**Goal:** distinguish preserved knowledge of harm with altered operative moral judgment from misinformation or legitimately different obligations.

**Population:** consenting adults in a low-stakes, ethically reviewed vignette study; no clinical labeling or shaming.

**2×2×2 design (every cell required):**
- Role description: person as individual vs person as institutional agent.
- Source framing: concrete human consequence vs euphemistic administrative consequence.
- Duty clarity: genuine independently described competing duty vs no duty conflict.

**Hold constant:** quantitative harm, controllable options, knowledge of likely outcome, benefits to evaluator.

**Before measuring judgment:** quiz to verify accurate comprehension of harm facts; separately ask factual attribution, perceived duty, emotional response, preferred action, and chosen justification. Participants failing comprehension must not be used to infer intact-model routing.

**Rivals' predictions:**
- Routing account: role/euphemism shifts evaluative weighting even among equally competent factual comprehenders, with a stronger effect when duty is absent.
- Information account: effect disappears after factual-comprehension and ambiguity controls.
- Duty account: effect appears mainly with a defensible duty conflict, and follows explicit duty justification.
- Demand/identity account: effects disappear under anonymous, norm-neutral wording or reverse when political/identity cue is swapped.

**Predeclared outcomes:**
- *Pass for limited framing contribution:* substantive role/euphemism effect remains under comprehension, duty, and anonymity controls in holdout stimuli.
- *Fail:* no such effect with adequate power, or all of it is mediated by factual misunderstanding.
- *Ambiguous:* measurement unreliable, sample underpowered, role manipulation also changes perceived real-world obligations, or participants suspect study purpose.

**Controls:** positive (known unambiguous wrongdoing framed clearly), negative (beneficent act with same formal role), null (administrative change with zero harm), mutation (swap victim/agent names with effect expected not to depend on label), holdout vignette domain. Pre-register primary outcome and effect-size threshold.

**Safety:** do not expose participants to graphic accounts of actual victims. Use fictional, ordinary incidents.

## C. Probe 2: constraint reification versus sober survival modeling

**Goal:** test whether people who anticipate punitive responses infer the policy is morally legitimate or ontologically inevitable.

**Manipulations:** cost of challenge (low/high); endorsement by authority (present/absent); explicit availability of a genuine alternative (yes/no); domain (work, club, platform rules).

**Measures:** perceived real-world cost, perceived available control, moral evaluation of the rule, willingness to comply, confidence that it could change under altered institutions.

**Rival predictions:** 
- Reification hypothesis: authority exposure shifts *legitimacy or necessity* judgments beyond its effect on expected consequences.
- Pure prudence: compliance changes under cost pressure while legitimacy remains independent.
- Rational expertise: effects occur only where provided role-holder demonstrably possesses task knowledge.

**Controls:** authority statement correct/incorrect, policy benevolent/exploitative, alternative available/not, comprehension and skepticism checks; holdout participants and new contexts.

**Result boundary:** do not infer any participant's self-worth or integrity from a single choice under threat.

## D. Probe 3: first-domino audit of real historical institutions

**Object:** records of how a notice, complaint, intelligence warning, or safety report moved among departments.

**Design:** reconstruct *event-by-event*, with time stamps, sender/recipient, source family, evidentiary sufficiency, permitted actions, public accountability, and downstream preventable harm. Compare:
1. a well-documented case of failure, such as selected Epstein bank/DOJ oversight periods or 9/11 agency information sharing;
2. a comparable case with warnings and **successful** intervention;
3. a negative-control case with plausible warning but no later disaster.

**Establish rival mechanisms:**
- missing information;
- access without usable authority;
- incentives to ignore;
- genuinely ambiguous evidence;
- failures of technical communication;
- ex post blame shifting with no preexisting duty;
- grossly inadequate process design.

**Required measurement:** identify what an actor *actually knew at the time*. Later newspaper synthesis cannot be back-projected into prior individual cognition.

**Success:** one prospective role/process feature predicts ignored warnings in held-out cases beyond ordinary information volume and warning quality.
**Failure:** supposed failure pattern dissolves when contemporaneous knowledge, role powers, and benign comparison cases are included.
**Ambiguous:** records sealed, missing, selective, or incomparable.

**Source provenance:** normalize government records, court documents, journalism, memoirs, and later commentary into separate families. Run leave-one-family-out checks. Same document quoted by five journalists counts as **one root source**, not five independent confirmations.

## E. Probe 4: viable bootstrap threshold for cooperation

**Baseline:** operationally define a collective problem (local mutual-aid access, volunteer coordination, worker protection, open-source maintainer capacity) without exposing anyone to employment retaliation.

**Model:** measured budget/slack $b_i$; participation costs $c_i$; minimum coalition $k$; actual commitments and protection offered. Distinguish first-step feasibility from ex post benefits.

**Rivals:** genuine PD, assurance, free-rider, information-only bottleneck, institutional-prohibition boundary.

**Discriminating intervention:** neutral public information alone versus credible small first-step downside insurance versus group-threshold assurance. Prefer retrospective natural experiments or consent-based lab games where real-world risk is trivial.

**Pass:** specified modification shifts formation probability or costs, across new cohorts and a holdout task.
**Fail:** payoff estimates don't match the claimed game, or no shift under correctly implemented manipulation.
**Ambiguous:** external constraints change simultaneously or group composition differs.

**Important:** never pressure a financially vulnerable person to take a real-world employment, housing, legal, or safety risk in the name of a game-theory experiment.

## F. Probe 5: existential habitability without survivor selection

**Unit:** a newly situated person (or fictional entrant scenario), not a deservingness rank.
**Vector:** housing/food viability, rule discoverability, available legal advice, safety, ability to exit harmful contracts, error forgiveness, noncoercive opportunities, and severe-harm tail risks.

**Comparison:** predeclare consistent groups and cohort boundaries; record mortality, attrition, migration, exclusion, and nonresponse as outcomes, not as zero-cost removal.

**Rivals:** one scalar "average happiness" metric; income-based welfare; a capabilities approach; severity-weighted avoidable harm.

**Fail the model** if it can reward a society merely for losing its most vulnerable members. Require separate mean, bottom-decile, disaster-rate, and missing-population terms with a transparent normative rationale.

**Other falsifier:** if the proposed no-manual construct adds no predictive discriminatory value beyond established measures of material poverty, legal access, and capability, report redundancy—not novelty.

## G. Probe 6: cross-context One Ring consistency

Choose narrow contexts $U_1,U_2$, an actually stated moral invariant $I$, state observation maps, and two policies. Determine when:
$$
C_A\circ\pi_1\ \overset{?}{\approx}\ \pi_2\circ C_X.
$$

**Positive controls:** trivial shared policy, same case expressed in different vocabulary, explicit justified role exception preserving dignity.
**Negative controls:** silent change in personhood/value based on employment rank; same harm described as acceptable when profitable and unacceptable otherwise.
**Mutation controls:** swap role labels without changing outcome, invert evaluative ownership, alter one assumption at a time.
**Ambiguity controls:** verify that fine/coarse action spaces and feasibility maps are well-typed.

**Do not** claim a nontrivial sheaf, gauge field, cocycle, physical entropy, or quantum-like phase without constructing the actual mathematical object.
