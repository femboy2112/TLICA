## Executive synthesis — ten linked theses, not ten established facts

1. **Consent is not inferred from mere survival.** A person's inability to exit an imposed arrangement cannot, without additional premises, establish agreement with that arrangement.
2. **Accommodation does not establish legitimacy.** A prudent response to threats, scarcity, or hierarchy does not imply that the threatening arrangement is fair, necessary, or ontologically fundamental.
3. **The first domino is not the causal boundary.** Remote contributions, distributed oversight, and cumulative interactions are morally relevant; responsibility nevertheless remains conditional on knowledge, control, foreseeability, choice, and normative duties.
4. **Institutions can be coherent locally and fail at their interfaces.** Distributed role systems can preserve authority while fragmenting accountability or blocking valid ethical objections at role boundaries.
5. **Preserving a single ethic is a dynamical viability problem.** A cross-context moral principle is cheap to state; maintaining it under costs, reflex, resource constraints, and hostile incentives is hard.
6. **Collective action is itself a collective-action problem.** Building institutions that make cooperation cheap often requires prior cooperation; distinguishing prisoner's dilemmas from assurance games is essential.
7. **Referent routing matters independently of factual recognition.** One can adequately model another's suffering while shifting its operative relevance away from one's moral self-evaluation; this connection to moral disengagement is conjectural.
8. **Population-level progress can be a selection artifact.** Surviving-community averages may improve through extinction, exclusion, or migration rather than within-community moral improvement. Survival selects neither truth nor virtue automatically.
9. **Moral harm must not be reduced to a GDP or liability ledger.** Income, procedural compliance, short-term profit, and legal wins do not independently establish the legitimacy of a social arrangement.
10. **The ethical design target is existential habitability.** How forgiving, intelligible, safe, and autonomy-preserving is a world for a newly situated, informationally disadvantaged being who did not choose their initial conditions?

## The author's recurring image: the One Ring

The author rejects ad hoc workplace, artistic, religious, economic, and civic moralities glued together by weak excuses. The aim is one stable normative commitment with context-dependent *implementations*, not context-dependent worth. Quantum/classical scale-consistency was used as an analogy, **not** as proof of an ethical invariant or as a physics theory of society. Formal transport must declare the objects and maps before importing claims.

Candidate invariant (normative proposal):

> No being's basic dignity should depend on productivity, rank, conformity, wealth, creed, or flawless behavior; agency and accountability still matter, including accountability for avoidable harms and efforts to repair them.

This is **not deductively derivable from the cogito alone**. It is an ethical commitment to examine, strengthen, and expose to conflicts. It protects even ideological opponents' humanity without granting anyone unrestricted permission to persecute or destroy others.
