# Claim ledger — do not promote metaphors to results

**Evidence vocabulary:**  
**DISCLOSED** = a valid deduction or calibrated decisive result within declared assumptions, not universal truth.  
**CORROBORATED** = sufficiently independent evidence/routes survived provenance, residual and holdout scrutiny within a stated scope.  
**OBSERVED** = directly present in this conversation or a specific verified record, with scope stated.  
**CONJECTURED** = plausible proposed mechanism with unpaid empirical truth debt.  
**UNVERIFIED** = no adequate relevant probe has been run.  
**DARK** = no current discriminator/source can reach the claim; specify needed lamp.  
**REFUTED / CORRECTED** = a counterexample or check defeats a stronger claim; preserve the corrected version.

## Higher-level source and status hazards

**Fit contamination:** the entire conversation shaped these proposed models. Its examples are training/fit provenance, not out-of-sample tests.

**One narrator, multiple citations:** the original assistant's synthesis of web results is a single intermediary interpretation, not many independent fact witnesses. To claim corroboration, reconstruct actual source independence.

**Policy vs morality:** if a statute authorizes an act, that supplies a legal description—not a direct proof of ethical legitimacy.

**Status of empathy/routing claims:** psychological mechanisms are not observable from media caricatures, labels such as "Uday-Brain", or a politician's job title. The label is metaphorical data.

**Status of global claims:** neither a rate of civilizational improvement nor monotone social entropy nor "all legal systems are captured" was measured here.
