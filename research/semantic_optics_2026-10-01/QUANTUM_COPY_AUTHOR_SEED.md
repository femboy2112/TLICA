## Research discipline

The package developed from this seed separates four claims:

1. a **copy/transport groupoid** can be formalized exactly;
2. recursive “copies of itself acted on by itself” have established mathematics in self-similar group actions and wreath recursion;
3. actual **quantum entanglement requires monoidal/tensor and superposition structure** beyond Yoneda transport;
4. projective quantum symmetries permit transport composition up to phase, governed by a 2-cocycle.

No claim is made that this is the fundamental ontology of quantum mechanics.
