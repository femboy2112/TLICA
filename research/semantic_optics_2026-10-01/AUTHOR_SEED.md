The research package in this directory is a formal reconstruction and adversarial pressure test of these observations.

Three corrections are deliberately imposed:

1. “Yoneda equivalent” is not treated as a standard theorem statement until two categories and a comparison functor are defined.
2. “Categorical Optics + TLICA deviation” is not treated as literal addition unless the relevant hom/fiber objects carry additive structure.
3. The work does not assume that formal similarity establishes empirical truth, novelty, or foundation-level necessity.
