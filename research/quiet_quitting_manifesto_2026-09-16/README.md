## Version interpretation

v1.4 is the literary and moral parent explicitly preferred over the v2.0 rewrite. Final v1.5 and its patches return to that parent while retaining useful factual and conceptual repairs. **v2.0 is preserved as a rejected editorial direction, not promoted by its larger version number.** The separate preliminary v1.5 that overcorrected toward worker/employer symmetry is also retained as a rejected working draft.

v1.5.3 remains an identifiable checkpoint. v1.5.4 subsequently adds a narrow autobiographical disclosure about the author's route to the ethic. “Latest recovered revision” does not mean a newly approved canonical release. The recovered release documents retain their historical pending-clearance status. Leah's current request authorizes this branch archive for future integration; it does not convert old draft outreach into messages to send or establish completion of old publication gates.

## The preserved distinction

The manuscript's object is *individual quiet quitting proper*: competent, safe, honest, sustainable performance of the defensible job while withdrawing unowned excess after a material, persistent mismatch and unavailable or exhausted reasonable correction. The ordinary public label covers other acts and motives. Ordinary optional boundaries, collective pressure, and outright refusal are distinct questions.

> We withdraw the subsidy, not the integrity.

The historical handoff also says the public manifesto should stand on its own rather than require TLICA knowledge. This dossier honors that editorial scope. The retrospective theory bridge is separate from the preserved manuscripts.

## Archive layout

- `originals/` retains delivered Markdown, TeX, PDF, HTML, source and audit documents, public companion material, the Ford brief, New Labor Forum adaptation, and complete ZIP packages. Extracted package members share this folder so their historical relative links have their companion files.
- `working/` retains distinct intermediate drafts, source and hostile audits, publication strategy, and rendering templates recovered from the earlier working session.
- `history/` records source prompts, retrieved excerpts, qualified quotations, contextual summaries, and unresolved conversation gaps.
- `provenance/` records source identity, dates, duplication and package membership; file-creation dates are not substituted for chat timestamps.
- `checks/` contains the repeatable preservation check and its recorded results.

The foundation, current application list, and existing manuscripts are unchanged. This is a source-bearing research dossier, not a newly registered application paper or evidence that the workplace mechanisms have been empirically validated.
