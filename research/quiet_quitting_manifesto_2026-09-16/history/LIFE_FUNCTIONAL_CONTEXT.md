## What the assistant proposal reportedly contained

The following is a summary of retrieved prior-assistant context, **not a verbatim reconstruction of its full reply**. Search `pc_fifth` reports the proposed title *The Life-Functional of Labor: Quiet Quitting as Global Feasibility Restoration*, and describes the central connection as preventing a local workplace optimizer from consuming the worker's global life-functional as an unpriced input. It connects competent, safe, honest baseline work with withdrawing discretionary, uncompensated, unsustainable or identity-consuming excess after reasonable repair pathways close.

That retrieval also returned these quoted assistant formulations, with source timestamp reported as 2026-08-27T01:04:58Z:

- “Hidden heroics are borrowed viability.”
- “Ethical de-buffering is institutional truth-telling and personal re-buffering.”
- “Quiet quitting proper restores global feasibility without abandoning local integrity.”

These phrases preserve a research direction: short-term local output can be supported by consuming a person's longer-horizon capacity, and a boundary can restore room for a wider life. They do not prove that an aggregate objective exists, has been measured, or is improved by every boundary. A workplace objective, a worker's values, operational capacity, attention, finances and lifetime welfare cannot be combined merely by naming one functional.
