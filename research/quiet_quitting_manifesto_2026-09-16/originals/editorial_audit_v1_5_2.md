## Governing definition and power asymmetry

The definition states the manuscript's exact object without turning a worker's boundary into an employer permission process:

- **Labor-refusal category boundary:** quiet quitting proper retains the defensible baseline and withdraws only unowned excess. Whether outright refusal is ethical is expressly left as a separate question (line 13). This is scope, not condemnation, a legal claim, or an employer permission rule; Section 9 still treats strikes, boycotts, slowdowns, and coordinated work-to-rule as a distinct use of power (lines 642, 674–678).
- **Definition:** quiet quitting proper is competent performance of paid and reasonably expected work while ceasing discretionary, uncompensated, unsustainable, or identity-consuming excess absorbed into the institution's operating model (lines 29–31).
- **One-way structural condition:** an ethical quiet quitter proper implies structural or managerial expectations above the defensible job baseline (lines 33–39). The manuscript does not assert the converse or label every worker facing a bad workplace a quiet quitter.
- **Necessary-not-sufficient guard:** excess above the defensible baseline is necessary but does not alone settle the ethical classification; pathway, integrity, proportionality, and action-category conditions distinguish quiet quitting proper from ordinary boundary-setting, misconduct, and collective pressure (line 41; lines 616–627).
- **No permission gate:** the baseline is not whatever an employer can compel, and no worker must earn permission to stop a genuinely voluntary gift (lines 39–49). Declining a genuinely optional extra may be wholly ethical ordinary boundary-setting without being “quiet quitting proper” (line 51).
- **Three mismatch routes:** the gap can exist in the codified role, the lived role, or operating conditions that make competent sustainable performance impossible. Nominal extras enforced through pressure, evaluation, or discipline are not optional in practice (line 51).
- **Correction versus assimilation:** correction clarifies, reconciles, prioritizes, resources, or negotiates duties the worker can actually reject; assimilation makes the worker absorb the defect as identity and duty (lines 53–55).
- **Healthy-workplace control case:** in a properly resourced workplace, the same competent sustainable conduct is ordinary or average work. The phenomenon becomes visible only where an institution had mistaken an unsustainable subsidy for job capacity (lines 57–59).

The responsibilities remain deliberately non-mirrored. The worker exercises limited, local, economically constrained power and owns chosen acts within what they can reasonably know and control; the employer controls the action-field and therefore bears the larger affirmative repair and system-foresight duty (lines 15–23, 422, 572–590, 612, 627). Section 8 is expressly a worker's self-audit, not an employer form or fiction of equal power; repair attempts remain qualified by safety, usefulness, retaliation, futility, channel viability, and assimilation-only pseudo-correction (lines 616–627). Managers are instructed to repair or genuinely renegotiate contradictions, role expansion, and impossible operating conditions, while retaining proportionate authority over actual misconduct (lines 769–771).

**Power-asymmetry verdict: PASS.** No load-bearing false symmetry, worker proof burden, employer permission gate, or accidental condemnation of ethical ordinary boundary-setting remains.
