## Governing frame

- Reframed the work as a **case-anchored normative manifesto**: it begins in Leah's lived experience and offers a conditional generalization rather than claiming to represent every quiet quitter or workplace.
- Preserved the thesis that reasonable, safe, proportionate repair ordinarily precedes deliberate withdrawal of an established hidden subsidy.
- Added the distinction between ordinary prospective boundary-setting and *quiet quitting proper*. A worker does not need to earn permission to stop a genuinely voluntary gift.
- Moved responsibility toward the level that controls resources and decision rights: **Humanness is symmetric. Power is not.**
- Clarified that the framework governs individual quiet quitting proper and does not purport to adjudicate lawful collective action.

## Definitions tightened

- **Quiet quitting proper:** retained as a stipulative term; added that "quiet" means withdrawal of hidden excess, not secrecy.
- **Core role:** now inferred from lawful agreement, written duties, professional standards, compensation, reasonable instructions, and settled mutual practice, bounded by safety, feasibility, and sustainability.
- **Pathway closure:** narrowed to repeated material evidence such as nonresponse, retaliation, refusal to rank demonstrated incompatibilities, or an inert remedy. A good-faith adverse answer is not closure by itself.
- **Ethical de-buffering:** retained as the central mechanism but made conditional on competent work, safety, honesty, proportionality, appropriate notice/handoff, and no manufactured deficit.
- **Counterfeiting capacity:** retained with an intent qualifier; it names a false system-level appearance that may arise without anyone intending deception.

## Repair-first section rebuilt

- Replaced a universal sequence with duties scaled to safety, information, authority, reliance, urgency, and worker capacity.
- Made clear that truthful notice can satisfy the worker's duty even when they cannot diagnose or repair the system.
- Expanded the first-line channel to include trusted coworkers, union representatives, safety officers, formal systems, or protected external routes.
- Added an explicit bypass for retaliatory, unsafe, destructive, or obviously futile channels.
- Required only lawful, safe, privacy-respecting documentation and described a source-map as preserving causal hypotheses, not proving one cause.
- Added revisability: material repair warrants reassessment but does not entitle the employer to restored heroics.

## Ethical de-buffering corrected

- Retained the schematic `Y = F(D,N,E,H)` because it isolates the morally relevant hidden input, but labeled it incomplete and non-empirical.
- Added omitted rivals: skill mix, management, process design, task composition, learning, absenteeism, capital, and external shocks.
- Replaced the claim that consequences are an "honest measurement of under-capacity" with the narrower conclusion that they are evidence of dependence on the withdrawn effort.
- Separated acceptable visibility of noncritical delay/backlog from foreseeable serious harm, which now triggers slow/stop/refuse/transfer/shutdown/escalation duties.
- Preserved unchanged: **The worker's body is not the slack variable in a staffing model.**

## The fuck-it factor protected and repaired

- **The name remains unchanged.** No euphemism was substituted.
- Defined it as Leah's phenomenological coinage for a conscious transition into triage under perceived constraint closure.
- Added explicit status limits: not an established human-factors construct, validated scale, diagnosis, legal defense, or excuse.
- Distinguished objective closure from the action set the person can perceive within available time, information, equipment, and authority.
- Added stop, slow, refuse, transfer, escalation, and safe shutdown to the action set.
- Separated unintentional error, intentional procedural departure, and mixed cascade.
- Deleted `P(M_t | F_t=1) > P(M_t | F_t=0)`. The comparison was confounded, partly circular, and contradicted by the possibility that deliberate triage reduces total harm.
- Deleted the scalar threshold recurrence. Replaced it with conditional evidence about routinization, normalization, learning, debriefing, recovery, and redesign.
- Labeled the long-horizon/short-horizon relationship as Leah's conceptual synthesis rather than an established empirical duality.
- Anchored managerial reach to control of shared constraints rather than diagnosing identical internal phenomenology.

## Exit, grind, and American Dream claims narrowed

- Removed the binary implication that viable exit is always empty; described liveness as a continuum shaped by transition costs and worker-specific risk.
- Calibrated quiet quitting as a possible bridge for some workers and named counter-risks including discipline, dismissal, and lost advancement.
- Added current evidence on household financial fragility, employer-linked health-insurance job lock, and schedule instability, with explicit limits.
- Preserved the conditional defense of hard work and cut the pseudo-formal grind equations.
- Attributed **identity wage** to Alison R. Buck and distinguished authentic pride from identity rhetoric substituted for agreed reciprocity.
- Recast the American Dream as one influential, plural, unequally accessible American work narrative rather than one universal social contract.
- Restricted "all input and no output" to *additional exceptional sacrifice here* and *believable future output*, preserving present wages and portable returns as real.
