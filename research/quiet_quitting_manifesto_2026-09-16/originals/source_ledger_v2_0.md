# Source Ledger for The Ethical Quiet Quitting Manifesto v2.0

**Canonical text audited:** output/ethical_quiet_quitting_manifesto_v2_0.md  
**Comparison text:** ethical_quiet_quitting_manifesto_v1_4.md  
**Audit date:** August 16, 2026  
**Purpose:** identify what each material empirical, historical, theoretical, normative, rhetorical, and coined claim can properly bear.

This ledger is not a second bibliography. It is a claim-to-source map. A source is credited only for what it actually establishes. An adjacent literature is not treated as validation of Leah’s coined constructs.

## Status key

- **VERIFIED:** the linked primary, official, or peer-reviewed source supports the final wording at approximately its stated level.
- **VERIFIED WITH LIMIT:** the source supports a narrower or context-bound claim and the final text states the boundary.
- **PARTIAL / SOURCE DEBT:** part of the claim is supported, but an additional source or narrower wording is still advisable.
- **CONJECTURED SYNTHESIS:** Leah’s reasoned model; adjacent evidence exists, but the complete claim has not been directly tested.
- **LIVE UNKNOWN:** no direct supporting evidence was identified in the verified source set.
- **CORRECTION REQUIRED:** a factual or bibliographic defect remains.
- **RESOLVED REMOVAL:** an overclaim in v1.4 was removed from v2.0.
- **NORMATIVE, RHETORIC, COINAGE, or AUTHOR ATTESTATION:** not an empirical generalization; it must instead be defensible, clearly owned, and not masquerade as a research finding.

## Immediate corrections and live source decisions

1. **Buck citation correction completed.** The canonical manifesto and article draft now give the verified record: Alison R. Buck, “The Price of Consent: Identity Wages in the Games Industry,” *Journal of Contemporary Ethnography* 51, no. 6 (2022): 868–894, [https://doi.org/10.1177/08912416221085558](https://doi.org/10.1177/08912416221085558). The earlier draft's *Gender & Society* metadata was wrong and has been removed.
2. **The American Dream history is responsibly narrowed but not fully sourced as history.** The Library of Congress page verifies the Adams passage and itself presents the Dream as changing and contested. It is an official educational source, not a complete historiography of unequal access. For an academic venue, add a scholarly historical source for the sentence that access “has never been equal,” or let that sentence stand as a plainly general historical qualification rather than a sourced result.
3. **The “bridge” mechanism remains Leah’s hypothesis.** Current financial-fragility, health-insurance, and schedule evidence shows why exit may be constrained. It does not show that quiet quitting restores exit capacity or improves eventual mobility.
4. **The claimed internal fuck-it-factor transition remains unvalidated.** The surrounding risk ecology is sourced. Distinctness, prevalence, incremental predictive power, and causal effect remain live empirical questions.
5. **“Repetition can also improve expertise and safer triage” is a live unknown in this ledger.** It is a sensible counterpossibility, but the verified sources do not directly establish it. Keep it as a possibility or add task-specific expertise research.
6. **Managerial reach is supportable; identical inner phenomenology is not.** Safety-climate intervention evidence supports the effect of supervisory priorities at subunit level. Scaling should remain anchored to control of shared constraints.
7. **The AI-development disclosure requires Leah’s personal confirmation.** The current disclosure truthfully uses future tense for Leah's still-pending complete review, independent original-source check, approval, and acceptance of responsibility. Do not convert those acts to past tense until Leah performs and confirms them.
