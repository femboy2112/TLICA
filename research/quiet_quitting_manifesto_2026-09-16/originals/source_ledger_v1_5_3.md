## Claim-state key

- **NORMATIVE / DEFINITIONAL:** Leah's ethical position or stipulated meaning; it does not become empirical merely because it is written formally.
- **TAXONOMIC / SCOPE:** a boundary on how the manuscript uses a label or limits its claim; illustrative categories are not a prevalence estimate.
- **OBSERVED:** grounded in Leah's stated workplace experience, with that case scope only.
- **SOURCED:** supported by the cited evidence within the limits stated here.
- **SYNTHESIS:** Leah's interpretation connecting case experience, ethics, and adjacent research; not directly established by a source.
- **PROPOSED:** an explicit model, hypothesis, mechanism, or extension awaiting direct validation.
- **SCHEMATIC / RHETORICAL:** notation or analogy used to clarify reasoning, not a fitted measure, historical equivalence, or population estimate.
