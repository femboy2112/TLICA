### Argument and voice

- **American Dream genuinely leads.** The headline and opening establish one influential American input/output story before quiet quitting is introduced. The essay does not claim that the Dream has one definition, ever guaranteed success, or offered equal access.
- **The stipulated category remains narrow.** Quiet quitting proper retains competent, safe, honest baseline work and withdraws unowned excess. Outright refusal and collective action remain separate ethical objects.
- **Minor defects remain below threshold.** A hard week, minor defect, or isolated repairable mistake is expressly insufficient. Material, persistent baseline mismatch and failed or unavailable meaningful correction remain minimum conditions rather than automatic permission.
- **Repair is not an employer gate.** Safe, useful repair serves truth-testing and causal legibility. The essay rejects endless chances, danger, retaliation, futility, and assimilation masquerading as correction.
- **The personal disclosure remains bounded.** Earlier broad-label conduct is not retroactively certified. Felt structural validity is a clue rather than a verdict; a real structural mismatch can still be answered unethically. Leah's disclosure is not made a confession or moral-purity test for other workers.
- **Power is not falsely balanced.** The worker owns chosen acts within reasonable knowledge and control. The employer bears the larger affirmative burden because it controls more of staffing, priorities, process, discipline, classification, and repair.
- **The managerial analogue is observable rather than diagnostic.** The essay judges decisions and reach, not a manager's private mental state.
- **Protected rhetoric survives with its limits.** “Let them eat cake” is labeled deliberate hyperbole, with no equivalence of suffering or authorship claim. The fuck-it factor remains Leah's first-person phenomenological label and is expressly not a validated construct, diagnosis, law, independently established risk increment, defense, excuse, or permission to trade away safety.
