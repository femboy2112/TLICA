# Parking Lot: Ideas Preserved outside *The Ethical Quiet Quitting Manifesto* v2.0

These ideas may be valuable, but they are excluded from the canonical manifesto or publication adaptation because they would create a second thesis, require a separate evidence base, or dilute the repair/de-buffering argument.

## 1. The king, serfdom, and private hierarchy analogy

**Core intuition:** A modern workplace can contain a privately governed hierarchy in which one party controls access to income, time, discipline, and the daily terms of action. The analogy asks when managerial authority begins to resemble a localized sovereignty and when worker dependence makes nominal consent ethically thin.

**Why parked:** The analogy is rhetorically large, historically loaded, and liable to collapse differences among employment, feudal status, law, mobility, and coercion. It would require its own treatment of property, contract, dependence, exit, jurisdiction, and democratic legitimacy. The present manifesto does not need it to establish the narrower right to withdraw an unsustainable subsidy.

**Possible future form:** An essay comparing public republican freedom with private workplace domination, explicitly identifying both similarities and non-equivalences.

## 2. Republican freedom and economic independence

**Core intuition:** Freedom is not only noninterference; it can also mean protection from arbitrary power. Economic dependence may leave a worker formally free to leave while practically exposed to domination. Usable voice, collective power, savings, portable benefits, and viable exit can therefore be understood as institutional conditions of freedom.

**Why parked:** This is a broader political theory of work. It could deepen the Exit Fallacy and pathway-closure analysis, but fully developing it would shift the manifesto from an applied ethic into a theory of republican political economy.

**Questions for a future paper:**

- When does workplace discretion become arbitrary power?
- Which forms of material independence make consent and exit meaningfully live?
- Are portable benefits, sectoral bargaining, unions, codetermination, or a social wage anti-domination institutions?
- How should small-firm fragility affect, but not erase, this analysis?

## 3. What being American means after the work bargain fractures

**Core intuition:** The hard-working-American identity may survive the loss of confidence in any particular employer. A worker can continue to value building, independence, responsibility, family, contribution, and ownership while redirecting effort away from an institution that no longer connects sacrifice to a self-directed future.

**Why parked:** The manifesto retains only the narrow supporting claim that one influential American work narrative links effort to a more secure and owned future. A paper about American identity would need to address plural traditions, racial and gender exclusion, immigration, civic membership, consumerism, property, labor history, and the difference between aspiration and entitlement.

**Possible future thesis:** Quiet quitting can sometimes be read not as abandonment of an American ethic of building, but as a dispute over which institutions still deserve to mediate that ethic.

## 4. The fuck-it factor as an empirical research program

**Core intuition:** Test whether Leah's phenomenological term identifies a distinct event rather than simply renaming overload, frustration, routine violation, or workaround use.

**Why parked:** The manifesto can name the experience but cannot validate a construct. A research paper would require preregistration, operational definitions, ethics review where applicable, and data.

**Minimum discriminating design:**

1. Define an event only when a person initially attempts to preserve multiple live requirements, perceives no timely fully compliant route, knowingly relaxes an ethically eligible requirement, and reports a recognizable transition into triage.
2. Measure objective constraints separately from the person's perceived and authorized action set.
3. Match time pressure, task complexity, staffing, interruptions, expertise, fatigue, equipment, and authority before asking whether the reported event predicts unintended error or harm.
4. Test the counter-hypothesis that deliberate triage reduces total harm under objectively impossible conditions.
5. Test whether repetition changes later transitions and whether the sign depends on feedback, outcome, rest, repair, training, sanction, and team norms.
6. Model propagation by the number of downstream constraints controlled, not simply by job title.

**Falsifiers worth welcoming:** no discrete transition; no predictive value beyond ordinary overload; consistent harm reduction; no stable separation from known workaround/violation constructs; or propagation explained fully by formal authority.

## 5. A full theory of causal legibility at work

**Core intuition:** Institutions often receive outcomes without the histories needed to interpret them. A negative metric becomes a projective surface onto which managers can place incompetence, laziness, demand, equipment, staffing, or chance. Contemporaneous source-maps preserve rival explanations and prevent the resource context from being erased.

**Why parked:** The manifesto uses this as a practical rule, not a theory of causal inference. A separate paper could distinguish documentation, provenance, causal identification, counterfactuals, audit trails, and the politics of which data organizations choose to count.

## 6. Heroics, local adaptation, and organizational learning

**Core intuition:** Front-line rescue can be locally responsible and organizationally damaging when it restores service while hiding recurring defects. Ethical de-buffering and organizational learning may therefore be linked, but only conditionally.

**Why parked:** The adjacent research spans resilience engineering, safety science, workarounds, high-reliability organization, and operations. The manifesto needs only the narrower warning that successful local adaptation can conceal dependence.

## 7. A collective counterpart to quiet quitting proper

**Core intuition:** Individual quiet quitting, coordinated work-to-rule, strikes, concerted refusal, and union bargaining all withdraw labor or discretion, but their moral structures differ. Collective actions add democratic authorization, solidarity, notice, legal frameworks, bargaining targets, and shared risk.

**Why parked:** The canonical manifesto explicitly limits its anti-sabotage and anti-hostage rules to individual quiet quitting proper. A full ethics of collective leverage cannot be responsibly fitted into a scope paragraph.

## 8. The worker's pledge as a separate public artifact

**Core intuition:** The v1.4 pledge has cadence and movement-building potential even though it repeats the logical core.

**Why parked:** It made the manuscript longer without adding argument. It may work better as a one-page broadside, poster, spoken-word piece, or social graphic after the canonical framework is fixed.

**Lines worth preserving for that future artifact:**

- We will not make impossible workloads appear ordinary by consuming ourselves in private.
- We will ask those with authority to choose among incompatible priorities.
- We will not sabotage. We will not falsify.
- We will help during genuine emergencies without allowing emergency effort to become the invisible permanent baseline.
- We will withdraw the hidden subsidy, not our integrity.

## 9. Formal models removed from v2.0

The probability inequality, scalar threshold recurrence, argmin model, and most input-output arrows are preserved as part of the development history but not recommended for republication as findings. They may become testable hypotheses only after variables, confounders, outcomes, and counter-hypotheses are made explicit.

The one retained schematic, `Y = F(D,N,E,H)`, is deliberately modest: a reminder that hidden heroic effort can be an omitted input, not a fitted production function or proof that `H` is the unique cause of observed output.

## 10. Public-first publication ecosystem

**Core intuition:** A canonical owned version, a shorter high-reach essay, social excerpts, audio/video readings, and labor-community discussion can form a publication ecosystem rather than one gatekept placement.

**Why parked from the manifesto:** Distribution strategy is not part of the ethical thesis. It belongs in `submission_notes.md` and should be decided after final author review, platform-policy verification, and a deliberate choice about closing prior-publication options.
