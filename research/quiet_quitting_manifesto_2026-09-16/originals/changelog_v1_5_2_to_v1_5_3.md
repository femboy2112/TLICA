# Changelog — v1.5.2 to v1.5.3

## Why this is a clarification release

v1.5.3 keeps the v1.5.2 architecture and moral center while making three boundaries explicit: ethical ideals are not perfection tests; the public label *quiet quitting* covers heterogeneous conduct that is not one moral object; and Leah's consciously examined case may have tacit structural relatives without establishing their prevalence or requiring workers to possess a manifesto before their circumstances are taken seriously.

This release adds no source and changes no existing source-fit judgment. Its new claims are normative, definitional, taxonomic, authorial, or expressly proposed—not new measured findings.

### 2. Preamble: ideals, asymmetry, and the minimum threshold

The v1.5.2 unequal-power paragraph was expanded at v1.5.3 lines 17–19 to state that:

- the worker-side and employer-side ethics are orienting ideals rather than descriptions of perfect people or institutions;
- the ideals and burdens are not mirror images;
- greater authority, information, and reach create the employer's larger affirmative repair burden and wider consequences;
- ordinary friction, a hard week, a minor defect, or an isolated repairable mistake does not by itself establish quiet quitting proper; and
- at minimum, the case requires a material, persistent mismatch plus correction pathways that are absent, unsafe, reasonably judged futile, unreasonably costly, or limited to assimilation rather than repair, while the later ethical conditions still apply.

The last-boundary sentence at line 21 now says **quiet quitting proper**, eliminating ambiguity with the broader public label introduced in Section 1. The repair-not-permission paragraph and case anchor remain intact at lines 23–27.

Measured from `# Preamble` through the line before Section 1 using the same whitespace-delimited count, the preamble changed from 378 words in v1.5.2 to 536 words in v1.5.3.

### 3. Section 1: public label versus narrow construct

New lines 35–39:

- identify illustrative, ethically non-equivalent uses of the broader public label, including conserving capacity for moonlighting or a second job, ordinary boundary-setting, disengagement unrelated to job structure, and minimizing labor regardless of the bargain;
- state that a shared label does not settle the ethics of those acts;
- frame Leah's narrower case as an explicitly examined attempt to do good, avoid unnecessary harm, seek truth, and give affected parties—including the employer—a fair shake while withdrawing unowned excess;
- define a fair shake by knowledge and control rather than fictional equal footing;
- disclaim moral superiority and any verdict on every worker bearing the broader label; and
- establish that unqualified *quiet quitting* below means *quiet quitting proper* unless the broader public label is explicitly under discussion.

New lines 99–103 give different warnings under unequal power. Workers cannot use a label to convert bad faith, retaliation, neglect, or another category into ethical action. Employers cannot use unlike cases to classify every withdrawal as laziness or misconduct and, because they control classification, discipline, staffing, and repair, bear the larger duty to distinguish the cases fairly. The structural warning that a workplace can teach quiet quitting before a worker names it is expressly non-statistical and does not make articulate self-explanation another condition of being taken seriously.

### 4. Section 2: tacit structural resemblance is a hypothesis

The former generic opening was replaced at lines 107–111. The narrower case no longer says workers “often” follow one path or ranks them by whether they “care enough.” It now says care may persist after actionable repair pathways close.

Leah's further claim is marked as a **working hypothesis**: many cases described by the broader public label may share the structure without sharing the manifesto's explicit reasoning. Environment, circumstances, and values may produce a tacit posture without a formal ethical program. The manuscript expressly calls this a proposed explanation rather than a measured prevalence claim.

### 5. Manager warning

New line 789 tells managers that ethical quiet quitting proper is one explicitly articulated warning case, not a prediction that all structurally produced withdrawal arrives with an explicit ethic. A worker's lack of a manifesto is not proof of lack of cause, while apathy, refusal, sabotage, or collective pressure elsewhere is not a shortcut for classifying competent baseline work in the case at hand. The larger classification and repair duty remains with management because management controls classification and discipline.

No other canonical prose changed.
