## v1.5.3 substantive delta

**Delta verdict: PASS.** The new material closes classification gaps without turning the framework into a worker proof test or an employer permission process.

- **Unequal ideals, unequal burdens:** the Preamble now defines both sides' ethics as orienting ideals while expressly denying that they are optional courtesies, descriptions of every workplace, equal burdens, or mirror images. Worker responsibility is bounded by limited power and reasonable knowledge and control; greater employer authority, information, and reach create the larger affirmative burden and wider consequences (lines 15–17).
- **Minor defects remain subthreshold:** ordinary friction, a hard week, a minor defect, and an isolated repairable mistake do not create quiet quitting proper. The minimum case requires both a material, persistent baseline mismatch and correction pathways that are absent, unsafe, reasonably judged futile, unreasonably costly, or assimilation-only; further ethical conditions still apply (line 19).
- **Broader public label separated from the stipulated object:** ordinary speech may group together ordinary boundaries, second-job capacity, tacit adaptation, unrelated disengagement, neglect, retaliation, and misconduct. The manuscript neither condemns nor morally equates those cases; it states that the label alone settles none of them, then limits its own shorthand to quiet quitting proper (lines 31–39).
- **Warnings weighted by power:** a worker may not convert bad faith or deliberate neglect into ethical action by relabeling it, but owns consciously chosen acts only within reasonable knowledge and control. Employers may not use unlike cases to erase structurally produced withdrawal; because they control classification, discipline, staffing, and repair, they bear the larger duty to distinguish before acting (lines 97–103).
- **Tacit adaptation is a hypothesis, not prevalence:** Section 2 proposes that some workers may arrive at a similar posture through environmental learning filtered by their circumstances and values, without formalizing an ethic. It explicitly labels this a working explanation rather than a measured prevalence claim and does not make articulate self-explanation a condition of structural recognition (lines 103–111).
- **Managerial echo:** the Message to Managers treats ethical quiet quitting proper as a warning case rather than a claim about every broadly labeled worker. It forbids using apathy, refusal, sabotage, or collective pressure elsewhere as a classification shortcut and assigns management the greater distinguish-and-repair duty (lines 787–789).

The Preamble's refusal boundary, unequal-power map, repair-first/no-permission guard, and case/anti-proof-template anchor remain in their load-bearing early positions (lines 13–27). No new sentence creates false equal footing, a worker duty to perfect the institution, or a right for the employer to demand exhaustive proof.

## Governing definition and power asymmetry

The definition states the manuscript's exact object without turning a worker's boundary into an employer permission process:

- **Labor-refusal category boundary:** quiet quitting proper retains the defensible baseline and withdraws only unowned excess. Whether outright refusal is ethical is expressly left as a separate unsettled question (line 13). This is scope, not condemnation, a legal claim, or an employer permission rule; Section 9 still treats strikes, boycotts, slowdowns, and coordinated work-to-rule as a distinct use of power (lines 690–694).
- **Unequal action field:** economic necessity constrains the worker's limited power, while the employer controls pay, staffing, schedules, rules, discipline, and much of the field in which labor acts (line 15).
- **Non-mirrored ideals:** worker responsibility concerns competent, safe, honest baseline labor and chosen acts within reasonable knowledge and control. Employer responsibility includes humane restraint and affirmative repair proportionate to greater authority, information, control, and reach. The text expressly says these are not mirror images and confines the anti-pathological-tolerance qualification to concentrated power (line 17).
- **Materiality and closure threshold:** imperfection calls for proportionate repair, not automatic quiet quitting. A material, persistent baseline mismatch and meaningful pathway closure are minimum conditions rather than a complete sufficiency test (line 19).
- **No permission gate:** repair first serves truth and causal legibility; it does not grant endless chances, compel danger or retaliation, or transfer institutional repair to the least powerful person (lines 21–27). No worker must earn permission to stop a genuinely voluntary gift, and silence under credible retaliation is not automatically dishonest (lines 53–55).
- **Precise definition and one-way condition:** quiet quitting proper is competent performance of paid and reasonably expected work while ceasing unowned excess (lines 31–33). It implies structural or managerial expectations above the defensible baseline; the converse is not asserted, and the excess condition is necessary rather than sufficient (lines 41–49).
- **Codified, lived, and operating mismatch:** the gap may lie in vague or contradictory words, unagreed and unresourced lived-role expansion, or operating conditions that make sustainable competent performance impossible. Nominally optional extras enforced through pressure or penalty are not optional in practice (lines 57–59).
- **Correction is not assimilation:** correction clarifies, prioritizes, resources, or genuinely negotiates; assimilation makes the worker absorb the defect as identity and duty (lines 61–63).
- **Healthy-workplace control case:** the same competent, sustainable conduct is ordinary or average in a properly resourced workplace. It appears as withdrawal only where the institution mistook excess for capacity (lines 65–67).
- **Broad-label guard:** less articulated cases are neither automatically ethical nor automatically causeless. The worker's lack of a manifesto cannot become an institutional proof demand, and unrelated bad-faith cases cannot erase structural withdrawal (lines 99–109).

Section 8 remains expressly a worker's self-audit, not an employer form or a fiction of equal power. Repair attempts are qualified by safety, usefulness, retaliation, futility, channel viability, cost, and assimilation-only pseudo-correction; management retains system-level foresight, staffing, and safe-stop responsibility (lines 632–643). The conclusion again rejects fifty-fifty responsibility and distributes it by choice, knowledge, power, and control (line 825).

**Power-asymmetry verdict: PASS.** No load-bearing false symmetry, worker proof burden, employer permission gate, institutional escape hatch, or accidental condemnation of ethical ordinary boundary-setting remains.
