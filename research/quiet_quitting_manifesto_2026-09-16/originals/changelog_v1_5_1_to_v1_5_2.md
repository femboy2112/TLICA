# Changelog — v1.5.1 to v1.5.2

## Why this is a structural release

The frozen v1.5.1 parent carried several complete section summaries inside the preamble and then developed the same ideas again in their proper sections. That architecture delayed the category boundary and made the opening do the work of an abstract, introduction, and roadmap at once.

v1.5.2 shortens the preamble to four functions: define the refusal/baseline boundary, state the unequal-power moral frame, give the central repair-first rule with its non-permission clarification, and identify the lived case anchor. The removed material was relocated or absorbed where the argument actually develops it. This is a structural edit, not a change in the manifesto's moral center, constructs, evidence base, or protected positions.

Measured from `# Preamble` through the line before Section 1, using the same whitespace-delimited count on both Markdown blocks, the preamble changed from **685 words to 378 words**: 307 words shorter, a 44.8% reduction.

## Protected positions unchanged

- **“Let them eat cake”:** the comparison and its immediate clarification remain at lines 128–130. It is deliberate hyperbole about constraint-blind reasoning, not an equivalence of suffering or a historical authorship claim.
- **American Dream:** Section 2.3 retains the hard-working-American input / American-Dream output framing, the “here” limitation, the proportionality rule, and the claim that quiet quitting can preserve rather than reject the work ethic.
- **Fuck-it factor:** the name is not changed or sanitized. Its definition remains at line 432; line 434 now places the lived-experience reason directly beside that definition; the manuscript continues to distinguish phenomenology from later factual verification and from a scientific construct, diagnosis, law, defense, or excuse.
- **Power asymmetry:** lines 15–23 retain conscious and responsible worker power alongside the greater repair and restraint duties attached to employer control. Lines 578–590 and 807 retain unequal reach and reject fifty-fifty responsibility.
- **Refusal boundary:** line 13 still stipulates that quiet quitting proper retains defensible baseline labor and withdraws unowned excess; whether outright refusal is ethical remains a separate question the manifesto does not purport to settle.
- **Collective action:** line 89 and Section 9 continue to treat collective pressure as a distinct use of worker power governed by truth-testing, consequence awareness, and ownership—not by a blanket ban on disruption.
