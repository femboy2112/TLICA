# Quiet Quitting Manifesto — bridge to the current TLICA corpus

**Prepared:** 2026-09-16. **Purpose:** a retrospective integration map accompanying the recovered historical archive. **Status:** research and editorial synthesis; no new foundation commitment or validated workplace model. **Theory pin:** `femboy2112/TLICA`, commit `34768a437eda09f076018f0042818dcf3098a41d`, foundation v5.5.0. The repository describes this foundation as frozen for applications but **main-but-experimental**, especially §§8.9–8.11. References below name sections at that commit and use in-repository relative paths.

The August manifesto already distinguishes real constraints from their interpretations, nominal options from reachable ones, institutional outputs from the hidden labor sustaining them, and causal explanation from moral permission. Current TLICA supplies a vocabulary for examining those distinctions. This is a **September retrospective mapping**, not evidence that August's drafts were derived from September's formal apparatus. The source texts remain authoritative records of what each historical version actually said.

## 1. What the recovered text contributes

The complete v1.5.3 and v1.5.4 Markdown sources are both dated August 16, 2026. Their central object is *individual quiet quitting proper*: competent, safe, honest, sustainable performance of the defensible role while withdrawing an unowned excess that operative expectations have incorporated into the job. The excess must be material and persistent, and meaningful correction must have failed or be absent, unsafe, reasonably judged futile, unreasonably costly, or assimilation-only. Merely declining an optional gift can be ordinary boundary-setting without meeting this narrower definition.

The manuscript is case-anchored ethical argument. It neither estimates how prevalent the pattern is nor certifies every act called quiet quitting. Its clearest claim about a worker's conduct is conditional and normative; an employer's classification of that conduct remains another judgment to examine. Institutional power is unequal: the worker owns the choices within their knowledge and control, while staffing, schedules, priorities, system information, and much of the repair burden lie with those holding greater authority.

Its main analytical components are:

- **Pathway closure:** effort, voice, and repair attempts can cease to lead toward meaningful correction or a self-directed future; perceived closure can also be mistaken (§2).
- **Exit capacity:** an alternative vacancy can exist while the transition is presently unreachable because of money, time, schedule, transport, benefits, or survival constraints (§2.1).
- **Reciprocity and identity:** discretionary effort depends on a relation among person, work, return, ownership, future, support, and available capacity. Identity rewards cannot establish that the bargain is materially reciprocal (§§2.2–2.3).
- **Truth-preserving repair:** identify the constraint, use a safe functioning channel, propose a feasible change, and request explicit priority choices when demands conflict (§3).
- **Provenance-bearing communication:** a bad output alone is ambiguous; records of demand, staffing, equipment, and priority decisions help investigate it (§4).
- **Ethical de-buffering:** reduce hidden heroic compensation while retaining safety, honesty, and professional integrity; normal output may previously have depended on that hidden input (§5).
- **Acute triage:** under a deadline, the worker may experience no fully compliant available action. The coined “fuck-it factor” describes that felt transition, with perceived and actual infeasibility kept distinct (§6).
- **Scoped responsibility:** an explanation of withdrawal or overload is not a moral exemption. Individual boundaries, deliberate collective pressure, and outright labor refusal are different objects (§§7–12).

The direct v1.5.3 → v1.5.4 comparison found three substantive additions, all in §1: an autobiographical disclosure that earlier conduct under the broad label does not necessarily meet the present framework; the statement that a persistent intuition was a clue requiring truth-testing; and the clarification that this disclosure is not a confession other workers owe. The preamble changes “arrived at” to “finally crystallized,” and the version field changes. The remaining argument is unchanged. This narrow delta matters: v1.5.4 adds an ethical genealogy, not a new empirical validation or a general admission about quiet quitters.

## 3. Distinctions an integration must not erase

### Diagnostic coordinates remain separate

For a bounded workplace claim, keep at least the following records: what was encountered; what was experienced; what the person inferred; the sources and their dependencies; the available verification routes and tests; the focus placed on those tests; identity involvement; and the outcomes of attempted repair. These records need not be numeric.

TLICA's κ concerns contact; φ concerns toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability where a verification pathway is constructible; ρ concerns integration with the indexed lived I. Their formal non-reducibility is not statistical independence. Undefined φ means no constructible verification pathway within the declared toolkit, not falsehood, low intelligence, unconsciousness, or a worker who lacks reasons. A clear verification story can coexist with an inadequate reconstruction of where the evidence came from. The probe set `Q` and its availability must be distinguished from `μ`, which weights probes in the foundation's formal construction. None is a general truth probability or moral score.

This preserves a valuable feature of v1.5.4's autobiographical passage: the feeling, its structural interpretation, and the ethics of the response are three separately examinable claims. A feeling may accurately register a problem without licensing the action taken in response.

### The two slack formulas have different types

The manifesto's §6.4 writes an illustrative operational margin:

$$
S_t^{\mathrm{operations}}=K_t^{\mathrm{base}}+H_t-W_t.
$$

It compares effective work capacity and workload. It is meaningful quantitatively only after capacity, hidden effort, and load have compatible units and a defined time window. The historical source does not fit this expression to a workplace dataset.

The v5.5.0 foundation's §8.11 writes a reflexive-operating budget:

$$
S_{m,t}^{\mathrm{reflection}}=M_m(t)-\mathrm{Pressure}_{m,t}.
$$

`Pressure` is an open function expressed in the units of the focus-capacity bound `M`. The threshold at zero is itself a modeling posit; the prediction that felt choice changes near that threshold remains **UNVERIFIED** until an independent operationalization exists. A queue with inadequate operational capacity may increase pressure on a worker, but the equality of the two margins does not follow. A deadline, a financial reserve, a sense of ownership, and a unit of attention cannot be added into one common fuel by notation alone.

Likewise, an empty fully compliant action-set under current constraints does not mean no action exists. Slowing, stopping unsafe work, asking for priorities, escalating, and transferring control remain actions when available. The manifesto explicitly keeps these possibilities and refuses to make safety another ordinary speed tradeoff. Its §6.3 use of “dual” identifies a proposed structural resemblance across timescales; no formal mathematical duality is established.

### A changed identity weight is not the v5.5.0 actuator

If future work gives a dynamical reading to chronic adaptation or deliberate boundary formation, it must use the repaired mechanism. In §§8.10–8.11, the baseline law is

$$
\dot b=\gamma(f-b),\qquad \rho=R(G),\qquad f=F(G,\mathsf A).
$$

The shared slow structure `G` and its two readings are **Posit G**. Changing the metric weight `ρ` alone does not move the baseline in this law. Osmotic or self-directed imprinting may rewrite `G`; under the stated nontrivial dependence of `F` on `G`, that can move the target `f` the baseline follows. This is a conditional formal account, not a measured equation for Leah or coworkers. It does not establish that a boundary is a Mode-B event, that every automatic response is myopic, or that persistent contact must cause permanent emotional instability. The baseline's ongoing movement and the felt magnitude's stability are distinct questions.

### Institution, situation, and individual perspective are distinct

The distributed-institution manuscript defines a micro-realization `X=(P,R,D)` from indexed agent profiles, typed relations, and artifacts/records. A task-relative institutional macrostate groups realizations that give the same response signature for a declared task. A separate situation and input activate that organization to yield an outcome (§§4–9). This is more specific than saying that the workplace is “the sum of its people.”

For the manifesto, the practical benefit is to compare a formally declared workflow with the workflow actually producing output. A schedule can say the job is staffed while actual production depends on unofficial coverage, skipped recovery, tacit sequencing, or an unrecorded intermediary. Which paths matter is a question for evidence. The institutional quotient's formal factorization result does not validate a workplace diagnosis. Nor does it turn the detached but indispensable worker into a contradiction: identity-correlation and operational participation are explicitly distinct (§15).

### Explanation does not generate the ethical premises

TLICA can organize evidence about constraints, options, contact, source paths, and control. It does not derive the manifesto's duties of safety, honesty, humane restraint, proportionality, or non-exploitation from a coordinate value. Those commitments need their own normative argument. An empirically adequate structural account could still describe an unethical response; an ethical boundary could still have difficult consequences. Individual quiet quitting proper must remain distinct from collective pressure and from outright refusal, rather than allowing the narrower definition to legislate all labor action.

## 4. Grounded next probes, all unrun here

The archive is a recovery and integration task. No workplace experiment, causal evaluation, or new source-validation study was performed for this bridge. The following are bounded continuations of tests the recovered manuscript already suggests; they are not findings.

| Question and live rival | Discriminating observation | Support, refutation, and ambiguity |
|---|---|---|
| Are repair pathways closed, or is there a correctable communication or authority-routing failure? (§§2–3) | Where reasonably safe and useful, record a concrete constraint, an addressed priority or resource request, the recipient's authority, acknowledgment, and actual action. | A usable response producing correction weakens the closure claim for that route. Repeated acknowledged non-action by an actor with relevant capacity supports local closure. Unknown authority, missing receipt, or changing demand remains ambiguous. No universal “try again” obligation follows. |
| Does nominal staffing explain output, or does the model omit hidden compensation? (§§4–5) | Compare documented ordinary operation with documented episodes involving extra coverage or exceptional effort, recording demand, equipment, skill, sequencing, and priorities. Prefer existing records and ordinary safe operating variation. | Output dependence on the extra contribution supports a local omitted-input account. Accurate prediction without it weakens that specific account. Simultaneous changes prevent unique attribution. Harm is not an admissible measuring instrument. |
| Is no compliant action genuinely available, or does the person lack a usable map, time, or authority? (§6) | Reconstruct an episode's deadlines, constraints, available actions, information, and authority. Include safe-stop and escalation actions. Contrast the contemporaneous perceived action-set with a later reconstruction. | An admissible compliant route that was available and usable at the time refutes actual infeasibility for that episode. A defensible exhaustive reconstruction with no such route supports infeasibility within the reconstructed boundary. Incomplete records remain ambiguous. Hindsight cannot silently supply knowledge or time the worker lacked. |
| Does reclaimed capacity enable a later valued action? (§2.1 and §2.2) | Track the particular margin reclaimed and the subsequent activity separately: usable search time, applications completed, recovery, art, learning, or another declared goal. | Improved feasibility supports the proposed enabling role in that case. No change or a worsened tradeoff constrains it. A self-rating alone cannot establish the whole pathway, and one success is not population evidence. |
| Does the actual workflow explain the result better than the official one? (§§1, 4–5; institutional manuscript §12) | Write both bounded workflow descriptions before evaluating new ordinary episodes, then compare specific predictions about bottlenecks, authority, and outcomes. | Better predictions from independently recorded lived paths support the claim of omitted organization. No gain weakens it. Fitting the workflow to every observed outcome without a later test is not validation. |

Any later application should retain an ordinary explanation as a competitor—staffing, scheduling, communication failure, equipment, training, incentives, or inaccurate inference—and ask what additional discrimination TLICA provides. Renaming an ordinary explanation is not evidence of added explanatory value. Sources already listed in the August ledger are inherited references; archiving them does not independently reverify their metadata, access scope, findings, or fit to the new application.

## 5. Integration contract

1. Keep the historical files and exact user prompts recoverable as sources, with their dates, versions, provenance, and any truncation recorded. Assistant-written reconstructions and memory summaries are not verbatim user testimony.
2. Preserve the distinction between an archival version, the preferred reading version at a particular time, a rejected rewrite, and a publication-ready or author-cleared version. A later filename alone does not settle those states.
3. Preserve v1.5.4's denial of retroactive certification; do not rewrite the early drafts as though their author had already resolved every later distinction. The development and correction are part of the contribution.
4. Retain the asymmetric responsibility frame, the non-perfection standard, repair as a bounded truth test, the voluntary-gift distinction, and the difference between role correction and assimilation.
5. Preserve the exact scope of “quiet quitting proper,” the separate treatment of collective pressure, and the explicit refusal to settle every case of outright labor refusal.
6. Carry the proposed status of the fuck-it-factor mechanism, the repeated-workaround feedback loop, and the nonlinear workplace extension. Historical adjacent evidence does not validate the coined construct.
7. Keep operational slack separate from reflexive slack and from other practical margins. Preserve actual/perceived distinctions for both option availability and slack.
8. Treat the current TLICA reading as an appended interpretation. A future application may cite the manifesto as a source and describe its genealogy, but should not silently overwrite it or promote its proposals into foundation doctrine.
