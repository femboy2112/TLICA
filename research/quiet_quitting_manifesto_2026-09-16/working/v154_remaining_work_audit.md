## Claim and guardrail checklist for the 1,200–1,800-word companion

### Required claim spine

- [ ] **Stipulated category:** Say that *quiet quitting proper* is Leah's precise ethical category: competent, safe, honest performance of the defensible paid role while unowned excess is withdrawn. It is not a measured population category.
- [ ] **Outright-refusal boundary:** State once that quiet quitting proper retains baseline labor. Whether outright refusal is ethical is a separate unsettled question; do not imply condemnation of strikes or collective refusal.
- [ ] **Necessary condition, not automatic permission:** Structural or managerial expectations must exceed the defensible baseline. That is necessary, not sufficient. A hard week, minor defect, or isolated repairable mistake does not meet the threshold.
- [ ] **Healthy-workplace control:** Preserve the clarifying counterexample: in a well-structured workplace, the same competent sustainable worker looks ordinary or average. The phenomenon appears where an institution mistook excess for capacity.
- [ ] **Repair first without permission-seeking:** Explain that safe, useful repair attempts help the worker test what is true and preserve the causal story. They are not employer permission, an infinite-appeal duty, or an obligation to enter dangerous, retaliatory, futile, or assimilation-only channels.
- [ ] **Hidden subsidy:** Describe chronic rescues, unbounded role absorption, skipped recovery, or unsustainable pace as possible hidden inputs that make inadequate systems look functional.
- [ ] **Ethical de-buffering:** Define it as proportionately withdrawing that unowned input while safety, honesty, feasible professional quality, and competent baseline work remain. A changed outcome shows dependence under the observed conditions, not unique causation or automatic moral blame.
- [ ] **Unequal power:** State that worker and employer are not on equal footing. The worker owns chosen acts within reasonable knowledge and control; the employer bears the larger classification, staffing, priority, investigation, restraint, and repair burden because it controls more of the action field.
- [ ] **Exit constraint:** “Another job exists” does not prove that exit is presently viable. Avoid claiming that exit is impossible for everyone or that quiet quitting is a guaranteed bridge.
- [ ] **American Dream boundary:** If used, frame it as one influential American work narrative. The claim concerns **additional exceptional sacrifice here**, not a historical guarantee that every hard worker succeeds or that every ordinary job owes wealth, ownership, or limitless advancement.
- [ ] **Fuck-it-factor boundary:** Preserve the name if the concept appears. Define it as Leah's blunt phenomenological label for the felt turn from attempted total compliance to forced triage under acute constraint. It is not a validated construct, diagnosis, law, excuse, or independently established risk increment.
- [ ] **Perception versus fact:** A fully compliant route may truly be absent or merely appear absent within available time, information, equipment, and authority. The feeling is real; which condition obtained remains a factual question.
- [ ] **Power changes propagation:** A worker's acute decision may affect a task or shift; a decision by someone controlling staffing, schedules, priorities, or reporting can reshape many workers' conditions. “Humanness is symmetric. Power is not.”
- [ ] **Collective-action separation:** Individual quiet quitting proper is not a complete theory of strikes, boycotts, coordinated work-to-rule, or organizing. Collective actors should test premises, represent uncertainty honestly, understand consequences, and own chosen pressure; the companion must not turn those disciplines into a demand for powerlessness.
- [ ] **Responsibility distribution:** Close with responsibility following choice, knowledge, power, and control—not a fifty-fifty split and not automatic exoneration of any worker response.

### Autobiographical-disclosure guardrail

- [ ] Preserve the distinction: some earlier broad-label quiet quitting did not meet the present ethic; the manifesto does not retroactively certify it.
- [ ] Preserve the phenomenological line or a faithful paraphrase: something felt structurally right before Leah could name it. Immediately state that the feeling was a **clue, not a verdict**.
- [ ] Preserve the two-sided truth: a worker may perceive a real structural mismatch and still respond to it unethically.

- [ ] Mark the disclosure for Leah's explicit privacy, voice, and reputational approval before release.

### Rhetorical guardrails

- [ ] Keep the prose direct and first-person where the source is lived experience. Do not translate the manuscript into institutional or academic neutrality.
- [ ] Use at most one schematic relation, if any. Prose should carry the short essay; notation must not impersonate measurement.
- [ ] “Ceasing to subsidize a broken system is not manufacturing damage” may serve as the central distinction, but the word **broken** should follow an actually described persistent mismatch rather than substitute for diagnosis.
- [ ] “The worker's body is not the slack variable in a staffing model” may be retained without qualification because its surrounding claim is normative, not an engineering result.
- [ ] If “let them eat cake” appears, put its qualification in the same paragraph: deliberate hyperbole about constraint-blind reasoning, no equivalence of suffering, and no historical authorship claim.
- [ ] Do not rename, euphemize, or remove the fuck-it factor without bringing the placement issue back to Leah. Keeping it out of a headline while retaining it in the body is a distribution choice, not an automatic correction.
- [ ] Do not claim moral superiority, portray employers as a monolith, or attribute malice where incapacity, bad information, demand shock, disagreement, or a constrained front-line supervisor remains possible.
- [ ] Do not balance the prose by inventing equal worker/employer duties. Greater power must retain the larger affirmative burden.

### Empirical and source guardrails

- [ ] Reuse only claims already represented in the v1.5.4 ledger unless the companion receives its own source-hardening pass.
- [ ] Do not say the evidence validates *quiet quitting proper*, *ethical de-buffering*, the tacit-adaptation hypothesis, or the fuck-it factor as scientific constructs.
- [ ] Do not estimate how “most,” “the vast majority,” or a typical share of quiet quitters think or behave. The manuscript offers a working structural hypothesis, not prevalence data.
