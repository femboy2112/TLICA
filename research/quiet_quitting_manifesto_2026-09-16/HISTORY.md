# History of the Quiet Quitting Manifesto

This is a recovery-based history assembled on 2026-09-16. It distinguishes what surviving files demonstrate from what a retrieval summary reports. It is not a reconstructed full transcript. The [prompt appendix](history/USER_PROMPTS_VERBATIM.md) and [provenance record](PROVENANCE.md) identify the original-text boundary.

## 1. The problem from which the manifesto grew

The surviving early manuscript already centers a particular workplace pattern: workers can sustain observed output by absorbing missing staffing, incompatible priorities, inadequate equipment, and unacknowledged roles. Their private compensating effort can hide the institutional defect. When voice, excellent work, and repair attempts repeatedly fail to produce correction or a reachable future, withdrawing additional discretionary effort can become intelligible without implying indifference to craft or coworkers.

The distinctive authorial move is an ethical constraint on that withdrawal. The question is how to stop supplying the hidden subsidy while continuing to act competently, safely, honestly, and responsibly toward others. The original governing rule is preserved in the uploaded handoff: attempt reasonably available ethical repair, then withdraw unowned excess when meaningful pathways remain closed. The work does not infer that every dissatisfied worker is ethically justified or that every claimed route closure is real.

The [v1.5.4 account](originals/ethical_quiet_quitting_manifesto_v1_5_4.md), §1, adds a genealogy that earlier versions lacked. Leah had previously acted under the broad public label in ways she would not certify under the later ethic. A recurring intuition that withdrawal addressed something real was a clue requiring examination. Reflection could preserve the structural insight while rejecting some responses to it. This is a later first-person account in an AI-assisted manuscript, not independent evidence verifying every workplace event.

## 3. The uploaded handoff and the rejected direction

The complete [nineteen-section editorial/publication handoff](history/source_prompts/editorial_publication_handoff_2026-08-16.md) survives as an uploaded file. Its SHA-256 matches the reference recorded in the [remaining-work audit](working/v154_remaining_work_audit.md). This is strong evidence of the exact instruction artifact used during revision, including its errors and formatting. It does not identify who originally drafted every sentence or supply a missing chat timestamp.

The handoff asks for hostile criticism, conceptual repairs, factual sourcing, sharper prose, a canonical v2.0 and a shorter publication version. It protects the governing repair-first ethic, ethical de-buffering, the fuck-it-factor name, exit reachability, grind's conditional value, and unequal power. It also directs that the manifesto stand alone without requiring TLICA knowledge. Kings/serfdom/private hierarchy, broader republican and economic independence, and a future American-identity paper are explicitly parked rather than allowed to expand the central work.

The [v1.4-to-v2.0 changelog](originals/changelog_v1_4_to_v2_0.md) records genuine precision repairs alongside extensive restructuring. Those repairs included separating experienced from actual constraint closure, treating output changes as diagnostic rather than uniquely causal, distinguishing intentional departure from error, and removing unsupported mistake-rate and threshold expressions. But the full pledge and cake analogy disappeared, and the voice and structure were compressed.

A retrieved user message reported at 2026-08-17T15:55:36Z explicitly rejects making v2.0 canonical merely because of that rewrite and asks to preserve v1.4, with an optional v1.5 closer in spirit to it. Its full returned wording is in [M01](history/USER_PROMPTS_VERBATIM.md). That timestamp establishes a retrieved preference statement; it does not establish the first moment of rejection. Some final-v1.5 artifacts already carry earlier Aug 17 creation timestamps. The archive does not force these sources into a false minute-by-minute sequence.

There was a second editorial wrong turn. The preliminary [false-symmetry v1.5](working/ethical_quiet_quitting_manifesto_v1_5_false_symmetry_draft.md) used contract-like language, reliance-based duties and a stronger burden for workers to demonstrate institutional causation. The [final-v1.5 changelog](originals/changelog_v1_4_to_v1_5.md) explains the rejection: equal human worth does not make workplace power or repair duties equal. An [intermediate rebase](working/ethical_quiet_quitting_manifesto_v1_5_rebase.md) also survives; it predates the final additions on defensible baseline, ordinary optional boundaries and assimilation.

Final v1.5 restores the literary parent while preserving the useful critical work. The result retains the pledge, direct address, profanity, grind declaration, American Dream section and manager warning. It also states that a worker need not earn permission to stop a voluntary gift and that institutional dependence on hidden sacrifice does not, by itself, manufacture a new worker duty. Responsibility follows choice, knowledge, power and control.

## 4. The v1.5 patch sequence

The [1.5.1 changelog](originals/changelog_v1_5_to_v1_5_1.md) records a small scope clarification. The explicit labor-refusal sentence was added under a new version so one version label would not describe two texts. A TeX spacing adjustment was a production change, not a new argument.

The [1.5.2 changelog](originals/changelog_v1_5_1_to_v1_5_2.md) records a structural edit: opening scope and power, followed by definition and reasons, with relocated material absorbed rather than simply duplicated. This is the immediate parent of the later clarification releases.

The [1.5.3 changelog](originals/changelog_v1_5_2_to_v1_5_3.md) records three authorial priorities: neither side's ideal requires perfection; the broad quiet-quitting label does not identify one moral object; and a consciously articulated ethical case does not prove other workers lack reasons or integrity. A retrieval-reported full prompt for this change is retained as [Q01](history/RETRIEVED_QUOTATIONS.md), with its weaker provenance stated explicitly. The revision avoids converting an intuition about many workers into an unsupported statistical claim.

The [1.5.4 changelog](originals/changelog_v1_5_3_to_v1_5_4.md) adds the personal genealogy without retroactively certifying earlier behavior. This is also a constraint on future integration: the mature framework must not be projected backward as though it had governed every earlier act. The disclosure is not a confession other workers must supply before their circumstances count.

## 5. Source work, derivatives and publication preparations

Each final-v1.5 release has a preserved manuscript, TeX, PDF, editorial audit, source ledger, changelog and publication handoff. All six recovered ZIP packages are retained intact, and their 43 file members are individually represented. The alternate `v1_5_rebased_from_v1_4.pdf` is byte-identical to `v1_5.pdf`; both names remain preserved because both were delivered.

The [New Labor Forum adaptation](originals/new_labor_forum_submission_draft.md), titled *When Caring Becomes Camouflage*, is a separate shortened derivative associated with the heavily audited publication effort. It is not the full manifesto or a substitute for the preferred parent. Its [publication notes](originals/submission_notes.md) record an unresolved venue/AI-policy concern at that time. This archive preserves that historical assessment; it does not certify current venue policy.

The [Ford companion research brief](originals/ford_companion_research_brief.md) and [parking lot](originals/parking_lot.md) preserve proposed extensions. The parking lot also reflects the rejected v2.0 stage: its removal of the full pledge and selected schematics was superseded by final v1.5, while the broader adjacent topics remained parked. The [public-first preparation](originals/public_release_preparation_manifest_v1_5_4.md) includes a [public companion draft](originals/ethical_quiet_quitting_public_companion_v1_5_4_draft.md), review HTML, source ledger, handoff and [derivative audit](originals/public_derivative_audit_v1_5_4.md). Public-first reach, transparent AI disclosure, a canonical page and possible outreach were plans. Draft first-person messages in those files are proposed copy, not actual messages sent or original user prompts.

The historical documents repeatedly mark author clearance as pending and state that no publication or outreach had occurred at their recorded boundary. This current non-main archive is explicitly authorized by Leah's new request. It does not retroactively change what those earlier files said, certify external factual claims, or execute their unsent messages.
