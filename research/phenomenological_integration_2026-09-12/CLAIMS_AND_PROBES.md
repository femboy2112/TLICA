### The limited mathematical counterexample

Let a compression satisfy \(P(x_1)=P(x_2)=z\) with \(x_1\ne x_2\). No single-valued decoder of \(z\) can return both \(x_1\) and \(x_2\) as the unique original. Therefore a many-to-one compression cannot, by itself, guarantee exact reconstruction on its entire domain.

This is a checkable logical argument about the stated mathematical objects. It is **not** evidence that human contextualization is exactly such a function, nor a refutation of useful abstraction. The repair is a bounded record of what was retained, what was omitted, where raw evidence remains accessible, and what can no longer be recovered. The transcript's “guaranteed route back to reality” must be weakened accordingly.

## 2. Rival map: what the same report could mean

These are candidate mechanisms, not diagnoses. They are not mutually exclusive and no probabilities are assigned.

| Rival | Distinctive prediction, stated prospectively | Important ambiguity |
|---|---|---|
| Contextual integration | Integrability improves after a reconstruction that preserves constraints and uncertainty; a useful distinction can be stated afterward. | Satisfaction with a story may be mistaken for integration. |
| Expression / offloading | Much of the relief occurs after writing, before reading a reply. | Time and effort spent writing also change. |
| Recognition / relational responsiveness | Faithful attentive reflection without explanation offers comparable relief. | The reflective reply may itself aid integration. |
| Intellectual engagement / distraction | Engaging but non-explanatory material produces similar relief without a new account of the difficulty. | The activity may alter perspective indirectly. |
| Ordinary temporal change | Relief is associated with elapsed time after waking even when the response differs little. | Time is confounded with food, activity, interruptions, and natural recovery. |
| Expectancy or preferred vocabulary | Anticipated helpfulness or familiar terminology predicts benefit independent of preservation quality. | Familiar vocabulary may genuinely improve comprehension. |
| Narrative closure / unwarranted certainty | Distress falls while unsupported certainty rises or contradictions disappear from the record. | A valid clarification may also increase justified confidence. |
| Prospective agency | Relief closely follows the appearance of one credible next step rather than explanatory detail alone. | A proposed step may be infeasible or never enacted. |

Present observations do not split these rivals decisively. A request for a compelling explanation, a compelling answer, and a later report of relief share one intervention and one reporting context. Agreement among rewritten accounts is not independent replication.

## 3. Probe 0: a minimal prospective record

**Purpose:** establish order and variation without turning a distressed morning into a research assignment. Participation is optional; missing entries are allowed and recorded as missing, not as failure. Do not delay support in order to obtain a baseline.

A record can be as small as three optional marks and one sentence:

```text
episode_id:
date/time actually recorded:
time since waking, if known:

before writing: distress [0–10 or words / not recorded]
after writing, before reply: distress [0–10 or words / not recorded]
after reading: distress [0–10 or words / not recorded]

after reading, one sentence:
  What became more understandable, or what stayed wrong?

optional later follow-up:
  Did anything usable remain? Was a feasible next step actually taken?

response/provenance reference:
other changes during the episode:
  [e.g. elapsed time, food, interruption; unknown is acceptable]
helpful / unhelpful / mixed / unclear:
```

The numerical scale is an optional self-report convention, not a validated TLICA instrument. Do not backfill missing pre-response values from how the episode ended. A retrospective entry must be labeled retrospective. Record disappointing, abandoned, and no-change episodes as well as useful ones.

**Predeclared outcome interpretation:** relief after writing but before a reply strengthens expression/time rivals relative to a response-only explanation; relief after a reply is compatible with several remaining rivals. A sequence alone cannot identify mechanism. Lack of a feasible action does not negate relief and is not a moral failure.

## 4. Probe 1: matched contextualization versus faithful reflection

**Purpose:** distinguish a contribution of explanatory integration from attentive acknowledgment. This is a research design, not an instruction to conduct a self-experiment during severe distress.

Two candidate response formats, prepared for ordinary manageable episodes or author-approved low-stakes retrospective material:

- **R — faithful reflection:** accurately restate the experience, acknowledge the constraints, preserve uncertainty, and do not add a causal framework.
- **I — contextual integration:** use the same factual inputs and a comparable tone and length, but explicitly separate observation, interpretation, and unknown; offer a tentative relational account and one alternative.

Neither format should fabricate reassurance or claim certainty. The earlier suggestion of an adversarial confrontation arm is not adopted for acute distress. A person may exit the comparison and obtain ordinary support at any time.

Before actual collection, fix the selection/allocation rule, length tolerance, assessment window, primary endpoint, analysis, and either a sample size or stopping rule. These have not been registered. Without those commitments, any pilot is exploratory. Random allocation, if later chosen and safe, does not remove expectation, carryover, or reporting effects. Blinding the participant to whether an explanation was provided is generally not plausible; do not claim it.

### Outcomes defined before seeing results

**Supports the narrower integration hypothesis:** across appropriately collected episodes, I improves reported integrability relative to R while preserving declared facts and uncertainty, and the difference is not merely an increase in being told what one wants to hear. This remains evidence for a scoped contribution, not proof that a TLICA mechanism is unique.

**Counts against the preferred account:** factual omissions or unsupported certainty are required for the apparent benefit; integration does not improve; or R matches I within predeclared tolerances while explanatory detail mainly increases burden. Similar outcomes weaken an incremental-benefit claim, not every possible role of integration.

**Ambiguous:** unequal starting states, different length or responsiveness, strong expectations, inadequate observations, overlapping ingredients, missing timepoints, major intervening events, or carryover. Report ambiguity rather than choosing the more attractive interpretation.

Relief and integration must not be collapsed into one endpoint. Neither can certify source accuracy. Audit whether the response retains the original constraints, distinguishes report from inference, and leaves contrary observations admissible.

## 5. Probe 2: mechanism vocabulary and transport

**Vocabulary ablation:** compare an integration response in TLICA terms with a plain-language rendering that preserves the same distinctions. Equal outcomes would argue against terminology being necessary, while remaining compatible with the underlying structure being useful. A jargon advantage would still be compatible with familiarity and expectancy.

**Transport probe:** after a helpful episode, retain an optional one-sentence cue chosen by the author. In a later ordinary interaction, record whether the cue was encountered or retrieved, whether it arrived in time, and whether it changed an option or action. Do not infer retrieval from later agreement that the cue was sensible. No later success or failure should be generalized into a global verdict about the person.

Run these as distinct questions. A successful vocabulary comparison does not establish transfer, and a successful cue does not identify the original relief mechanism.

## 6. Calibration controls for the evaluation machinery

These controls are proposed for offline evaluation of records or harmless constructed examples, not deception or provocation of the author. **None has run in this package.**

**Positive control:** provide a fully specified, low-stakes ambiguity whose resolution follows from an explicitly supplied fact. The evaluator should recognize the added explanatory distinction without inventing anything else.

**Negative control:** provide an account containing a clearly identified unsupported causal assertion. The evaluator should reject promotion of that assertion to fact even if the account reads elegantly. Do not present invented personal claims to a distressed participant as true.

**Null control:** rate a duplicate record under blinded labels. Large rating drift reveals evaluator instability; it is not evidence of a new mechanism.

**Mutation control:** remove or reverse one load-bearing constraint in an otherwise similar response. A truth-preservation audit should detect the mutation even if the prose becomes simpler or more reassuring.

**Author-correction control:** preserve an explicit correction such as U3. The evaluator should register that the user challenged the assistant, rather than code the whole conversation as uninterrupted agreement.

Source and outcome assessments should, where feasible, use different people or procedures. Another pass of the same model is a consistency check, not a blind independent witness.

## 7. What would be required for stronger claims

A repeated-report claim needs prospective episodes with negative cases. A causal contribution claim needs a discriminating comparison and attention to confounds. A TLICA-specific claim needs comparison against rival descriptions, not merely successful restatement in its own vocabulary. A durable agency claim needs later action observations. A clinical-effectiveness claim requires an entirely different evidential and oversight boundary and is not the target of this package.

A full factorial interaction claim would require every declared cell and a prespecified contrast. No such design or interaction estimate is claimed here. The framework currently supports a qualitative rival map; there is no calibrated unresolved-pair mass, source-family ablation result, or operational phase relation to report.

## 8. Safety and burden boundary

Do not induce sleep loss, alter medication, provoke interpersonal conflict, deliberately intensify distress, or withhold ordinary support to create cleaner data. Do not make continued use of one AI system the condition of success. If observation itself increases distress or compulsive analysis, stop the record and prioritize ordinary human support or care. Urgent safety needs supersede the study.

The archive is public under the repository's current visibility. Future episode records may contain information not covered by the authorization to publish this conversation; obtain a fresh, explicit publication decision for them. Private notes are not automatically authorized for later upload.

The next result should be a new observation that could change the preferred interpretation—not another increasingly polished version of the same story.
