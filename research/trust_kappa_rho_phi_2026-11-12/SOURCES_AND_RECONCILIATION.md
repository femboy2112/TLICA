## 1. Preserve the author's corrected claim

The proposal concerns a generative loop: context-dependent contact supplies encounters; integrated interpretations guide habitual action and future attention; those encounters and integrations develop the verification capacities through which truth-indistinguishability becomes available. Trust names a candidate mechanism within this loop, including reliance on one's models of other people and of reality. It may operate without a fresh conscious decision on every occasion.

This is stronger than merely saying that trust accompanies confidence. It does not assert that belief creates exterior truth. Nor is it the scalar identity $\phi=\kappa\rho$. Contact strength does not contain the whole encountered signal, and an integration score does not contain the whole historically acquired form. A proposed generative account must retain those structures and their context.

Two notation distinctions are load-bearing. The current cogito standing is **$1-\delta$**, while its identity integration is **$1$**. In ordinary eligible contents, **$\epsilon$** denotes the empirical robustness term. The author's informal near-one expression must not silently exchange these roles.

The foundation's **$\mu$ is a probability weighting over probes**. Later EPIC material also uses the symbol for effective availability. Preserve weighting, constructibility, availability, execution, and observed result as separately typed records; none follows merely from another's label. Likewise, the foundation's **$\sigma$ is an outside-perspective source map to world items**. An observer's proposed source attribution and an assessment of its adequacy are additional epistemic objects, not the actual source map by definition.

## 3. Existing applications: integration sites, not independent confirmations

**Cave.** [Out of the Cave](../../applications/out_of_the_cave_v0_1_2.md), route R3 in §4.6, already identifies a trust gate. Another I can extend contact, tools, and probes, while affective support sustains a difficult transition. Its legitimacy proposal asks for discriminating predictions accessible to the recipient's toolkit. Trust therefore has an existing operational role; the current proposal generalizes its reach. The application's claims do not establish a universal psychology of trust.

**Agency.** [Agency Architecture reader guide](../../docs/app-agency-architecture.md), “The orders of willing,” “The taxonomy of variation,” and “Responsibility, done carefully,” separates skilled automatic operation, manipulation, ownership, responsibility, and accountability. An action may feel coherent and owned while its options have been externally shaped. This is a useful location for the proposed trust–power connection; felt coherence alone cannot diagnose either trustworthy sourcing or manipulation.

**Affect.** [Affective Epistemic Compression](../../docs/affective-epistemic-compression.md), “The affective closure loop,” permits increased commitment without changed evidence. Its proposed affect-to-probe-suppression mechanism remains conjectural. Apply the same distinction to trust: increased willingness to rely can be a real state change without being new discriminating evidence.

**Social feedback.** [Semantic Wake Drag](../../docs/semantic-wake-drag.md) already includes trust updates, expectations, permissions, and persistent institutional responses among changes to the next action's environment. This gives a location for studying how trust changes future contact. The central empirical mechanism is CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED.

**Osmotic inheritance and cumulative capability.** [Recursive Epistemic Ratchet](../../docs/recursive-epistemic-ratchet.md), especially “Epistemic compilation,” “Osmotic inheritance,” “Distributed epistemic possession,” and “The ratchet is not guaranteed,” already describes engineered environments making inherited capabilities usable before recipients can reconstruct their derivations. It also identifies provenance loss, institutional lock-in, and unequal effective access. This is the closest existing home for the author's social-learning extension. The [original research note](../recursive_epistemic_ratchet_2026-10-02.md) preserves its provenance.

**Institutional realization.** [Distributed Institutional Realization](../../docs/distributed-institutional-realization.md) describes partial local agents, role relations, artifacts, and records jointly realizing institutional behavior. It requires concrete causal paths from records through messages and role-conditioned agents to action. This supplies implementation constraints for claims that a social environment transports a model; a shared label or a commuting square alone supplies no such causal path.

**EPIC.** [The v0.5.2 master](../tlica_epic_path_geometry_2026-10-03/TLICA_EPIC_v0.5.2_MASTER.md), §§2–3 and its read-off rules, supplies typed world encounters, internal reconstructions, provenance, and probe records. Use its separation of world items and internal contents. Resolve its overloaded availability/weighting notation before numerical implementation. The [foundation extension gate](../tlica_epic_path_geometry_2026-10-03/FOUNDATION_EXTENSION_GATE.md) governs any later promotion.

These sources share the TLICA program's provenance. Their recurrence supports architectural fit, not multiple independent empirical replications.

## 4. Compact primary-source boundary

**Hawley (2014), “Trust, Distrust and Commitment,” Noûs 48(1), 1–20.** [Publisher text and DOI](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nous.12000). Sections 3 and 6 distinguish reliance, trust, distrust, and commitment. Her account treats trust as relying on a person to meet an attributed commitment. This supplies a rival account and counterexamples to equating interpersonal trust with predictability. It is a philosophical proposal, not a consensus definition or a validation of TLICA. Its commitment concept is not identical to TLICA's integration coordinate.

**Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman (1995), “An Integrative Model of Organizational Trust,” Academy of Management Review 20(3), 709–734.** [Original article PDF](https://www.makinggood.ac.nz/media/1270/mayeretal_1995_organizationaltrust.pdf). Pages 712–714 distinguish willingness to accept vulnerability from cooperation, confidence, and predictability; pages 717–720 develop ability, benevolence, and integrity as perceived antecedents. This provides comparison dimensions for scoped interpersonal trust. It is a theoretical organizational model, not evidence that every form of experiential reliance has the same structure.

**Bajcsy (1988), “Active Perception,” Proceedings of the IEEE 76(8), 996–1005.** [Original article PDF](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~yang/courses/cs294-6/papers/Bajcsy.active%20Perception.pdf). Section II makes data-acquisition strategies depend on current interpretation and task, with feedback from processed features and prior models. This establishes relevant prior art for interpretation guiding future sensing. It does not establish the trust relation or a complete contact–integration derivation of $\phi$.

**Boyd, Richerson, and Henrich (2011), “The cultural niche: Why social learning is essential for human adaptation,” PNAS 108(Suppl. 2), 10918–10925.** [Original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3131818/), DOI 10.1073/pnas.1100290108. The authors argue that social learning supports cumulative capabilities beyond what an individual invents during one lifetime, including practical packages whose users do not possess the whole causal explanation. This is prior art for the shoulders-of-giants claim, not evidence of universal convergence or universally optimal model transfer.

**Laland, Odling-Smee, and Feldman (1999), “Evolutionary consequences of niche construction and their implications for ecology,” PNAS 96(18), 10242–10247.** [Original article PDF](https://files.cercomp.ufg.br/weby/up/102/o/2012-selecao-Laland_etal_1999.pdf), DOI 10.1073/pnas.96.18.10242. The paper models organisms modifying resources and the resulting feedback and ecological inheritance. Its model explicitly permits positive and negative niche construction. It supplies a comparison for inherited environmental modification, not a theorem about human epistemic improvement or social commutation.

The cited primary texts were inspected for these narrow uses. This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive literature review. **Novelty remains UNVERIFIED.** No cited work supplies a proof of the proposed generative closure, and none licenses a probability-of-truth reading of $\phi$. The ideal-observer proposition and learning/transport square in the [addendum](IMPRINTING_AND_SOCIAL_SCAFFOLDING.md) are separately stated assistant formalizations of the author's proposal.
