# Integration Handoff — Trust as an Operating Relation

**Status:** proposed research agenda, ready for later integration work.

**Foundation impact:** none; the frozen foundation is unchanged.

**Evidence map:** [sources and reconciliation](SOURCES_AND_RECONCILIATION.md).

## 1. Preserve the target

Develop the author's proposal that contact and integrated interpretation jointly generate the developmental conditions for truth-indistinguishability. Contact supplies context-sensitive encounters; integrated forms guide habitual action, attention, and future encounters; the resulting history shapes tools, pathways, and probe use. Trust may describe how a source or model gains practical influence inside that cycle, from ordinary reliance to existential exposure.

Do not restart by treating the coordinates as causally independent. Formal separation preserves distinguishable questions inside a coupled system. Equally, do not replace the loop with a product of two scalar scores. The question is what the full generative state must retain, and whether trust contributes a necessary relation that existing machinery cannot express cleanly.

## 2. First pass: reconstruct one complete episode

Use a concrete object-persistence or familiar-tool episode: an object is encountered, temporarily occluded, expected to persist, acted toward, and encountered again. Record the world state, sensory conditions, action, observed signal, remembered interpretation, active tool, available alternatives, and outcome. Repeated encounters are not automatically independent observations; context and the sampling action belong in the record.

Map each transition to foundation §§3.2–3.6 and §§8.2–8.7. Distinguish an acquired expectation from a verified pathway, and successful routine action from a universal guarantee of persistence. Include a changed-context case in which the familiar expectation fails.

**Acceptance:** another reader can identify which observation could change the current interpretation and can reconstruct why that observation was or was not sought. Unspecified update functions remain explicitly open. A toy episode is a worked representation, not evidence of human prevalence.

## 3. Second pass: identify what the rich form adds

Compare three candidate representations:

1. Current coordinate summaries plus existing toolkit, source, focus, and action records.
2. A richer historical integration structure retaining the dependencies needed to reconstruct those records.
3. The same structure with an explicit, scoped trust relation.

The trust relation should initially record what is relied on, for which task and horizon, how it affects interpretation or action, what exposure follows, and what can revise or interrupt the reliance. Automatic operation must remain representable; reflective endorsement need not occur before every trusted action.

**Acceptance:** exhibit a pair with identical coordinate summaries but different relevant behavior, then ask whether existing state already distinguishes it. Adding a field only counts as explanatory progress if it has an independently specified update or yields a discriminating prediction. Merely copying the desired output into the richer form is not a generative derivation.

## 4. Third pass: connect three substantively different applications

| Application | Concrete integration question | Decisive comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perception and familiar tools | How does practiced interpretation guide the next contact? | Repeated confirmation versus a withheld changed-context observation. |
| Interpersonal learning and the Cave | How can a person sustain reliance during revision of their own model? | Helpful guidance versus equally fluent guidance whose predictions fail accessible checks. |
| Delegated agency and institutions | How does reliance alter another actor's effective discretion and the recipient's future evidence? | Voluntary delegation versus compelled compliance, with similar visible behavior. |

These are representation targets, not already executed experiments. Add the affective-compression work to distinguish increased reliance from evidential gain, and semantic wake to model how observers' reactions alter later contact. Use EPIC to preserve world/internal-content typing and provenance rather than to manufacture a new numerical trust score.

## 5. Fourth pass: test the mechanism and its rivals

Keep a calibrated source, an unreliable source, a predictable adversary, an honest but insufficiently capable source, and a coerced-reliance case. Vary task stakes and reversibility while holding the evidence fixed. Separately vary whether the observer can select new contact or receives a fixed stream. Declare the preferred account's predicted differences before evaluating the cases.

The key rivals are ordinary reliability estimation, learned action policy, commitment-based interpersonal trust, vulnerability-based trust, and the existing TLICA state without an added trust relation. Include a null in which the proposed trust field adds no predictive or representational value.

**Acceptance:** a reported gain survives removal of a supporting source family and a held-out task not used to design the representation. Distinguish directly observed outcomes, finite-model consequences, and proposed human mechanisms. When uncertainty remains because no accessible probe splits the rivals, name the missing discriminator rather than awarding the preferred account truth by coherence.

## 6. Promotion and publication decision

Apply the existing foundation-extension gates only after the representation work. Require recurrence across at least three substantively different applications; ablation showing a distinction that current machinery cannot recover cleanly; a simpler rival; a predeclared discriminator; a fresh holdout; hostile and degenerate cases; notation reconciliation; and author review.

Three documents repeating one insight are not three independent source classes. Distinguish conceptual argument, independently collected observation, and independently implemented finite models, and record dependencies among them. A finite model can disclose an implication of its assumptions; it cannot establish that people instantiate the assumptions.

There are three legitimate integration outcomes: standardize a missing name for existing machinery; retain a useful application-level relation; or propose an explicitly reviewed foundation amendment. Prefer the least additional structure that preserves the successful distinctions. The immediate deliverable is an auditable research bridge. A new primitive, a psychological law, and a complete derivation of epistemic standing remain separate possible later achievements.
