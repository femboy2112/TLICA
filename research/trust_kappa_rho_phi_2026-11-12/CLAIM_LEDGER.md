## 2. Overstrong interpretations excluded by the dossier

The following are not conclusions of the author's strongest generative proposal:

- **A universal `φ = κ × ρ` rule.** The coordinate summaries discard relevant structure; the factorial fixture is a designed access–selection mechanism, not a numerical identification of the coordinates.
- **Every repetition adds independent truth support.** Exact replay of one deterministic constraint adds no separating condition. Whether stochastic repetition adds information requires a declared dependence and noise model.
- **More integration must raise φ.** Integration can support useful tools, narrow attention, or retain an erroneous source attribution. The coupling need not be monotone.
- **Contact with a signal establishes its apparent object.** Source substitution and replay are explicit rival cases.
- **Trust is identical to confidence, prediction, compliance, or influence.** Each admits separating cases. Domain, commitments, options, and exposure remain relevant.
- **A high local φ certifies the source map or exterior truth.** Toolkit-relative assessment and source-map adequacy remain different questions.
- **The finite witness validates the human mechanism.** It checks declared mathematical consequences and guards against specific interpretive mistakes.

These exclusions target stronger claims, not the corrected author thesis. In particular, showing that a scalar reduction fails does not refute derivation from richer contact–integration forms.

## 3. Independence and provenance

The TLICA foundation, its application papers, this conversation, and the new synthesis share substantial conceptual provenance. Multiple mentions across those documents are not independent empirical confirmations. The assistant reviewers are not blind human observers or independent experimental replications. Their role is to locate mistakes in typing, logic, and implementation.

The proof arguments and executable fixtures provide different checking routes for narrow finite/mathematical claims, while sharing the same definitions. Their agreement does not establish those definitions as an adequate model of people. The primary trust and active-perception sources supply distinct prior-art constraints; they do not prove the new synthesis by association.

## 4. Dominance boundary

The proposal earns its place as an **explicit research bridge**: it identifies where trust may operate in an already coupled architecture and supplies failures a candidate representation must distinguish. It has not yet earned a claim to outperform an existing psychological theory, supply a uniquely necessary new primitive, or derive epistemic assessment from first principles.

The next verdict-changing result is a declared representation and update rule that predicts an independently specified holdout more accurately or with fewer unsupported assumptions than the same TLICA machinery without the added trust relation. A negative result can still justify standardizing vocabulary while declining foundation promotion.
