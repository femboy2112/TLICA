## A realistic first probe: harmless vignette/task panel

Use consenting adult volunteers (or archival non-sensitive behavioural datasets) only. Do **not** deliberately induce personal trauma, real workplace retaliation, financial loss, religious guilt, threats, or social ostracism.

**Define before collection:**
- Modes $r$: at least three innocuous forms of reversible rule reconsideration, plan change, or belief update, with comparable objective content.
- Contexts $c$: at least one neutral reference plus two safe changes in framing or personally meaningful relevance, randomized and counterbalanced.
- Input drive $J_r$: standardized prompts with measured effort/knowledge to avoid confusing a stronger instruction with stronger stiffness.
- $\rho$-related self-report and separate salience, fatigue, threat, attention and available-control metrics.
- Response dynamics: repeated low-stakes deviations under step/release/variable-duration prompts. Fit $K,\Gamma,M$ where jointly estimable, with confidence intervals and model checks.

**Primary preregistered criterion:** In held-out contexts/modes, a fixed-one-field rank-one model of $\Delta K$ must predict as well as a prespecified error tolerance, **and** outperform salience-only and one-factor-fatigue alternatives on a proper held-out score. If not, no HIGGS mechanism claim.

### Outcomes

**Pass (conditional support only):** rank-one shifts survive controlled inference, remain stable in new subjects/contexts, and add distinct predictive information beyond $\rho$, $\Gamma$, effort, incentives, and arousal. This supports a *common background loading factor*, not literal symmetry breaking or uniqueness of the Higgs analogy.

**Fail:** reliable $\mathrm{rank}\ge2$, persistent opposite signs where single $h^2$ shift predicts same sign, no coupling-specific effect, or no incremental performance beyond simpler rivals. Record an explicit negative result.

**Ambiguous:** drive not controlled, data insufficient to estimate frequency dynamics, $\rho$ and $h$ inseparable, nonstationary $g$, or partial observability invalidates factorization. Do not repair the model post hoc by freeing all its parameters.

### Controls

1. **Positive mathematical control:** synthesize exact rank-one data with nonzero couplings.
2. **Negative mathematical control:** deliberately perturb one cell to force a nonzero minor.
3. **Null controls:** $g_r=0$ mode or $h_c=h_0$ context must produce no field-derived shift.
4. **Sign mutation:** $h\to-h$ leaves every induced $K$ unchanged.
5. **Mechanism mutation:** vary $\Gamma$ but not $K$; see whether fitting correctly separates damping from gap.
6. **Held-out modes:** unseen content/referents not used to fit $g_r$.
7. **Held-out contexts:** new background variations not used to fit $h_c$.
8. **Observation-map test:** run independent measurements of drive $J$, attention, salience, and response; merely rating a phrase “heavy” cannot identify $K$.
9. **Source adequacy:** use independent analyst/preregistration and report $\sigma$ source-map limitations, not just same-model agreement.

## Red-team counterexamples to preserve

- **High identity correlation but low transition resistance:** a well-integrated skill or sincerely held value that is smoothly updated by evidence. If found, supports separation of $\rho$ and $g$ rather than redefining either.
- **Low identity correlation but high resistance:** hard external penalty or unavoidable physical constraint. A psychophysical field alone should not absorb this.
- **Large apparent discomfort but rapid policy adjustment:** salience may be high while control remains available; weight isn't action inertia.
- **Large delay but ordinary eventual response:** inertia/damping rather than restoring gap.
- **Same content, no known context change, non-reproducible effect:** ordinary measurement noise or individual variability rather than universal field.

## Review path and integration gate

1. Keep this in a dated research dossier. Obtain author feedback on whether “mass” means *change threshold*, *reaction latency*, *exhaustion*, or *downstream consequence*. The current package intentionally distinguishes all three.
2. Validate the mathematical toy with an independent implementation (e.g. SymPy symbolic + NumPy eigenanalysis) and mutation control. **Current 14 tests are one local Python implementation, not independent verification.**
3. Seek existing research on dynamical decision making, cognitive inertia, predictive processing, switching costs, attractor dynamics, hysteresis and motivation before asserting novelty.
4. Run the smallest discriminating study only if an ethical and reliable design is possible.
5. Do not modify TLICA's $\rho,\kappa,\varphi,\sigma,\mu$, the $\lambda$-routing distinction, Posit G, or its baseline law until a separate foundation proposal passes independent review.
6. This project does not prescribe a diagnosis, a technique for influencing unconsenting people, or a political belief. Use it to study generic dynamics of meaning/resistance, with safeguards for autonomy.

**Claim debt:** the biggest unearned assumption is the existence of a separable shared background $\mathcal H(G,c)$ with fixed couplings $g_r$. The rank-one test is a lamp for this—not proof there is exactly one Higgs-like field in consciousness.
