It might arise because the proposition's response mode couples to a **slow background of learned identity, institutional expectation, and anticipated consequence**.

## The core construction

For dimensionless response-mode amplitudes \(q_r(t)\), a *scalar-sector analogy* takes the potential
\[
U(h)=\frac{\eta}{4}(h^2-v^2)^2,\qquad \eta>0,\ v>0,
\]
which has two degenerate minima \(h=\pm v\), and a background-dependent mode coefficient
\[
\boxed{K_r(h)=m_{0,r}^2+g_r^2h^2.}
\]
With \(h=\pm v\), that mode gains an additional **quadratic gap** \(g_r^2v^2\). If \(g_r=0\), it gains none. If \(v=0\), it gains none. Different modes can therefore remain comparatively light or become heavy in *the same background*.

This is a pedagogical real-scalar portal-like toy, **not the literal Standard Model Higgs Lagrangian**. The double well is a proposed learnable order-parameter model; no empirical symmetry breaking of cognition has been demonstrated. The \(h\mapsto-h\) sign is unidentifiable from a quadratic coupling alone.

With damping introduced explicitly, a frozen-background mode obeys
\[
\ddot q_r+\Gamma_r\dot q_r+K_r(h)\,q_r=J_r(t).
\]
Under sustained constant \(J_r=J\), positive \(K\), and stable damping, the asymptotic response is \(q_r^{\star}=J/K_r\). The field thus changes the **steady-state compliance/deflection sensitivity**; it does *not* by itself dissipate energy.

## A risky consequence: single-field rank-one transport

The strongest empirical challenge to the analogy isn't a story about someone “feeling weighed down.” It is **cross-context factorization**.

Holding mode-specific \(g_r\) and baseline \(m_{0,r}\) fixed, let the shared background be \(h_c\) in context \(c\). Then:
\[
\Delta K_{r,c}=K_{r,c}-K_{r,c_0}
=g_r^2(h_c^2-h_{c_0}^2).
\]

**Therefore the entire matrix \((\Delta K_{r,c})\) has rank at most one.**

For any two modes \(i,j\) and two contexts \(c,d\), every 2×2 minor must vanish:
\[
\Delta K_{i,c}\Delta K_{j,d}
-\Delta K_{i,d}\Delta K_{j,c}=0.
\]

If independently estimated, comparable resistance changes systematically violate this structure (beyond predeclared noise tolerance), the **single-field, fixed-coupling hypothesis fails**. Rather than silently allowing arbitrary context-specific \(g\), log the refutation and consider a multi-field or ordinary interaction model as a separate hypothesis.

A measured rank-one pattern alone **does not establish** a phenomenological Higgs field: generic arousal, fatigue, response styles, and other common factors can generate the same pattern. A one-field construction buys a stringent necessary condition, not unique mechanism identification.
