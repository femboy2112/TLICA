Source identifiers in this file refer to the standalone bibliography in the [application paper](../../applications/when_the_map_becomes_a_mandate_v0_1_0.md).

The composite archive page S05 remains a provenance warning, not another witness. Original recording and publication facsimile remain unavailable.

**Manifesto, S03:** paragraph 96 of the original publication route was inspected for the narrow claim that an ordinary publication alternative is represented and evaluated. The paper does not reproduce the argument at length. A rejected alternative is not, solely on that evidence, an alternative the actor could not represent.

**Prosecution selection, S06:** the relevant April 1971 discussion was revisited. The web document is legal advocacy with selected attributed writings, not a neutral diary corpus or a judicial finding that settles motive. Original exhibits remain unacquired.

**FBI and judicial records, S01/S11:** relevant chronology and the bounded competency/disclosure account were revisited. Neither source is treated as a complete phenomenological or clinical description.

**Bandura and McCauley–Moskalenko, S09/S10:** still abstract-level positioning. No full-text empirical review is claimed. These remain reading obligations before a stronger literature comparison.

## 4. Provenance and chronological access

Browser text and metadata were inspected. No original web-response bytes, original archival exhibits, clinical file, or interview audio are claimed to be preserved. The produced Markdown and code can be hashed; those hashes establish artifact identity, not the historical authenticity of the sources they discuss.

Source-family removal in the executable means removing specified records from a declared citation graph. It is not a blinded reanalysis, an assessment of the source's truth, or an estimate of a mechanism. The broad actor-dependent removal includes later scholarship insofar as the selected documentary claim depends on the subject's surviving records. That strict dependence check is useful precisely because it is not independent corroboration.

All material read here is development material for any future human mechanism test. Changing a filename, model, or session cannot convert previously inspected passages into a genuinely fresh holdout.
