## Conditional formal results

**P1: metric-only variation.** Under fixed baseline, field-reading, and positive tracking rate, varying ρ does not change `db/dt=γ(f-b)`. The symbolic proof is elementary substitution; 32 rational matched pairs are construction checks, not the proof of a human law. A change in G that changes f is a different intervention.

**P2: evaluation presupposes some representation.** At the same evaluative act, explicit negative evaluation of an alternative requires at least a minimal representation of that alternative. It does not establish sufficient detail, executable access, stable availability, or willingness. Evidence of public mention is not automatically evidence of a psychologically live choice.

**P3: legitimate quotient transport.** A property factors through an equivalence-class map exactly when it is invariant on each class. Proof and a finite counterexample are supplied. The eight-state institutional construction preserves its task response in two macrostates while losing an independently stipulated consent property. No real institution or consent state was measured.

**P4: permission is underdetermined by the supplied description.** With normative bridges left unspecified, the same stipulated descriptive facts admit different actor-level permission assignments. The paper does not endorse both, and it explicitly adopts a prohibition on instrumental attacks on nonconsenting people. This is a conditional non-entailment, not a claim that all normative theories are equivalent.

## Mechanism and incremental-value claims

**H1 — Attention-mediated foreclosure:** CONJECTURED. Relevant attention could alter later interpretation, source selection, and evaluation. Decisive next evidence is dated pre-action material distinguishing antecedent narrowing from post-action justification. Counterevidence includes maintained corrective uptake or a chronology reversed relative to the proposal.

**H2 — Identity bottleneck:** CONJECTURED. The available writing is compatible with intense self-relevance but does not reconstruct a max-flow graph. Counterevidence includes multiple independently sustaining commitments or revision without the alleged identity cost.

**H3 — Developmental nonstationarity mattered in this individual:** UNVERIFIED. The current framework permits it; no subject-specific measure or source-to-neural mapping establishes it. The author's personal developmental history is not a comparison sample.

**H4 — Knowing authorization rather than absent representation:** live rival, not a global verdict. A later statement of harm awareness challenges a blanket ignorance account. It does not settle whether facts, valuation, and restraint aligned at any earlier event.

**H5 — TLICA adds empirical value:** UNVERIFIED. A plain-language baseline, independent annotations, and fresh source material are required. More precise vocabulary alone is not predictive improvement. The framework must be allowed to lose this comparison.

**H6 — A corrective intervention would have prevented violence:** UNVERIFIED. Conceptual repair is not prevention efficacy. No intervention or reliable individual risk forecast is claimed.

## Remaining rivals

The paper preserves R1 attention-mediated narrowing, R2 revenge-first rationalization, R3 reciprocal development, R4 audience/genre dependence, R5 clinical/substrate contribution, and R6 knowing authorization. They are not exhaustive or necessarily exclusive. R3 must earn its extra flexibility with temporal evidence rather than absorbing every outcome.

The original [prospective protocol](../kaczynski_focus_closure_2026-09-15/PROTOCOL.md) remains useful but must be frozen anew before any confirmatory sample. Every currently inspected passage is development material. Source omissions are not evidence that the actor lacked a probe. An analyst's unknown pathway is not the actor's undefined φ.

## Executed and not executed

Executed: sixteen software controls, 32 tracking pairs, an eight-state quotient witness, permission countermodels, a narrative non-identification construction, and declared record-dependency removals. The original fourteen package tests were rerun. Raw output and actual logs are retained.

Not executed: independent historical coding, new holdout evaluation, original-archive replication, clinical assessment, empirical causal identification, or whole-repository `make validate`. The dependency audit is not a complete factorial analysis, phase transport, interference experiment, or truth-probability computation.
