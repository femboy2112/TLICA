## Family map and ablation questions

Keep separate the regional lexical family (S1), local music/ethnographic interpretations (S2-S3), discursive/industry authentication accounts (S4-S5), paired lyric analysis (S6), repertoire theory (S7), linguistic indexicality (S8), analogical representation (S9), and synthetic diffusion (S10). These are different methods, not proven statistical independence. Shared references inside them need tracing.

No quantitative leave-one-family-out analysis, Mobius interaction, meta-analysis, or independence estimate was run. Useful qualitative checks are: without music ethnography, the lived-music motivation weakens; without analogy research, originality would be overstated; without industry history, authenticity risks being treated as purely private. None of these removals changes the elementary graph proofs, which depend on stated definitions rather than authority.
