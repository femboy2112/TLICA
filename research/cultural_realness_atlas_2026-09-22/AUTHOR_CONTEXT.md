## Corrections to the preceding assistant answer

1. Overlapping memberships do not establish a sheaf. No topology, restriction system, locality, gluing rule, or empirical obstruction was supplied. The implemented object is much weaker: typed annotated graphs with declared partial transports.
2. A long bibliography is not independent corroboration of this synthesis. Abstracts, publisher descriptions, full selected passages, and deferred readings are distinguished in SOURCES.md.
3. Neither genre's commercial success is explained by authenticity alone. Felt authenticity, institutional authentication, liking, exposure, and sales are distinct outcomes.
4. Matching a social motif does not establish common historical descent, causal equivalence, or identical first-person experience. Even a shared national context does not identify the particular causal pathway connecting two episodes.
5. Repeated interaction may reproduce a local pattern; a steady state or attractor requires an explicit dynamical model and evidence. An individual-level TLICA equation cannot simply be reassigned to a whole culture.
6. No address, accent, race, class, generation, or genre determines a person's repertoire. The urban/rural example is a question generator, not a two-population finding. Care, humor, craft, celebration, grief, and cooperation belong alongside conflict in the sample.
7. TLICA contact is not generic emotional intensity; identity integration is not verified truth; the source map is not merely a person's explicit source story. No numerical TLICA coordinates are estimated here.
8. Gentner's structure-mapping theory is essential prior art missing from the first answer. Armstrong already compared rap and country. Novelty must be sought in validated measurement and prediction, not claimed for relational analogy or the genre pairing.
