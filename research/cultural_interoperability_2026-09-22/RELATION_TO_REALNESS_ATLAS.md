# Relation to the Cultural Realness Atlas

Two dossiers dated 2026-09-22 bootstrap the same author seed from two directions. They
are **one research program, not rival theories**, and were landed together on `main`.

- **This dossier — [Cultural Interoperability](README.md)** (*Shared Reality, Different
  Lives*) is the **umbrella**: the mission is reality-grounded mutual understanding and
  workable, non-coercive coexistence between particular people in overlapping cultural
  worlds. Music is explicitly *one observation channel*; the target is concrete episodes —
  disputes, constraints, consent, third-party burdens, and legitimate option generation.
  Its instrument (`interop.py`) checks reality/standing/party-floor/consent gates, returns
  the non-dominated supported options, and reports minimal blocking sets.

- **[Cultural Realness Atlas](../cultural_realness_atlas_2026-09-22/README.md)** is the
  **music/realness probe** inside that program: when does recognizing a *transported
  relation* make an artifact feel *real* to a listener? Its instrument
  (`transport_probe.py`) validates a declared partial fact-preserving injection between
  typed annotated episodes.

## Why they are one program

Both grow from the same verbatim seed (rap/country "realness" → the moonshot of connecting
overlapping American subcultures; see each dossier's author record). The realness atlas is
the built-out instance of this dossier's second observation channel — the
`music_as_one_probe` fixture here asks, in miniature, the question the atlas develops in
full. Both dossiers:

- keep **structure / history / phenomenology / factual truth / liking** strictly separate;
- treat a declared graph transport as evidence about *annotations only* — never a claim
  about a person's mind, a shared causal history, or identical experience;
- **defer the sheaf explicitly** — overlap is not a topology; no restriction maps, gluing
  theorem, or global-section claim is made;
- anchor to the frozen foundation as analytic vocabulary (File 3 §6–8.7), compute **no**
  person-level κ/φ/σ/ρ/μ value, and add **no** foundation primitive;
- separate disclosed finite results from **CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED** empirical hypotheses
  in a claim ledger; and
- keep the central empirical mechanism **UNVERIFIED** — no participants, corpus, audio, or
  mediation outcomes exist in either branch.
