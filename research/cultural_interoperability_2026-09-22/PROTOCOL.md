# Probe program and empirical release gates

**Status:** synthetic calibration executed; all human interventions, corpus studies, and efficacy claims UNRUN / UNVERIFIED. This is a preregistration-ready outline, not a completed preregistration. Sample sizes, analysis details, smallest effects of interest, and recruitment must be finalized before collecting confirmatory data.

## 1. Target outcomes remain separate

Assess reciprocal reconstruction accuracy, task-relevant factual/counterfactual accuracy, self-reported felt recognition, each participant's acceptability and satisfaction, voluntariness, burdens on affected outsiders, option feasibility, implementation fidelity, and durability. Do not collapse them into one success score. A pleasant conversation with a false plan is not success; an accurate explanation followed by a justified refusal can still improve understanding.

Keep the developmental claim separate: showing that a proposed representation helps now does not establish which historical experiences caused it. A cultural atlas should earn incremental predictive value beyond direct structured elicitation; it is not entitled to that value by definition.

## 2. Concrete low-stakes pilot sequence

Begin with invented episodes. Obtain human ethics and domain review appropriate to the planned work before recruitment. For an initial consenting adult pilot, restrict scope to low-stakes coordination tasks with genuine ability to decline. Do not begin with emergencies, coercive relationships, workplace retaliation risks, or disputes needing legal or clinical judgments.

Collect each person's account separately with explicit sharing choices. Record what can be shown to the other party and what remains confidential. Do not publish private narratives, sensitive attributes, or identifiable conflict records in this repository. A mediator-facing model must not reveal confidential constraints to the counterparty without permission.

Ask each participant to edit the task model, then test it on fresh paraphrases and counterfactual cases. Their endorsement validates intended meaning only to that extent, not every external causal claim. Check factual propositions with independently appropriate observations; retain disagreements and unknowns.

Generate options and show consequences, uncertainty, and burdens in each person's preferred vocabulary without changing those consequences. Obtain separate, revocable consent to any trial, with stopping conditions and review dates. The software must never execute an arrangement or contact people on the strength of a predicted preference.

## 3. Discriminating study P1: reconstruction versus agreeable wording

Compare three matched conditions: ordinary unstructured discussion, structured elicitation/paraphrase, and structured elicitation plus an explicit relational/constraint model. Match time, facilitator attention, and task information where practical. Randomize assignment with an analysis appropriate to dyadic dependence; evaluator blinding must be documented, not asserted merely because an AI produced a second answer.

**Preferred prediction:** the relational condition improves held-out prediction of each person's interpretation and acceptable consequences, not only ratings of empathy. Cultural-atlas features must then improve prediction on held-out people or contexts beyond direct elicitation, or their incremental claim fails.

**Pass:** predeclared improvements in held-out reconstruction with no unacceptable loss in factual accuracy, voluntariness, or burdens, assessed under prespecified uncertainty bounds. **Fail:** no meaningful incremental benefit, systematic worse interpretation, or apparent agreement obtained by omission/coercion. **Ambiguous:** weak manipulation, unreliable outcomes, wide intervals, or failure to deliver distinct conditions. Do not relabel ambiguous results as corroboration.

Include a target-confirmed accurate paraphrase as a positive control, a fluent but wrong relational paraphrase as a negative control, and surface rewording that preserves the same misunderstanding as a null control. Clearly separate stimulus construction from holdout evaluation.

## 4. Discriminating study P2: encoding versus underlying relations

Use a complete 2-by-2 design: familiar/unfamiliar surface encoding crossed with preserved/altered relational structure. Hold the actual options and stakes constant where intended; independently check that participants see the manipulations as designed. Evaluate comprehension, recognition, authenticity/realness, liking, and truth judgments separately.

The music version is one module alongside workplace instructions, household timing, shared-space use, and unfamiliar rituals. Do not infer listener biography from preference or treat a participant's cultural identity as a fixed assignment. Original, permission-cleared materials are preferable; no full commercial lyrics or audio are redistributed here.

Only claim an interaction after all four cells are observed. The factorial contrast is the difference of the two within-encoding structure effects; a nonzero term is not a mechanism proof. Alternative explanations include familiarity, craft quality, aesthetic reward, demand characteristics, affiliation, and aspiration.

## 5. Discriminating study P3: meaning repair versus genuine incompatibility

Construct cases where a misunderstanding blocks a feasible arrangement and matched cases where the participants already share the relevant causal model but their stated floors conflict. Add an unknown-physical-effectiveness condition and a missing-candidate condition.

The preferred system should distinguish: repair the mapping; obtain evidence; search for a new option; report a bounded incompatibility; or respect refusal. A method that declares harmony in every condition fails calibration. High satisfaction ratings do not rescue a knowingly infeasible plan.

## 6. Multi-party composition and durable trials

Extend from pairs to three or more parties and shared resources. Include the pairwise-intersection counterexample as a negative control. Check whether agreements create incompatible obligations elsewhere. Repeat outcome checks after implementation, including what happened to unrepresented people and whether refusal remained viable.

Claims of durable coexistence require follow-up observations. No fixed follow-up interval is intrinsically sufficient: specify the task horizon and recurrence frequency before analysis. Distinguish temporary de-escalation, stable procedures, trust repair, and value convergence.

## 7. Provenance, ablations, and withheld data

Split data by person/dyad and relevant source family; near-duplicate statements, repeated songs, common artists, and shared interview origins must not leak across train/test boundaries. Track participant testimony, observed behavior, institutional records, and cultural literature as different evidence types. Multiple URLs to one source are not independent witnesses.

Preserve the unadjusted data and all source adjustments. Run leave-one-family-out analyses where the design makes them meaningful. Compare simplified baselines: words alone, direct elicitation alone, relation structure alone, and the full proposed representation. Remove unnecessary complexity when a simpler account survives the same holdouts.

Same-model re-analysis is not a blinded independent witness. The present code's set oracle and procedural solver differ in implementation expression but share authoring provenance. They corroborate software consistency, not a human mechanism.

## 8. Privacy, standing, and failure conditions

Participants retain correction, refusal, withdrawal, and reasonable data minimization. Do not profile protected traits from names, voices, addresses, genres, or inferred affiliations. Group-level findings never silently fill person-level missing data. Every atlas claim names its population and collection period.

Disclose incompatible normative premises rather than hide them inside an objective function. Mutual desire cannot license harms to absent people. No persuasion targeting or ideological conversion is part of this project. A culturally fluent formulation must preserve factual content and decision authority, not exploit identity coupling to secure agreement.

All relevant side effects and ordinary alternatives remain part of the assessment. Stop or narrow the intervention when data are unreliable, consent is compromised, one party cannot safely refuse, or the task exceeds the team's competence.
