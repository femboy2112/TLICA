## Next work, in priority order

**1. Harden the representation contract.** Separate normative declarations, empirical measurements, participant interpretations, and permissions into typed records with timestamps and expiry. The current source-status flag is only a synthetic exercise assumption. Add partial preference orders, explicit affected-party inventories, task horizons, review/withdrawal rules, and a format for unresolved rival meanings. Do not add inferred demographic profiling. Acceptance: malformed, stale, mismatched-task, and missing-consent controls fail closed without silently converting uncertainty into impossibility.

**2. Build actual mismatch localization.** The current engine receives hand-encoded gates; it does not parse natural language or infer why anyone clashes. Start with a small human-reviewed, original vignette corpus covering work, family timing, shared spaces, rituals, and music. Keep meaning/fact/value/resource/trust/procedure obstructions separate. Acceptance: fresh vignettes distinguish missing evidence from real incompatibility and identify where the preferred interpretation is wrong. Compare against simple structured paraphrase.

**3. Expand options, not only descriptions.** Implement explicitly permitted candidate-generation operators: schedules, sequencing, separate-use arrangements, reversible trials, or new resources. Evaluate against the same unreduced constraints and possible externalities. Acceptance: a search can produce a new catalog-relative feasible option without changing a participant's floor or treating their predicted benefit as consent. Also retain cases where no such option is found.

**4. Earn the cultural atlas.** Verify the remaining literature and create provenance-bearing entries about practices and interpretations, with population/time boundaries and variation. Use entries to ask better questions. Acceptance: incremental benefit over direct elicitation on withheld people/contexts, not a visually appealing cluster map or replay of fitted examples.

**5. Test the human benefit.** Finish the ethics, consent, sampling, measurement, and analysis plans in PROTOCOL.md. Conduct low-stakes pilot work only with appropriate review and voluntary participation. Acceptance: each participant's outcomes, reality accuracy, burdens, and durable implementation are assessed separately. No real-person efficacy claim before suitable data exist.

**6. Study composition.** Add multi-party resources, conflicting obligations, and institutional context. The three-party counterexample is mandatory regression coverage. Consider sheaf/presheaf or other categorical machinery only after defining maps and a concrete gain over finite constraints. Pairwise success cannot certify global compatibility.
