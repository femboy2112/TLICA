The older application's section 2.3 uses looser glosses for phi and kappa than the current foundation. Do not propagate them as definitions. This dossier records that seam without modifying the old paper or its political examples.

## Canonical diagnostic boundary

[Foundation File 3](../../foundation/3_formal_apparatus.md), sections 6.1, 6.3, 7.1–7.3, and 8.7 supply the relevant boundary:

- Kappa is contact, not aesthetic liking, cultural credibility, or a synonym for felt intensity.
- Phi is toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability/pathway standing, not raw confidence, social agreement, external correspondence by itself, or a feeling that something is real. Undefined phi means no constructible verification route, not falsehood.
- Rho concerns identity/commitment integration. It is not a warrant to manipulate someone or an evidence weight for factual truth.
- Sigma is a source map; its adequacy must not be inferred from knowing a source's name. The full foundation source map is outside-perspective, not directly inspectable through introspection.
- Probe availability and repertoire/closure remain distinct from coherence and discrimination.

No person-level kappa, phi, rho, sigma, or mu value is calculated by the prototype. Its three-valued gate bookkeeping is an application-level software interface, not an implementation of the foundation's numerical coordinates. Keep proposition/pathway resolution and source adequacy separate.

## What is added

Application objects: a task boundary, participant-correctable episode models, typed correspondence contracts, a named constraint/consent ledger, finite option catalogs, supported/possible option bounds, source-family ablations, non-dominated candidate sets, minimal blocking sets, and an empirical comparison protocol. Most of this mathematics is elementary and intentionally so.

No new consciousness primitive, universal cultural law, governing preference metric, or validated psychological mechanism is introduced. A more ambitious categorical model must improve operational discrimination before it becomes load-bearing. Research-tier integration on main would preserve that status rather than promote the work to a proven foundation result.
