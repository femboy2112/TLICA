No independent coder, external reviewer, original interview recording, original sentencing exhibit set, clinical assessment, prospective holdout, or empirical source-family ablation was completed. Do not read test success as evidence of any of those activities.

For a complete-checkout continuation, rerun both package commands and `make validate`; preserve their output as a dated addition. Repository changes are intended to be additions only in this research directory. Remote file-object verification is performed separately during commit publication.
