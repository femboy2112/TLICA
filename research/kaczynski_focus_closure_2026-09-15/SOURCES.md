# Sources and access register

**Inspection date:** 15 September 2026. Bibliographic dates below are original publication or event dates, not retrieval dates. This is a bounded exploratory search, not a systematic review.

A source ID identifies a record, not an independent witness. `K-authored` is a shared actor-level provenance across different Kaczynski documents. The manifesto, interview, and selected journal passages remain distinct document families but cannot be counted as independent access to his internal state. Mirrors never add witnesses. A publisher's abstract supports only its stated scope, not an uninspected full argument.

No full extremist text, operational material, clinical file, or copyrighted article is redistributed. Browser-rendered text was inspected; original response bytes, audio, and archival exhibits were not captured or hashed. External URLs provide retrieval routes, not a promise of continuing accessibility.

## S01 — Campaign chronology

Federal Bureau of Investigation. *Unabomber*. Institutional historical case page, undated.

URL: https://www.fbi.gov/history/cases-and-criminals/unabomber

**Family:** FBI-case-history. **Access:** relevant page text inspected. **Locators:** opening campaign account and arrest narrative. Supports 1978 onset, three deaths, approximately two dozen injuries, and 3 April 1996 arrest. It does not establish a psychological mechanism. Injury totals vary across FBI summaries; this package does not adjudicate the exact count.

## S03 — Public manifesto

Original publication route: https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-srv/national/longterm/unabomber/manifesto.text.htm

Inspected reproduction: https://www.thetedkarchive.com/library/ted-kaczynski-industrial-society-and-its-future-washington-post-version

**Family:** ISAIF-1995; **actor provenance:** K-authored. **Access:** relevant passages inspected in original and reproduction; no complete edition collation. **Locators:** numbered paragraphs 33 and 93–96. The mirror and original are one underlying text. They document an articulated argument, not its genesis or sincerity. Paragraph 96 explicitly represents ordinary publication before dismissing its impact; this matters for the distinction between rejection and unavailability.
