# Prospective discrimination protocol — v0.1.0

This protocol was written after inspecting the Phase 1 material. It is **not a preregistration of that pilot**. All current evidence records are development data. No fresh holdout, independent coding, ablation study, or empirical factorial design has run.

## 1. Outcome and unit

The target outcome is a documented change in interpretation, evaluation, or authorization around a specifically dated decision. A life-level story is not the unit. Each unit is a dated passage or event-linked passage bundle, with enough surrounding text to distinguish author, quotation, audience, editorial voice, and uncertainty.

Separate the date represented from the date written, recorded, published, and retrieved. Month/year precision must remain month/year precision. Unknown dates remain null. A late account of an early event cannot be treated as an early observation.

## 2. Acquire before confirming

Create an inventory of lawfully accessible early correspondence, private writings, relevant institutional records, and public arguments. Record item identifiers, completeness, restrictions, and provenance dependencies before choosing a confirmatory sample. Seek the original sentencing exhibits and interview publication/audio where permitted. Do not acquire sealed or irrelevant intimate material.

Then freeze the sampling rule and codebook in a new commit. Reserve a date-stratified set of previously unexamined items. No passage already seen by the drafting model counts as unseen for that model. A repository hash proves a snapshot, not that a coder was blind.

Read the complete Moen, Bandura, McCauley–Moskalenko, and earlier Fleming articles before making detailed literature-comparison claims. Acquire sources lawfully; missing access is not evidence against their arguments.

## 3. Coding fields

For every passage retain the source ID, document and actor provenance, exact locator, time precision, audience, genre, paraphrase, and competing readings. Code reported encounters, articulated warrants, admitted uncertainty, explicit counterarguments, revision costs, represented alternatives, evaluations, harm awareness, and permission claims separately.

Do not estimate κ/φ/ρ from prose frequency. Do not call φ undefined because a coder lacks evidence. Do not infer suppressed probes from an omitted topic. Do not translate a morally objectionable premise into a factual source-map error without showing the misattribution. Keep raw annotations and later TLICA mappings separately.

Two coders should produce independent first-pass annotations before discussion. Document prior exposure and shared instructions. Same-model re-reading is sensitivity analysis, not an independent witness. Report all disagreements, missing values, and exclusions. Reliability measures require a declared unit, category system, sample size, and actual data; do not fabricate κ statistics or numerical certainty.

## 4. Rival-specific discriminators

| Rival | Supporting pattern | Contrary pattern | Ambiguous result |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1: attention-mediated narrowing | Before relevant decisions, documented correction opportunities are progressively rejected with identifiable identity costs | Openness and correction persist through actions; alleged narrowing is only retrospective | The text gets more emphatic without evidence about alternative access |
| R2: revenge-first rationalization | Revenge commitments precede the claimed rationale, and later reasons poorly predict earlier choices | Ideological distinctions demonstrably constrain pre-action decisions | Both appear in one selected passage |
| R3: reciprocal development | Separately dated changes support both ideas-to-action and action-to-justification directions | A simpler unidirectional account explains the temporal sequence without additional assumptions | General compatibility with both motives; this alone does not support R3 |
| R4: audience/genre effects | Near-contemporaneous accounts differ systematically by audience | The same shift appears across genres and audiences with corroborating action changes | Audience and date are confounded |
| R5: clinical/substrate contribution | Appropriate clinical evidence links an assessed process to the relevant period under qualified review | Specific proposed process is contradicted by suitable evidence | Archival humanities material cannot identify it; keep unresolved |
| R6: knowing authorization | Harm and alternatives are explicitly represented but subordinated without the proposed factual error | Correction of the specific error reverses the authorization in contemporaneous evidence | Harm awareness appears only in later recollection |

These are qualitative decision rules, not probability estimates. Mixed motives are permitted. The most flexible account does not win simply by accommodating every observation.

## 5. Controls

**Positive textual controls:** clearly dated institutional facts and explicit representations of alternatives should be recoverable without speculative psychology.

**Negative controls:** technical or ordinary-life passages without the target constructs should not be coded as ideological closure merely because the author is known. Redact identity and outcome when doing so preserves meaning.

**Null controls:** remove the purportedly discriminating sentence; coders should report missing evidence rather than infer its content. Undated records must not acquire invented ordering.

**Mutation controls:** swap report/event dates, duplicate a mirror as an alleged new witness, replace uncertainty with certainty, and change “considered and rejected” to “unavailable.” The audit should flag the altered inference rather than accept the smoother narrative. Mutated texts must be clearly synthetic and never enter the historical corpus.

**Outcome controls:** compare nonviolent technology critics and explicitly violence-rejecting passages selected under a documented matching rule. Do not treat ecological concern, solitude, trauma, mathematics, sexuality, or unconventional identity as violence indicators.

## 6. Provenance sensitivity

Normalize at least three levels: hosting venue, document family, and underlying actor/archival selection. Run leave-one-document-family-out comparisons for the interview, manifesto, and prosecution-selected material. Also inspect dependence on the broad K-authored family. Preserve full and reduced views. Report which claims disappear, weaken, or stay unchanged.

No Möbius interaction, phase, interference amplitude, or mixed causal term is inferred from missing cells or literary analogy. There is no declared common-basis phase transport here; none is needed. A complete factorial design would need its own operational factors and every required cell. Family ablation is a robustness diagnostic, not independent replication or a truth probability.

## 7. Incremental value of TLICA

First annotate using plain-language historical and moral-psychological categories. Only afterward map to TLICA. Compare whether the added distinctions identify errors or discriminators missed by the baseline, improve reproducibility, and survive a fresh holdout. The framework fails this incremental-value test if it adds terminology without a defensible gain, or if its flexibility conceals counterevidence.

## 8. Advancement gates

A stronger draft requires verified edition/locator records, a frozen sampling rule, new material, independent coding or clearly labeled single-coder limitations, family-sensitivity results, and a completed comparison with simpler accounts. Causal language remains conditional until these gates provide actual support. No prevention efficacy, clinical diagnosis, individual risk forecast, or universal trajectory is an authorized output of this design.
