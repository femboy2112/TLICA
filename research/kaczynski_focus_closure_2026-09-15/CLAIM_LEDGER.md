# Claim ledger — v0.1.0

Statuses attach to the exact statement, not to a whole source or person. **OBSERVED** means an inspected record says something within the stated scope. **DISCLOSED** is reserved for the conditional chronological or logical result. **CONJECTURED** remains untested. **UNVERIFIED** means the required probe has not run. The ledger makes no claim of independently corroborated causal reconstruction.

## C01 — Campaign onset

**OBSERVED institutional record.** S01 dates the campaign's beginning to 1978. This is the date used in the pilot, not a newly established forensic result. A corrected primary chronology could revise this anchor.

## C02 — The 1983 road episode

**OBSERVED retrospective report, not externally confirmed event.** S04 attributes the episode and a subsequent shift of priorities to summer 1983. Original recording, contemporary diary, or dated land-use records could change its date, wording, or interpretation.

## C03 — Particular origin story excluded

**DISCLOSED conditional chronological result.** Given C01 and C02, an event in 1983 cannot be a preceding cause of an onset in 1978. This refutes that ordering, not every environmental influence. The report can still be an escalation account or narrative reconstruction. Revision requires altered dates or a changed outcome definition, explicitly declared.

## C04 — Berkeley departure

**OBSERVED institutional record.** S02 records resignation effective 30 June 1969. Why it mattered phenomenologically is **UNVERIFIED**. Contemporaneous correspondence is the relevant next evidence.

## C05 — Articulated argument

**OBSERVED text.** S03 presents an account of agency/autonomy and an attention-seeking justification for violence. Its existence does not establish sincerity, genesis, necessity, or moral validity. Earlier dated writings and audience comparisons distinguish these claims.

## C06 — Early revenge-oriented material

**OBSERVED mediated record.** S06 attributes revenge-oriented content and acknowledged uncertainty to April 1971 journal material. Original exhibits and fuller context are required to reduce selection uncertainty. This is not adoption of the prosecution's full causal theory.

## C07 — Rejection is not unavailability

**DISCLOSED logical distinction; OBSERVED textual application.** S03 paragraph 96 represents ordinary publication and rejects its expected impact. That passage alone does not establish that nonviolent alternatives were unavailable. Evidence of actual inability to construct or act on an alternative would be needed for the stronger claim.

## C08 — Awareness does not settle restraint

**OBSERVED report.** S04 attributes awareness of severe collapse consequences to the subject. This is counterevidence to a blanket claim of unrepresented harm, not proof that every relevant consequence was understood at every earlier action. Pre-action records are required for temporal transport.

## C09 — Revenge and ideology need not exclude each other

**DISCLOSED logical compatibility; OBSERVED scholarly challenge.** S07 argues against treating personal revenge as excluding political motivation. This does not establish their actual causal weights. Dated, independently contextualized materials must distinguish sequencing and interaction.

## C10 — Focus-mediated narrowing

**CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED.** Repeated selected attention may have changed later interpretation and response evaluation. No longitudinal measure, identified transition, or independent coding currently establishes this. Evidence before relevant actions and negative controls are required.

## C11 — Wilderness identity bottleneck

**CONJECTURED.** The interview is compatible with high self-relevance but does not establish a bottleneck, a max-flow graph, or numerical ρ. Comparison with other commitments and evidence of revision costs could support or weaken the proposal.

## C12 — Solitude degraded correction

**UNVERIFIED.** Geographical solitude is not a measure of probe quality. The corpus does not establish a causal effect of solitude. Correspondence and reading records, including hostile material, could contradict a narrowing account.

## C13 — Harvard or childhood event caused violence

**UNVERIFIED; no affirmative finding.** Relevant primary records were not inspected. These claims are not explanatory premises. Both a causal assertion and a categorical assertion of no effect exceed this pilot.

## C14 — Moral authorization is distinct from source error

**DISCLOSED conceptual distinction.** A person can accurately represent another's situation yet act against their interests. This logical possibility prevents every harmful act from being coded as σ failure. Whether it explains particular historical choices remains **UNVERIFIED**.

## C15 — Agency was entirely intact, absent, or captured

**UNVERIFIED.** The 1998 competency finding in S11 does not establish any global agency verdict. T05 supplies separate questions, not a retrospective clinical instrument. Event-indexed evidence is required.

## C16 — TLICA improves the explanation

**CONJECTURED / UNVERIFIED.** No comparison with a plain-language baseline, independent coders, or new holdout has run. If it adds vocabulary without reliability or discrimination, the incremental-value claim fails.

## C17 — Interview reproduction is composite

**OBSERVED source metadata.** S05 lists both a journal and a later documentary. It cannot be silently treated as an unmodified interview transcript. Version collation and original audio/facsimile are the next checks.

## C18 — Appropriate interpretation of the foundation

**OBSERVED pinned theory text.** T01–T02 locate the actuator through G's effect on f, not a separate ρ force in the baseline equation. No historical measurement follows from this architecture. A later theoretical revision requires a new pin and compatibility review, not silent substitution.

## C19 — A particular intervention would have prevented the crimes

**UNVERIFIED.** No intervention or causal counterfactual was tested. Proposed opportunities for correction are research questions, not efficacy findings.

## Current verdict

The chronology test resolves one narrow ordering. It does not resolve the causal source map, the rival mechanism set, or the subject's verification state. The study is neither contextually saturated nor causally corroborated. Missing early material is an access gap; unresolved mechanisms are probe gaps; no single combined confidence score is assigned.
