# Sources and claim ledger

**Research date:** 2026-09-09. **Scope:** targeted primary-source research and conceptual analysis, not a systematic review or representative corpus study. The historical sources below are not claims about the subjects' latest positions.

Read the [manuscript](MANUSCRIPT.md) for interpretations and the [protocol](PROBES_AND_LIMITATIONS.md) for tests. Source descriptions below identify access and provenance; they deliberately do not reproduce whole articles or repeat their substantive summaries.

## Reading and provenance rules

An authored text is primary evidence of a public statement or reported experience, not direct access to the author's mind. An interview preserves an edited encounter. A published exchange has multiple speakers but shared selection and editing. An opposing philosopher is a primary source for that philosopher's objection, not a neutral verdict on the dispute. Metadata establishes publication details, not unseen content.

All linked items were located during this pass. Access depth is recorded per item. Search-index retrieval is distinguished from a successful direct page opening. No paywall was bypassed; no full book, private correspondence, or participant-level dataset is claimed to have been inspected. Author text is distinguished from comments underneath it. Canonical URLs, not transient authentication-bearing download URLs, are retained.

## Source resolution versus proposition resolution

For C-03, C-08, C-10, and C-12, attribution to an inspected source is resolved within the publication/access boundary. This is a limited provenance result, not full access to the author's internal source map. The broader causal or psychological claims remain unresolved. We do not call a proposition phi-resolved merely because its author is identified. No numeric phi or sigma-adequacy score is calculated.

## Corrections to the motivating conversation

The conversation is preserved as hypothesis provenance, not cited as external proof. This paper withdraws the descriptions “natural experiment” and “Baldwin as control.” It does not retain a literal quantum-mechanical analogy for Harris, an unqualified inference from disciplinary expertise to general incompetence, a diagnosis of Dawkins's private identity, or an attribution that Dennett's reconstruction rule requires equal probabilities. The flattering claim that Hitchens learned moral courage unusually early is also not established by this evidence.

The assertion that Harris treats Muslims as inherently dangerous is replaced by examination of his actual published policy argument and its qualifications. The attempted critique of his probability reasoning is corrected because the exchange expressly contains the distinction the crude critique would deny. These revisions prevent the paper from reproducing the very attribution failures it analyzes.

## Search coverage and residual access gaps

Research targeted author essays, direct interviews, publisher and university records, a published philosophical disagreement, and a security-policy exchange. Hostile searches sought corrections, qualifications, and rival causal accounts. Broad internet commentary, image captions, reader comments, and unsourced biographical judgments were not used as verdicts.

The principal residual gaps are the complete authenticated Rapoport chapter, the full body of Dennett's 2007 response, a representative annotated episode corpus, the original empirical datasets, and direct operational testing of the policy alternatives. Search-index access to W-01 should be replaced by an authenticated stable copy before journal submission. No claim of comprehensive literature coverage, independent human coding, or successful external link availability at future dates is made.
