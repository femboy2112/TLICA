## Ledger distinctions

### Source adequacy versus outcome

- **Phenomenological seed:** one source family; *observed report*, not calibrated mechanism.
- **Mathematical witness:** tests only the abstract construction under its declared definitions.
- **External literature:** independently supports specific antecedent effects (e.g. reconstructive memory or effort allocation), not the combined architecture.
- **Fresh holdout:** not yet run, so **no claim is corroborated as the new psychological model**.

### TLICA axes stay disaggregated

Keep $\kappa$ (contact), $\varphi$ (toolkit-relative discriminability and pathway status), $\sigma$ (source-map adequacy), $\rho$ (identity/commitment coupling), $\mu$ (probe availability), toolkit closure, independence, coherence, and discrimination as separate typed diagnostic questions. A strong $\rho$ suggests stronger blinding; it is not evidence that a claim is true. An undefined $\varphi$ says a suitable path is not constructible from the current tools, not that a proposition is false. High local $\varphi$ can coexist with defective $\sigma$.

### Correction protocol for future researchers

If a theorem fails, demote the **exact** proposition with its assumptions; do not quietly substitute a weaker one under the same identifier. If a human study fails to identify the proposed mechanism, preserve all raw outcomes and update the surviving rival class. No amount of internal consistency or model-generated examples counts as a clean human holdout.
