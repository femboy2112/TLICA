### Frozen coordinate non-equivalence

- \(\kappa\): contact; not stimulus intensity alone.
- \(\varphi\): toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability and pathway state; not subjective confidence or salience.
- \(\sigma\): provenance/source-map adequacy; not a scalar accuracy guarantee.
- \(\rho\): identity/commitment integration; not evidence of factual correctness.
- \(\mu\): availability of a probe capable of distinguishing competing hypotheses.
- \(S\): foundation's specific reflexive-operating capacity residual. Wider "slack" belongs to a typed family of different margins, not a universal shared unit.

Coherence, discrimination, observability and effective action remain separate from all of these.

## B. External primary literature (verified reference metadata)

These publications are **nearby constraints**, not confirmations of the combined proposal. Metadata and abstracts checked against publisher/PubMed/archived article pages; full methodological audit and replication review remain open.

### Memory reconstruction and goal-dependent retrieval

Conway, M. A. & Pleydell-Pearce, C. W. (2000), "The construction of autobiographical memories in the self-memory system," *Psychological Review* 107(2), 261–288. DOI: [10.1037/0033-295X.107.2.261](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.107.2.261); [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10789197/).

**Supports:** the self-memory system theory describes autobiographical remembering as transient construction guided partly by the working self and its goals. **Does not establish:** that memories are stored as a literal fractal, that identity is a mathematical sheaf, or that the three hypothesized time regimes are universal.

### Deliberate suppression of retrieval

Anderson, M. C. & Green, C. (2001), "Suppressing unwanted memories by executive control," *Nature* 410, 366–369. DOI: [10.1038/35066572](https://doi.org/10.1038/35066572); [publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/35066572).

**Supports:** controlled experimental suppression can impair later recall under studied conditions. **Does not establish:** erasure of factual memory, a precise mechanism for every self-directed frame update, or a clinical recommendation to suppress anxiety.

### Expected value of deploying control

Shenhav, A., Botvinick, M. M. & Cohen, J. D. (2013), "The expected value of control: An integrative theory of anterior cingulate cortex function," *Neuron* 79(2), 217–240. DOI: [10.1016/j.neuron.2013.07.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.07.007); [author-hosted manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3767969/).

**Supports:** a normative account of effort allocation that combines control effectiveness, expected payoff, and cost. **Does not establish:** a single neural salience threshold in any individual or the TLICA-specific factorization.

### Progressive norm violation under repetition: a restricted comparison

Garrett, N., Lazzaro, S. C., Ariely, D. & Sharot, T. (2016), "The brain adapts to dishonesty," *Nature Neuroscience* 19, 1727–1732. DOI: [10.1038/nn.4426](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4426); [open manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5238933/).

**Supports:** within the authors' experimental dishonest-decision paradigm, self-serving dishonesty escalated with repetition alongside changes in amygdala response. **Does not establish:** a general mechanism of all frame changes, brain-wide desensitization, an inability to correct, or universal escalation toward harm.

### What remains outside the verified source map

- Direct experiments discriminating emotional rise, action-coupling, and interpretive globality.
- Prospective evidence for local-to-global frame authorization drift as a distinct human mechanism.
- Controlled assessments of how much original objection provenance is spontaneously recoverable after prolonged voluntary reinterpretation.
- Cross-context self-state transport reliability.
- Independent empirical calibration of the broader slack family.

Do not cite the papers above as if they had tested any of these mechanisms.

## C. Analogy bridge contracts

### C1. Quantum tunneling / barrier traversal

**Transports:** missing direct edge does not preclude a multi-step path.  
**Does not transport:** physical quantum amplitudes, energy barriers, wavefunctions, Born probabilities, or violation of objective constraints. This analogy is graph reachability only.

### C2. Holographic boundary encoding

**Transports:** a present summary can retain a compressed imprint of past information, potentially inadequate for perfect reconstruction.  
**Does not transport:** AdS/CFT duality, entropy-area laws, invertible holography, actual cortical encoding format, or guaranteed monotone dissipation.

### C3. Signal slew rate / emotional buffer

**Transports:** eventual peak and time-course are separable observational quantities.  
**Does not transport:** evidence of a literal nervous-system slew-rate limiter, identification of a neurotransmitter action, or a unique prediction about decision delays.

### C4. Changing the judge while changing the state

**Transports:** a state-dependent local acceptance predicate can change during sequential updating, and local approval need not imply a fixed global guard.  
**Does not transport:** proof that a person is consciously deceptive, morally culpable, psychotic, or permanently unreachable by correction.

### C5. A self as locally compatible views

**Transports:** a single globally continuous object might admit multiple context-relative descriptions, with partial transport needed to compare them.  
**Does not transport:** an actual sheaf, stack, Yoneda embedding, or nontrivial gluing theorem until a category, overlaps, mappings, and naturality conditions are defined and checked.

## D. Relation to truth-respecting choice

The core ethical proposal is *not* "maximize self-resonance, minimize discomfort." It is a two-sided obligation:

1. External facts and consequences must remain able to falsify a favored interpretation.
2. Lived experience, needs and commitments must be represented accurately, without making every subjective interpretation unfalsifiable.

For a practical consequential action, neither present anxiety nor its disappearance is a sufficient risk measurement. Hold an independent source record, calibrate real consequences, preserve reversible options, and permit an explicit UNRESOLVED verdict. A choice to wait must account for harms of delay, not assume that non-action is inherently safer.
