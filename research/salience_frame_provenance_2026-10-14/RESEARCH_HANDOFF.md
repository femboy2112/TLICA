## 1. Load-bearing object to preserve

**Question:** how can a bounded self maintain reality-coupled correction while affective salience, contextual opportunity, identity commitments, and access to its own reasoning history change?

Do not reduce this to "emotional regulation," "motivated forgetting," "identity instability," "reward processing," or "rationalization." These are individually informative but none is the full object. The novelty candidate (not established novelty) is their *interaction*:

- capacities can exist without being contextually actionable;
- a target may become compelling because of real instrumental necessity rather than intrinsic interest;
- an event can alter current feeling without rewriting the broader interpretive frame;
- locally acceptable updates can traverse to a state that would be rejected under an earlier/global guard;
- the memory available about those updates can become compressed or reweighted;
- a drive to resolve contradictions can lead either to correction or a self-confirming attractor;
- slack changes what correction pathways are *available*, but low slack does not logically eliminate eventual correction;
- reliable abstention and deliberate uncertainty are genuine control outputs, not cognitive failure.

## 5. Anti-degeneration rules

- A genuine fixed invariant cannot be bypassed by small legal moves. "Illegal global change via legal local steps" only works when "legal" means something weaker or state-relative.
- Provenance loss is not assumed irreversible; reconstructible outside logs are a decisive alternative instrument.
- Strong felt identity resonance warrants stronger blinding and source checks, not stronger truth claims.
- Avoid person-level diagnosis or risk prediction. The goal is an ethical, falsifiable model, not a way to induce unsafe self-persuasion.
- Do not use actual distressing personal decisions as the default experiment. Use consented low-stakes or fictional tasks.
- Keep \(\kappa,\varphi,\sigma,\rho,\mu\), slack, confidence, coherence, independence, and discrimination distinct.
- Retain refutations in the ledger, especially if a simpler model wins.
- Never cite synthetic software witnesses as empirical psychology.
- Keep the inability to decide as an explicit legitimate output, and evaluate the independent cost of delay.
