## Boundary conditions

1. **Dissonance is not a truth detector.** Aversive mismatch may be produced by true evidence, false evidence, misunderstanding, source manipulation, or conflict among action tendencies.
2. **$\phi$ and $\sigma$ remain separate.** A content can survive the current toolkit while resting on an inadequate provenance map.
3. **Undefined $\phi$ is not falsity.** It means no constructible verification pathway is presently available under the defined toolkit.
4. **$\rho$ is not evidence weight.** Identity integration is a bias-risk variable in this application, not support for truth.
5. **The toy theorem is not a human universal.** Its purpose is to prove that evidence-censoring alone is sufficient to generate the inversion the prose describes.
6. **No partisan prevalence claim is made.** The paper criticizes a procedure, not a voter bloc or ideological population.
7. **No intelligence insult is needed.** The argument is stronger when the reasoner is assumed competent: a sophisticated inference engine can still fail on a selectively censored stream.
