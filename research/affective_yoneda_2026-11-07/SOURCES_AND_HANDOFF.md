## 2. External primary-source family: common ground

- Herbert H. Clark and Deanna Wilkes-Gibbs (1986), **"Referring as a collaborative process,"** *Cognition* 22(1): 1–39. DOI: [10.1016/0010-0277(86)90010-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0277(86)90010-7). Publisher abstract describes interlocutors iteratively creating mutually accepted references while minimizing joint effort. This directly challenges any claim that partner-specific efficient reference is newly discovered here.
- Herbert H. Clark and Susan E. Brennan (1991), **"Grounding in Communication,"** in *Perspectives on Socially Shared Cognition*, pp. 127–149. [Author-linked text (PDF)](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~illah/CLASSDOCS/Clark91.pdf). The common-ground and conversational-coordination lineage is an essential baseline, not confirmation of the Yoneda mechanism.
- Herbert H. Clark (1996), **"Communities, commonalities, and communication,"** in *Rethinking Linguistic Relativity*. [Author-hosted PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1990s/Clark%2C%20H.H.%20_Communities%2C%20commonalities%2C%20and%20communication_%201996.pdf). Distinguishes communal and personal common ground; the latter is especially pertinent to sender-specific inside jokes.
- Standard category-theoretic background: [Yoneda embedding](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/Yoneda+embedding), [dense functor](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/dense+functor), [restricted Yoneda embedding](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/restricted+Yoneda+embedding). The finite graph-probe proof in [FORMALISM.md](FORMALISM.md) is self-contained; no empirical mechanism follows from these references.

These references represent **related prior-art families**, not independent tests of the new hypothesis. Specific partner-dependent naming studies and recent psycholinguistic experiments should be read in full before asserting novelty or designing causal interventions. Abstracts alone are insufficient for detailed effect sizes or exact experimental boundary conditions.

## 3. The distinguishing addition, if one exists

**Established baseline:** negotiated reference, personal common ground, and linguistic compression are well studied.

**Proposed added object:** a typed, context-transformable relational probe profile whose *cross-context coherence on unseen variations* predicts reconstruction accuracy, with separately measurable sender attribution and affective experience.

**Unpaid truth debt:** construct that category and map; show a held-out prediction the strongest baseline model misses. A purely renaming correspondence does not constitute a new scientific mechanism.
