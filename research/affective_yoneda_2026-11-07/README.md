# Affective Yoneda: partner-indexed meaning and intersubjective reconstruction

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Status:** research-tier, author-originated hypothesis. Standard category-theoretic and information-theoretic identities are established; the interpersonal Yoneda interpretation is **CONJECTURED** and its distinctive empirical predictions are **UNVERIFIED**.  
**Foundation impact:** none. The TLICA foundation remains frozen.  
**Scope:** ordinary, voluntary interpersonal communication; not a proof of other minds, an emotional metric, or a theory of human worth.

## Originating question

The author's 2026-11-07 seed considers Alice and Bob embedded in one world but each limited to their own first-person access. Each must encode an internal meaning into language; the other reconstructs meaning through a separately learned internal representational history. The phenomenological target is the distinctive experience in which highly particular wording from the *right person, in the right context* immediately communicates a rich network of shared experience (inside jokes being the simplest example).

> "You hear something *from them/in the right context*, and you immediately know."

The author asks: **"Bro is that emotional Yoneda?"**

The epistemic premise is TLICA's perspectival asymmetry: one's own experiencing is directly given, while another's experience is accessed through appearance, behavior, testimony, and inference. This is **not** a theorem that only one's own mind exists, that all non-cogito statements have numerical probabilities, or that successful communication gives direct access to another subject's phenomenal life. See [Other Minds](../../docs/other-minds.md).

## Proposed thesis (not yet established)

**Affective Yoneda reconstruction** is the candidate name for a process in which a dyad's shared history supplies a partner-specific family of interpretive probes, allowing an expression to carry reliably recoverable relations whose content is not determined by its public wording alone.

The daring version is not "people with inside jokes have a secret dictionary." That much is familiar from common-ground research. The more precise conjecture is:

> **Deep interpersonal understanding may be partly experienced as the capacity to reconstruct and transport relational meaning through *new* contextual transformations, without ever requiring identical first-person representations.**

The proposed Yoneda role is **relational reconstructability**, not instantaneous mind reading: an object can be characterized in a mathematical category by its complete, natural profile of relationships to probes. Real partners possess only limited, learned, fallible probe families. What matters is whether those probes preserve the task-relevant structure and generalize to cases not rehearsed.

## Example: "the yellow chair"

Suppose Alice says "the yellow chair."

- A stranger hears an ordinary phrase and may recover only an object description.
- Bob may recover an episode, its comic timing, a private act of care, and the reassurance Alice intends *now*.
- The same words spoken by another person, or spoken by Alice in an altered context, can lose or reverse that implication.

The expression is a compact **cue**; the remembered relations are **side information**; the recovered meaning is an **interpretation**. Those must not be collapsed into the external world-event itself, Alice's actual present intention, or Bob's felt certainty.

A small signal can support rich reconstruction when sender and receiver already possess correlated, relevant context. It does not transmit an unbounded amount of new information or violate coding limits: much of the reconstructable structure was previously acquired.

## How this differs from three easier ideas

1. **Memorized associations:** Bob associates a known phrase with a known episode. A lookup table can do that. The Yoneda-shaped hypothesis demands structured *transfer*: intelligible changes of phrasing, perspective, emotion, or circumstance should produce appropriately related changes in meaning.
2. **Generic intimacy / warmth:** closeness may increase trust, confidence, and salience. None of those guarantees accurate reconstruction. We must test accuracy and calibration independently of feeling understood.
3. **Omniscient Yoneda:** full Yoneda's faithful representation concerns an actual mathematical category and *all* its morphisms. A person's available interactions are at best a **restricted probe family**, and the category of human meanings has not been defined or shown empirically adequate.

**Success condition for a future TLICA contribution:** produce a specified, composition-respecting category and observation map; predict a phenomenon not already captured by lexical familiarity, common ground, task learning, and ordinary contextual inference; defeat those simpler rivals on held-out evidence. Only then elevate the candidate mechanism beyond an interpretive research note.

**Boundary:** the feeling of being understood is real as a reported experience. It is neither proof that Alice and Bob have the same qualia nor proof that Bob has recovered Alice's actual private state.
