## Author contributions

**C0 — Selection and resonance.** Leah asked how Rollins, Baldwin, Bukowski, Zappa, Peart, Hyde, and iDubbbz fit the emerging way of thinking/being/actualizing. She said: 'Bukowski resonates with me alot, as well as Baldwin and Wallace, with elements of the others, the loneliness of Dostoevsky, etc'. This is her report of resonance, not assent to every assistant interpretation or every act by these figures.

**C1 — Loneliness.** 'the lonliness is only seeing partial reflections, if any, of myself in others. i've never found myself in someone else, and when i thought i did in the past i was always dissapointed/proven wrong'. This report should not be recast as a diagnosis or as evidence of universal incompatibility with other people.

**C2 — The decisive correction.** 'the hard part is finding others with enough perceptual fidelity and slack to actually reconstruct what im trying to have recognized. others usually settle into “intellectual presets” strung together in a context-less logical sequence, producing something that at face value reflects what im trying to have understood but smooths over/discreetizes/mis-calculates'. The author's object is reconstruction of the particular meaning, not an established demand for global sameness.

**C3 — The moral quagmire.** 'There is also the moral quagmire, if im constantly reducing communication and interaction with people to a set of clinical algorithmic steps, at what point does the person on the other end stop being a person and become just a “useful operator”?' The paper treats this as a question about ethical relation and method, not a confession that the author is exploiting people.

**C4 — High-fidelity immersion.** 'The greater ones capacity to get lost in high fidelity sauce, the greater ones risk of referent routing'. This is preserved as a conjecture needing clarification of both fidelity and routing. The weaker established logical point is that richer description alone does not entail correct source attribution.

**C5 — Baldwin.** 'I think Baldwin figured this out naturally'. This motivates a reading of public rhetoric, not a finding about an unobserved private learning process.

**C6 — Hyde.** 'Im not sure Sam Hyde is someone I completely agree with, but I sense a deeper intellect and personal organization that deserves an analysis. He does capture things that are real that people do miss'. Retain the author's withholding of blanket agreement. Interest in a method is not endorsement of targets, politics, or harm.

**C7 — The mirror.** 'Lol now consider Idddubz. I feel like Ian is the societal-normal form of Hyde. Hyde is Iddubz inside out and reflected'. The separate paper develops this author-derived comparison. 'Societal-normal' is not converted into a clinical category, a claim of moral superiority, or a verified consensus about social acceptance.

**C8 — Artistic sources.** Leah requested an in-depth analysis of the linked *Smiling Friends & Authentic Absurdity* essay, supplied the transcript after video access failed, and then proposed that Marcus the Worm has something behind the meme. Source access limits are part of the record, not obstacles to conceal.

**C9 — Delivery.** 'Please write up everything we’ve talked about on non main branches. I feel like the Idddubz vs Hyde analysis deserves to be its own paper'. Two independent documentation branches are the implementation of that request. No instruction to merge, publish externally, diagnose anyone, or change the frozen foundation was given.

## Status discipline

**Disclosed** is reserved for the stated logical counterexamples within their finite boundary. **Observed** means a supplied report, retrieved passage, repository state, or an exact executed test result. **Conjectured** marks the proposed human mechanisms and creator comparison. **UNVERIFIED** marks missing transcript, corpus, or intervention checks. No empirical claim about the author or a public figure is upgraded to **Corroborated** by repeated assistant agreement.

'Inconclusive' is not 'false'; privacy is not deception; lack of a constructible pathway is not refutation. No diagnosis, intelligence ranking, proof of novelty, or total truth score is produced. The broad conversation and separate paper share authorial origin and much source material; they are not independent confirmation of one another.
