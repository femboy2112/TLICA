## Prospective probes: none run on humans

**P1: recognition fidelity.** With consent, ask for a reconstruction of a short, bounded position. Predeclare two or three distinctions that change its implications, then present a new case that changes one distinction. Compare agreement, paraphrase quality, correct predictions, and willingness to mark uncertainty separately. Positive control: a supplied faithful paraphrase. Negative control: a paraphrase that preserves nouns but deletes a condition. Null control: an irrelevant wording change. Mutation: reverse the load-bearing condition. Fail the proposal if its preferred reconstruction does no better than the coarse paraphrase on new cases. Ambiguous if participants disagree on the intended source object or the new case adds an uncontrolled distinction.

**P2: attribution versus affective uptake.** Use fictional or voluntarily supplied low-stakes vignettes. Separately record what happened, inferred cause, person model, felt relevance, and chosen action. A change in cause need not erase acknowledged harm, and a change in affect need not establish new evidence. Controls separate additional context from additional emotional intensity. No clinical diagnostic inference.

**P3: model accuracy versus ethical relation.** Compare policies with the same predictive model but different rules for refusal, consent, and relevant welfare. This tests an annotation distinction, not the universal validity of one moral theory. A person remains a subject even when perfectly predicted. No real manipulation is needed.

**P4: externalized preservation.** Compare a brief record-and-return practice with an equally timed ordinary pause, using consenting low-stakes activity. Record re-entry, retained objections, revisions, and delayed burden separately. Do not call immediate relief a preserved truth. Failure: the note produces more rehearsal or worsens later fidelity. Ambiguous: unequal tasks, unavailable rest, or ongoing external pressure.

**P5: creator analysis.** Use existing public material; preserve source families and edit lineage. Blind creator names where feasible, retain contextual versions, freeze judgments, and test held-out material. Full protocol belongs to the separate paper. Do not provoke real people to obtain cleaner data.
