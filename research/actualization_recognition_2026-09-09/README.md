## Governing distinctions

The recognition problem is **faithful reconstruction of what is being expressed**, not an established demand for a duplicate person or total access to another mind. Immersion is not inherently a defect; captivity is the concern. Effort can serve an endorsed aim without manufacturing the aim. Sincerity, fidelity, truth, practical access, and ethical justification are different questions.

The repository's existing referent-routing application distinguishes modeling another person (T) from that modeled person being taken up affectively (R). The conversation also used 'routing' for evidence attribution. This dossier distinguishes those operations instead of silently renaming the canonical one. Likewise, phi is not model richness, rho is not an automatic measure of defensiveness, and sigma is a source map rather than an invented numerical adequacy score.
