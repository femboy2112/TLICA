WHAT IT ILLUSTRATES (and what it does NOT)
------------------------------------------
It makes the manuscript's central control-theory claim concrete and checkable:
maximizing the integrand u(x,a) at each step (GREEDY) does not maximize the
integral J = sum_t u(x_t, a_t) when the state is coupled, x_{t+1} = F(x_t, a_t);
and a feedback policy pi(x) that prices future option value beats it, precisely by
refusing gratuitous |A(x_{t+1})| << |A(x_t)| moves. This is a standard result
(Bellman); the toy just concretizes the manuscript's figures. It is a toy model,
NOT evidence about any person and NOT corroboration of the phenomenological
reading. |A(x)| proxy throughout = the open-door count d.

MODEL
-----
State x=(d,t). d = open doors in {0..5} (the |A(x)| proxy), start d0=3, horizon T=8.
  INDULGE : reward 4, d -> d-1  (legal iff d>=1)   -- resonant present, closes a door
  MAINTAIN: reward d, d -> d                        -- sustainable present, scales with d
  OPEN    : reward 0, d -> min(d+1,5) (legal iff d<5)-- buys nothing now, widens tomorrow
GREEDY   = myopic argmax of immediate reward (ties: INDULGE>MAINTAIN>OPEN).
FEEDBACK = full-lookahead optimal pi(x)=argmax_a[u + V(F(x,a))], V by backward induction.
