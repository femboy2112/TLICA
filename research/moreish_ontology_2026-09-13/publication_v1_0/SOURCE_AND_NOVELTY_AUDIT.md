# Source and novelty audit

**Paper:** *This Ontology Is Really Moreish*  
**Branch:** `moreish-publication-v1.0-2026-09`  
**Audit date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** active. This is a provenance instrument, not a bibliography dump.

## Purpose

The publication pass has two different source problems:

1. **correctness debt** — are the standard mathematical, psychological, philosophical, cultural, and religious attributions accurate?
2. **novelty debt** — after the nearest established families are represented fairly, what remains distinctive about the Moreish synthesis?

Do not collapse them. A perfectly accurate bibliography can still reveal that the novelty claim is too broad; a genuinely novel synthesis can still be unpublishable if its references are sloppy.

## Initial nearest-neighbor map

### N1. Present bias / dynamic inconsistency

**Verified anchor:** David Laibson, "Golden Eggs and Hyperbolic Discounting," *Quarterly Journal of Economics* 112(2), 1997, 443-477. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1162/003355397555253

**Source checked:** Harvard DASH author/institutional record and author publication page.

**What it establishes:** hyperbolic discounting can generate dynamically inconsistent preferences and demand for commitment devices.

**What Moreish must not claim:** that it discovers present bias, hyperbolic discounting, dynamic inconsistency, or the idea that current choice can conflict with longer-horizon interest.

**Live distinction:** Moreish contains temporal weighting, but its proposed pathology is a governance/scope architecture among heterogeneous sensors: epistemic skepticism, present reward, and moral-cost sensitivity.

---

### N2. Present-biased self-control

**Verified anchor:** Ted O'Donoghue & Matthew Rabin, "Doing It Now or Later," *American Economic Review* 89(1), 1999, 103-124. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.89.1.103

**Source checked:** American Economic Association article record.

**What it establishes:** a formal model of present-biased self-control problems, including naive/sophisticated distinctions and differences between immediate rewards and immediate costs.

**Publication use:** cite this when distinguishing Pavlov's Veruca from a claim to have invented a self-control anomaly.

**Probe:** search whether the "sensor promoted to governor" language has an established formal cousin in multi-objective control, metacognitive control, or hierarchical decision theory. If yes, narrow novelty further.

---

### N3. Intertemporal-choice heterogeneity

**Verified anchor:** Shane Frederick, George Loewenstein & Ted O'Donoghue, "Time Discounting and Time Preference: A Critical Review," *Journal of Economic Literature* 40(2), 2002, 351-401. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1257/002205102320161311

**Source checked:** American Economic Association article record.

**What it establishes:** the intertemporal-choice literature contains multiple mechanisms and observed behavior should not be naively reduced to a single primitive "time preference."

**Publication use:** useful support for Moreish's refusal to reduce Hans + Veruca + moral sensor to one discount scalar.

---

### N4. Dynamic programming / continuation value

**Anchor:** Richard Bellman, *Dynamic Programming* (1957).

**Status:** standard reference; exact edition metadata still needs a clean bibliography check before submission.

**What it establishes:** optimal sequential control generally depends on successor-state / continuation value, not only immediate reward.

**Boundary:** the paper's mathematical skeleton is standard. The publishable object is the structural mapping and governance proposal, not a new dynamic-programming theorem.

**Required wording:** "when present actions alter successor states, maximizing present reward **need not** maximize total reward." Never restore a universal "greedy fails under coupling" claim.

---

### N5. Viability / maneuverability

**Verified anchor:** Jean-Pierre Aubin, *Viability Theory*, Birkhäuser, 1991.

**Source checked:** viability-theory.org bibliographic record / book description.

**What it establishes:** an established mathematical program studies dynamical systems under state constraints, viable trajectories, feedbacks, and control.

**Publication use:** identify viability theory as a real mathematical neighbor to option preservation.

**Boundary:** `Omega_H` in Moreish is schematic. Do not say that money, sleep, mobility, relationships, and skills literally instantiate one computed viability kernel. No such reduction has been earned.

**Verdict-changing probe:** attempt one rigorous mapping from the toy state machine to a small viability problem. If it works, it clarifies the analogy. If it requires ad hoc semantics, keep viability as literature context only.

---

### N6. First-person methodology

**Anchor:** Francisco J. Varela & Jonathan Shear, "First-person methodologies: What, why, how?", *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 6(2-3), 1999, 1-14.

**Bibliographic corroboration checked:** PhilPapers / microphenomenology bibliography. A primary publisher record was not located in this pass.

**What it contributes:** first-person material can be treated methodologically rather than either dismissed or granted automatic authority.

**Boundary:** Moreish is **not** a completed neurophenomenological experiment, Husserlian reduction, or qualitative phenomenological study. Cite this as a neighbor and say exactly that.

**Next source probe:** obtain the paper or an authoritative archived copy and verify the passages used before final submission.

---

### N7. Mathematized phenomenology

**Verified anchor:** Robert Prentner, "Mathematized phenomenology and the science of consciousness," *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences* (2025). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11097-025-10060-z

**Source checked:** Springer Nature article page.

**What it contributes:** direct venue-local evidence that mathematical approaches to first-person perspective are within the journal's live methodological conversation.

**Boundary:** venue fit is not evidence that Moreish's formalization is correct.

## Candidate novelty statement

Current publication hypothesis:

> The distinctive contribution is not present bias, Bellman continuation value, viability, or first-person method separately. It is the proposed integration of **scope compilation**, an **asymmetric epistemic ratchet**, and **self-seasoning feedback** into an **authority architecture** in which heterogeneous locally-valid sensors are denied unilateral global governance, developed as a bounded first-person structural specimen.

**Grade: CONJECTURED.**

This statement is only safe after searching at least these literature families:

- hierarchical / supervisory control;
- multi-objective and constrained control;
- metacognitive control and cognitive control;
- self-regulation and implementation intentions;
- predictive processing / active inference treatments of policy precision;
- reinforcement-learning models of habitual vs model-based control;
- commitment-device and sophisticated-present-bias literature;
- philosophical work on defeaters, self-sealing belief systems, and epistemic injustice to one's own future states;
- phenomenological and enactive work on temporality, agency, and self-regulation.

If a prior source already contains the whole bundle, narrow the paper. Do not defend territory by renaming known machinery.

## Cultural and literary source audit

The paper uses cultural figures as **constructed conceptual amplifiers**, not as evidence.

- Dostoevsky's Underground Man: verify any specific interpretive attribution against *Notes from Underground* before final bibliography.
- Super Hans: **do not represent paper dialogue as quotations from *Peep Show*.** The publication working master now declares the dialogue author-composed unless explicitly cited.
- Veruca Salt: same rule. Character-name allusion is the conceptual device; dialogue should not masquerade as Dahl quotation.
- Jesus / scripture: distinguish Gospel sayings from Pauline material exactly. The current draft already notes that "bear one another's burdens" is Galatians 6:2 rather than a saying of Jesus.
- Wallace / Hitchens / Buddhist traditions: provenance currently operates at work/tradition level. Any claim stronger than "this source influenced the author's framing" needs a specific verified locus.

## Toy-model audit

The existing companion note caught a subtle issue that must survive publication:

- `d` is an **option-richness / policy-relative maneuverability proxy**.
- It is **not** literal legal-action cardinality `|A(x)|`; legal actions remain available even at `d=0`.
- both policies in the toy are feedback/state-responsive;
- the tested contrast is myopic immediate reward vs full-horizon continuation value, **not** open-loop vs feedback.

Before the supplement is submitted, fix every stale sentence that says greedy's literal `|A|` falls to zero.

## Source-map adequacy (sigma) checklist

A publication-ready source map should have independent families, not five citations all inheriting one textbook summary.

- Dynamic programming: primary/classic source + modern control text if needed.
- Intertemporal choice: Laibson + O'Donoghue/Rabin + Frederick et al.
- Viability: Aubin + optional modern viability/control source.
- First-person method: Varela/Shear + a modern phenomenological-method source if the section grows.
- Mathematized phenomenology: Prentner as venue-local neighbor.
- Cultural sources: primary works/episodes only where the text makes a specific attribution.

## Do not cross these boundaries

- Same-model agreement is not independent corroboration.
- The finite toy validates code/math behavior, not the autobiographical mechanism.
- A citation to a neighboring literature does not prove the paper's mapping.
- A vivid autobiographical fit does not establish population prevalence.
- A venue publishing mathematized phenomenology does not imply editorial acceptance.


## 2026-09-19 author correction — moral intent is not moral outcome

Leah added a load-bearing clarification to §3.3 of the publication working master.

The moral controller's objective,

[
	ext{drive perceived negative moral perturbation toward zero},
]

is a statement about **intended control direction**, not evidence that the resulting actions minimized actual harm.

The corrected causal separation is:

[
	ext{moral intent}
	o
	ext{perceived harm model}
	o
	ext{chosen correction}
	o
	ext{world-state consequences},
]

with no licensed shortcut

[
	ext{good intent}
otRightarrow	ext{good outcome}.
]

Indeed, model error can reverse the sign: an attempted correction can generate greater real perturbation than less intervention or no intervention would have produced.

This strengthens rather than weakens the sensor/governor thesis. The moral sensor can be sincere, locally informative, and strongly coupled to identity while still being a poor estimator of the total external consequences of the policy it drives.

**Author's present evaluation:** the long arc is morally better in important respects because correction, ownership of error, and policy revision have become increasingly explicit. That remains an authorial moral judgment, not a disclosed external measurement and not a certificate of current correctness.

**Current grade:**
- intent/objective reconstruction — **AUTHOR-OBSERVED / CONJECTURED as retrospective mechanism**;
- claim that some attempted corrections produced greater harm than doing less — **AUTHOR-ASSERTED / UNVERIFIED here** unless tied to specific receipts;
- claim that the long arc is morally better — **AUTHORIAL EVALUATION**, not an empirical theorem;
- present moral adequacy — explicitly **UNVERIFIED**.

**Publication requirement:** preserve this firewall anywhere the Jesus/WWJD section appears. Do not describe Leah as a moral exemplar, unusually moral agent, or successful optimizer merely because the controller had a moral objective.
