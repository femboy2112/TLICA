## Verified submission requirements

The journal currently states:

- **double-blind peer review**;
- the author is responsible for anonymizing the manuscript and associated materials;
- title page is submitted separately with author/contact/disclosure information;
- average submission is approximately **9,000 words including references**;
- submissions larger than **10,000 words are discouraged**;
- abstract: **150-250 words**;
- keywords: **4-6**;
- editable source files required;
- Word is the ordinary text format, while manuscripts with mathematical content may also be submitted in **LaTeX**;
- references use author-year in-text citation style;

The current Moreish working abstract is 191 words and has six keywords, so those two gates are already aligned.

## Unaffiliated author handling

The journal explicitly says that authors who are temporarily unaffiliated can be recorded by city and country. Institutional affiliation is therefore not a prerequisite.

## AI / LLM policy

There are two layers to record.

### Journal-specific author guideline

The journal states that LLMs do not satisfy authorship criteria and that substantive LLM use should be documented in a Methods section or suitable alternative location. AI-assisted copyediting alone need not be declared.

### Springer Nature's September 2026 risk framework

The publisher now classifies AI manuscript use by risk rather than by a binary "used/not used" rule.

- **Green:** grammar, readability, formatting, translation, summarizing author-written text.
- **Amber:** outlining, drafting sections based on author input, alternative framing/phrasing, reference-management support, and related content-shaping uses. These require caution, verification, disclosure, and human accountability.
- **Red:** AI replacing authorship or independently generating scholarly claims/interpretations without human oversight.

The Moreish workflow belongs in the **amber** category, not green. The publication strategy must therefore be:

- disclose substantive assistance;
- identify AI roles with as much specificity as the provenance permits;
- verify every scholarly claim/reference;
- keep the conceptual contribution author-led and documented as such;
- preserve the author/assistant boundary where the repository already records it.

Springer Nature's policy explicitly says that declaration itself does not negatively influence editorial decisions and is intended to enable transparent evaluation.

## Preprints

Springer Nature's current policy says posting a preprint is **not prior publication** and does not jeopardize consideration at Springer Nature journals.

Operational consequence: a Zenodo preprint can come first. At journal submission, disclose the preprint DOI/link wherever the submission system asks for prior public versions.

1. novelty relative to established self-regulation / intertemporal-choice literature;
2. whether the first-person method is disciplined enough to count as more than memoir;
3. whether the cultural/comic register is judged compatible with scholarly presentation;
4. whether the TLICA bridge distracts from a self-standing paper;
5. whether the prose overstates psychological generalization.

The publication pass is explicitly targeting those five risks.

- remove "Leah";
- remove city/contact metadata;
- remove GitHub username/repository URLs from manuscript body if they identify the author;
