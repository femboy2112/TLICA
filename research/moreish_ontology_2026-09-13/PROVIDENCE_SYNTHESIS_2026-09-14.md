## 1. The childhood premises and the hidden identity constraint

The author reports discovering, as a child / adolescent, a cluster of ideas that were treated as reality-level invariants:

- quantum mechanics is real;
- the uncertainty principle is real;
- special/general relativity undermine naive privileged-frame intuitions;
- the cogito supplies an unusually strong first-person certainty;

- God could not be safely ruled out;

None of those premises by itself generates the pathology reconstructed here.

The hidden constraint was:

\[
\boxed{\text{I am smart} \Rightarrow \text{I must not be wrong}.}
\]

That transforms ordinary corrigibility into an identity threat. The optimization target quietly becomes not merely “believe well” but:

\[
\boxed{\text{construct a procedure that prevents epistemic error}.}
\]

No finite embedded agent can satisfy that target globally.

The result was therefore not ordinary skepticism but an authorization policy that systematically downgraded information channels capable of producing contradiction, ambiguity, or revision.

## 2. Illicit transports: where good intuitions became bad operators

The reconstruction contains several **transport errors**: a valid structure in one domain was moved into another domain without a declared common basis.

### 2.1 Quantum uncertainty → generalized epistemic distrust

The uncertainty principle constrains particular pairs of quantum observables. It does not establish that every higher-level proposition is proportionally unreliable merely because macroscopic systems are composed of quantum matter.

The childhood controller nevertheless extracted a broader intuition:

\[
\text{fundamental uncertainty}
\Rightarrow
\text{global suspicion of projected certainty}.
\]

That intuition is useful as humility. It is destructive when used as a blanket authority discount.

### 2.2 Relativity → normative anti-privilege

Relativity supplies coordinate-dependent descriptions together with invariant physical structure. It does not imply that all interpretations are equally accurate, nor that an agent is forbidden to privilege its own survival or telemetry in control decisions.

The childhood moral transport was closer to:

\[
\text{no privileged physical frame}
\Rightarrow
\text{my own frame deserves no moral exemption}.
\]

This later interacted with the Golden Rule and self-sacrificial ideals.

### 2.3 Cogito floor → epistemic ceiling

The cogito supplies a particularly resistant certainty:

\[
\text{thinking occurs} \Rightarrow \text{existence is not wholly deniable from this frame}.
\]

The transport error was to make that unusually strong anchor into a standard other evidence had to approximate before receiving policy authority.

### 2.4 Causal perturbation → moral conservation

The physics-shaped intuition

\[
\text{action} \Rightarrow \text{environmental state change}
\]

was silently strengthened toward

\[
\text{negative moral perturbation I cause} \Rightarrow \text{debt I must cancel}.
\]

There is no established scalar moral conservation law corresponding to this move. The controller imported the *shape* of physical law without an operationally defined moral state space, metric, conserved quantity, or observable global residual.

## 3. Hans as evidentiary gatekeeper

The original Moreish draft characterizes Underground Super Hans as a valid skeptical sensor promoted to governor.

The autobiographical receipt sharpens the failure.

Hans also became the process deciding which other signals were admissible **at all**.

Let:

- \(H\) = the reasoning chain / ontology currently judged correct;
- \(E\) = severe emotional distress produced while applying the resulting policy.

The controller recognized a valid point:

\[
E \not\Rightarrow \neg H.
\]

Emotional suffering does not logically refute a proposition about ontology.

The catastrophic strengthening was:

\[
E \not\Rightarrow \neg H
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
E \text{ has little or no authority to alter the policy generated by }H.
\]

That inference is invalid.

A signal can be weak evidence about a proposition's truth while being extremely strong evidence about the **cost, stability, or viability of the controller implementing it**.

The missing discriminator was:

\[
\boxed{
\text{epistemic validity of }H
\neq
\text{policy validity of repeatedly applying }H.
}
\]

The missing question was therefore not:

> Does my suffering prove the ontology false?

but:

> Regardless of whether the ontology is true, what is this policy doing to the organism running it?

## 4. Affect as telemetry rather than oracle

The childhood policy treated affect as if its value depended on whether it could correctly adjudicate external truth.

That collapses distinct source maps.

An emotion can have poor adequacy for

\[
\text{emotion} \rightarrow \text{external proposition}
\]

while having high adequacy for

\[
\text{emotion} \rightarrow \text{current organism state}.
\]

Examples:

- fear may misidentify external danger while accurately reporting threat-system activation;
- anger may misattribute blame while accurately reporting perceived violation;
- shame may produce a false global self-model while accurately reporting intense social/self-evaluative distress;
- desire may recommend a poor action while accurately reporting current reward valuation.

The mature rule is therefore dialectical:

\[
\boxed{
\text{validate the observation}
\neq
\text{certify its external source-map}.
}
\]

In TLICA terms, the contact event and the source-map assignment must not be collapsed. Affective telemetry may be **real and control-relevant** while its external causal interpretation remains revisable.

## 5. The self-trauma / BPD hypothesis: claim ledger

The author has a clinical BPD diagnosis and reports that the prolonged application of this controller felt phenomenologically like torture. She therefore uses the compressed autobiographical phrase:

> “I gave myself BPD.”

The phrase is intended as a developmental causal hypothesis: a recursively maintained, chronically distressing, self-invalidating regime may have functioned as an adverse developmental exposure.

### 5.1 What current evidence supports

The National Institute of Mental Health states that the exact cause of BPD is unknown and describes genetic, biological, environmental, cultural, and social risk factors; many people with BPD report childhood trauma, neglect, abandonment, hardship, or unstable/invalidating relationships. BPD is therefore not currently reducible to a single established exposure.

- NIMH: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/borderline-personality-disorder

Research also treats emotional invalidation and self-invalidation as relevant to BPD models and phenomenology, while the empirical literature remains more complicated than a one-factor story.

- Perceived emotional invalidation / BPD features: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24700736/
- BPD and emotion dysregulation review: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31169118/
- Longitudinal partial support for biosocial model: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37702069/
- Self-invalidation in BPD: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35021964/
- A study not supporting the full biosocial genesis as originally formulated: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24585693/

### 5.2 Claim ledger

**Observed (author report):**

- a clinical BPD diagnosis exists;
- the author reports a prolonged period of severe emotional suffering while deliberately continuing a self-invalidating reasoning policy;
- the author reports that the suffering itself was interpreted as downstream consequence rather than as a reason to revise the policy.

**Corroborated at the broad-mechanism level:**

- BPD is multifactorial;
- trauma/adversity and invalidating environments are recognized risk domains;
- emotion dysregulation is central to BPD models;
- self-invalidation is documented as relevant to BPD phenomenology.

**Conjectured:**

- the author's internally generated invalidation loop materially contributed to the developmental trajectory that culminated in her BPD diagnosis;
- an embedded agent can function as part of its own adverse developmental environment by repeatedly imposing a control regime upon itself.

**UNVERIFIED:**

- that this loop was the unique, primary, or sufficient cause of the author's BPD;
- that the specific epistemic architecture described here constitutes a clinically established BPD exposure class.

The disciplined formulation is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{The controller may have been etiologically active; its unique causal share is unresolved.}
}
\]

## 6. The carcinogen analogy — useful and bounded

The author's analogy is not that mental illness literally shares cancer's molecular mechanism. The analogy concerns **causal exposure without deterministic sufficiency**.

A carcinogenic exposure can causally increase cancer risk without every exposed person developing cancer and without one being able to reconstruct the exact molecule that initiated an individual's eventual tumor.

The proposed structural analogy is:

\[
\text{repeated severe self-invalidation / distress}
\rightarrow
\text{developmental pressure on regulation and self-model}
\rightarrow
\text{possible contribution to later pathology}.
\]

The analogy is useful only at that level. It must not be used to pretend that the clinical causal evidence for this particular self-imposed epistemic regime is as mature as the evidence linking known carcinogens to cancer.

## 7. The Karmic Accountant: moral integral control

The autobiographical reconstruction adds a third sensor/governor.

### 7.1 Inputs

The moral controller appears to have combined:

- Golden Rule / reciprocity;
- uncertainty about God and therefore refusal to dismiss moral stakes;
- causal-physics intuition: actions perturb the environment;
- relativity-coded anti-privilege: my perspective should not receive a magical exemption;
- Jesus / WWJD as an idealization of radical sacrificial goodness;
- identity constraint: being wrong about harming someone is morally dangerous.

### 7.2 Derived internal target

The resulting internal rule was approximately:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Drive the negative moral perturbations I produce toward }0.
}
\]

That turns reciprocity into an error-cancellation problem.

Let \(h_t\ge0\) denote perceived harm attributable to the self and \(r_t\) perceived repair. A schematic moral-debt accumulator is

\[
D_{t+1}=D_t+h_t-r_t.
\]

The controller attempts

\[
D_t\to0.
\]

But the residual is not globally observable:

- downstream consequences are incompletely known;
- inaction also perturbs the world;
- harms and benefits are heterogeneous rather than obviously scalar;
- counterfactual worlds are unavailable for direct measurement;
- some losses cannot literally be “cancelled” by unrelated later benefit.

This makes exact zero an ill-posed setpoint.

### 7.3 Moral integral windup

When a controller integrates an error it cannot actually drive to zero, the accumulated command can continue increasing despite actuator limits.

The moral analogue is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{uncertain/unavoidable harm}
\rightarrow
\text{debt}
\rightarrow
\text{compensation}
\rightarrow
\text{no certificate of zero}
\rightarrow
\text{more debt}.
}
\]

The self becomes the obvious available actuator.

## 8. The privileged-frame contradiction

The moral system was intended to reject selfish privilege, but it quietly introduced a different privilege:

\[
w_{\text{others}}=1,
\qquad
w_{\text{self}}\approx0.
\]

That is not frame-neutral. It designates the self as the location onto which error may be dumped.

If the protected state is the world of morally relevant persons and the author is one such person, then the self must remain inside the moral objective:

\[
X=(L,E),
\]

where \(L\) is Leah and \(E\) the remainder of the environment.

Reducing a small loss in \(E\) by creating an arbitrarily large loss in \(L\) is not automatically moral merely because \(L\) is the acting coordinate.

The Golden Rule therefore cuts both ways.

## 10. Accidentally weaponized WWJD

The author did not identify as straightforwardly Christian. Her upbringing was, in her words, **Christian-coded**: baptized, early Sunday school, stronger exposure through grandparents than through parental enforcement, alongside a mother described as “woowoo spiritual” and a father described as a “spiritually embedded realist.” Later development includes the author's TJ/TAA arc, Hitchens, and other skeptical / plural influences.

Nevertheless, the Jesus-figure — **as allegedly represented to the author**, not as a historically adjudicated claim here — remained a moral idealization.

The important object for *Moreish* is therefore not Christian doctrine.

It is the internal question:

\[
\text{WWJD?}
\]

compiled by this particular controller.

A legitimate Jesus-shaped sensor can encode:

- solidarity with people in pain;
- refusal to treat status as moral worth;
- willingness to bear real cost for others;
- mercy;
- attention to the excluded;
- moral seriousness about how one's actions affect others.

The failure occurs when that sensor is promoted to a global actuator rule:

\[
\boxed{
\text{goodness} \approx \text{willingness to absorb the cost personally}.
}
\]

Coupled to the zero-debt controller, this becomes:

> if harm exists and I can pay some of the cost, perhaps I am morally required to pay it.

Coupled to Hans, affective protest cannot easily veto the policy because pain does not refute the moral proposition.

Coupled to Veruca, prolonged deprivation eventually produces present-reward revolt.

Thus the joke is technically accurate:

\[
\boxed{\text{accidentally weaponized WWJD}.}
\]

This is an autobiographical interpretation of how an ideal was internalized, **not** a claim that Jesus, Christianity, or WWJD inherently implies pathological self-sacrifice.

## 13. The central correction

The mature system need not kill any of the three sensors.

Hans remains useful.

Veruca remains useful.

The sacrificial moral ideal remains useful.

The correction is an authority architecture:

\[
\boxed{\text{sensor} \neq \text{governor}.}
\]

and a source-map discipline:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a signal need not adjudicate ontology in order to constrain policy}.
}
\]

Affect can say “this controller is hurting me” without proving a metaphysical thesis false.

Moral concern can say “other people matter” without making the self morally weightless.

Skepticism can say “this is only a model” without deleting causal coupling.

Present reward can say “this life must be inhabitable” without deleting future option value.

Sacrifice can remain sacred without becoming compulsory self-erasure.
