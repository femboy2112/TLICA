# Abstract

This paper proposes a failure mode of coercive policy: defeating present adversaries through conduct that helps reproduce future adversarial commitments. A conflict can transmit more than a narrative. It can repeatedly generate the losses, threats, and humiliations through which successive generations experience an inherited interpretation as newly confirmed. The Two-Layer Identity-Correlation Architecture supplies distinctions among injury, contact, source attribution, identity integration, modeling another subject, affective uptake, and the availability of alternative courses of action. These distinctions allow the paper to separate accurate grief from generalized blame, causal explanation from moral permission, and immediate control from intergenerational success.

Three extensions organize the analysis. First, demands for understanding or empathy are goal-relative tasks whose feasibility depends on material, developmental, cultural, social, and institutional conditions. A policy may demand interpretive work while continuously removing the slack required to perform it. Second, prolonged conflict can cross an epistemic access horizon and undergo motivational renewal: even an authoritative resolution of the originating dispute need not remove reasons for fear created since. Third, divergent cultural projects can remain operationally coupled within shared institutions while their composition or alternation becomes increasingly disruptive. An exact linear counterexample demonstrates why the stability of individual operators, or their average, does not establish the stability of switching between them; it does not diagnose a historical society.

Hearts and minds is reconstructed as reciprocal, equal-standing inquiry into what success means to affected people, followed by accountable changes to action and outcomes. It is neither indoctrination nor a requirement that victims forgive. Its permissibility depends on an ethically defensible mission, rights that are not traded for compliance, representation of internal disagreement, and a real possibility that listening revises the mission. Existing research constrains several links but does not validate the complete TLICA mechanism. The paper closes with discriminating probes rather than a claim that compassion, communication, or force has a context-free effect.

# 1. Defeating an opponent and reproducing one

A war can defeat its present opponents while creating the experiences through which the next generation learns that the war must continue. The relevant strategic object is therefore not only the adversary who can act now, but the process through which future actors acquire motives, affiliations, options, and capacities.

The title names an adversarial classification that the paper interrogates. It does not declare the children enemies. Nor does it assume that their parents were culpable: a child can inherit the consequences of acts committed by other people, by institutions, or by nobody in their family. The phrase “sins of their parents” describes a structure of unearned inheritance, not an assignment of historical guilt.

Consider a child punished at school for association with other children who caused trouble. Immediate order may be restored. A different question remains: did the child encounter an authority that distinguishes conduct from membership? The event might teach that distinction, erase it, or be understood in some other way. Compliance alone cannot decide.

Now consider a child whose parent is killed during a justified struggle against an oppressive order. The justice of ending that order, the permissibility of this particular killing, and the child's loss are distinct objects. A legitimate institutional objective does not dissolve its developmental consequences. The child's attachment does not settle the parent's innocence.

The paper's proposed failure occurs when policy evaluates the first object while exporting the others beyond its assessment horizon. It counts present control and neglects the conditions under which successors will later choose whether to cooperate, resist, retaliate, or remain silent.

The initiating hypothesis identifies this as the main catastrophic failure of U.S.-and-others foreign policy. This draft preserves that ambition as a question but does not claim its comparative dominance. Establishing “main” would require a declared universe of interventions and comparison against institutional collapse, elite incentives, failed deterrence, economic interests, and other mechanisms. The currently defensible thesis concerns **a potentially major recurrent failure mode**, not an empirical ranking already earned.

# 2. What TLICA contributes, and what it does not

The analysis uses four application-level resources. [T1, The Cold Frame and Its Sources](../../applications/cold_frame_v0_4_3.md), especially sections 1, 3, 4, 6.1, 6.5, and 6A, distinguishes modeling another as a subject from routing that modeled experience into one's affective configuration. [T2, Out of the Cave](../../applications/out_of_the_cave_v0_1_2.md), sections 2 and 4, distinguishes absent verification pathways from available but inaccessible discriminating probes. [T3, This Is Water](../../applications/this_is_water_truth_respecting_choice_v0_1_0.md), sections 1–2, distinguishes possessing an understanding from making it causally operative before selection closes. [T4, Shared Reality, Divergent Maps](../../applications/shared_reality_divergent_maps_v0_2_0.md), especially its abstract and opening framework, distinguishes semantic reconstruction, truth, rights, material interests, and a genuinely feasible compromise.

These are conceptual dependencies, not independent confirmation. Their placement on main does not empirically validate their predictions. The present paper inherits T3's explicit warning that coherence, resonance, identity, and perceived truth do not provide a sufficient ethical theory.

A parent, friend, home, or community can, within this architecture, be deeply integrated into the lived self. Injury to an attached person can consequently register as disruption of one's own security and identity. The relation's political label does not control its first-person significance.

The proposed sequence is:

> Injury or credible threat → interpretation through available sources → attribution of responsibility → possible generalization to a population → identity-linked commitments and action → further injury and renewed interpretation.

Every arrow is a question for investigation, not a deterministic transition. Material coercion, recruitment opportunities, institutional incentives, and continuing danger can sustain conflict with little hatred or narrative distortion. Conversely, profound anger need not become indiscriminate violence.

T1 identifies a particular simplification: a threatening subgroup H is contained in a population P, but the operative representation treats P as though it were H. Nonparticipants then disappear as separately situated subjects. This is a representational error; it does not, without an exposure or targeting distribution, supply a numerical estimate of who will be harmed.

The failure can preserve an accurate core. “My parent was killed” can be true. Identifying the responsible actors requires additional evidence. Extending their responsibility to every associated person requires a further argument. Permitting harm to those people requires a moral justification not supplied by grief itself.

The TLICA diagnostics must remain separate. Contact kappa concerns encounter, phi toolkit-relative truth-indistinguishability, sigma source attribution, rho identity integration, and mu the accessibility or weighting of probes. An account can be coherent and strongly integrated while its sourcing is wrong. An alternative can lack a constructible pathway, making phi undefined rather than false. The distinctions between understanding a person, feeling for them, endorsing their goals, and enacting a justified response survive throughout.

# 3. What is inherited: conditions, not guilt

Three registers must not be collapsed.

**Personal culpability** concerns an actor's conduct, knowledge, control, and relevant obligations. Ancestry alone cannot supply it. A child does not become responsible for an originating act merely by entering the world on one side of its consequences.

**Causal inheritance** concerns what conditions a person receives. Institutions, property arrangements, insecurity, memories, and public classifications can persist without their current bearers having authored them. Explaining the resulting formation of a person does not establish that every subsequent act is permissible.

**Present remedial responsibility** concerns what existing actors or institutions must now stop, protect, restore, or repair. This need not be identical to inherited guilt. A current institution can continue a wrong after its founders die; an unresolved deprivation does not disappear because its original perpetrators are dead.

The ethical commitments of this paper include equal basic standing, rejection of collective guilt, protection of nonparticipants, and answerability for present conduct. They are declared commitments, not deductions from a descriptive architecture. International humanitarian law supplies a narrower legal boundary: Fourth Geneva Convention Article 33 prohibits collective penalties and punishment of protected persons for offenses they did not personally commit. That provision does not mean that every indirect cost suffered by a relative is legally a collective penalty. Applying law to an actual operation requires facts and legal analysis beyond this draft. [E6](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

The distinction also prevents a false ending to historical conflict: “The original parties are dead, therefore all claims expire.” Some claims expire; others concern conditions that remain. The right question is which present acts, risks, institutions, and obligations survive scrutiny, not whether everyone can be instructed to forget.

# 4. A painful truth can contain more than one moral fact

The slaveholder's-child example is a hypothetical, not a reconstructed historical incident. Its force is that a child may eventually need to hold several propositions together: my parent loved me; my parent enslaved others; their freedom was not an offense against me; my loss was real; and that loss does not authorize restoring their subordination. The circumstances of the killing remain separately examinable.

This is not an invitation to equate enslavement with opposition to enslavement. It separates what the child's relationship explains from what the parent's acts warrant. Loving a person and recognizing their wrongdoing are not logical contradictions, though they may be difficult to integrate within an inherited frame.

T1 proposes that such complexity can produce a load under which simplifying the model becomes easier than revising the frame. The person may not consciously invent a lie. They can stop representing whichever subjects or distinctions make the truth painful. The model becomes easier to inhabit and may feel clearer. This is a hypothesis about a possible process, not a diagnosis of every bereaved person. [T1](../../applications/cold_frame_v0_4_3.md)

The corresponding developmental possibility is not compulsory forgiveness. It is an available identity in which loyalty to the dead no longer requires injustice to the living: “I can love my parent without defending everything they did,” or “I can preserve the truth of our loss without assigning guilt to a stranger's child.”

The soldier presents a related but different problem. Repeated loss may narrow the scope of concern, increase vigilance, or make emotionally costly reflection less available. It may also intensify commitments to protecting others. Evidence of increased local cooperation after war cautions against treating exposure as simple depletion of all moral concern. [E3](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

Necessary firmness, refusal of unwarranted trust, inability to perform emotional tenderness, punitive humiliation, and gratuitous harm must remain distinguishable. A soldier need not feel affectionate to respect a person's protection. Exhaustion may bear on an episode's explanation and responsibility; it does not make cruelty right merely because a legal threshold has not been crossed. Organizational conditions matter as well: the ICRC's research on restraint examines formal and informal norms and socialization across different armed organizations. [E5](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

# 5. Four clocks and three horizons

At least four timescales must be distinguished. These are analytical levels, not measured universal stages.

| Scale | Narrow acceptance criterion | Additional question |
|---|---|---|
| Momentary control | Disturbance stops | What did the encounter teach about conduct, membership, and authority? |
| Biographical formation | Operation or punishment completed | What future interpretations and identities can contain this loss? |
| Campaign and institutional adaptation | Immediate capacity reduced | What happens to restraint, recruitment, trust, protection, and credible alternatives? |
| Generational succession | Control persists | Can successors pursue safety and justice without renewing collective violence? |

A harm can be decisive in one biography without determining every later choice. Repeated smaller events may matter more than a single dramatic one. Cohort replacement is gradual and overlapping, not a clean reset in which all inherited structure disappears.

The proposed information horizon contains three different issues.

**Responsibility does not cross the birth boundary by ancestry.** This is not an information-theoretic threshold reached after enough years. It is an ethical distinction from the outset. New actors can acquire responsibility for new acts without acquiring their ancestors' guilt.

**An epistemic access horizon limits which explanations can be examined.** Records may survive while the means to find, trust, interpret, or safely discuss them do not. In T2's terms, there may be a pathway gap or a probe gap. No universal number of years establishes this condition. It is relative to people, sources, tools, and opportunities, and may change. [T2](../../applications/out_of_the_cave_v0_1_2.md)

**Motivational renewal supplies new reasons for continuing.** Even a definitive account of the originating dispute may not remove a present threat, a recent killing, an organization that punishes exit, or a grievance created during the attempted remedy. The war can become an important mechanism of its own continuation without becoming the sole cause of it.

This last possibility sharpens the thesis. A conflict need not survive because people fail to remember accurately. It can survive because they accurately remember additional things that happened yesterday. Correcting history is not a substitute for changing what keeps happening.

Neither a horizon nor motivational renewal proves that compromise is possible. Nor does either show that the previous cause was false. They require current justification and current prevention to be investigated separately from historical explanation.

# 6. Goal-relative slack: what understanding are we demanding?

The original proposal asks whether a child could eventually rationalize what happened within a grown-up frame. That cannot be answered by a single idealized test of maturity. The author clarifies that the answer depends on the child's environment and conditions, socioeconomic and cultural circumstances, and the actual goal. A demand for empathy becomes Sisyphean when its required work exceeds what the surrounding conditions let the person sustain.

The operative question is therefore not “Can this child empathize?” It is: **what particular response is sought, from whom, by when, under which conditions, and with what burden?**

Acknowledging a factual event, distinguishing a perpetrator from a population, accurately reconstructing another's priorities, feeling another's grief, trusting a commitment, cooperating in an institution, accepting an outcome, and forgiving are different targets. They are not a universal ladder whose highest rung everyone must reach. A durable arrangement may require reliable protection and tolerable coexistence without requiring affection or agreement about the past.

For this application, *slack* names the resources and permissions left available for a specified task after other demands and constraints are considered. This is a proposed extension of the bounded will-room in T1 section 6.5, not a newly measured TLICA coordinate. Relevant dimensions may include physical safety, continuity of shelter and care, time, linguistic and interpretive access, reliable sources, social permission to dissent, protection from retaliation, and credible opportunities to act on a revised understanding.

These dimensions are not freely interchangeable. More instruction cannot necessarily compensate for exposure to retaliation. A financial benefit cannot automatically supply trustworthy evidence. A protected conversation cannot by itself make tomorrow's environment safe. Conversely, wealth does not guarantee interpretive openness, and deprivation does not establish an absence of moral agency. Capacity, motivation, perceived cost, actual risk, and justified refusal must not be diagnosed from one another.

A minimal formalization makes the goal dependence explicit. Let Reach(m,E,tau) be the set of outcomes reachable by person m under environment E, timeframe tau, and a declared set of permitted interventions. Let G be the observable target set. Then success under that model requires:

$$
\operatorname{Reach}(m,E,\tau)\cap G\ne\varnothing.
$$

An established empty intersection rules out the specified demand under those assumptions. Failure to find a path does not establish emptiness. Nor does low measured capacity necessarily rule out a developmental path that builds capacity before attempting the target. The definition must state whether such scaffolding belongs to the permitted interventions.

This is a feasibility statement, not a theorem about empathy. It also exposes a policy failure: officials may change the demand while leaving the claimed resources vague. If “successful understanding” quietly shifts from tolerating coexistence to endorsing the official narrative, a previously attainable outcome can be reclassified as failure. The requested target and its ethical justification must be fixed before evaluating people against it.

The Sisyphean version is dynamic. A program may create a temporary opportunity to hold a richer interpretation while surrounding conduct repeatedly destroys that opportunity. Progress in a workshop and failure to maintain it afterward need not show hypocrisy or defective comprehension. The relevant hypothesis is that the intervention's developmental gains are overwhelmed or rendered unusable by renewed danger, deprivation, social penalties, or contradictory institutional behavior. This hypothesis must predict failures and successes in advance; it cannot become the unfalsifiable explanation “there must not have been enough slack.”

There are at least three legitimate responses to a demanding target: make the environment less obstructive, build a supported route over a longer period, or adopt a less demanding outcome that still respects rights and the defensible mission. “They must become more empathetic” is not a substitute for choosing among those possibilities.

The required accounting is reciprocal. The powerful institution must examine the costs it imposes and the evidence it supplies, rather than treating the less powerful person's response as the only variable. Yet a participant may also understand an offer accurately and reject it for reasons that survive examination. Not every refusal is a missing skill, mistrust bias, or shortage of slack.

Most importantly, basic protection is not a reward for performing the requested understanding. Children do not owe an authority a psychologically sophisticated justification of its actions in order to retain their standing. Adults' disagreement likewise does not, by itself, remove rights. The feasibility of a demanded response and the permissibility of an institution's conduct remain separate assessments.

# 7. Hearts and minds as equal-standing discovery of success

The author's intended meaning is neither image management nor indoctrination. It begins by approaching affected people as persons with standing, listening in good faith to what success means to them, and seeking a course whose downstream effects realize defensible parts of those goals alongside an ethically defensible long-term mission. Listening must be able to alter conduct, constraints, and sometimes the mission itself.

Equal standing does not require identical power, identical knowledge, identical historical responsibility, or endorsement of all claims. It means that neither party is treated merely as an instrument for the other's plan. Their testimony can correct the model; their refusal is not automatically evidence of irrationality; their rights are not contingent on agreement. Good faith is a discipline of engagement, not an empirical assumption that every interlocutor is candid or that protective verification can be abandoned.

T1's discussion of the golden rule identifies the danger of projecting one's own preferences onto another. Substituting the other's presumed preferences only relocates the risk: an inaccurate model can support confident paternalism. The present paper adds a procedural demand. The people represented must have safe, meaningful ways to contest the representation and its consequences. [T1](../../applications/cold_frame_v0_4_3.md)

An institution cannot simply announce that “these people want security” and count any imposed security arrangement as consent. Security may include different threats, tolerated risks, political freedoms, attachments to place, and expectations of accountability. The same word can name different acceptable futures. T4 makes this a problem of semantic reconstruction and constraint closure, not merely civility. [T4](../../applications/shared_reality_divergent_maps_v0_2_0.md)

A workable inquiry would make the competing meanings concrete. What would count as being able to return home? Which guarantees would be credible? Who bears the costs of the transition? What can be criticized or revised? What happens when an official promise is broken? Which affected people are absent from the discussion? What outcomes would participants judge worse than the current arrangement, and why?

These questions concern children too, but not by making them negotiate geopolitical settlements. Their interests require age-appropriate participation, protection, and representation that can be challenged as they mature. Adult leaders, military organizations, prominent families, and outside facilitators cannot be assumed to exhaust a population's preferences. Internal minorities and nonparticipants must remain visible.

A set-valued notation helps resist a premature single utility score. Let Z_E be outcomes feasible in the declared environment, R the rights and ethical constraints, M an independently examined mission, and A_i the revisable acceptable-outcome sets of affected constituencies. Then a candidate joint outcome belongs to:

$$
\mathcal J=Z_E\cap R\cap M\cap\bigcap_i A_i.
$$

This is a specification of a desired settlement, not proof that one exists. The A_i are elicited representations with uncertainty and internal disagreement, not mind-reading. Preferences are not automatically rights, and an insistence on domination is not made permissible by sincere endorsement. Rights constrain proposed outcomes even when a powerful constituency rejects the constraint.

An empty J is informative. It may expose a mistaken feasibility model, an ethically defective mission, a rights-violating demand, missing guarantees, incompatible claims, or an inadequate representation of participants. It does not authorize manipulating testimony until a convenient intersection appears. When an aim is inherently impermissible, the answer is to reject or revise that aim, not to make its victims comfortable with it.

The standard also distinguishes **shared benefit** from **shared authorship**. A program may provide something useful while leaving the beneficiaries unable to correct its priorities. That does not make the benefit unreal, but it does limit what the program can claim about equal-standing agreement. A stronger test asks whether inconvenient information or legitimate refusal actually changes what the institution does.

The author's phrase “fight our war such that what they want is the outcome” is therefore conditional, not an authorization to wage war. Where force is independently justified, its objectives, conduct, and consequences must remain compatible with the defensible future that affected people have reason to accept. Diplomacy, restraint, cessation, reform, and reconstruction can be necessary revisions to the mission; ongoing force is not a fixed premise that dialogue must serve.

Humanitarian protection and essential relief must not become payment for political compliance. Nor should children's grief become a data source for covert persuasion. An ethical account cannot discover a person's attachments only to exploit them more accurately. That would reproduce the cold frame while improving its model.

Finally, this process need not originate outside the conflict. Local institutions, civic actors, trusted intermediaries, and external guarantees can play different roles. An outsider has no automatic access to a truer frame or a more legitimate objective. The required property is answerable engagement and effective protection, not foreignness.

# 8. Defining long-term victory without requiring universal affection

For this paper, durable victory under an ethical mission means more than preventing a defeated organization from immediately acting. It means securing defensible objectives without making permanent domination, indiscriminate violence, or the systematic renewal of avoidable grievance the mechanism of maintenance. This is a normative definition; other actors may call a different outcome victory.

Under that definition, some interruption of conflict-renewing mechanisms is necessary. A branded hearts-and-minds campaign is not thereby necessary, and empathic affection from every person is not a necessary condition. The strongest strategic hypothesis is that legitimate protection, credible alternatives, and reciprocal responsiveness can be important means of interrupting renewal. Their contribution must be established under the relevant conditions.

“Hate begets hate” is useful as a warning to inspect a feedback mechanism, not as a universal law. Injury can produce fear, withdrawal, resistance, solidarity, reconciliation, revenge, or combinations that change over time. Some conflict is sustained through incentives and coercion rather than hatred. Some coercive interventions stop real predation and make a better order possible. The paper's demand is to evaluate which mechanism a particular action strengthens, not to preassign every action the same sign.

A settlement also need not make everyone agree that the past was justified. It can protect room for incompatible memories, grief, criticism, and political opposition while reducing the ability and incentive to harm others. Survival under silence is not the same as consent, and dissent is not itself proof of concealed violent intent.

The annihilation scenario is excluded as a resolution of this normative problem. Eliminating the persons whose future must be protected abandons the problem rather than solving it. Equally, defeat does not entail that every survivor becomes a hidden propagandist. That would repeat the population-to-threat collapse the paper criticizes.

# 9. Alternative cultural operators and the instability of composition

The author clarifies the Civil War shorthand as an alternative-operator hypothesis. A military outcome can leave divergent cultural projects operating inside a shared system. Their divergence may remain compatible with institutional continuity for a period, while later alternation produces larger corrective swings and disturbances across other domains.

This is not yet a verified chronology of the United States. “North” and “South” cannot serve as invariant operators across generations, party realignments, migration, and internal disagreement. The war can be examined as an episode that transformed and entrenched divergences, not assumed to be their unique origin. T4 explicitly treats political labels as persisting over changing coalitions and programs. [T4](../../applications/shared_reality_divergent_maps_v0_2_0.md)

To give the analogy a literal mathematical boundary, let X be a declared common state space and F_c an update map associated with a specified institutional or cultural configuration c. This notation attributes no collective mind. The map summarizes identifiable practices, rules, actors, and conditions. A common state space is essential: without it, apparently opposite changes may be incomparable descriptions rather than opposing operations.

Operational coherence can mean that shared interfaces still permit action and exchange. Viability can mean remaining within a declared acceptable set V. If every admissible map sends V into V, any sequence of those maps keeps an initial state in V, by induction. This is a conditional invariance result, not proof of fairness, agreement, or convergence. An oppressive arrangement can be operationally persistent; making it less persistent is not automatically an ethical failure.

More surprisingly, stability of each update rule separately does not imply stability under switching. Consider synthetic deviations x in the same two-dimensional real coordinate system and the fixed matrices

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&9/10\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\\9/10&1/2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Each has both eigenvalues 1/2, so repeated application of either alone tends to zero. Their arithmetic mean has eigenvalues 19/20 and 1/20, so repeated application of the mean also tends to zero. But alternating A then B yields

$$
BA=\begin{pmatrix}1/4&9/20\\9/20&53/50\end{pmatrix},
$$

whose largest eigenvalue is

$$
\lambda_+=\frac{131+9\sqrt{181}}{200}>1.
$$

An exact certificate is its characteristic polynomial p(z)=z^2-(131/100)z+1/16, for which p(1)=-99/400. The positive leading coefficient then guarantees a root greater than one; the displayed roots follow by the quadratic formula. A state with a component along the expanding eigenvector grows under repeated BA.

The [reproducible script](toy_models.py) checks the products, characteristic polynomials, a closed form for powers of A, null controls, and a finite trajectory. The general claim in this example rests on the algebra, not the finite trajectory. The [recorded output](validation.json) contains 13 passing checks.

This demonstrates exactly one relevant possibility: evaluating separate regimes, or averaging them, can conceal a composition failure. It does not establish that political alternation is generally harmful, that sameness is desirable, or that the United States follows these matrices. Growth in this linear example is not deterministic chaos. The author's contemporary “chaos” hypothesis needs specified observables, historical periods, rival models, and an independently tested mapping.

A simple sufficient safeguard in a linear model would be one common norm in which every allowed map contracts by a factor at most q<1. Product bounds then give q^n. Whether institutions supply any analogous common protection or correction rule is a substantive question, not a consequence of the notation.

# 10. The historical and role-reversal examples

For Israel–Palestine, the motivating thought experiment is that a person can imagine acquiring compelling within-frame reasons after being born into either environment. The warranted lesson is humility about the formation of one's own convictions. It is not that every claim made by either side is true, that every action has equal justification, or that the distribution of power and harm is symmetrical.

Children's basic standing and noninheritance of guilt can be symmetrical while actors' responsibilities, capacities, and specific obligations differ profoundly. The analysis must preserve governments, armed organizations, civilians, dissidents, institutions, and internal minorities as distinguishable referents. A population is not a unitary agent. An explanatory model that speaks for all its members repeats the very thinning operation under examination.

This draft does not adjudicate particular current military operations, reconstruct all historical claims, or present a casualty accounting. Its bounded empirical connection is a longitudinal study of children in Palestinian, Israeli Jewish, and Israeli Arab populations, which supports investigating multiple social environments rather than treating exposure as a single isolated event. It does not establish that any particular child will radicalize. [E2](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

The Civil War example makes a second boundary visible. The continuation of a political project after military defeat need not be confined to private resentment. The National Park Service's Reconstruction account documents both the effort to secure newly freed people's rights and the organized violence and subsequent erosion of those protections. [E7](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

That history is compatible with investigating the survival and transformation of rival projects within a shared polity. It is not, by itself, a derivation of present polarization from one original regional split. The suggested genealogy remains to be tested against changing coalitions, institutions, economic conditions, and other causal accounts.

The ethical lesson is also not that ending slavery was mistaken because some people resented defeat. It is that defeating an oppressive order, protecting those it oppressed, and incorporating former opponents into a defensible political future are distinct tasks. Reconciliation among dominant groups can coexist with abandonment of others. A coherent national arrangement is not an acceptable success criterion when its coherence depends on that abandonment.

# 11. External evidence as constraints, not certification

The neighboring literature precludes claiming discovery of the general cycle. Bar-Tal and colleagues analyze collective victimhood as part of collective memory and the interpretation of intractable conflict. This provides a conceptual predecessor for the distinction between an injury and its socially maintained meaning. It does not make the injury imaginary. [E1](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

Boxer and colleagues report three annual waves across three childhood age cohorts in Palestinian, Israeli Jewish, and Israeli Arab populations. Their analysis supports a pathway linking ethnopolitical exposure with violence in surrounding environments and aggression. Its observational design and outcome measures do not establish the full intergenerational political mechanism. [E2](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

Bauer and colleagues synthesize evidence that war exposure can increase local cooperation, with indications of especially in-group effects. This challenges simple global moral-depletion accounts without showing that violence is beneficial overall. [E3](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

Mousa's randomized soccer intervention in post-ISIS Iraq improved several behaviors toward Muslim peers, but changes did not substantially generalize to other contexts or consistently change generalized attitudes. Individual re-thickening and broad political repair are therefore distinct targets. [E4](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

A separately versioned 2025 Lebanon field-experiment draft reports limited benefits from contact, some negative behavioral responses, more promising modest effects of empathy education on selected outcomes, and no detected parent spillover. This cautions against assuming that contact or educational gains propagate automatically. It neither measures the proposed slack construct nor validates it. The draft has descriptive count inconsistencies and has not been independently reanalyzed here; no quantitative interaction is inferred from its abstract. [E4b](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

The ICRC's organizational research on restraint provides another constraint: norms and socialization cannot be replaced by an explanation entirely in terms of individual feeling. [E5](EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIMS.md)

These sources differ in methods and scope. They do not add up to a truth probability for TLICA. Shared authorship, reviewed datasets, and institutional hosting must be tracked rather than counted as independent replications.

# 12. A minimal renewal model and discriminating probes

For one deliberately restricted model, let x_g be a nonnegative vector of specified conflict-sustaining capacities or practices at cohort step g. It is not a population's moral worth or a single hatred score. With fixed K and external inputs u_g, write

$$
x_{g+1}=Kx_g+u_g.
$$

When u_g=0, x_g=K^g x_0. A spectral radius below one implies decay in this finite-dimensional fixed linear system. A radius above one supplies growing modes, not growth from every possible initial state. Persistent inputs can sustain activity even when the homogeneous dynamics decay. Switching, changing institutions, nonlinear responses, and capacity limits require additional models.

A short-term reduction in x does not, by itself, establish a reduction in K. An intervention could reduce a present capability while strengthening some mechanisms that rebuild it, or could weaken both. Neither sign can be inferred from the model alone. No coefficients have been estimated here.

The first verdict-changing empirical task is to distinguish the proposed route from live rivals. Does generalized responsibility attribution help predict later support for collective harm after present threat, organizational coercion, material incentives, and relevant pre-existing attitudes are accounted for? Do accessible alternatives and goal-relative burdens improve held-out predictions? A positive result would still need comparison with existing social-psychological theories; prediction by TLICA vocabulary is not unique validation.

A second probe targets the slack clarification. Begin with consenting adults in low-risk settings, not children under active coercion. Before observing outcomes, specify the target behavior, the timeframe, baseline constraints, and which resources count as relevant. Essential support must not be withheld. Where ethical and feasible, use a complete 2-by-2 design comparing an additional task-relevant support package and a truthful perspective-exchange intervention, with an appropriate active comparison condition. Keep all four cells. A combined effect cannot be called an interaction without the complete contrast and uncertainty assessment.

The registered prediction should concern a specified burden and target, not a universal claim that additional resources improve every response. For example, reducing a independently assessed language or scheduling barrier should improve access to a defined interpretive task more than an equally intensive but task-irrelevant addition. Safe refusal, accurate disagreement, and nonparticipation remain distinguishable outcomes. A support manipulation that does not change its intended access measure leaves the mechanism test ambiguous rather than refuted.

Positive controls should recover a known accessible distinction; negative controls should use information irrelevant to the target; null controls should separate attention and time from the hypothesized contribution. Label-swap and source-attribution mutations can check whether scoring rewards the preferred side rather than evidentially warranted distinctions. Such synthetic mutations are test fixtures, not manufactured evidence about real persons.

A third probe concerns equal standing. Compare genuinely participatory procedures whose scope of influence is disclosed in advance, rather than covertly staging sham consultation. Record whether affected participants correct officials' models, whether defensible corrections change decisions, and whether downstream outcomes satisfy independently specified protections. Agreement with officials alone must not be scored as success. Institutional responsiveness and participant welfare are separate outcomes.

Longer-term work would need ethically governed cohorts, multiple settings, adverse-event reporting, and protection against using findings for manipulation or targeting. Responses to loss must not be reduced to a binary of reconciliation or radicalization. Legitimate dissent, anger, withdrawal, grief, cooperation, and endorsement of collective violence require distinct measures.

The historical operator hypothesis needs its own study: operational definitions of update rules, a common representational basis, a dated observation window, and a model of transition costs that competes with ordinary policy disagreement and external shocks. The synthetic counterexample cannot validate those choices. Data used to construct an operator cannot also serve as its independent holdout.

The account should lose preference if simpler rivals explain the observations at least as well, if its burden measures do not improve held-out discrimination, or if purported model revision merely tracks material threat reduction. Calling every failure “insufficient slack” or every disagreement “referent-thinning” would make the theory immune to the evidence it claims to organize.

# 13. Conclusion

The child's future cannot be assessed only from the authority's present objective. What an institution does to a parent, community, or home can become part of the conditions through which the next person learns who is dangerous, what loyalty demands, and whether alternatives are real. That is a mechanism to investigate, not a fate to impose.

The revised strategic question is not how to make people approve of what has been done to them. It is how to act, explain, listen, repair, and constrain action so that people can pursue defensible futures without being required to deny their losses, inherit others' guilt, or renew avoidable harm.

This requires neither a single memory nor universal affection. It requires distinguishing the future we claim to seek from the burdens our methods impose on those who must inhabit it.

> The child must be allowed to become someone who can understand what happened without having to become its next perpetrator—and without being required to deny what happened in order to stop it.
