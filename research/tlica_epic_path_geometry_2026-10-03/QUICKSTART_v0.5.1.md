# TLICA–EPIC v0.5.1 — Quickstart

## The 30-second picture

Read **inward across the top**, turn at the slack gate, then read **outward across the bottom**:

```text
WORLD  →  SUBSTRATE  →  SELF  →  [ S gate ]
WORLD  ←  SUBSTRATE  ←  ACTION ←────┘
```

The lower lane is later causal time, **not** time reversal.

## Five questions, in order

| ask | look for | read off |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Where did the world touch it?** | contact wire `~>` with χ | **κ** |
| **2. How mediated was the arrival?** | substrate vertices `▣` | **D** |
| **3. How bound is it into the I?** | weighted integration wires from `î` | **ρ** |
| **4. Does verification actually close?** | `⇒` chain reaching ground `⊥`; `q!` vs `q?` | **φ** |
| **5. What became action?** | sign of `S = M − Pressure` | automatic vs slack-mediated |

Mnemonic:

> **touch → mediate → integrate → verify → act**

## Five visual families

1. **Anchor/content:** `◉ î`, `○ x`, `◎ x`
2. **Integration:** weighted wire + optional imprinting event `⊕`
3. **Verification:** `⇒`, `⊥`, `q?`, `q!`
4. **World/substrate:** contact wire + mediation vertex `▣`
5. **Actualization:** slack gate with two exits

The full master has ten reference glyphs because some families contain multiple symbols. You do not need to memorize ten disconnected icons.

## Three rules that prevent most mistakes

- `q?` is only a visible hole. It contributes **nothing** until executed as `q!`.
- A φ-chain that does not reach ground is **undefined**, not “low.”
- Dashed conjectured bridges are drawn but **never enter a computed number**.

## Open-node rule

A dot may be opened into an internal circuit only when its internals matter. The boundary meaning must stay the same:

`⟦circuit⟧ = ⟦node⟧`.

That is the whole recursive “circuit inside circuit” trick.
