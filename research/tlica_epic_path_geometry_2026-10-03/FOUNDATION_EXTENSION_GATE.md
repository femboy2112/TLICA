# Foundation Extension Gate — TLICA–EPIC

**Purpose:** isolate what, if anything, is worthy of a future TLICA foundation revision.  
**Current verdict:** application-grade formalism; several foundation-extension candidates; **no foundation mutation yet**.

---

## 1. Immediate application-level use

Safe to use now without altering TLICA's frozen commitments:

- ambiguity fibers \(\mathcal F_Q\);
- local kernels \(\mathcal K_Q\);
- correlation/provenance-aware resolution tensors;
- typed arrows;
- source-map rival diagrams;
- constructible-vs-executed probe annotations;
- semantic-wake reconstruction;
- discrete/continuous path aggregation;
- Möbius interactions;
- perturbative Feynman bookkeeping where the mathematical assumptions hold.

These instruments can sit around TLICA without becoming TLICA primitives.

## 2. Candidate extension A — pathway-state triplet

### Candidate

Standardize:

$$
\boxed{
\text{constructible pathway}
\neq
\text{effectively available pathway}
\neq
\text{executed pathway}.
}
$$

Suggested notation:

$$
q\in\mathrm{Cl}(\mathrm{Tools}_t),
\qquad
\mu_t(q)>0,
\qquad
q\in Q_t^{\mathrm{exec}}.
$$

### Why it may belong

TLICA's \(\phi\) is toolkit-relative. Multiple applications now depend on distinguishing:

- having the tool;
- being able/willing to deploy it locally;
- actually running it.

The distinction may therefore clarify the access conditions under which \(\phi\) is evaluated.

### Why it may not belong

It may be ordinary application-level executive/access bookkeeping rather than a universal identity-correlation commitment.

### Promotion discriminator

Remove the triplet from at least three independent applications. If each loses a structurally necessary distinction that cannot be recovered cleanly from existing foundation apparatus, promotion gains force.

## 3. Candidate extension B — source-map resolution firewall

### Candidate

Standardize:

$$
\boxed{
\phi\text{-resolution}\not\Rightarrow\sigma\text{-resolution}.
}
$$

### Meaning

A claim can survive a current verification pathway while the source/generator attribution remains ambiguous or wrong.

### Constraint

\(\sigma\) must remain a separate diagnostic unless a future derivation proves otherwise.

It is **not** a proposed fourth coordinate.

### Promotion discriminator

Find cases where existing foundation source/pathway language already captures the distinction without additional machinery. If it does, standardize terminology only; do not add structure.

## 4. Candidate extension C — typed update semantics

### Candidate notation rule

> An arrow in a TLICA formal diagram must name the operation that updates its target.

### Why it matters

It blocks recurrent category errors:

- observation vs inference;
- inference vs imprinting;
- Mode B vs generic deliberation;
- action vs wake;
- wake vs observer reconstruction;
- feedback vs holonomy;
- source support vs truth support.

### Likely tier

Documentation / formal-notation standard first. Foundation only if later work shows the rule carries substantive theoretical content.

## 6. Mandatory promotion gates

A candidate enters a future foundation revision only after:

1. **typing audit** — no collision with current symbols or mode/layer terminology;
2. **coordinate firewall** — \(\kappa,\phi,\rho\) remain independent and uncollapsed;
3. **cross-application recurrence** — same structural need in at least three substantively different applications;
4. **ablation** — current foundation machinery fails to represent the need without the candidate;
5. **rival formalism** — at least one simpler competing representation;
6. **discriminating probe** — predeclared outcomes separating candidate from rival;
7. **provenance audit** — support is not multiple descendants of one author insight;
8. **holdout** — one case not used in design;
9. **counterexample search** — hostile regimes and degenerate cases;
10. **foundation reconciliation** — explicit proof that prior commitments, exclusions, and the cogito anchor survive;
11. **claim ledger** — derived / posited / conjectured / unverified status explicit;
12. **human author review** — Leah can explain, defend, revise, or abandon every load-bearing commitment.

Until then:

$$
\boxed{
\text{application-level power is evidence for usefulness, not automatic evidence for foundation status.}
}
$$

## 7. Candidate extension D — folded dual-pass structured-point grammar

### Candidate

Standardize the diagram skeleton

$$
W_t\to S_t\to I_t\to I_t^+\to S_{t+1}\to W_{t+1}
$$

with:

- upper lane = reconstruction / uptake;
- lower lane = realization / action;
- outer radial geometry = EPIC shell paths;
- inner radial geometry = TLICA internal traces;
- structured vertices carrying explicit shell/core coupling.

### Why it may belong

The grammar unifies epistemic uptake, internal transformation, action realization, semantic wake, probe dynamics, and zoomable internal structure without collapsing their edge semantics.

### Why it may not belong

It may remain a powerful application-level notation rather than a new architectural commitment. The foundation already contains the relevant dynamics without requiring this exact graphical packaging.

### Promotion discriminator

1. Use the grammar in at least three independent applications.
2. Remove the grammar and identify a load-bearing distinction that becomes ambiguous or unrepresentable.
3. Test a holdout application.
4. Search for counterexamples where the folded form falsely suggests invertibility, temporal symmetry, or a clean world/substrate/self partition.
5. Verify that shell/core language remains scale-relative and does not introduce an illicit ontological dualism.

**Current verdict:** notation/formal-language candidate; not foundation yet.
