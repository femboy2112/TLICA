# TLICA–EPIC v0.5.2 — Quickstart

## First rule: world first, content second

Never let one node silently mean both an external event and the I's internal representation of it.

```text
WORLD EVENT y_t  →  SUBSTRATE  →  INTERNAL CONTENT x_y
```

Example:

```text
wet clothes in dryer  →  cognitive mediation  →  "I encounter wet clothes"
```

`source x : ...` names **world items only**.

## Read the round trip

```text
WORLD  →  SUBSTRATE  →  SELF  →  [ S gate ]
WORLD  ←  SUBSTRATE  ←  ACTION ←────┘
```

Upper lane: reconstruction.  
Lower lane: realization at later causal time.

## Five questions

1. **κ — touch:** where did the world contact the system?
2. **D — mediate:** how many substrate stages shaped the incoming content?
3. **ρ — integrate:** how strongly is the resulting content/hypothesis bound into the I?
4. **φ — verify:** does its verification chain actually reach ground?
5. **S — act:** which actualization exit fired?

Mnemonic:

> **touch → mediate → integrate → verify → act**

## Dryer specimen

Correct order:

```text
prior wake + constraints
        ↓
already-low slack
        ↓
anger / further pressure
        ↓
WORLD: wet clothes
        ↓
CONTENT: "I encounter wet clothes"
        ↓
combine with prior P = "my method normally works"
        ↓
H1 = "they ran it wrong"
        ↓
S <= 0 → automatic action before q? becomes q!
```

This does **not** prove H1 false. It says the source attribution becomes behaviorally active before its discriminator closes.
