# Claim ledger — Phenomenology–Analogy–Model–Probe Loop

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Scope:** methodology dossier in this directory  
**Foundation impact:** none

Status vocabulary follows the repository's research discipline:

- **Disclosed** — established by proof, definition, exact derivation, or calibrated decisive measurement within a stated boundary.
- **Corroborated** — survives materially independent routes with provenance, residual, source-map, configuration, and holdout checks.
- **Observed** — seen/reported with exact scope; not automatically mechanistically explained.
- **Conjectured** — supported but carrying unpaid truth debt.
- **UNVERIFIED** — a suitable external discriminator has not yet run.
- **Dark** — no present discriminator reaches the claim; the missing lamp should be named.
- **Refuted** — a decisive counterexample/probe killed the stated claim.

Shared project provenance is not independence. A claim inherits the weakest load-bearing premise.

## C-003 — analogy is weaker than mechanism

**Claim:** Phenomenological resemblance between source and target does not logically entail identity of mechanism, ontology, or law.

**Status:** **Disclosed.**

**Why:** The entailment fails without additional bridge assumptions. A countermodel can preserve relational resemblance while changing the underlying mechanism.

**Claim:** Explicitly listing source properties that are *not* transported reduces the risk that irrelevant source ontology is silently imported into the target model.

**Status:** **Conjectured / methodologically motivated.**

**Probe:** Compare error modes in blinded modeling tasks with and without a required non-transport list.

## C-006 — shared reduced structure can differ from source identity

**Claim:** A source analogy and target phenomenon may share a reduced relational structure \(Q\) without the target being identical to the source system.

**Boundary:** Existence of such a \(Q\) for any particular empirical case remains to be earned.

## C-007 — formalization propagates assumptions, not warrant

**Claim:** Once assumptions are encoded in a formal model, deductive consequences can be exact even when the empirical interpretation of the assumptions remains uncertain.

**Status:** **Disclosed.**

**Consequence:** Mathematical certainty inside a model does not transport backward into empirical certainty about the model.

## C-008 — motivating evidence is not clean holdout evidence

**Claim:** Evidence materially used to select the analogy, bridge, model family, parameters, or interpretation is not independent validation of that same construction.

**Status:** **Disclosed as a provenance constraint.**

**Boundary:** This does not require literal statistical independence; it requires honest accounting of dependence.

## C-009 — out-of-fit drive is the generative hinge

**Claim:** The transition from explanatory analogy to risky model occurs when the formal object is driven into a regime not used to construct it and produces a constrained consequence.

**Status:** **Conjectured as the load-bearing methodological hinge.**

**Rationale:** This is where the model can surprise its author instead of merely redescribing fit data.

**Probe:** Compare model discrimination/calibration with and without mandatory out-of-fit predictions.
