## What is actually new in this branch

The **research object** is vernacular expressions as observable, context-indexed labeling acts with multiple latent candidate dynamical senses. Its novelty here is **project packaging and typed research questions**, not any claim to mathematical discovery. The finite synthetic demonstration makes ambiguous tokens and split probes runnable, and explicitly separates what an utterance conveys from what a person is.

## The compatibility promise and the refusal to overclaim

- Do not repurpose phi for feeling, sigma for a generic popularity score, rho for approval, mu for slack, or the "two layers" for developmental Modes A/B.
- No diagnostic claims or person-level predictions are licensed.
- Nothing here modifies frozen foundation, registered applications, documentation wiki, or published material.
- No new datatype is declared to be a literal TLICA–EPIC node until its world/content distinction is type-checked against the final accepted EPIC standard.
- Per current execution environment, external Git clone failed due to unavailable DNS. Only an isolated Python test run was performed; **make validate was not run**. The full local checkout must run make validate before any merge, along with the package-specific tests.
