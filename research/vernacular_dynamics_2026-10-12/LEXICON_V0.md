# Lexicon v0: initial three records

Each entry separates **term usages**, **candidate TLICA interpretation**, **observable discriminator**, **negative control**, and **limits**. Examples below are invented and do not constitute corpus statistics.

## 1. Vibe

**Attested usage families:** an intuitive mood/atmosphere of a person or setting; getting along with someone; relaxing or going with an activity. See [Urban Dictionary, vibe](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=vibe) and [Wiktionary, vibe](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/vibe). "Vibe" is also sometimes used for biased exclusions: observer impressions can be prejudicial rather than perceptive.

**Candidate structure:** an observer k decodes limited cues from world w, then reports a coarse context-indexed label V_{k,c}(w). A perceived good vibe may reflect reciprocal rapport, politeness masking discomfort, shared rhythm, or individual preference. The world, the decoded content, and the report have distinct types. Repeated exposures can change the decoder, the response policy, and the world encountered next (Semantic Optics feedback).

**Split probe:** gather separately consented accounts of the same low-stakes interaction, compare the immediate global label to task-specific predictions such as voluntary return to the interaction or independently stated comfort. Stratify by observer, setting, and social context. Avoid treating shared reactions as ground truth.

**Counterexample/negative control:** two people report "good vibes" while one is strategically polite and does not seek another interaction. Equal surface labels cannot prove reciprocal warmth. Conversely, blunt communication may be productive and respectful despite initially poor vibes.

**Status:** the formal non-identification result is Disclosed within its finite model; specific psychological decoding channels are UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Cope

**Attested usage families:** ordinary handling of adversity; purported self-protective rationalization; online dismissal or insult, sometimes without an argument. See [Urban Dictionary, cope](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=cope) and [Know Your Meme, Cope/Seethe](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cope-seethe).

**Candidate structures:** (A) adaptive coping lowers distress while leaving reality-testing intact; (B) defensive reinterpretation preferentially retains identity-relevant interpretations despite *credible and independently verified* disconfirmation; (C) rhetorical "cope" is a speaker's act of discrediting someone, not a fact about the target. The speculative TLICA pathway B may involve high identity correlation of a commitment and constrained updating, but **rho and phi cannot be read off an accusation**.

**Split probe:** compare independently recorded belief updates before/after vetted corrective evidence against matched evidence of low diagnosticity. Distinguish protecting a true proposition, revising a false one, emotional regulation, and deliberate social mockery. Pre-register how to assess evidence quality and what counts as update.

**Counterexample/negative control:** someone processes grief while accurately acknowledging the loss; that is coping, not necessarily self-deception. Someone calling a sound argument "cope" demonstrates an utterance, not the soundness of the rebuttal. A rational refusal to accept poor evidence is not defensive rationalization.

**Status:** sense distinction Observed in source material; proposed identity-mediated mechanism CONJECTURED; human predictions UNVERIFIED.

## 3. Lost in the sauce

**Attested usage families:** confused/out of depth; pleasurable or ecstatic immersion; intoxication; excessively absorbed in success, desire, or another high-reward environment. See [Urban Dictionary, lost in the sauce](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=lost+in+the+Sauce). Historical etymology and priority assertions inside crowdsourced definitions are not treated as settled facts.

**Candidate structures:** in the absorption usage, focal resources remain committed to a narrow set of contents across time. In a *distinct*, disorientation usage, task-relevant corrective probes become ineffective or delayed relative to predeclared goals. No generic equation from "high focus" to "impairment" is permitted. The same profile may make creative flow possible and still remain correctable.

**Split probe:** in a low-stakes consenting task, measure separately (i) sustained engagement, (ii) response to a scheduled external checkpoint, (iii) retrieval of a prior goal, and (iv) task performance. Compare creative flow to impaired reorientation without attributing either from the label alone.

**Counterexample/negative control:** absorbed research that easily pauses and checks its original goal is not established as disoriented, no matter how many hours elapsed. A user may jokingly call that state "lost in the sauce" anyway; the pragmatic usage can be true without the disorientation mechanism.

**Status:** multiple senses Observed in definitions; geometric/metacognitive dynamical explanation CONJECTURED, behavioral claims UNVERIFIED.
