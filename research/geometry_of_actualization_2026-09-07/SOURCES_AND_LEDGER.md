## 1. Author contribution and conversation provenance

The paper's central move belongs to Leah. The author corrected an assistant suggestion that Bataille exposes an otherwise missing kind of agency in TLICA:

> TLICA handles this once you consider that the field contains the self.

The author compared painting for eight hours with strenuous résumé-oriented work. In each example, emotional significance, self-congruent thought, and sustained action can have the same coarse shape. The proposed difference concerns how the thought connects information across past, present, future, self, not-self, and neutral context. The manuscript develops that correction; it must not repackage it as an assistant discovery or quietly restore the missing-faculty claim.

The original requested title was **Bataille, Dostoevsky, and Wallace: The Geometry of Actualizaton**. The working manuscript normalizes only the spelling of *Actualization*. No substantive title change is intended.

## 2. External source ledger

### D1. Dostoevsky

**Primary text:** Fyodor Dostoevsky, *Notes from Underground*, Constance Garnett translation, [Project Gutenberg eBook 600](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/600/600-h/600-h.htm). [Catalog and translation metadata](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/600). Accessed 2026-09-07.

**Inspected locations:** Author's note; Part I, Chapter I on petitioners and the narrator's immediate complications of his own self-description; Chapters II–III on reflection, humiliation, and the wall; Chapters VII–IX on advantage, calculation, and the piano-key; Part II, Chapters IX–X on Liza and the narrator's treatment of intimacy and domination. The final Part II material was inspected by targeted passages, not a claim to have newly reread the whole novella in this session.

**Supports:** Specific textual tensions used as interpretive cases. It does not establish that readers share the narrator's mechanism or that his philosophical assertions are Dostoevsky's endorsed conclusions.

**Provenance family:** One literary text in one translation. Multiple passages and mirrors do not provide independent empirical replication. Future edition-level scholarship should preserve translation differences and narrative context.

### B1. Bataille passage, mediated access

**Work:** Georges Bataille, *The Accursed Share*, Volumes II and III, translated by Robert Hurley, Zone Books.

**Access actually used:** [On Work's attributed sovereignty excerpt](https://onwork.edu.au/bibitem/1993-Bataille%2CGeorges-The%2BAccursed%2BShare%2BAn%2BEssay%2Bon%2BGeneral%2BEconomy-excerpt%2Bp.302/), a scholarly database associated with Jean-Philippe Deranty and Macquarie University. Accessed 2026-09-07.

**Scope:** The excerpt connects labor, useful ends, and temporal deferral with a contrastive conception of present enjoyment. It supports the narrow interpretive entry point, not a comprehensive account of sovereignty.

**Citation limitation:** The database labels the passage p.302 and a 1993 edition. That page number was not checked against a physical or scanned edition. Cite as Bataille **as excerpted by On Work**, with the database's locator explicitly identified as uncollated. Do not silently convert this into direct edition-level verification. The database itself asks users to preserve mediated attribution.

**Outstanding work:** Directly collate the passage and its surrounding argument in the chosen Hurley edition, then examine the text's broader relation to expenditure, subjectivity, communication, knowledge, and sovereign impossibility. Until then, strong claims that present support closure exhausts Bataille are **UNVERIFIED**.

### W1. Wallace's address

**Target:** David Foster Wallace, May 21, 2005 Kenyon commencement address.

**Inspected textual witnesses:** [Harvard-hosted delivered-address transcription](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/links/culture/dfw_kenyon_commencement.html); [Kenyon's published excerpt](https://bulletin-archive.kenyon.edu/x4280.html). Accessed 2026-09-07.

**Limits:** No recording was played in this writing session. The shorter Kenyon text and fuller transcription are not assumed word-identical. They are witnesses to the same speech, not independent evidence for a cognitive mechanism. The later book presentation is not the controlling version for this draft.
