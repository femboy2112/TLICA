# Immersion extension: probes, source map, and claim ledger

**Version:** 0.1.0 — 2026-09-08.

Companion to [Letting Go of the Underground](IMMERSION_AND_REORIENTATION.md). Preserve the original [formal specification](FORMALISM_AND_PROBES.md) and [source ledger](SOURCES_AND_LEDGER.md). Nothing below reports a human experiment, validated scale, or independent replication.

## 1. Research object and competing explanations

The object is a bounded episode of involvement, persistence, reconsideration, or inability to switch. The desired outcome is discrimination among explanations, not a flattering label for the participant.

Before observing the outcome, declare the task, relevant wider commitments, time horizon, available actions, and what would count as success or harmful mismatch. Do not redefine the main dish after each outcome so that every persistent action preserves it by definition.

| Candidate explanation | Distinguishing prediction to operationalize |
|---|---|
| Productive skilled absorption | Low intrusive monitoring can coexist with appropriate task corrections. |
| Task-local success with a frame/source-map defect | Local performance remains good while a separately specified wider requirement fails. |
| Identity-protective frame retention | Equally informative correction produces different responses when tied to identity, after credibility and practical cost are controlled. |
| Recursive monitoring burden | A monitoring manipulation affects execution differently from a time-matched task-relevant cue; mere delay is insufficient evidence. |
| Material constraint | Belief already changes, but implementation changes only when a feasible alternative becomes available. |
| Missing or unreliable probe | An adequate correction route is absent or untrusted for valid reasons; supplying one changes discrimination. |
| Rational persistence | An apparent discrepancy is noise or irrelevant; staying with the frame performs better under the declared criterion. |
| Simpler task-specific account | Skill, incentives, fatigue, practice, or ordinary switching costs explain the same results without the new vocabulary. |

These are overlapping possibilities. Do not force every episode into exactly one type. The model must be allowed to lose to the simpler explanation.

```text
  task_performance; self-reported_absorption; self-monitoring
  mismatch_detection; interpretation; belief_revision
  action_selection; action_feasibility; implementation
  unnecessary_switches; delayed_effects; effects_on_others
verdict: pass / fail / ambiguous; limitations
```

Unknown is not zero. Reported absorption is not observed source adequacy. Another person's behavior does not establish their internal frame. A prompt asking for motives changes the context and may change the activity it is intended to measure.

Keep detection, interpretation, decision, and implementation separately timed where possible. A participant may detect the defect, reject the purported correction for a good reason, or accept it without having any safe way to act. These trajectories require different explanations.

## 3. Verdicts and controls fixed before a probe

**Pass:** A registered separating prediction occurs, relevant manipulations are credible, controls work, and the designated rival does not predict the same pattern. This supports only the declared contrast.

**Fail:** An adequately implemented and sufficiently sensitive probe contradicts the registered prediction. Relabeling an unobserved identity relation afterward does not convert failure into success.

**Ambiguous:** The manipulation changes credibility or stakes, implementation is unavailable, the relevant signal was not perceived, the measurement is inadequate, carryover dominates, or rivals remain observationally equivalent. Ambiguous is not a weak pass.

Before real data collection, register sample size or sequential stopping rules, power assumptions where applicable, measures, exclusions, effect directions, tolerances, and analysis. These details are not supplied by the conceptual model. The protocols below are designs awaiting those decisions, not completed preregistrations.

Use an obvious task error as a positive detection control; an irrelevant perturbation as a null control; an inaccessible or absent signal as an access control; and a deliberately changed model rule as a mutation control. Avoid deploying a detector whose control outcomes have never been checked.

## 4. Probe A: task correction versus frame correction

**Setting:** A harmless, consented puzzle or synthetic planning exercise. Keep money, employment, medical decisions, safety, and essential needs outside the experiment.

**Construction:** The local display reports success on one criterion. A second, independently specified requirement is either unchanged or altered by a disclosed scenario event. In the altered scenario, familiar locally successful actions no longer satisfy the full declared task. The independent requirement must be available to the participant; otherwise the probe only tests hidden information.

**Comparison:** Cross a scheduled frame-review cue with the presence or absence of the meaningful frame-level change. Record all four cells. Include a separate simple local error to verify that participants can correct ordinary execution mistakes.

**Outcomes:** Local accuracy, wider-requirement violations, detection time, belief revision, implemented switch, false alarms, and reported interruption cost. Do not combine them into one unregistered score.

**Separating prediction:** The proposed distinction predicts that some episodes can retain good local correction while failing the wider requirement. Whether review cues improve this without excessive false alarms is a separate empirical hypothesis.

**Defeaters:** Local feedback alone reliably produces the same appropriate frame revision; differences disappear after information availability is controlled; or the cue merely provides new facts instead of changing their use. A study cannot claim a monitoring benefit when only one group was told the relevant rule.

**Controls:** No-change/null scenario; obvious local-error control; an unmistakable wider-rule change; time-matched irrelevant cue; negative accessibility control. Mutation: change the synthetic local signal to include the wider requirement and check whether the purported hidden-variable failure disappears.

## 5. Probe B: practical priority without inflated certainty

**Question:** Must sustained commitment increase certainty, or can a person act while preserving unresolved alternatives?

**Setting:** A reversible reasoning or creative task with several plausible plans. Compare an instruction to select a provisional plan and record its reopen condition with time-matched generic planning and unrestricted reconsideration conditions.

**Outcomes:** Work completed, quality under a frozen rubric, calibration of claim status, spontaneous or cued detection of counterevidence, appropriately retained alternatives, and unnecessary switching. Treat initial confidence, skill, and plan quality as possible confounds.

**Separating prediction:** The extension allows useful continuity without increased belief certainty. If this organization works, action can increase while appropriately expressed uncertainty and response to counterevidence remain intact. It is not enough that participants merely repeat uncertainty language.

**Defeaters:** Reported uncertainty becomes ceremonial while counterevidence is ignored; the apparent improvement is fully explained by added planning time; or there is no advantage over a simpler checklist. Actual falsification of the universal claim that action requires certainty needs only a well-established counterexample, but the success of this intervention requires much more.

**Controls:** A decisive correction requiring revision; an irrelevant objection that should not force a switch; a deliberately overconfident instruction in a synthetic agent model, not pressure placed on vulnerable participants. Do not reward participants for concealing uncertainty.

## 6. Probe C: identity attachment versus practical constraint

**Question:** Is continuation caused by defending a self-related method, or by having no workable exit?

First establish whether the mismatch was detected and whether the participant accepts the source. Then vary a benign alternative's feasibility independently of non-distressing personal relevance as far as ethically possible. A genuine refusal or boundary must remain permitted.

**Observable separation:** Belief changes without action change can support an implementation bottleneck. Action changes after a practical barrier is removed, without additional persuasion, distinguish that route from a belief-only account. Identity-related defensive persistence needs its own credible comparison; it cannot be inferred by subtracting all other explanations from an anecdote.

**Defeaters and confounds:** Changing identity framing also changes credibility, task meaning, social evaluation, or reward. Making the alternative easier may teach the solution. Then the intended factor was not isolated.

No manipulation may threaten identity, provoke humiliation, or jeopardize livelihood. No covert tests on coworkers, customers, dependents, or service recipients. Use hypothetical records or synthetic agents first where ethical isolation is doubtful.

## 7. Probe D: recursive audit as its own absorbing activity

**Question:** Is repeated self-scrutiny producing useful discrimination, or maintaining an unmet standard of certainty?

**Design:** In a low-stakes task, compare bounded evidence review with equally long unstructured reflection and a task-focused control. Record which questions are reopened, whether their evidence changes, and whether execution is actually prevented. The audit can continue in parallel with work; do not define all reflection as a pause.

**Separating prediction:** The proposed meta-frame account predicts repeated reopening without new discriminating evidence in some episodes. But delay alone cannot identify that mechanism. New evidence, ethical objections, or genuine underdetermination can make further review appropriate.

**Defeaters:** Reflection improves error detection or outcomes enough to justify its cost; reopening tracks genuinely new information; or interruptions, rather than reflection, explain lost performance. Retain cases in which the correct decision is not to act.

**Controls:** One hidden but discoverable error; one already resolved objection with unchanged evidence; one genuinely ambiguous case; and a material implementation barrier unrelated to deliberation. Do not label appropriate caution Underground paralysis.

## 8. Factorial and source-family discipline

For a declared scalar outcome in Probe A, let \(y_{ij}\) denote the cell mean, where \(i\) indexes review cue and \(j\) indexes frame change. A complete contrast is

\[
\Delta=y_{11}-y_{10}-y_{01}+y_{00}.
\]

All cells are necessary. The order fixes the sign convention. The contrast is scale-dependent and does not establish a psychological mechanism. Missing cells, unknown coding, or unhandled carryover prohibit claiming the interaction. Each outcome needs its own declared analysis.

Preserve raw observations before any adjustment. Add held-out tasks with delayed feedback, genuinely useful reflection, strong but flexible commitment, and rational refusal of a bad correction. The same case cannot both tune the account and validate it.

Treat the TLICA corpus as one connected theoretical family. Treat the conversation and this assistant's editorial passes as another dependent family. The literary works, the flow literature, and passion research have different origins but were selected through the same developing interpretation. Their agreement is not blind triangulation. Mirrors of a text are not new witnesses.

A leave-one-family-out check can ask whether the argument still stands without the author's anecdote, without one philosopher, or without the new terminology. No such independent check is claimed here. A stronger next probe is a blinded task comparison against a plain model using only skill, available information, incentives, and action costs.
