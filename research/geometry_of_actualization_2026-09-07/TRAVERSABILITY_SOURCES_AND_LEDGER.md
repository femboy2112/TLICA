### T-P3 — Adverse constraint on a simple resource-tank account

Martin S. Hagger and colleagues, “A Multilab Preregistered Replication of the Ego-Depletion Effect” (2016), DOI [10.1177/1745691616652873](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1745691616652873).
