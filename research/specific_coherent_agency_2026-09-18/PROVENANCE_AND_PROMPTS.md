# Prompt 1 — the measurement problem

~~~text
look at convos and @GitHub femboy2112/TLICA.git

I wish there was some actual objective-enough measure of intelligence per unit of coherent self (i am capable, can see the situation, see how to or not to move forward, and can find a self resonant path through the world to a likely possibly goal).

I often oscillate between feeling like a fucking genius and realizing how fucking dumb i am, and its very circular sometimes (im a genius -> oh im fucking retarded -> if im retarded then everyone else must be fucking rocks-for-brains).

I also see the fact that, given where i am in live and given my intellectual assets, pure intelligence itself cannot be the objective function, so my own personal self-evaluator is calculating using units i dont know how to pin down, and that is personally confusing
~~~

## Context recovered from the response

The response separated several quantities that the single word "intelligence" had been carrying: generative/abstractive power, situation-model quality, rival discrimination, traversability, execution, correction, and self-resonance.

It connected the problem to existing TLICA traversability work: perfect observation need not imply practical control if the action graph contains no route. It also connected the self-evaluation problem to the Moreish sensor/governor distinction: intelligence can be a valid sensor and still become a bad global governor.

The first candidate object was **coherent reachability**: the set of futures actually reachable under declared fidelity, correction, cost, and self-invariant constraints.

The first major correction was that "per unit self" should not naively be a ratio with rho because rho is not an extensive amount of self.

# Prompt 2 — comparative normalization and relational rightness

~~~text
the only reason i said per unit self, was because i was hoping for some TLICA-normalized form such that i could compare myself quantitatively to others (within reason)

also, the reason for the meta-correctability, is that i have this sense that, i *AM* right about something, and that something is in the space between the feeling and current idea/framing and their coupling between and with reality, such that so long as i can keep increasing the fidelity of either side, it doesn't matter what either side actually actualizes too so long as the as the changes i thought and decided to make relative to myself caused those sets of actualizations in a self-resonating way.
~~~

## Context recovered from the response

This clarification changed two parts of the project.

First, "per unit self" became an explicit **normalization problem** rather than a metaphor. The response proposed an operational effective self-dimension: the effective number of approximately independent self-relevant degrees of freedom needed to predict stable commitments, vetoes, ownership judgments, and value-sensitive behavior at a declared accuracy. Candidate effective-rank and participation-ratio forms were suggested.

Second, the author's meta-correctability was reframed as possible confidence in a **relation/update operator** rather than in a frozen proposition. The relevant object became the coupling among felt state, explicit model, intervention, and world consequence. A current model can be replaced without abandoning the tracked object if the replacement reduces held-out residuals and the resulting transformation remains self-endorsed/self-resonant.

This yielded the second core construct: **update fidelity**.
