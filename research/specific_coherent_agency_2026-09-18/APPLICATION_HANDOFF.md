# Application Promotion Handoff
## Specific Coherent Agency

**Current tier:** research precursor  
**Target tier:** eventual application paper, only after conceptual hardening  
**Foundation impact intended:** none

# 1. Why this deserves application placement

The object is not merely autobiographical. It applies existing TLICA distinctions to a general problem:

> How should a coherent agent's intelligence-like effectiveness be compared when modeling, action access, execution, correction, identity/commitment structure, and environment all vary independently?

The application contribution is the decomposition and measurement interface, not a new consciousness primitive.

A mature application paper could sit naturally beside agency, free choice, self-motion, geometry of actualization, and Moreish.

# 2. What should remain research-tier

Keep the following in research until validated:

- any numerical "intelligence score";
- claims that effective self-dimension is a natural denominator;
- any population norm;
- any statement that SCA is superior to IQ/g-factor/executive-function measures;
- any person-level estimate for Leah or anyone else;
- any claim that relational rightness explains a particular person's meta-correctability;
- human benchmark cutoffs.

# 3. What may be application-ready earlier

A conceptual application paper can responsibly develop:

1. the metric-collapse problem;
2. the distinction between model quality and control;
3. coherent reachability as a constrained application-level construct;
4. the difference between sentence-level correctness and update/relational fidelity;
5. self-resonant actualization as a constraint on successful movement;
6. why a vector profile is epistemically safer than one intelligence scalar;
7. the benchmark program as a falsifiable research proposal.

The empirical scalar must remain labeled UNVERIFIED.

# 5. Proposed eventual application outline

1. **The wrong question:** "How intelligent am I?"
2. **Metric collapse:** why smart/dumb judgments change units.
3. **Observation is not control:** finite disclosure and human interpretation.
4. **The capability vector:** model, discriminate, route, execute, correct.
5. **Coherent reachability:** reachable futures under fidelity and self constraints.
6. **Per unit self:** why rho is the wrong denominator and effective self-dimension is only a candidate.
7. **Relational rightness:** being wrong in representation while tracking a useful relation.
8. **Update fidelity:** correction as prospective residual reduction.
9. **Self-resonant actualization:** successful transformation without counterfeit selfhood.
10. **Benchmark program:** unfamiliar causal worlds, factorial access probes, synthetic agents.
11. **Limits:** no moral worth, consciousness, or universal leaderboard.
12. **Research agenda:** denominator invariance, incremental validity, ecological holdout.

# 6. Required hardening before promotion

## Formal

- choose or compare at least two self-dimension estimators;
- prove invariance properties under redundant/orthogonal feature transforms where applicable;
- specify the exact admissibility relation for coherent reachability;
- either justify scalar aggregation or make Pareto/vector reporting the formal primary result;
- define missing/unknown handling.

## Computational

- implement finite graph-world benchmark;
- include oracle/random/fixed-wrong/greedy/over-correcting/calibrated/option-preserving agents;
- add positive, negative, null, and mutation controls;
- store raw trajectories and scoring outputs;
- test denominator redundancy mutations;
- run fresh unseen world seeds.

## Adversarial

- attempt to game the denominator;
- attempt to game option preservation by never committing;
- attempt to game self-resonance by declaring trivial invariants;
- attempt to game update fidelity by overfitting after every residual;
- test whether verbal sophistication can score without causal accuracy;
- test whether a simple conventional metric explains all apparent SCA advantages.

## Empirical, later

No human comparison should be described as validated until there is a protocol with ethical review where required, accessibility analysis, preregistered weights, reliability, and conventional comparators.

# 7. Integration discipline

If/when promoted:

- do not modify foundation definitions to make the application fit;
- cite the existing traversability and agency machinery rather than silently duplicating it;
- keep rho distinct from self-dimension;
- keep phi/sigma/rho/mu and other TLICA diagnostics separate from the SCA vector;
- state that SCA coordinates are application-level estimators, not new foundation coordinates;
- preserve the prompt/provenance archive;
- preserve the raw vector even if a scalar is reported.

# 8. Next strongest probe

The highest-leverage next step is a **synthetic graph-world implementation**, not further prose.

Reason: it can simultaneously test whether

- observation and control dissociate as intended;
- update fidelity distinguishes calibrated correction from random switching;
- option preservation adds nonredundant information;
- the scalar behaves pathologically under rescaling;
- candidate self-dimension measures survive redundant-feature mutations.

A failed synthetic calibration would cheaply kill or reshape the quantitative proposal before any human interpretation.

# 9. Promotion criterion

Promote the conceptual paper when:

- the central distinctions are stable;
- at least one synthetic implementation exists;
- the main failure modes are demonstrated rather than only listed;
- the scalar is either defended with preregistered weights or explicitly demoted behind the vector/Pareto object;
- the denominator is presented with its unresolved model dependence;
- the prose can stand without relying on the originating author's biography.

Promote a quantitative instrument only under a much higher empirical bar.
