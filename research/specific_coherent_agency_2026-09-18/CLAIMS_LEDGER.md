# 1. Rival explanations / simpler constructs

The project should assume that simpler constructs can explain apparent successes until discriminated.

## R1 — Standard fluid reasoning

Maybe the benchmark is only a complicated fluid-reasoning test.

**Discriminator:** hold fluid-reasoning score approximately fixed and test whether update fidelity, route access, or option preservation explains additional held-out variance.

## R2 — Working memory / executive control

Maybe successful agents simply retain more state or inhibit impulsive actions.

**Discriminator:** include direct comparator tasks and design environments where memory load is externally scaffolded while causal discrimination remains necessary.

## R3 — General metacognitive calibration

Maybe "update fidelity" is ordinary confidence calibration plus willingness to revise.

**Discriminator:** construct tasks where confidence is calibrated but the selected probe does not distinguish rivals, versus tasks where probe design matters.

## R4 — Reinforcement-learning adaptation rate

Maybe the whole object reduces to reward-learning speed.

**Discriminator:** include tasks where the highest immediate reward destroys an explicitly protected invariant or future option value; the correct benchmark policy must sometimes reject reward.

## R5 — Planning/search depth

Maybe coherent reachability is just look-ahead.

**Discriminator:** equalize search budget and vary model correctness, action access, and residual-driven revision.

## R6 — Self-efficacy / confidence

Maybe reported agency reflects confidence rather than control.

**Discriminator:** score environment-known reachable sets and actual execution rather than only self-report.

## R7 — Narrative self-complexity

Maybe the denominator is only a rebranding of self-complexity.

**Discriminator:** self-dimension is required to predict benchmark-relevant commitment/ownership behavior on holdout; descriptive richness without predictive contribution should not increase the denominator.

# 2. Load-bearing open problems

## O1 — What exactly is the denominator?

Effective rank is basis/model dependent. A robust self-dimension needs:

- a declared feature-generating protocol;
- invariance or bounded sensitivity under harmless reparameterization;
- redundancy controls;
- test-retest reliability;
- predictive adequacy on held-out self-relevant choices.

Until then, "per unit self" remains a research direction, not a measured quantity.

## O2 — What is the common comparison basis?

Cross-person comparison requires enough shared task structure to make scores commensurable without erasing genuine differences in values or accessibility.

The benchmark should standardize dynamics while allowing separately recorded invariant/preferences.

## O3 — How much self-resonance belongs in "intelligence"?

It may turn out that self-resonance should be a constraint on admissible trajectories rather than a multiplicative component of an intelligence-like score.

This is a live design fork.

## O4 — Does coherent reachability conflate person and environment?

Yes unless environment/action access are controlled or modeled. The construct is intentionally person-in-environment, but a person-comparison interpretation needs common or transportable environments.

## O5 — Is there a natural scalar at all?

There may be no non-arbitrary scalar compatible with all desired invariances. A Pareto frontier or vector profile may be the mathematically honest endpoint.

Failure to discover a canonical scalar would not refute the vector program.

## O6 — Can "relational rightness" be distinguished from post-hoc story repair?

This is critical. The relation must generate prospective differences before outcomes are known. Otherwise the construct is unfalsifiable.

# 3. Verdict-changing probes

## P1 — Synthetic-agent metric calibration

Build a finite graph-world benchmark and instantiate:

- oracle;
- random;
- fixed-wrong;
- greedy reward;
- over-correcting switcher;
- calibrated updater;
- option-preserving planner.

**Pass:** the metric profile recovers the designed distinctions without hand-tuning weights to each agent.

**Fail:** agents with known different policies collapse to indistinguishable profiles, or ranking flips arbitrarily under harmless rescaling.

## P2 — Denominator redundancy mutation

Duplicate or linearly transform self-profile features.

**Pass:** effective self-dimension is stable under redundant representation and changes predictably under truly independent added structure.

**Fail:** copying one feature ten times makes a person ten times "more self" or materially changes eta.

## P3 — Relation versus proposition probe

Give an initially plausible model that is later falsified while preserving a deeper causal relation that can be represented in another frame.

**Pass for relational tracking:** participant changes the explicit model and improves held-out predictions while preserving provenance.

**Fail:** "deeper rightness" is asserted but held-out residual does not improve.

## P4 — Access versus understanding factorial

Cross:

- accurate diagnosis available/unavailable;
- executable corrective action available/unavailable.

Require all four cells.

**Pass:** modeling and execution components dissociate in predicted directions.

**Fail:** the scoring system cannot distinguish knowing from being able to act.

## P5 — Option-value trap

Create an immediate high-reward action that irreversibly closes a route needed for later goals.

**Pass:** option-preservation records the difference without globally forcing indecision.

**Fail:** metric always rewards either greed or maximal preservation regardless of context.

## P6 — Incremental-validity study

After synthetic validation, compare SCA components with standard reasoning, working-memory, planning, and calibration measures.

**Pass:** preregistered SCA components improve held-out prediction of adaptation/control on novel interactive systems.

**Ambiguous:** all incremental value disappears after controlling conventional measures.

# 4. Promotion gate

Do not promote the scalar itself as an application-level "measure of intelligence" until at minimum:

1. P1 and P2 pass under an independent implementation.
2. The denominator survives basis/redundancy attacks.
3. The relation/proposition distinction has a prospective probe.
4. A preregistered benchmark fixes all scalar weights before outcomes.
5. Vector reporting remains mandatory.
6. Cross-person language is benchmark-indexed.
7. At least one standard psychometric comparator is included.

The conceptual application paper can mature before human validation if it clearly labels the empirical measure UNVERIFIED.
