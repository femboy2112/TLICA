## One-sentence object

This dossier asks whether a person's effective intelligence-like capacity can be compared across persons not as a single decontextualized "smartness" scalar, but as **reality-coupled, self-resonant, correctable agency normalized by the effective dimensional load of the integrated self being coordinated**.

The working umbrella name is **Specific Coherent Agency (SCA)**.

Two sub-objects carry most of the load:

1. **Coherent reachability** — which futures are genuinely reachable by this person, under declared conditions, while preserving epistemic fidelity, executable access, correction, and declared self-invariants?
2. **Update fidelity** — how accurately and efficiently can this person use contact with reality to revise the coupling among felt state, explicit model, action, and world, including when the current model is wrong?

## Why this exists

The motivating problem was a recurrent self-evaluation instability:

- a strong intellectual success is compressed into "I am a genius";
- a basic failure or poor life outcome is compressed into "I am stupid";
- the contradiction is then repaired by changing the comparison class rather than by changing the metric.

The dossier treats this as a **metric-collapse problem**. TLICA already distinguishes contact, verification-path state, source-map adequacy, identity/commitment integration, probe availability, option construction, action access, execution, and correction. None of these should silently become a universal intelligence or worth score.

The author's clarification was more specific: the desired measure is **comparative and TLICA-normalized**. "Per unit self" was intended to ask whether two people can be compared on a common basis after accounting for the amount/complexity of integrated self-structure each must coordinate.

A second clarification supplied the proposed reason for strong meta-correctability: the author's confidence is often not attached to the current sentence or frame. It is attached to an experienced **relation** among felt structure, current explicit framing, and their coupling to reality. A model can therefore be replaced without the author experiencing the central tracked object as abandoned, provided the revision increases fidelity and the resulting changes remain self-endorsed and self-resonant.

## Current epistemic posture

**Observed in the originating conversation:** a stable demand for a quantitative, comparative quantity more informative than IQ or life outcome alone, and a self-description in which correction often preserves a deeper felt relation while replacing the explicit frame.

**Derived conditionally:** in any stipulated finite action model, perfect observation need not imply control; therefore observational/model quality and executable reachability cannot be identified without additional assumptions.

**Conjectured:** a useful cross-person comparison can be built from a common benchmark family by combining model fidelity, discrimination, route construction, execution, correction, self-resonance, and option preservation while normalizing by an operational effective self-dimension.

**UNVERIFIED:** the proposed SCA scalar is stable, reliable, cross-person comparable, or superior to simpler psychometric/decision-theoretic measures.

**Boundary:** no quantity in this dossier measures moral worth, consciousness, personhood, or a metaphysical amount of self.
