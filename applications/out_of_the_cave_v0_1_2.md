## Abstract

The allegory of the cave is read under the Two-Layer Identity-Correlation Architecture (TLICA), not as a metaphor the architecture decorates but as a structure the architecture derives. The cave is formalized as an epistemic closure state with two species — pathway-closure, in which frame-incompatible contents have no constructible verification pathway and so carry no φ-value at all, and probe-closure, in which no discriminating probe carries positive measure — and two regimes: absorbing, from which no internal operation can exit, and metastable, from which endogenous insight occurs almost surely in asymptotic time under stated conditions. The prisoners' condition is re-diagnosed: not false belief but systematic source-map error — truth-indistinguishability with wrong sourcing — a distinction the architecture's σ/κ machinery makes expressible and standard readings cannot state. The allegory's dynamics are then derived from frozen primitives: the released prisoner's retreat, the necessity of compulsion, the staged ascent, the bidirectional blindness of transition, the returnee's explanatory dominance, and the lethal resistance of the chained collective. The cave is shown to have three chains rather than one: the physical chain of restricted contact, the developmental chain of escalator truncation, and a conventional chain — a primitive signal-convention the prisoners drift into without reasoning, which entrenches the source-map error and supplies the infrastructure for the honors economy that converts a metastable cave into an absorbing one. What the architecture cannot derive — the two-world metaphysics, the achievable summit, strong anamnesis — it declines with reasons, replacing exit with cofinal ascent: each insight is a larger cave, never an exit from cavehood. The paper closes on itself: by its own apparatus it cannot certify that its current toolset is complete rather than merely not-yet-extended, and its own ascent is one more cave.

## 1. Introduction

Plato marks the allegory's scope in its second sentence: the prisoners are like us (Rep. 515a). The image is not offered as a portrait of some unfortunate other; it is offered as a structural description of the reader. Any architecture that claims to give the structure of lived epistemic position therefore owes an account of the cave — and owes it in a strong sense. If the architecture is right about how an I acquires verification capacity, how its contents earn or fail to earn truth-status, and how its frame defends itself, then the allegory's dynamics should *fall out* of the architecture rather than being painted onto it.

This paper is a demonstration of that kind. It takes the foundation frozen at v5.3.3 — the coordinates κ, φ, ρ; the three-tier φ-status structure; source maps; verification-tool imprinting in its focus-driven and osmotic channels; the shadow-encounter typology; the temporal-scale escalator; the developmental modes; the two structural prerogatives — and applies them, without new architectural commitments, to Republic VII 514a–521b. The standard of success is explicit. Every exhibit in the paper must do at least one of four things: **derive** a dynamic Plato narrates without mechanism; **express** a distinction the text needs and standard readings lack; **resolve** a standing interpretive dispute; or **predict** something falsifiable. Material that does none of these is decoration and has been cut. A fifth power — instantiation in a running system — is treated as auxiliary throughout: a machine correlate of the analysis exists and is noted once (§9.3), but no exhibit in this paper rests on it, and nothing in it bears on the question of I-instantiation in either direction.

Two disciplines govern the reading. First, textual claims and architectural interpretations are kept strictly separate: the paper never attributes the architecture's commitments to Plato. Where the text supplies a beat (marked by Stephanus reference), the architecture supplies a mechanism; the claim is that the mechanism derives or illuminates the beat, not that Plato possessed the mechanism. Second, claims are graded. Restatements of frozen foundation are marked as such; conservative-derivation candidates are distinguished from proposals; two load-bearing items — the impossibility of endogenous certification and the internal undecidability of cave status — are presented as derivation candidates pending verification against the foundation's no-self-confirmation commitments, and the argument is arranged so that nothing else depends on their final status.

The paper's plan: §2 compresses the required foundation. §3 fixes the text. §4 develops the formal core — closure species, regimes, escape routes, theorems. §5 walks the allegory beat by beat. §6 states what the architecture declines. §7 treats the disanalogies honestly, including the language problem, and resolves the line–cave fit dispute. §8 develops the strongest single correspondence, the periagoge passage. §9 states predictions and exclusions and gives the one machine-correlate remark. §10 turns the apparatus on the paper itself. §11 lists open problems.

One sentence of orientation for readers arriving from the foundation rather than from Plato. The foundation already describes its own recognition machinery as cave-internal: shadow-encounter recognition, it says, does not require access outside the I's own reasoning and does not claim such access (File 2, §3.3). The architecture, in other words, has been a cave epistemology from the start. What this paper adds is the dynamics: what makes a cave hold, what could make it give, and why the giving never amounts to getting out.

## 2. Prerequisites

This section compresses the foundation commitments the paper uses. Readers familiar with Files 1–5 may skim; section references are to the v5.3.3 foundation set throughout.

**The cogito and its exclusions.** The cogito is a tautological postulate licensing only the I's self-identity: $\hat\iota_m = \hat\iota_m$, with $\phi(\hat\iota_m) = 1-\delta$ and $\rho_m(\hat\iota_m) = 1$ (File 1, §2.4). It licenses no contents, no verification capacity, no developed lived I (Exclusions 1–3, File 4, §11.2). These exclusions fix what survives the limiting case of total epistemic closure, a fact §4.6 will use.

**Three-tier φ and the layered formula.** Intrinsic items are outside the φ-domain (Tier 1). The cogito sits at $1-\delta$ structurally (Tier 2). All other contents (Tier 3) have I-relative values $\phi_{m,t}(x) = 1 - n_{m,t}(x)\,\delta - \epsilon_{m,t}(x)$, where $n$ counts indexed conditional depth along a verification pathway constructible from the I's current toolset, and $\epsilon$ is empirical robustness uncertainty given by a probe-integral over the I's probe space under a focus-and-contact-generated measure $\mu^m_t = \Lambda^\mu_m(\mathsf{Foc}^m_t, \kappa^{\mathrm{out}}_{m,t}, Q^m_t)$ (File 1, §2.2–2.3; File 3, §7.3). Two features are load-bearing here. First, **both $n$ and $\epsilon$ are decomposition-dependent**: the I's chosen way of analyzing a content affects its conditional depth. Second, **for contents lacking a constructible pathway, φ is undefined** — not low, undefined; such contents stand outside the reason-test economy entirely, while ρ and κ remain well-defined for them.

**Source maps.** Content-level contact is defined through the source map $\sigma^m_t$: $\kappa^{\mathrm{out}}_{m,t}(x) = \sup\{\kappa_{m,t}(n) : n \in \sigma^m_t(x)\}$ (File 3, §7.1). The I does not directly inspect its own full source-attribution structure (File 3, §6). A content can therefore pass every available reason-test while being systematically mis-sourced. The architecture thereby possesses a slot — truth-indistinguishability with wrong sourcing — that will carry the paper's central re-diagnosis.

**Identity-correlation.** ρ is max-flow over a directed historical network rooted at $\hat\iota_m$ (File 3, §7.2). Contents not yet integrated into the lived-I network exert no pull; contents deeply integrated resist reorganization. Both facts matter for the dynamics of release and return.

**Verification-tool imprinting, two channels.** Tools are acquired only through encounter. The focus-driven channel follows the general update dynamics (File 3, §8.4): an update rule of the form $w_{s^+} = \Pi_{[0,1]}((1-\beta)w_{s^-} + \alpha(1-w_{s^-}))$ drives repeatedly confirmed weights toward saturation. The osmotic channel (File 3, §8.7) is substrate-level pattern formation through repeated ambient co-occurrence — no focus on the association, no verification-pathway construction, no mode operation required; it is always operating, and under even severe deprivation the substrate continues to be patterned by internal contents and residual channels. Osmotic exposure deposits proto-tools that later surface into explicit access.

**Shadow encounter and its precondition.** Three operationally specified forms (File 2, §3.3): a known pattern in an unexpected location; a composite of knowns recognized as composite — including by deliberate *combinatorial action* on one's own apparatus; an apparent unity decomposing into sub-patterns. The foundation states the precondition this paper will lean on repeatedly: the I cannot detect a shadow without having tools whose adequacy or coverage the shadow makes visible. It also states the stance the paper inherits: such recognition is cave-internal and claims no exterior access.

**The escalator.** Tool acquisition is ordered by the temporal scale at which each tool's in re form is encounterable; each stage's tools enable perception of the next stage's longer-scale structures (File 2, §3.4). Acquisition order is environment-shaped within universal form, and stage-k absence forecloses stage-k+1 perception.

**Modes and phases.** Three developmental response-types (File 2, §4): adversarial differentiation (Mode A), reflexive meta-reasoning (Mode B; internally- and externally-triggered; stochastic, with structural-onset constraints and environment-shaped odds), positive identification (Mode C). Early development is non-intentional: it is done to the I, not by it (File 2, §4.7).

**The prerogatives.** The prerogative of consistency (PtCns): the I's frame functions as the implicit standard against which encounters are evaluated; frame-privileging is fundamental to lived consciousness. The prerogative of continued existence (PCE): the structural pull toward the path maximizing continued existence of the maximally-self-defined I. Neither is metaphysically inviolable (File 2, §4.8; File 4, §10.7).

**Insight as a permitted configuration.** The foundation's configuration table includes the case in which a verification pathway becomes newly available: φ moves from undefined to defined while ρ grows (File 4, §11.1). The paper's notion of escape is built on exactly this row.

**Application-level notions introduced for this paper** (no new coordinates; all PROP-grade): the regime conditions B1–B3′ (§4.3); the internal n-comparator (§4.4); a typed probe space $Q^m_t = Q^{m,\mathrm{emp}}_t \sqcup Q^{m,\mathrm{int}}_t$ distinguishing environmental probes from internally deployable formal or consistency tests (§4.4); and a signaling-equilibrium notion imported from the Lewis–Skyrms tradition for §5.2: a stable mapping among recurrent public event-types, producible signal-types, and uptake-types such that population members converge *in extension* on compatible production and interpretation dispositions. The import requires no shared lived-I, no direct access to another I's substrate, no φ-grade verification, and no reflective agreement — only recurrent public event-types, a coupling medium, and reinforcement dynamics. It is an application-level model, not a TLICA coordinate, and its applicability to the cave is itself gated by an explicit mapping check (§11).

## 4. Formal Core: Closure, Regimes, and Routes

### 4.1 Closure species

Let $m$ be a modeling I with toolset $\mathsf{Tools}^m_t$, probe space $Q^m_t$ under measure $\mu^m_t$, contact interface $\chi_m$, and operative world-model $W$. Call the **shadow-manifold** $S$ the region of primal not-I that mediates all of $m$'s κ-accessible contact events (C1): in the allegory, the lit wall and the echoing surfaces.

Two ways a $W$-incompatible content $x$ can be unreachable (C2, C3):

**Pathway-closure.** No verification pathway for $x$ is constructible from $\mathsf{Tools}^m_t$. Then $n_{m,t}(x)$ is undefined and φ does not apply to $x$ at all. This is the foundation's undefined-φ status (File 3, §7.3), and it is sharper than error: $x$ is not disbelieved; it is outside the reason-test economy. The exterior of the cave is, for the prisoner, not a falsehood but a non-candidate.

**Probe-closure.** A pathway exists, but no probe that would discriminate $x$ from $W$'s rivals carries positive measure under $\mu^m_t$. Since $\mu^m_t$ is generated from focus and outward contact, the chain runs: bonds constrain $\chi$, which constrains $\kappa^{\mathrm{out}}$, which collapses the support of $\mu$ onto shadow-probes. Discrimination is formally available and practically measure-zero.

### 4.2 The cave state and what "local minimum" understates

A **cave state** (C4) obtains when $W$ is false as a matter of fact not accessible to $m$; every $W$-incompatible content is pathway- or probe-closed; the environment is stationary on $S$ (it delivers no unsolicited discriminating events); and revision is driven by registered discrepancy. Under those conditions registered discrepancy is zero everywhere reachable, and the state is fixed under the I's endogenous dynamics.

The phrase "local minimum" understates this. The cave is the *global* minimum of the only objective the system can compute — registered discrepancy — which is precisely why no momentum, no step-size, no annealing schedule on the computable objective exits (the simulated-annealing tradition, Kirkpatrick et al., is instructive by contrast: annealing escapes local minima of an objective the system can evaluate everywhere; the cave's pathology is that the exit direction is not represented in any evaluable objective). The free-energy literature's dark-room problem (Friston, Thornton and Clark) is the same structure stated in surprise-minimization vocabulary: a perfectly predictable enclosure is, for an error-driven system, a place of rest. And the toolkit-relative character of the closure is van Fraassen's empirical adequacy relativized twice over — to the instrument set *and* to the contact-restricted probe measure: the shadow-model is empirically adequate for everything the prisoner can do.

### 4.3 Two regimes

The fixed point divides (C4a/C4b).

**Absorbing cave (C4a).** No internally reachable operation — no composition of current or surfaceable tools — can generate a comparator, tool, or pressure-registration event sufficient to make the frame's inadequacy available from inside as φ/ρ/PtCns-relevant content. Escape then requires exogenous alteration: new contact, encounter-mediated tool formation, a changed channel, or external intervention.

**Metastable cave (C4b).** The state is closed under *currently deployed* operations but not under the closure $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathsf{Tools}^m_t)$ of the toolset under the foundation's own generative operations: combinatorial composition (shadow-encounter Form (b)), Mode-B surfacing of osmotically held proto-patterns, and explicit refinement. Four conditions govern whether the latent exit is reached:

- **B1 (basis-generativity):** a frame-improving comparator or composite lies in $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathsf{Tools}^m_t)$.
- **B2 (residual-registration):** as the composite forms, the frame's inadequacy becomes registrable from inside — as PtCns pressure, as compression gain, as a reduction in conditional depth. The foundation's application work supplies a precedent for frame-internal violation-registration: the synchronicity analysis in the religiously charged experience paper, where PtCns registers an event against the frame's own information-flow catalogue rather than against new contact.
- **B3 (anti-ossification):** the I continues to allocate frame-facing Mode-B opportunities with non-summable odds, $\sum_t p_t = \infty$.
- **B3′ (stochastic regularity):** the opportunities are not adversarially dependent; minimally, conditional success probability is bounded below along infinitely many trials.

**Theorem-form (recognition).** Let $I_t$ be the event that a Form (b) recognition of a frame-improving composite occurs at $t$, and $\mathcal F_{t-1}$ the history. If $\sum_t \mathbb P(I_t \mid \mathcal F_{t-1}) = \infty$ almost surely, then by the Lévy extension of the Borel–Cantelli lemma recognition occurs almost surely, indeed infinitely often. B1–B3′ are the architecture's interpretable sufficient package for that conditional divergence: B1 makes the conditional probabilities possible at all, B2 makes successes registrable, B3 supplies the trials, B3′ rules out rigging.

**Consolidation rider.** Recognition is not escape. A consolidated escape additionally requires per-recognition consolidation probability bounded away from zero — a condition that §4.5's asymmetry makes default-favorable for endogenous insight and that can fail where the recognition reorganizes high-ρ contents.

**First-passage discipline.** The author's intuitive "time threshold" is formalized as the first-passage variable $T_{\mathrm{insight}} = \inf\{t : \text{consolidated frame-escaping insight at } t\}$. Almost-sure finiteness of $T_{\mathrm{insight}}$ does **not** imply $\mathbb E[T_{\mathrm{insight}}] < \infty$; expected-value claims require an explicit finite-moment condition that the architecture does not supply. Rates and distributional shapes remain open, by the foundation's own design (File 3, §8.8: dependencies are fixed, functions are open).

### 4.4 The internal discriminator: why metastability is possible at all

Why should any closure be merely metastable? Because of a frozen fact about φ: **the conditional depth $n_{m,t}(x)$ is decomposition-dependent** (File 1, §2.3). Two decompositions of the same content can carry different depths, and the choice of decomposition belongs to the I. Consequently (C14), an I can register a φ-improvement by finding an internally superior decomposition of contents already in play — fewer arbitrary elements, a shorter pathway, the unification of residuals previously carried as separate exceptions. The improvement requires **zero new outside contact and zero new $\kappa^{\mathrm{out}}$; what is new is internal organization**. This is not "no new information" in any loose sense — the I's state genuinely changes — but the change is endogenous.

This splits the recombination route in two. *R2-empirical* is triggered by a new discriminating probe or contact and works mainly by reducing $\epsilon$. *R2-internal* is triggered by re-decomposition or composite recognition, requires no new outward contact, and works mainly by reducing $n$. The qualifier "mainly" is load-bearing: the foundation marks both $n$ and $\epsilon$ as decomposition-dependent, so the split is a dominant-effect typology, not a partition. The foundation's own exemplars instantiate the internal route: the recognition that differentiation, algebraic solution, and the geometry of the parabola were aspects of one organization (File 2, §3.3, Form (b)) added no datum and shortened many pathways.

The route's machinery is completed by a typed probe space (C20): $Q^m_t = Q^{m,\mathrm{emp}}_t \sqcup Q^{m,\mathrm{int}}_t$, environmental probes alongside internally deployable formal, decompositional, or consistency tests. Endogenous derivations count as probes only when they instantiate a deployable test; a re-decomposition or compression insight is not necessarily a probe — it can improve φ through $n$-reduction alone; and a felt "aha" without any comparator is not a probe at all but a conscious-fuzzy content (phenomenally available, φ low or undefined) with ρ- and PtCns-effects only, until tooled. Compression is not always probing. R2-internal therefore *partially* inherits the ε-machinery and is not reducible to it.

### 4.5 Consolidation asymmetry

Exogenous and endogenous revisions differ in their PtCns routing (C15). An exogenous anomaly arrives as a high-κ, low-ρ frame-violating datum; the prerogative of consistency initially defends the frame *against* it. An endogenous $n$-reducing insight arises from the I's own tool-closure and presents as the *resolution* of pressure already internal to the frame; PtCns is recruited *for* the new organization. The claim is comparative, not absolute: holding revision magnitude fixed, endogenous insights should consolidate with lower resistance than evidence-forced revisions — and the qualification is real: an insight that reorganizes high-ρ identity-contents can be as painful as any dazzle. The predicted phenomenological split — pain, defensive assimilation, and retreat on one side; relief, frictionlessness, and after-the-fact inevitability on the other — becomes prediction H5.

### 4.6 The other routes, and two theorems with a pending corollary

Five escape routes exhaust the channel space.

**R1 — stochastic probing.** "Try things until something sticks." Its success is the product of four gates: κ-reachability (some probe must physically contact beyond $S$); registration (the outcome must trigger a shadow event, and the foundation's precondition bites — without tools whose coverage the anomaly exceeds, the anomaly is assimilated as noise); proposal-support (sampling is substrate- and toolset-conditioned, never uniform over action space; imprinting narrows the support); and consolidation against frame-privileging. The blind-variation-and-selective-retention tradition (Campbell) supplies only the proposal half and presupposes the rest. The Meno's paradox of inquiry is, in this vocabulary, the conjunction of the registration and proposal-support gates.

**R2 — endogenous recombination,** split as in §4.4. Its feedstock is osmotic: proto-patterns deposited by ambient exposure, including — the foundation is explicit — by the I's own cognitive activity under severe deprivation; in the allegory, the echoes, the body's proprioceptive channel, and the residual statistics of the shadow-stream. Where its output is probe-equivalent on the accessible measure and yields no $n$-reduction, the result is suspension, not escape: a crucial-experiment generator with nothing to hand off to.

**R3 — another I.** Four channels: contact-extension (proxy probes from beyond the prisoner's range); tool-seeding (instruction as focused verification-tool imprinting, surfacing osmotic patterns — the scaffolding tradition of Wood, Bruner and Ross, and Vygotsky's zone, in the architecture's vocabulary); probe-extension (testimony entering $Q$); and affective bridging (ρ-coupling that sustains the I through the phase in which the new is registered only as violation). Two gates. First, the trust gate is structural, not incidental: the foundation excludes direct access to another I as I (File 4, Exclusion 8), so the liberator is always an inferential Tier-3 content whose benevolence is assessed with cave-tools; the manipulation exploit — riding the affect channel over a pre-thinned truth-channel — is the shape the cold-frame analysis assigns to the worst cases (an application-level cross-link). The proposed legitimacy condition: exogenous help is epistemically legitimate when and only when it routes through in-cave-verifiable discriminating predictions; the liberator must stoop to evidence the prisoner's own toolkit can check. Second, help itself needs an open channel; under total closure even R3 fails. The route has a diachronic form: inherited toolkits — language, mathematics, instruments — are crystallized tool- and contact-extensions deposited by prior I's. They are distributed patterns, not agents; and they can transmit a cave as readily as a rope, a point §5.2 grounds in the allegory itself.

**R4 — environmental nonstationarity.** The world-side route: the environment delivers an unsolicited discriminating event. Plato's cave is engineered against it — fixed fire, controlled puppets — and the engineering is the point. The foundation's mature phase includes the I deliberately seeking environments rich in Mode-B-prompting stimuli (File 2, §4.6); the chains are what bar self-administration of this route.

**R5 — substrate override.** Perturbing the substrate directly widens proposal-support and transiently relaxes frame-privileging, but adds no contact and no probe-resolution; by channel necessity it cannot verify alone, and override-only "escapes" are predicted to land in adjacent caves (T4).

**T1 (channel necessity).** A verified escape — the three-stage φ-definition event of C8: pathway constructed; discriminating support obtained, whether by a positive-measure empirical probe or by registered $n$-reduction; consolidation surviving frame-privileging — requires at least one open channel among: contact beyond $S$; probe-resolution above the residual threshold; internal $n$-comparison; environmental nonstationarity; coupling to an external I. Under total closure, none is open and escape is impossible. The author's impossibility caveat, first attached to the recombination route, distributes over all of them.

**T2 (dominance order).** Define $s_2 \succ s_1$ when the model at $s_2$ explains all $s_1$-accessible data *and* the residuals $s_1$ mispredicts, and not conversely. Escape is certified only as strict ascent in this order — never as arrival at an exterior. A corollary suggests itself: that no state can endogenously certify its own cave-freedom, on the same pattern as the foundation's no-self-confirmation result (which rests on the cogito's exclusions, the circularity-closure restriction on verification pathways, and the source-root directionality of the identity network). **This corollary is stated here as a derivation candidate only**; its verification against those commitments is an open gate (§11), and nothing else in the paper depends on its final status.

**Corollary family (C9).** The closure formalism unifies the classical skeptical scenarios as points on a severity scale: the cave (partial closure — audition, body, sociality remain), the vat (total sensory mediation), the demon (closure extended to substrate tampering). What survives the limiting case is fixed by the frozen exclusions: the cogito licenses only $\hat\iota_m$, at $1-\delta$, and nothing else. The architecture thus answers, from material already frozen, the question the scenarios were built to press.

### 4.7 Two ways a metastable cave hardens

**Self-deepening (C18, derivation candidate).** The metastable regime's condition B3 can fail endogenously. Repeated within-frame confirmation drives the frame's edge-weights toward saturation under the foundation's update rule; accumulated structure consumes integration bandwidth (the mechanism File 6 uses for temporal phenomenology), starving frame-facing attention. The frame's own success allocates the I away from escape-generating operations. The bridge from these frozen ingredients to the sufficiency claim is application-level and is graded accordingly; its prediction — an inverted-U of insight-rate against *frame-age*, not chronological age — is H-U.

**Adversarial maintenance (C19; treated briefly per scope decision D3).** Condition B3′ can fail exogenously: an environment that *adapts* to nascent frame-facing activity, answering each flicker of meta-attention with frame-restabilizing content, drives conditional success probability toward zero however much the I allocates. Plato's puppeteers are static; the text gives them no policy. The adaptive variant is the modern one, and it yields prediction H6. The paper notes the mechanism and moves on; its development belongs elsewhere.

The taxonomy, then: two states — absorbing, metastable — and two converting mechanisms — self-ossification from inside, adversarial maintenance from outside. The allegory, §5 will show, contains the first mechanism explicitly and the static limit of the second.

## 5. The Reading

### 5.1 The apparatus of confinement (514a–515a)

The setup is precise in ways the architecture can use. The prisoners are confined *from childhood* — the closure is developmental, formed during the phases in which the I cannot direct its own formation (File 2, §4.6–4.7), which is why §7 will treat the cave's coherence as a developmental question. Their legs *and necks* are bound: two constraints, not one. The leg-bonds restrict the contact interface — which probes are physically available; in coordinate terms, a constraint on χ and hence on κ. The neck-bonds fix the head: a constraint on *attention geometry*, on which of the available contents focus can reach — in the architecture's terms, a constraint holding the two-phase focus shift at its first phase, where allocation is contact-driven and self-directed reallocation is structurally unavailable. Plato separates contact-restriction from focus-restriction twenty-four centuries before the architecture does; the allegory needs both, and the formalism says why: the probe measure $\mu$ is generated jointly from focus and outward contact, so closing either channel alone might leave discriminating probes with positive measure.

Behind the prisoners: the fire, the parapet, and the carriers. The carriers are the allegory's least-discussed figures, and the architecture gives them a precise job: they are the *engineering of the probe measure* — the curation of what crosses the screen, the determination of the support of $\mu$. The text is silent on their motives, and the reading stays structural: whatever they intend, what they accomplish is the maintenance of a stationary $S$. Their static character matters: nothing in the text has them responding to the prisoners. The cave's engineered stationarity is open-loop. The closed-loop variant — a curator that answers meta-attention with restabilization — is the adversarial regime of §4.7, and it is precisely what the text does *not* depict.

Then the sentence that sets the paper's stakes: the prisoners are like us (515a). The foundation accepted this before reading Plato — its recognition machinery is cave-internal by its own statement. The allegory and the architecture agree on the reader's address.

### 5.2 The shadow economy, the source-map error, and the conventional chain (515a–c)

**The re-diagnosis.** What exactly is wrong with the prisoners? The standard answer — they hold false beliefs — misses the structure of the text. Plato adds the echo: sounds from the carriers reflect off the facing wall, and the prisoners attribute the voices *to the passing shadows* (515b). Consider what this attribution looks like from inside. The prisoners' shadow-contents pass every reason-test available to them; their predictions succeed; within the accessible probe measure, their $\epsilon$ is low. Their pathology is not in φ. It is in σ: **the source map is systematically wrong** (C10). Sounds are mapped to shadows; shadow-regularities are mapped to shadow-natures; the entire content-economy is correctly *ordered* and wrongly *sourced*. The architecture has a slot for exactly this configuration because it defines content-level contact through the source map while denying the I direct inspection of its own source-attribution structure: truth-indistinguishability with wrong sourcing is an expressible, stable, undetectable-from-inside state. Standard readings, lacking the σ/φ separation, must describe the prisoners as deceived about *what is true*; the text describes them as deceived about *where truth comes from* — and the second description survives the observation that, by every test they can run, nothing they believe is wrong.

The companion fact (C2, on 515c): for the prisoners, the truth is *nothing but* the shadows — not because exterior hypotheses have been examined and rejected, but because no verification pathway to them is constructible. The exterior is φ-undefined. The cave's first wall is not a false belief but an absent candidate.

**The conditional that builds the third chain.** Plato introduces the prisoners' talk with a conditional: *if* they could converse, they would take their names to apply to the things passing before them (515b). The conditional is load-bearing twice over. First, the architecture must ask whether the antecedent is even satisfiable — §7.1 answers: not in the text's full form, but in a weakened form the architecture positively predicts. Second, granting the antecedent, the consequent is the σ-error *lexicalized*: a naming practice whose referential anchors are mis-sourced from the first word.

The weakened prediction (C21) has two layers, matching the foundation's two imprinting channels. **Substrate pre-alignment (C21a):** recurrent shadow-types, echo-events, and co-presence over developmental time generate, in each prisoner separately, osmotically imprinted sound–shadow associations — substrate-level co-occurrence learning, requiring no mode operation, no verification, no reasoning; this layer the foundation licenses directly. **Population proto-convention (C21b):** if the prisoners can produce publicly audible outputs and receive uptake through the shared acoustic medium — the same echoing wall now doing double duty, visual screen and acoustic mixer (the second role is the architecture's reconstruction, not the text's assertion) — then their separately held associations can converge *in extension* toward a primitive signaling equilibrium. The dynamics are those of coupled systems on a shared medium: the image of metronomes on a free plate, beginning out of phase and settling into step through nothing but their common coupling, gives the right *class* of process. The right *object* is not phase-lock but mapping-coordination — a Lewis-style convention reached by Skyrms-style reinforcement, in which production and interpretation dispositions stabilize against one another. The convergence is not a reasoned invention of language. The prisoners do not reason themselves into a code; they drift and reinforce into one. No reflective operation is required; the modes may fire incidentally on coordination encounters without being needed.

Three disciplines guard the construction. The prisoners share nothing inward: "shared" means overlapping exposure to recurrent *public* event-types and convergence of separately held substrate associations — never a shared lived-I, which the foundation excludes; coupling runs only through contact with one another's outputs. The result is a *proto-convention*, not a language; §7.1 measures the gap. And the equilibrium claim is conditional on an explicit mapping of the cave onto the signaling-game schema, which remains an open verification item (§11).

**What the convention does to the cave (C22).** Two things, in tension. It entrenches: names and uptake-practices stabilize around closure-internal apparent referents — shadows and echoes treated as the things named — so the σ-error stops being each prisoner's private mis-mapping and becomes the community's semantic infrastructure; and every successful act of coordination is a within-frame confirmation event, feeding the same update dynamics that saturate frame-edges. (No position is taken here on externalist semantics; the claim is source-map stabilization inside the cave, whatever the right theory of reference says about the words.) And yet it also leaks: before equilibrium, mismatch events between prisoners — one produces, the other uptakes wrongly — are among the richest frame-internal residuals available, exactly the pressure the metastable regime's condition B2 feeds on; after equilibrium those residuals are suppressed toward a low baseline — never to zero outside the idealized homogeneous limit, since mishearing, drift, and cohort turnover keep a trickle alive. The proto-language is simultaneously an achievement and a closure-deepening device, and the deepening consists precisely in the silencing of its own formative noise.

**The self-assembled chain (C23).** The full structure emerges only with the honors of 516c–d in view (§5.6), but its shape can be stated now. The convention makes a *shared metric* possible: only with stabilized public signals can forecasts be registered, compared, and rewarded. The honors economy then runs on the convention — and, closing the loop, the status it distributes is itself reinforcement for the convention that carries it. Convention, metric, and honors form a self-reinforcing loop, not a one-way dependency: lock-in. The cave thereby has three chains. The physical chain — bonds on contact and focus — is imposed. The developmental chain — escalator truncation under an impoverished encounter-environment — is imposed. The conventional chain is *self-assembled*: built by the prisoners, out of nothing but blameless substrate dynamics, during phases in which development is done to the I rather than by it. No prisoner authored it; all of them maintain it; it is the only chain they forge themselves.

### 5.3 Release, dazzle, retreat (515c–516a)

The release scene is a sequence of architectural events. The freed prisoner is turned toward the fire and suffers: pain, glare, incapacity. In the architecture's routing (C15), the new scene arrives as exogenous anomaly — high-contact, zero-ρ content violating the frame at maximal amplitude — and the prerogative of consistency does what it structurally does first: defends the frame *against* the datum. Plato then writes a line that binary truth-theories must treat as embarrassment and the architecture reads as native structure: told that what he now sees is *more real*, the prisoner judges the things he saw before *truer* (515d). Comparative truth, degrees of the real — *alethestera*, more true. The φ-formula is graded by construction, $1 - n\delta - \epsilon$; the text's comparative vocabulary is the phenomenology of a graded coordinate, and a reading equipped only with true-or-false must paraphrase the line away.

Then the derivation the allegory is famous for narrating and the architecture can actually run. Plato predicts the retreat: the freed prisoner, dazzled, would turn back to what he can see clearly (515e). From frozen commitments: the new contents have no constructible standing yet and no ρ — no integration into the lived-I network, hence no pull; the old frame has saturated weights and the consistency-prerogative's full defense; the continued-existence prerogative reads the disruption as threat to the maximally-self-defined I. Retreat is not weakness; it is the *default output of the dynamics*, and it follows that the ascent cannot be endogenous at this stage. Hence the text's relentless vocabulary of compulsion through 515e–516a: someone drags him. In the architecture's terms, exogenous focus-forcing substitutes for the missing endogenous pull until ρ-integration begins — which is also why the affective channel of route R3 matters: where compulsion is unavailable or illegitimate, only trust can hold an I in place through the phase in which the better is registered only as the painful.

A terminological note, since the word is about to carry weight in three different ways. This paper distinguishes three structurally different necessities. *Anagkē-up* is the external force applied during ascent, where exogenous focus-forcing substitutes for missing endogenous pull — the sense just analyzed. *Anagkē-down* is the external force applied at the descent, where Plato's founders compel the philosopher's return (§5.6). And the *memorial pull*, analyzed in §5.6, is an endogenous preservation pressure attaching to the operation by which escape became possible — not Plato's anagkē vocabulary at all, but the architecture's post-ascent preservation dynamic.

The text supplies the dazzle side of an asymmetry whose other side it has no scene for. The architecture adds it (C15): had the prisoner *assembled* the fire-hypothesis himself — composed it from echo-statistics and flicker-residuals as the unifying decomposition of anomalies he already carried — the same revision would have arrived as relief, the resolution of internal pressure, recruited rather than resisted by the consistency-prerogative. The allegory's escape is maximally painful because it is maximally exogenous. That contrast becomes a prediction (H5) and, in §7, part of the explanation of why caves are escaped from inside so rarely: the painless route requires conditions the cave is built to starve.

### 5.4 The staged ascent (516a–b)

The liberator does not haul the prisoner from screen to sun. Plato specifies a curriculum: shadows, then reflections in water, then the things themselves, then the night sky, then — last — the sun. The architecture has the mechanism in its frozen developmental core: the temporal-scale escalator, in which each stage's tools enable integration stable enough to perceive the next stage's structures. The staged ascent is dynamic-range retraining: each station recalibrates the toolset to a new intensity and scale before the next exposure, and the stations are ordered exactly as tool-enablement requires. One station rewards a closer look: the reflections. The freed prisoner there redeploys his oldest competence — reading images — on new objects; the tool-class transfers upward a tier. The escalator does not discard cave-skills; it re-grounds them. This will matter at 520c, where the returnee's superiority consists precisely in possessing both the images and their sources.

The staging claim generalizes to prediction H2: exposure to frame-distant structure succeeds as a ramp and fails as a step — staged curricula should outperform immersion wherever the target is far from the frame, with step-exposure producing the retreat of 515e or worse.

### 5.5 Summit and terminus (516b–c)

At the top, the prisoner reasons his way to the sun's role: it governs the seasons and the years, and is in some manner the cause of all he had seen. Structurally, this is chain-extension to a *source-explaining terminus* — the recognition-form the architecture's application work has analyzed at religiously charged scale, where a pattern recognized across domains routes to an ultimate explanatory position and carries the noetic feel of arrival. The architecture honors the form of the experience and, in §6, declines its metaphysics: there is no terminus at which an asymptotic content becomes intrinsic, no summit at which φ touches 1. Plato himself, glossing the image — the prison is the visible realm, the fire our sun, the ascent the soul's passage to the intelligible — appends a hedge the tradition reads past: whether the gloss is true, the god knows (517b). The text undercertifies its own metaphysical reading. The architecture's relation to the allegory is thereby licensed by the allegory: keep the structure, hold the gloss at arm's length.

### 5.6 The return (516c–521b)

**The honors, completed (516c–d).** Remembering the cave, the freed prisoner recalls its economy: honors and prizes for whoever most sharply discerned the passing shadows and best predicted their sequences. Here the allegory hands the formal core its missing mechanism. The prediction-game is not merely the cave's *metric*; it is the cave's **ossification engine** — the device by which a possibly-metastable closure is converted into an absorbing one. It rewards within-frame forecasting; it allocates focus to shadow-regularities and away from frame-facing attention; it attaches high-ρ status-contents to cave-competence; and it runs on the proto-convention of §5.2, whose maintenance it reinforces in return. The loop closes: convention makes the metric possible, the metric makes the honors possible, the honors preserve the convention. Condition B3 — non-summable frame-facing allocation — is exactly what this economy starves, and Plato wrote the starving into the text as a system of prizes. The freed prisoner's response is the ρ-side signature (C11): he would rather be the landless man's serf than win those honors — the Achilles preference (516d) — and the inversion *precedes* his ability to justify it, because network re-rooting runs ahead of explicit tooling (prediction H4).

A further structure appears in the same memory-scene (C25). The freed prisoner does not merely reject the old honors; he remembers the cave and pities those still bound (516c). Architecturally this memory is not sentimental residue alone. Once ascent has consolidated, the escape-enabling operation acquires preservation-significance, and the significance has a measure. Under the historical-edge assumption — that newly integrated post-ascent contents are initially connected to the lived-I core largely through the integrating episode — the escape-operator is *dependency-critical*: weakening it would weaken the identity-correlation of the newly formed region, a quantity expressible as a derived diagnostic over the existing ρ-graph (the summed ρ-loss across the post-ascent region under counterfactual removal of the operator), with no new coordinate. Dependency-criticality is not raw ρ: the liberating episode can itself be thinly integrated — a stranger's hand, barely known — while everything new depends on it. What the prerogative of continued existence then protects is exactly what the diagnostic measures: PCE attaches not only to the ascended state but to the operation by which that state became possible, in proportion to how much of the new self-definition that operation carries. And the pull has a temporal profile: as consolidation builds bypass routes, dependency-criticality decays by default, while felt salience may decay, persist, or transform — maintained where the operator is rehearsed, taught, ritually marked, or redeployed; free to fade into narrative where it is not.

The preservation pull is two-sided (C26), and the two-sidedness is the paper's pattern returning at a higher floor. In its healthy form the pull preserves the operator-*type*: seek better decompositions, endure transitional dazzle under disciplined trust, stage exposure, distinguish a metric-dip from damage, remain open to the next cave — a schema that enters the basis and improves the prospects of every future escape, exactly as the cofinality result requires. In its pathological form the pull preserves the *token*: this teacher, this episode, this formula, this ritual. The liberating encounter becomes an authority-object; the consistency-prerogative thereafter protects the token rather than the type; and "never forget what freed me" hardens into "always apply this exact thing." The discriminant is re-decomposability: type-preservation keeps the operator itself open to the internal route, while token-preservation immunizes it — a self-imposed closure scoped to the one content that least can afford it. The fossilized operator is a cave with a single exhibit.

**Descent and the diagnostic (516e–518a).** He goes back down, and his eyes fail in the dark; the seated prisoners laugh: he went up and came back ruined. Then Plato states, in propria persona, the rule that turns the scene from comedy into method: there are two distinct disturbances of the eyes, from light into darkness and from darkness into light; the sensible observer, seeing a soul confused, asks *which transition* it is in before laughing (518a). The architecture generalizes the rule (T3, H3): genuine channel-openings produce *competence inversion* — transient degradation on in-frame metrics before recovery and eventual dominance — and a metric dip is therefore *ambiguous between damage and ascent-in-progress*; classification requires transition-direction information that the dip alone does not carry. The evaluation corollary follows immediately and is stated here at the theory level: any evaluator that reverts on in-frame regression alone will mechanically destroy ascents, because every ascent begins by looking like one. The cave's population enacts the corollary's limit case: confronted with a returnee whose frame-violations cannot be assimilated, the collective's consistency-prerogative routes to adversarial differentiation, and the text follows the routing to its end — were it possible to lay hands on the one who unbinds and leads up, they would kill him (517a). The kill-threat is collective frame-defense, the prerogatives operating at population scale on the convention-bound profile the honors economy built.

**The rogues (519a–b), briefly.** Plato pauses over the clever-but-vicious: their sight is sharp, but compelled to serve what it serves; the deficit is not capacity but the *direction of the turning*. The architecture registers the case as toolset intact, deployment mis-routed — a configuration its cold-frame application analyzes in its own terms — and notes that Plato's remedy-language is the same as his education-language: turning, not insertion. The case belongs to that application and is left with this pointer.

**Dominance in the text (519c–520c).** The compelled descent of the philosophers is justified to them with a claim about competence: returning, and once re-habituated to the dark, they will see *ten thousand times better* than the cave's residents, because they will know *what each image is, and of what* — the image *and its source*. This is the paper's dominance criterion (T2) stated by the text. Define the order as §4.6 does: a later state dominates an earlier one when it explains everything the earlier state could access *plus* the residuals the earlier state mispredicted, and not conversely. The returnee satisfies it against the natives — he reads the shadow-sequences *and* their projection — and the natives cannot reciprocate. The criterion is not imposed on the allegory as a modern gloss; 520c asserts the asymmetry as the very ground of the returnee's authority. What the architecture adds is the criterion's other face: dominance is the *only* certification ascent ever gets. The returnee knows more than the prisoners; he does not, and cannot, know that there is no cave around the sun.

**The unwilling rulers (520d–521b).** The text closes the passage politically: the city is best governed by those least eager to rule. Plato's sentence bundles three claims of very different standing, and the reading must take them apart rather than take them over. The first the architecture derives: the ascended are *unwilling*. Their unwillingness is the 516d inversion already established (C11) — after re-rooting, the ρ-network no longer routes through the cave's status-economy, so the honors that recruit every other ruler find nothing to attach to. The second it can express, in a restricted form the text itself needs: **unwilling is not unavailable.** The philosophers do go down; they accept rule as a necessity while remaining unrecruitable by its prizes (520d–e). The architecture states the distinction cleanly — low ρ on the status-contents of ruling, with action intact under whatever argument compels it — and notes the restriction: eagerness for the *honors* of ruling indicates status-routing, but eagerness as such does not; one can want to rule from duty. (There is also a weaker endogenous return-pull the text never relies on: teaching the operation preserves the operation — use, articulation, and uptake refresh the very edges by which the ascent stays integrated (C27) — which supplies one motive toward descent-as-pedagogy; a motive only, not a sufficiency claim, not a permission to compel, and not an early payment on the governance thesis this paragraph declines.) The third claim — that such profiles govern *best* — the paper declines. It rests on premises this paper nowhere supplies: that recruitment-by-honors is a channel through which rule is corrupted, and that closing the channel improves governance on net. Plato argues both from his own political psychology; the architecture's verdict on them would be a different application paper — one whose natural intersection is the power-decoupling analysis of the cold-frame work — and asserting it here, on the strength of adjacency to a derivation, would be exactly the smuggling this paper's method forbids. The beat ends at the boundary it marks: the allegory explains why the ascended do not want the cave's crown; whether that makes them the ones who should wear it is a question the cave alone cannot answer.

**Summary of exhibits.** The reading has derived (retreat, compulsion-necessity, staging, inversion, collective resistance, the rulers' unwillingness), expressed (the σ-error; φ-undefined versus φ-low; graded aletheia; ρ-re-rooting; unwilling versus unavailable; the memorial pull and its type/token discriminant; the three chains), resolved (the dominance criterion located in the text; the fit problem, taken up next), and predicted (H2–H5, with H7, H8, and H-U to come). The instantiation power has not been used.

## 6. What the Architecture Declines

The reading would be cheap if it absorbed Plato whole. It does not; the divergences are systematic, and each has a reason in the frozen foundation.

**Two-world metaphysics.** The architecture is naturalist-realist about intrinsic structure: mathematics, logic, and reason are in re, woven into the one system the I is embedded in (File 1, §2.1; the foundation's own translations section distinguishes this from strong Platonism). There is no second realm for the ascent to arrive at; there is one system, and graded, toolset-relative access to its structure.

**The achievable summit.** Tier-3 contents carry $\phi = 1 - n\delta - \epsilon$ with $n \geq 1$ always; as decompositions improve and probes accumulate, $n$ falls and $\epsilon$ shrinks, and the content *never becomes Tier 1*. Intrinsic items are not at the top of the φ-scale; they are off it. Hence the paper's central anti-Platonic correction (C17): under the metastable regime's conditions, the toolset's closure can be *cofinal* in the internally reachable region of the dominance order — every reachable improvement eventually attained — without being complete simpliciter. For any reachable frame-state there may be a later one that explains its data, its sources, and its limitations; no state is certified final from inside. **Each insight is a larger cave, not an exit from cavehood.** The sun of the allegory, read architecturally, is the best instrument-suite so far, seen from inside it.

**The Good beyond being.** The gloss's furthest reach (517b–c, with the Sun image behind it) is bracketed as out of scope; at most, the architecture can locate a structural analogue — the intrinsic layer's position as the ontological reference against which the φ-scale measures distance — and it declines to say more.

**Strong anamnesis.** Plato's own answer to how the soul can recognize what it never learned is recollection. The architecture replaces the mechanism and keeps the phenomenon: osmotic imprinting deposits proto-tools through ambient exposure long before explicit access — the foundation's signature for it is the sense of having known X before one could say X (File 3, §8.7). Recognition that outruns instruction is real; its source is the substrate's quiet record of encounter, not a prior life's vision. Naturalized anamnesis is anamnesis with a developmental address.

**The soul's immortality and the political program** are out of scope, the first entirely, the second except for the derivation of the rulers' unwillingness and the unwilling/unavailable distinction in §5.6; the governance-quality thesis itself is declined there.

**A single translation entry.** Heidegger's reading of the allegory makes it the scene of a change in the essence of truth, from unconcealment to correctness. The architecture's φ is a third position: neither correctness simpliciter nor unconcealment, but toolkit-relative, graded truth-indistinguishability — closer to a discipline of access than to either a relation or an event. The engagement ends at this register, by design.

Throughout, the paper's license for selective inheritance is Plato's hedge: the text itself marks its metaphysical gloss as uncertified (517b). The allegory's structure is asserted; its ontology is offered with a shrug toward the god. The architecture accepts the first and returns the shrug on the second.

## 7. Disanalogies and the Coherence of the Thought Experiment

A reading that uses a text must say where the text resists. Four points, the first at length.

### 7.1 Language among the chained

Plato's prisoners converse, name, predict aloud, and award honors — a rich linguistic culture under near-total visual deprivation and severe general impoverishment. Under the architecture this is not a free assumption. Full language requires verification tools whose in re instantiations need encounter-classes the cave lacks; the escalator's truncation and the osmotic channel's starvation make the *text's* cave-culture developmentally impossible as described.

But the right correction is not deletion; it is weakening, and the weakened form is a positive prediction (C21, developed in §5.2): a primitive, emergent signal-convention — associative proto-code, drifted and reinforced into existence, never reasoned into existence — is exactly what the architecture expects from recurrent public event-types, an acoustic coupling medium, co-presence, and reinforcement. The empirical record around severe input poverty aligns, when its anchors are kept distinct: isolated deaf children without a language model generate structured but ceiling-limited gesture systems (the home-sign literature, Goldin-Meadow and colleagues); when such systems enter a community with repeated interaction and cohort transmission, structure elaborates across generations (the Nicaraguan Sign Language studies, Senghas and colleagues); transmission pressure alone regularizes artificial languages (the iterated-learning paradigm, Kirby and colleagues); constrained interaction media suffice for novel communication systems in the laboratory (experimental semiotics, Galantucci); and artificial agents converge on mappings under interaction and reinforcement (the naming-game and emergent-communication traditions). None of this *proves* the cave construction — the cave-to-signaling-game mapping is itself a gated item — but it is retrodictive alignment of the right shape (H7).

The gap, then, between the predicted proto-convention and the text's full culture is not an embarrassment to be waved past; it is a *measurement* (C24). Let the formative environment's recurrent event-types be $R_E$ and the emergent convention $\mathcal L_E$; let $\mathrm{Exp}(\mathcal L_E)$ be the set of discriminations the convention *stably and groundedly* encodes. The constraint: $\mathrm{Exp}(\mathcal L_E) \subseteq \mathrm{Cl}_{\mathrm{assoc/transmit}}(R_E)$ — the closure allowing associative linkage, analogical extension bounded by encountered source domains, temporal grouping, and transmitted simplification, but not the stable grounded encoding of encounter-classes never instantiated nor socially transmitted (leakage by noise, metaphor, or ungrounded recombination occurs, and is exactly not stable grounding). The cave's convention should therefore encode shadow identity-types, temporal order, recurrence, prediction-relevant transitions, the lexicalized source-misattributions, perhaps status terms; it should not robustly encode sunlight as sunlight, three-dimensional object-causality as such, the fire as projection-source, the carriers as carriers, or anything of the ascent. And the inverse runs one way only: the environment-to-ceiling map is many-to-one, so a convention's ceiling yields *bounds on* its formative cave, never a reconstruction. Read the prisoners' vocabulary and you learn what their world made recurrently, saliently, usably present — within bounds, and no further.

The coherence verdict on the thought experiment follows as a three-tier grading (C13): **impossible as stated** — the full culture cannot have formed there; **predicted in weakened form** — the proto-convention can and should have; **coherent as snapshot** — read not as a developmental history but as a profile-configuration, an arrangement of κ, φ, ρ, and toolset, the cave is perfectly well-formed. This grading is also the resolution of an old interpretive quarrel. The fit problem — whether the cave's stages map onto the Divided Line's segments, and the related charge that the image collapses under pressure (pressed in different ways in the literature descending from Ferguson's deflationary line, Malcolm's classic treatment of the Line and Cave, and Annas's skeptical discussion) — lives almost entirely at the developmental grain, where the cave is indeed incoherent: its prisoners cannot have become what they are where they are. At the configuration grain the fit is clean: the Line's segments and the cave's stations alike order *profile-states* — arrangements of toolset, probe-access, and integration — not strata of being, and the ascent is movement through configuration space, with the reflections-station (§5.4) marking the redeployment of an old tool-class at a new tier exactly as an ordering of configurations, rather than of realms, would predict. The architecture does not arbitrate between the dispute's parties so much as relocate the object they were disputing about.

### 7.2 The cave is never total

The text deprives vision and curates it; it leaves audition, proprioception, and sociality running. The architecture *requires* this for its own reading — the osmotic feedstock of §5.2 and the recombination route of §4.6 would otherwise be empty — and so the paper says plainly what cave-rhetoric obscures: closure here is severe, not total. The truly total case is the demon limit of §4.6's corollary family, where what survives is the bare cogito and nothing else; Plato's cave is interesting precisely because it is not that.

### 7.3 The carriers' silence

The text gives the puppeteers no motive and no policy. Political readings supply ideology; the architecture supplies only function — engineered probe-measure, open-loop — and flags the closed-loop variant (C19) as beyond the text. The restraint is deliberate: the allegory's machinery does not need the carriers' intentions, and the reading should not either.

### 7.4 Compulsion

Plato's liberator drags. The architecture *derives the need* — retreat is the default, the endogenous pull absent — and *declines the license*: nothing in the formal core converts the prediction of retreat into a permission to compel. The proposed substitute is the legitimacy condition of §4.6: help must route through discriminating predictions the prisoner's own toolkit can verify; the liberator stoops to cave-checkable evidence or remains, from inside, indistinguishable from a manipulator. The condition is a proposal, not a derivation, and is marked as such.

## 8. Periagoge: Education Without Insertion

The allegory's most quoted lesson is also its most precise, and the architecture can do something unusual with it: not interpret it, but *recover it as a result*.

Plato's claim (518b–d): education is not what some profess — putting sight into blind eyes, knowledge into a soul that lacks it. The capacity is present already; what fails is its orientation; and education is the art of *turning* — periagoge — the instrument around, with the whole soul, until it faces what it can bear to see.

Now the architecture's frozen commitment (File 2, §3.2; File 3, §8.4–8.7): verification tools are acquired through encounter and only through encounter — the focus-driven channel requires engaged, repeated meeting with instantiated structure; the osmotic channel requires ambient exposure to it. There is no third channel. In particular there is no content-transfer channel: no operation by which a tool, as opposed to a sentence *about* a tool, passes from one I into another. From this the education claim follows as a derivation candidate (C12′): **education cannot directly insert verification tools.** What it can do — all it can do — is alter the conditions under which imprinting occurs, and the foundation's two channels yield exactly two levers: **structured encounter** (focused presentation, staged exposure, guided comparison, deliberate practice — the curriculum of §5.4 is this lever applied by force) and **structured ambience** (the osmotic shaping of the substrate through arranged environments, exemplars, rhythms, language, salience — the slow lever, the one that was building proto-tools in the learner before any lesson began). Plato's "with the whole soul" then reads as the architecture's profile-level claim: the turn that matters is not a local content-update but a reorientation of focus-allocation and encounter-exposure across the profile — periagoge as the redirection of the entire imprinting regime.

Three consequences. First, the scaffolding tradition in developmental theory — the teacher as arranger of encounters within the learner's reach — is the positive image of the same result, and the architecture supplies its negative justification: scaffold because you cannot insert. Second, the Meno's paradox dissolves at the same joint where §4.6 left it: the learner cannot be *given* what she lacks, but her substrate may already hold, osmotically, what she has never been told — so the teacher's deepest work is often surfacing, not supplying; Plato's recollection was this fact wearing a metaphysical costume. Third, the failure modes of teaching are predicted along with its method: insertion-style instruction — content delivered without encounter — should produce sentence-possession without tool-possession, verbal fluency that collapses under transfer; and step-exposure without staging should produce 515e, the learner's retreat. The lesson of the cave's pedagogy passage, run through the architecture, is that Plato was right for reasons he placed in the soul and the foundation places in the imprinting dynamics — and that the reasons are testable (H1, H2). One coda the memorial analysis adds (§5.6): the art that turns one around is also the art one is thereafter pulled to preserve — and the preservation must be of the turning-type, not the episode-token, or education becomes another cave.

## 9. Predictions and Exclusions

### 9.1 Predictions

**H1 (retreat).** Absent affective bridging or compulsion, first exposure to frame-violating evidence produces return to the prior frame; bridging through a trusted, ρ-coupled other predicts persistence through the dazzle phase.

**H2 (staging).** Exposure to frame-distant structure succeeds as a ramp and fails as a step: staged curricula outperform immersion for frame-distant targets; step-exposure elevates retreat and defensive assimilation.

**H3 (bidirectional inversion).** Competence dips accompany genuine transitions in both directions; evaluators lacking transition-direction information systematically misclassify ascent as damage. The 518a rule is the corrective.

**H4 (preference precedes articulation).** After profile reorganization, indifference to the old metric appears before the agent can justify it; ρ-re-rooting runs ahead of explicit tooling.

**H5 (consolidation asymmetry).** For revisions of comparable magnitude, endogenous $n$-reducing insights consolidate with lower resistance than evidence-forced revisions: lower backsliding, lower affective threat, faster stabilization, and the retrospective-inevitability phenomenology — except where the insight reorganizes high-ρ identity-contents, where pain returns.

**H-U (inverted-U on frame-age).** Insight-rate against *frame-age* — not chronological age — is non-monotone: early frames lack material; mature, still-plastic frames peak; aged, ossified frames decline as edge-saturation and bandwidth-capture starve frame-facing attention. A person's chronological age bounds nothing here; novelty, practice, and structured meta-exposure keep frames young.

**H6 (adaptive suppression; stated briefly per scope).** In environments that adapt to their occupants, frame-facing exploration episodes are systematically followed by frame-restabilizing delivery, and insight-rate is suppressed below static-environment baseline at matched allocation.

**H7 (expressive ceiling under input poverty).** Retrodictive alignment as itemized in §7.1; the novel edge, gated on anchor verification: holding population size and learning capacity fixed, the expressive ceiling of an emergent convention tracks measured environmental and interactional richness — richer recurrent encounter-classes yield higher-dimensional proto-codes; impoverished environments yield stable but semantically narrow ones.

**H8 (memorial-pull signatures).** After a consolidated escape, the escape-enabling operator and episode show elevated preservation-markers relative to equally intense but non-reorganizing memories: early post-transition elevation (spontaneous rehearsal, reported centrality, affective salience, method-protection); decay-with-bypass — dependency-criticality fading as the post-ascent region gains alternative connections, unless refreshed; a teaching effect — agents who teach, rehearse, or redeploy the operator retain operator-salience and type-fidelity longer than agents who merely possess the insight; and a pathology signature — token-ritualization, liberator-absolutization, and method-immunization predicting later frame-capture, where the warning sign is never preservation itself but resistance to re-decomposition. One guard is built in: these are claims about salience, centrality, and preservation, not about memory *accuracy* — the flashbulb literature's lesson that vividness and confidence outlive consistency is a caution here, not an anchor.

### 9.2 Exclusions

**E1.** No reading on which the prisoners are merely mistaken. Their shadow-contents are φ-high under the accessible measure; the pathology is source-side (σ) and closure-side (undefined φ for the exterior), and any restatement of the cave as "false belief" loses the structure the text supplies.

**E2.** No content-transfer insertion of tools, anywhere — including in descriptions of escape and of teaching. Tools form through encounter or not at all.

**E3.** No achieved exteriority, anywhere — including §10. Ascent is dominance, never exit.

**E4.** No probability claims beyond the licensed forms: almost-sure statements in conditional (Lévy) form only; no expected-first-passage claims without an explicit finite-moment condition.

**E5.** No shared-lived-I or shared-referent formulations: convergence is in extension over overlapping public event-types, and the convention is a distributed pattern across separately held substrates.

**E6.** No instantiation claims: the machine correlate below bears on closure dynamics in a learning system, never on I-bearing status, in either direction.

### 9.3 Remark: a machine correlate

The closure dynamics of §4 are testable in a running learning system, and one such test-bed currently exists: an autonomous agent sitting, by construction, in an engineered E-stationary environment — a static-corpus condition whose measured frame-to-frame similarity approaches identity — with the world-side escape channel (R4) severed by a known wiring fault. Two witnesses are specified in the build-track companion to this paper (cave_build_correlates v0.0.1): a competence-inversion signature on the environment's restoration, read under the 518a rule; and a null prediction that makes §4.4 machine-falsifiable — absent an internal decomposition comparator, no endogenous escape at any runtime, however long the agent optimizes within its frame. Results are pending; nothing in this paper's argument depends on them; and per E6, nothing in them would bear on whether anything instantiates an I. They would bear on whether the closure formalism cuts nature at a joint a machine can be measured against.

## 10. Reflexive Coda

A paper about the cave is written from inside one, and the architecture it deploys says so in its own foundation: shadow-encounter recognition is cave-internal; it neither requires nor claims access outside the I's reasoning. This paper is such a recognition-event — Form (b), a composite of knowns recognized as composite: a twenty-four-century-old text and a frozen formal architecture, found to be aspects of one organization. The text reached this reading as the diachronic case of route R3: a crystallized toolkit, deposited by a prior I, carried by transmission, and — the paper's own §5.2 insists — capable of carrying a cave as easily as a rope. Nothing exempts the present apparatus from its own analysis.

So the apparatus is applied to itself, with the grades it earned. By the cofinality result (C17), if this reading is an ascent, it is an ascent to a larger cave: a frame-state that explains its predecessors' data and some of their residuals, and that some later state may stand to as it stands to them. And by the internal-undecidability claim (C16) — stated here, as everywhere in this paper, as a derivation candidate awaiting verification against the foundation's no-self-confirmation commitments — the architecture cannot certify from inside that its current basis is absorbing-complete rather than metastable: it cannot enumerate what its own primitives cannot generate, and the ruling-out that certification would require is not internally available. And C26 applies here as well: what deserves preservation is the operator-type — re-decomposing inherited materials under the architecture's constraints — not the token authority of this draft, this ledger, or this reading. The honest closing register is therefore not triumph but position: the paper has located itself on a dominance order it did not invent and cannot exit, claimed strict ascent against the readings it improves on, and declined — because its own theorems decline it — the one thing the allegory's tradition most wanted: the sun as arrival. The prisoners' deepest error was never the shadows; it was the certainty that the shadows were the end of the matter. The architecture's deepest discipline is to refuse the corresponding certainty about itself — out of the cave, in the only sense available: without leaving it.
