## Abstract

A single behavioral signature — one person treating another as an instrument or an obstacle rather than as a fellow subject — turns out to have several structurally distinct causes. This paper isolates the signature, which we call the *cold frame*, and shows that it is produced by at least three different mechanisms that ordinary moral vocabulary collapses into one. The decomposition rests on a distinction the foundation leaves implicit: between the operation that *models* another as a subject (an exercise of the verification toolkit, yielding an accurate picture of the other's identity-correlation profile, preservation prerogative, and field of experience) and the operation that *routes* that modeled subject into one's own affective configuration (so that the other's preservation-disruption registers as a perturbation in one's own profile). Call these, for this paper, the modeling channel and the routing channel. They are usually coupled — to recognize a subject is, by default, to feel them — but they are dissociable, and the strength of their coupling varies across individuals and over a life.

From this single distinction three results follow. First, psychopathy is not a deficit of affect but a low or severed coupling on the *other-subject* class of referents: the affective system is intact and self-directed feeling runs normally, while the modeled other is simply not routed. Second, the disposition to extend the golden rule is not a faculty but a content held at some position in the identity-correlation profile; held deeply (constitutively) it is enforced internally and survives the removal of external accountability, while held shallowly (instrumentally) it is enforced by the social field and evaporates when that field is flattened. Third — and this is the result that completes the architecture — the cold frame can be produced in a fully moral, fully coupled agent by an operation performed not on the agent but on the *target*: the cultural-level thinning of a whole class of others until the routing channel, working faithfully, has almost nothing left to route. This is the structure of atrocity committed by ordinary people, and it is why such people experience themselves not as cruel but as correct.

The three mechanisms require three non-interchangeable repairs, and conflating them is shown to be the reason interventions misfire. The paper closes with a treatment of what individual will can and cannot do here — it works only the slack that unchosen parameters leave open, and its reach is bounded by the agent's own model of that reach — and with the meta-ethical consequence that follows throughout: the placements that determine whether one is good, and good-without-a-watchdog, are largely unauthored, so that moral bindingness survives while moral *desert* does not.

## 1. The cold frame, and the channels that produce it

Begin with the phenomenon, described before it is explained. There is a recognizable way of relating to another in which the other figures as a means or an impediment rather than as a center of experience whose disruption matters in its own right. The other is *handled*. This is not the same as hostility — hostility grants the other quite a lot of standing — and it is not the same as indifference born of distraction. It is a specific configuration in which the machinery that would normally make another's situation *land* is not engaged. Call this configuration the **cold frame**.

The cold frame has an unusual property: it looks the same from outside regardless of how it was produced, and it can be produced in several quite different ways. A clinical psychopath in a negotiation, a decent administrator signing off on a deportation, and an ordinary person who has stopped seeing a panhandler as a person are, behaviorally, doing something similar. The thesis of this paper is that they are doing it for structurally different reasons, and that the differences are not psychological color but architectural facts with distinct dynamics, distinct predictions, and distinct repairs.

To state those differences we need one distinction that the foundation supplies the parts for but does not itself draw in this form. The architecture's identity-correlation profile $P_{m,t}$ places three coordinates on the contents of a modeling I's field — contact, truth-indistinguishability, identity-correlation — and the *Differentiated Affect* treatment shows how affect arises as a configuration of that profile rather than as a separate faculty. Within that apparatus, modeling another person and feeling them are two different operations:

The first is an exercise of the **verification toolkit**: the construction and maintenance of an accurate model of the other as an I-bearing system — a system with its own profile $P_{m',t}$, its own prerogative of continued existence, its own field of experience. Running this operation well *is* reading the other correctly; it is the machinery of agency-attribution. For brevity this paper calls it the **modeling channel** (T).

The second is the operation by which the other's *modeled* experience is taken up as an input to one's own profile — by which the other's preservation-disruption registers as a perturbation in one's own affective configuration, in the sense *Differentiated Affect* makes precise. This is what makes another's pain something one feels rather than merely notes. The paper calls it the **routing channel** (R).

These names are local conveniences, not new foundation primitives. T is verification-toolkit operation directed at an other-I; R is the affect-configuration response to a modeled other. Nothing in what follows posits a new coordinate or a new faculty; the entire analysis lives inside the existing apparatus.

One feature of this machinery colors everything that follows, and is worth fixing before the claims begin. The modeling channel does not deliver the other; it delivers a *model* of the other, and that model carries the architecture's truth-indistinguishability coordinate (φ) — it is graded by how indistinguishable-from-true it is *to the agent*, which is not how true it is. Routing then takes up that model, not the actual other (the definition above is precise on this: R responds to the *modeled* experience). So the whole T→R pipeline runs on a φ-graded representation, and wherever the model and the other come apart, the agent's response — what is felt, what is extended, what is done — faithfully tracks the *model*, and therefore presents to the agent as correct, never as error. Call this the **φ-gap**. It is not a defect to be repaired but the medium the architecture works in, and the same fact surfaces at four points below: the thinning that *presents as clarity* rather than as a lie (§3), the driven route that runs on *apparent* load rather than real mistreatment (§4), the dual golden rule that can misfire into confident paternalism on a mis-modeled ranking (§6.1), and the ordinary atrocity-path agent who *feels correct* while routing a model thinned at the source (§6A).

The first substantive claim is that **T and R are dissociable**. The modeling channel can run at full fidelity while the routing channel is attenuated, suppressed, or simply not engaged for a given referent. One can see another with complete accuracy and feel nothing; one can feel a great deal toward a target one has modeled poorly. This dissociability is the load-bearing existence claim of the paper, and §2 defends it through the case where it is most consequential.

The second claim is that the dissociability is *latent*, not *manifest*: in the ordinary case T and R are **coupled**, so that establishing "this is a subject like me" automatically recruits the routing that makes the subject felt. The coupling is the default. It is not a necessity, and it is not uniform. Its strength — how reliably modeling recruits routing — varies across individuals and within an individual across time. This paper refers to that strength as the **coupling** between the channels and, where a symbol helps, writes it $\lambda_{T\to R}$. It is essential to everything downstream that $\lambda$ is not a chosen quantity and not a fourth coordinate. It is a developed parameter, a feature of how a particular profile is organized, and it is plastic in ways §4 makes precise.

With the channels and their coupling in hand, the three production paths of the cold frame can be stated, and the rest of the paper is their development.

## 2. Psychopathy is not the absence of feeling

The folk picture, and some of the clinical one, treats psychopathy as emotional emptiness — a person who feels little or nothing. The architecture predicts something different and, on inspection, better supported: the psychopathic configuration is not low affect but **severed routing on the other-subject class of referents**, with affect otherwise intact.

The evidence sits in plain view once the distinction of §1 is available. Psychopathy co-occurs with rich self-directed affect — the pleasure of winning, the enjoyment of power, euphoria, contempt — none of which is the profile of a person whose affective system is impaired. What is specifically attenuated is the uptake of *other* subjects: the routing of a modeled other into one's own configuration. The modeling channel is frequently not merely intact but sharp; skilled manipulation requires an accurate model of the target's profile and preservation-ranking. The deficit is that this accurate model is not routed. The other is seen clearly and felt not at all.

This is why the cold frame produced by psychopathy is more dangerous than emptiness would be. An empty agent would be a poor reader of others. The psychopathic configuration pairs accurate reading with absent routing, which is precisely the combination that makes another legible *as an instrument*: one knows what moves the other, and one is unmoved. The clinical literature on attenuated responsiveness to distress cues, alongside intact or superior mentalizing, is consistent with this picture and inconsistent with global affect-flattening; the architecture's contribution is to locate the deficit at a specific channel and a specific referent class rather than in "empathy" as an undifferentiated lump.

Stated in the paper's terms, the psychopathic configuration is **low $\lambda_{T\to R}$ on the other-I referent class** — not low affect, and not poor modeling. This is the first production path of the cold frame, and it is agent-side: the coldness is a fact about how the agent's channels are coupled, present in the agent before any particular other is encountered.

## 3. How the cold frame is entered without a lie

The most disquieting feature of the cold frame, and the one that does the most architectural work later, is the manner of its onset. One might expect that to treat a recognized subject as a non-subject would require an act of self-deception — telling oneself that the other does not really matter, does not really feel, is not really one's equal. For agents of a certain constitution this route is unavailable, and yet they can still arrive at the cold frame. Understanding how is the key to §6A.

Some individuals report a configuration in which the verification channel and the affective channel are coupled so tightly that the channel's output of *true* is not separable from a felt quality — for whom, in the first-person idiom, truth registers as a feeling and direct self-deception is not a move they can make. Within the architecture this is the high-coupling limit: T and R share so much that T cannot deliver "true" without R firing. The naïve prediction for such a person is that the cold frame is simply locked out. To occupy it they would have to hold "this is a subject like me" as true (T fires) while withholding routing (R suppressed) — but their R fires *with* T, so the withholding would require the modeling channel to deliver a falsehood, which they cannot perform.

The prediction is wrong, and the way it is wrong is the important part. The exit to the cold frame does not run through a lie. It runs through **referent-thinning**: the gradual attenuation of the *model* of the other until the routing channel has little or nothing to route. No proposition "the other is not a subject" is ever asserted. The model simply thins — the other becomes less detailed, less present as a full center of experience — and the affect, having less to take up, fires less. At the limit there is almost nothing there to feel, and at no point was anything false claimed. The affect did not cease because the truth was denied; it ceased because the referent was attenuated.

This matters for two reasons. First, it explains how an agent who genuinely cannot deceive themselves can nonetheless become cold: the route bypasses the faculty that blocks the lie. Second, and consequentially, it predicts the *phenomenology* of the transition. Because no falsehood is asserted, the thinned state does not present to introspection as deception. It presents as **clarity**. The agent does not experience themselves as failing to feel a person; they experience themselves as accurately perceiving that there is less there to feel. The cold frame, entered this way, arrives wearing the face of insight — the φ-gap of §1 in its starkest individual form, the thinned model registering as accurate perception so that nothing presents to introspection as false. This is the single most important fact for understanding how decent people do terrible things, and §6A is its application at scale.

## 4. Driven, not built: the coupling as a developed and distributed quantity

It would be convenient, and it would be false, to treat $\lambda$ as fixed hardware that sorts people into the warm and the cold. The coupling is developed and plastic, and where an individual sits with respect to it is distributed across the population in a way that resists any single modal story. This section states the distribution and develops the one route through it that is architecturally closed; the finer structure of one sub-population is a pending author call and is held in skeleton below.

**The distribution.** The capacity to occupy the cold frame toward a subject is not a uniform trait, not a simple deficit, and not a free choice. It is governed by the coupling $\lambda$, whose baseline is spread across individuals along at least three regions. Some begin past the threshold at which the cold frame is the standing baseline — for them coldness toward some class of others is where they started, not a place they were driven to. Some hold a high coupling that only sufficient accumulated load could lower — for them the cold frame is reachable, but only by being *driven* there over time. And some sit so far inside the coupling that no load they will realistically meet would bring them to it. Where any individual sits is, with respect to moral desert, arbitrary: it was not chosen and not earned. This is developed at the meta-ethical level in §7; here it is a structural premise.

**The driven route.** For the middle region — high coupling, plastic, load-sensitive — the cold frame is reached by a *state-to-trait conversion*: a durable lowering of the coupling driven by accumulated load. The load is *apparent* — *apparent* betrayal, *apparent* exploitation, the accumulated cost of routing others the agent registers as not having warranted it — and the qualifier is load-bearing: what does the imprinting is the cost *as registered*, not whether the betrayal was real, since a registration carries its somatic-affective cost whether or not it is veridical (the φ coordinate is truth-*indistinguishability*, not verified truth). So the conversion can be driven by a miscalibrated threat-model as readily as by real mistreatment: an agent who comes to register a benign class as exploitative has its routing toward that class lowered just the same, and the cold frame so reached need not have been earned by anyone's actual wrong. Such load is, structurally, repeated co-occurrence between routing a referent class and a somatic-affective cost; and by the foundation's account of osmotic imprinting (File 3, §8.7) — a primary mechanism by which dispositions form, operating on the third-order substrate that carries affect — that co-occurrence recalibrates the substrate that carries routing. The lowering therefore carries the foundation's own two-layer signature, the *formation/activation* distinction §8.7 draws for every osmotic disposition: a *formation* layer, in which the routing substrate is durably recalibrated so that the lowered coupling becomes the new baseline, and an *activation* layer, in which the recalibrated substrate fires attenuated routing toward that class whenever it is encountered. No new machinery is introduced — the driven route is §8.7 imprinting operating on the routing disposition — and its rates and reversibility are left, as the foundation leaves them, to empirical work. The first-person report that anchors this paper — *"I cannot lie to myself"* — is, on this analysis, a true description of a current high-coupling regime, not a law of the speaker's nature. It describes how the channels are coupled now, under the loads met so far. It is not a guarantee about loads not yet encountered.

This reframes a familiar moral observation. The person who was "too trusting" and "only got hurt" did not have too much routing; they had routing without discrimination — they extended the golden rule by projecting their own preservation-ranking onto others whose actual rankings differed, and were exploited at exactly the points where the projection failed (the mechanism is §6.2 below). The jadedness such a person accumulates is not the corrosion of their capacity to feel. It is the installation of the threat-model that prices routing correctly — that gates *whom* to route by measured profile rather than by default. Maintaining a high coupling while learning to gate it by warrant is not free; it has a cost, paid over years, and that cost is the felt texture of "earned" wariness. The naïve configuration was routing minus threat-model; the mature one is routing plus threat-model, not routing removed.

## 5. The cold frame is occupied per-relation, not held per-person

A natural misreading of everything so far is that the cold frame is a stance one *has toward a person* — that an other is either in one's warm circle or outside it. This is false, and a small domestic observation refutes it cleanly. The frame is occupied per *relation*, and it reassigns in real time as the salient relation in one's field shifts. The same other can be routed and unrouted within seconds, depending not on any change in one's regard for them but on the role they occupy in whatever relation is currently salient.

Consider the structure of an ordinary moment. A person is absorbed in something — music, work — and the salient relation in their field is self-to-task. A pet solicits attention and registers as friction: an annoying distraction. Note the encoding. The pet is not being routed; it is not even functioning as an instrument *of* anything; it is simply an impediment to the salient relation, granted little standing because nothing in the field requires it to have any. This is the universal default, and it is worth dwelling on that it carries no coldness in the pejorative sense: the non-salient other is handled lightly because it is non-salient, not because the agent is deficient.

Now a second pet does something to the first. The salient relation flips — the agent's action-orientation turns toward *correcting the second*. And here is the observation that matters: the agent may immediately, visibly lavish affection on the *first* pet. But the affection is not generated for the first pet's sake; routing toward it does not switch on. The display is an action aimed at the *second* pet, routed *through* the first one's body as a medium — a correction by demonstrated exclusion, "the one you mistreated is the one I favor." And the maneuver only works because the second pet is modeled as an agent capable of *reading* the display as favoritism and being stung by it. So in the same instant: the first pet is an unrouted medium, and the second is a fully modeled subject acted upon instrumentally.

This inverts the intuitive mapping. One expects kindness to track routing and instrumentality to track de-animation; here the animal shown kindness gets the instrumental treatment, and the animal being *corrected* gets the full agency-attribution, because the correction requires a subject who can perceive exclusion. The deed and the routing come apart, and they come apart on a timescale far too short for any change in trait or in standing. What changed was *which relation was salient*, and the standing of each party followed from its role in that relation.

The general result is that the cold frame's occupancy tracks *role-in-the-salient-relation*, not *identity-of-the-other*. This has a sharp consequence for the trait-level claims of §§2–4: those are claims about an agent's *standing baseline* coupling, not about whether the cold frame is ever locally occupied. Everyone occupies it locally, constantly, toward the non-salient. The psychopathy of §2 is not the occasional occupancy — it is the low baseline. And the genuinely cold operation, the one that deserves the name, is not the de-animation of §3 (which at least removes the subject from view) but its limit: modeling the other as a full subject *and* acting on them purely instrumentally, where the routing is available and simply never recruited into the action. That is colder than thinning, because the subject stays in full view and is declined anyway. §6 explains when and why that recruitment fails.

## 6. The golden rule is a placement, not a faculty

The operations of §§1–5 — modeling and routing — are not yet the whole of moral response. There is a further step, the one the golden rule names: having routed an other, one *back-propagates one's own preservation-ranking onto them* as the signal for correct action — "what ranking would I want applied to me here" — and lets that govern what one does. The question this section answers is what kind of thing that step is, and the answer reorganizes the moral landscape without adding any new machinery.

### 6.1 The projection inside the golden rule

First, a caveat that the rest of the section carries. Back-propagating one's own ranking onto the other is valid only under profile-similarity. Where the other's actual preservation-ranking differs from one's own, the projection misfires, and the misfire is exactly the vulnerability of naïve good faith: one assumes the other wants what one would want, and is exploited by those who do not. The mature form of the golden rule is therefore not the bare rule but the rule *gated by the measured profile of the other* — extend the symmetry where the profiles are similar enough for it to hold, and rely on the threat-model where they are not. This is the same gating §4 identified as the content of earned wariness. It holds regardless of how deeply the golden rule is held; depth of holding and profile-discrimination are independent.

There is, alongside this, a *dual* formation of the rule, and naming it sharpens what the gating is for. The bare rule fills its counterfactual with the agent's *own* preservation-ranking — *what would I want done to me here* — and gives the other that. Its dual fills the counterfactual with the other's *perceived* ranking instead — *given I had their preservation-ranking, how would I want to be treated* — and gives them that (the folk platinum rule, kept here as a counterfactual the agent still runs on themselves, with the other's ranking substituted). Both are golden-rule-shaped, both reason by a symmetric counterfactual, and they differ only in whose ranking fills the variable — so they fail in complementary places. The self-ranking form fails under profile-*dissimilarity*: plug in my ranking and I treat the other as *I* would want, which misfires precisely where their actual ranking diverges from mine. The dual form removes that failure — it has already substituted their ranking — but inherits another, relocated into the *model*: it acts on the other's ranking *as perceived*, high-φ content that need not be veridical, so a mis-modeled ranking yields confident, symmetric-feeling, and wrong treatment — the structure of paternalism and of well-meant harm. The two are thus two repairs for the single projection problem, trading a *similarity* requirement for a *modeling-accuracy* one: the self-form leans on routing — the default by which a routed other is felt as another instance of oneself, ranking included — while the dual form leans on the modeling channel (T) to represent where the other's ranking actually differs, and is only ever as good as that model. The mature rule uses both: substitute the other's ranking where the model of it is sound, and gate by similarity, falling back on the threat-model, where it is not. This is why the gating §4 named is indispensable to either — it tells the self-form when projection is safe and the dual form when the modeled ranking can be trusted — and it marks the dual form's specific exposure to §6A, where a class's model is thinned at the source until *treat them as they would want* collapses, the model having been hollowed of anything for them to want.

### 6.2 Least resistance — and its limit

Why hold the golden rule at all? One powerful answer is that, among other subjects, it is the path of least resistance. It is difficult to fault someone who is genuinely trying to do well by you; the rule minimizes retaliation, friction, and the withdrawal of cooperation. An embedded agent who depends on others — and every agent does; one does not build the roads or run the institutions oneself — finds that routing others and treating them by the golden rule is simply the cheapest way to live among them. Morality-as-decency and morality-as-cheapest-coordination-technology converge on the same behavior.

But this answer, taken alone, has a corrosive implication: if the golden rule is held *because* it is least-resistance, then it is only as stable as the resistance. Remove the other's capacity to retaliate, to fault, to withhold cooperation — flatten the field — and the least-resistance path no longer runs through the golden rule. On this story alone, anyone handed unaccountable power should drop the rule, because the gradient that selected it is gone. Since some people manifestly do not, the least-resistance story cannot be the whole account. It explains some moral behavior and mislocates the rest.

### 6.3 Two placements

The resolution is that the golden rule is not a faculty one either has or lacks, and not a single thing held one way. It is a **content held at some position in the identity-correlation profile** $P_{m,t}$, and its architectural significance is set by *where* it is held. There are (at least) two such placements, and they are observationally identical until the field is flattened.

Held **constitutively** — integrated deeply into the maximally-self-defined I — the golden rule is enforced from *inside*. To defect from it is not to abandon a strategy that stopped paying; it is a violation of the prerogative of consistency, a perturbation of the coherence of the self, a partial self-loss. Such a holding is **field-independent**: removing external accountability does not remove the cost of defection, because the cost was never external in the first place. The least-resistance story is simply not the floor under this placement; the floor is internal.

Held **instrumentally** — as a learned pattern in the verification toolkit, deployed because it is least-resistance — the golden rule is enforced from *outside*, by the field's structure of retaliation and cooperation. This holding is **field-contingent**: flatten the field and the pattern is dropped, because the only thing holding it was the gradient that flattening removes.

At baseline — in a field with intact resistance — the two placements produce the *same* conduct. Both holders do well by others. They diverge precisely and only when the field flattens. This is the discriminating fact, and it is the strongest operational handle the paper offers: genuine and instrumental morality are behaviorally indistinguishable until accountability is removed, at which point the constitutive holder is unchanged and the instrumental holder reverts. The everyday catastrophe of power — the decent-seeming person who curdles on acquiring unaccountable authority — is, on this analysis, not a faculty breaking. It is a flattened field acting on an instrumentally-placed golden rule. The faculty was never there to break; the incentive that mimicked it was withdrawn.

This also explains the limit case of §5. The genuinely cold operation — full modeling, available routing, golden rule never recruited — is exactly what the instrumental placement produces under field-flattening: the agent still sees the other and could feel them, and simply has no live reason to extend the rule, because the gradient that extended it is gone. And it places the truth-as-affect individual of §3 at the opposite pole: their golden rule is so deeply constitutive, so fused with the verification channel, that defection registers as felt incoherence — which is why they cannot reach the cold frame even under maximal field-flattening, even disinhibited, even with no one watching. Their floor is internal and the flattening does not lift it.

### 6.4 The cleanup

The folk binary — treat others "as people" or "as objects" — collapses three independent things. There is the question of whether the *model* of the other is intact or thinned (§3). There is the question of whether routing is *recruited* (§§2, 5). And there is the question of whether the golden rule is *placed* constitutively or instrumentally (§6.3). An agent can be a clear-eyed modeler whose routing is simply not recruited; a thinner of the other's model; or an accurate, routing, but projection-erroring extender of naïve good faith. These are distinct configurations with distinct dynamics, and the single axis "warm vs. cold" cannot tell them apart. The architecture's contribution to moral psychology here is precisely the disaggregation.

### 6.5 The will and its bounds

A reader will ask where choice enters. If placement determines whether one is good-without-a-watchdog, surely one *chooses* one's placement? The architecture's answer is no — and the no is what keeps the meta-ethics of §7 consistent.

Placement is a **derived** quantity, not a free one. It is set by the coupling $\lambda$, by the margin structure of §4, by the history of fields one has been embedded in, and by one further factor: the toolkit's own model of what one's will can do. None of these is chosen. The coupling is born or driven; the margin is given; the field-history is external; and the self-model of the will is itself a developed content. Placement falls out of these. Making it instead a *freely-willed* knob would contradict, head-on, the desert-removal §7 depends upon — for that argument turns on these placements being unauthored.

This does not abolish the will; it bounds it. Individual willing is real, and it operates on the *slack* that $\lambda$, margin, and field leave open. But its access to that slack is itself gated: **one can attempt only what one's toolkit certifies the will is capable of.** The constraint is reflexive. The will moves within the live options the toolkit constructs, and "what the will can reach" is one of the toolkit's own estimates — learned, positioned in the profile, and unchosen. So the will is constrained but not fixed: it works a genuine residual, and the *visibility* of that residual is set by a self-model the agent did not author. This explains the agent who is stuck while capable — whose coupling and history would *permit* a constitutive placement, but whose self-model never certifies the attempt as a live option, so the slack sits there unused and invisible. And it explains, again, the truth-as-affect case from the inside: that agent's toolkit correctly reads "will yourself cold" as *not a live option*, so the slack toward coldness is, for them, empty.

Two corollaries close the section, because the will's self-model is the kind of thing one is tempted to assess from outside, and the assessment is harder than it looks.

First, whether the self-model is *accurate* — whether it tracks the will's actual reach — is **not a property of any single outcome**. At a single instance there is no calibration to read; a correct attempt and a lucky one are indistinguishable, not because the difference is hidden but because the difference does not yet exist. Calibration is a property of the *pattern across instances*; it is constituted by the series and absent from any member of it. The commonsense notion of an "accurate self-model" only becomes meaningful, only becomes true-or-false, over repetition.

Second, even repetition is not enough if it is the wrong repetition. A long record of correct outcomes can be manufactured three ways, each of which mimics genuine structure and each of which demands a different control. A *fixed* estimate that happens to cross the truth where reality meets it produces hits at the crossings and misses everywhere else — the person forever certain of conspiracies who is, once, correct; the hit is no evidence the next call beats chance, and the control is to check whether the estimate *moves with conditions* or stays flat. A pattern that *survives because the environment selects on its output* — the idiosyncratic colleague who succeeds just often enough to keep the position — yields a clean-looking series that is clean only because the failures were filtered out before one observed them; the control is to sample instances *independently of outcome*. And a *field-contingent* producer — the polished operator who does right because, so far, doing right has paid — tracks *perfectly* across any number of instances *while the field holds*, indistinguishable from genuine virtue until the conditions vary; the only control is to vary the field, which is the flattening test of §6.3. The upshot is a specification rather than a slogan: to read genuine structure off conduct one needs multiple instances, *across varied conditions*, *sampled independently of outcome* — and absent all three, a spotless record means very little. The same epistemic limit returns in §7 as a fact about desert, and in §6A as a fact about who, under an atrocity, was actually who.

## 6A. Exogenous referent-thinning: the cold frame in ordinary moral agents

Everything to this point locates the cold frame in features of the *agent* — the coupling, the margin, the placement. There is a production path that does not, and it is the one with the highest stakes, because it is the structure of mass atrocity committed by people who are, by every agent-side measure, ordinary and moral. An architecture of the cold frame that cannot accommodate it is incomplete in the most important place.

**The phenomenon to be explained.** The historical record of genocide and systematic cruelty is not, in the main, a record of psychopaths. Its perpetrators were overwhelmingly ordinary moral agents — people with intact, often wide, constitutively-held golden rules, who loved their families, kept their word, and felt guilt over ordinary wrongs. Yet they produced the cold frame toward an entire class of others. Any account that locates coldness only in agent-side parameters cannot explain this, because on those parameters these agents were not cold. This section closes the gap. It treats the established historical cases — among them the Shoah, chattel slavery and the racial pseudo-science that serviced it, apartheid, the Armenian genocide — as the *explanandum*, the structure to be accounted for, not as rhetoric and not as contested matter. A boundary note at the end fixes what the architecture does and does not claim about present-day disputes.

**The mechanism.** Recall from §3 that the cold frame can be entered by *thinning the model of the other* until routing has nothing to take up, and that this requires no lie and presents as clarity. In §3 the thinning was *endogenous* — performed by the agent on their own model. The decisive addition here is that thinning can be **exogenous**: performed on a whole *class* of others at the level of the cultural-I, and inherited by the individual agent as an already-corrupted input to their modeling channel. The propaganda, the legal category that strips standing, the slur that does the stripping in a word, the pseudo-scientific apparatus that dresses it as knowledge — these are *thinning instruments*. They install, across a population of individual Is, a degraded model of the targeted class. The individual agent does not perform the thinning; they receive its product.

What such an agent then runs is *not* the configuration of §2. It is not accurate modeling with severed routing — that is psychopathy. It is **faithful routing over a model that was thinned at the source**. The routing channel works exactly as it should; it simply receives a degraded other and faithfully routes the degraded version, which is to say it routes almost nothing, because, as the agent's model reports it, there is almost nothing there to route. The agent's own architecture is intact. The coupling is intact. The golden rule is intact and constitutively held. None of it engages the targeted class, because the model that would trigger it has been hollowed out upstream.

And because — per §3 — the thinned model never registers as a lie, the agent does not experience themselves as cruel. They experience themselves as **correct**. Not "I am failing to feel a person," but "I am correctly perceiving something that is not a full person." The dehumanizing materials are believed, and believed without the friction a lie would generate, so the conduct that follows feels not like wickedness but like clear sight — the φ-gap of §1 at population scale, the routed model being high-φ and so indistinguishable, to the agent, from accurate sight. This is the answer to the oldest question about atrocity — how ordinary people do it and sleep — and it is an answer the architecture *predicts* rather than merely permits, because the no-falsehood character of referent-thinning was established in §3 for entirely independent reasons.

**The three sources, and why repair must match the source.** The cold frame now has three structurally distinct production paths, and they are summarized below precisely because they share an output and nothing else.

| Path | The agent | Modeling channel (T) | Routing channel (R) | What produces the coldness | What repairs it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Psychopathy** | born or driven to low coupling (§§2, 4) | intact, often sharp | severed / never coupled on the other-class | agent-side: the channels are decoupled | barely repairable; re-coupling is limited |
| **Power-decoupling** | high coupling, *instrumental* golden-rule placement (§6.3) | intact | available | field-flattening removes the incentive that ran an instrumental rule | **restore the field** — accountability, observability |
| **Atrocity-path** | high coupling, *constitutive*, wide; fully moral | **exogenously thinned** on the targeted class | intact, faithful | the model of the other was hollowed out at the cultural source | **restore the model** — re-thicken the other: contact, counter-evidence, re-humanization |

The practical claim is that these repairs are **not interchangeable**, and that cross-applying them is why interventions fail. Accountability — the right repair for power-decoupling — does little for the atrocity-path agent, because that agent already feels fully accountable, to their own intact morality, which the thinned model simply routes around. Re-humanization — the right repair for the atrocity-path — does little for the psychopath, whose model was never thinned in the first place and whose routing would not engage a thickened model anyway. Diagnosing the path is therefore not academic; it determines whether the intervention can work at all.

### 6A.1 The cultural-I does the thinning, and is not an agent

The thinning instruments — propaganda systems, legal categories, slur-networks, pseudo-scientific apparatus — operate at the level of the cultural-I: distributed encoding structures that install a corrupted model across many individual Is. It is essential, and it is the most load-bearing discipline in the paper, that the cultural-I is **not anthropomorphized**. It does not decide, intend, or will. It is a distributed pattern, not a mind. Intent is attributed only to identifiable individual and institutional agents where the record supports it — never to "the culture" as such.

This is not stylistic fastidiousness; it is a constraint forced by the architecture's own account of what a cultural-I *is*. The cultural-I is *emergent over* its individual Is and has no existence apart from them. Therefore any predicate attached to it does not stay there — it **distributes back down** onto every individual correlated with that culture. The discipline is consequently bidirectional, and both directions are real moral errors.

Attribute *intent* upward, to "the culture" or "the nation" or "the colonizer," and by the structure of emergence you have attributed that intent to each culturally-correlated individual — including those who held their model of the targeted class intact and did not participate. Anthropomorphizing the cultural-I *launders an unearned intent onto the uninvolved*; this is collective guilt, manufactured by a category error.

Refuse to attribute the wrong *downward*, to the actual participating individuals, and atrocity becomes nobody's deed — explained into a fog of "forces" and "systems" with no answerable agent in it.

Both errors share a single root: the misplacement of agency. The cultural-I *thins* — it is pattern, and carries no intent. Identifiable individuals and institutions *intend, build, run, comply with, or resist* — they are agents, and they are answerable. Intent lives only at the individual and institutional layer, in both the doing and the judging. The emergent layer carries pattern and never will.

### 6A.2 Who, under an atrocity, was actually who

Because intent cannot live at the cultural-I, the population under a thinning apparatus is not a uniform moral mass. It resolves into distinct, individually-attributable positions — and the resolution is exactly what the "ordinary moral agents" framing requires to avoid both errors above. Attribution here is *by production path*, not by deed alone: two people who perform the same outward act occupy different moral positions if their conduct was produced by different structures. This is the same deed-versus-disposition cut the paper has enforced throughout, now applied to virtue as well as to wrong.

There are the **architects and operators**, who built and ran the thinning apparatus — who authored the corrupted model. They bear full intent and full answerability.

There are the **compliant**, who acted on the thinned model without resisting the counter-evidence available to them. They are answerable for the thinning they permitted; that the model arrived pre-corrupted mitigates by degree but does not erase, because the counter-evidence was reachable and they did not reach for it.

There are the **genuine resisters**, who held their model of the targeted class *intact against* the thinning — whose wide, constitutive routing refused the corrupted input. These are the rescuers and the refusniks, and the architecture predicts them not as inexplicable saints but as the expected tail of the same distribution: the high-coupling, intact-model end of the population, often with independent access to un-thinned sources (cross-group contact, alternative information) that kept their model thick where the apparatus tried to hollow it.

There are the **performative resisters** — and this position is the one the architecture exposes that moral common sense tends to miss. An agent with an *instrumental* golden-rule placement, in a field that has *not* flattened — a field where visible resistance is observed and rewarded with status or reputation or future advantage — will resist *because resistance pays*. The output is identical to the genuine resister's. The model of the targeted class may be as thinned as the compliant agent's, or simply irrelevant to the calculation; the routing need not engage the victim at all. Their position is distinct from the genuine resister's precisely because their resistance was *contingent*: the victim was not harmed by them under these conditions, but would not have been protected by them under flattened ones. Performed virtue, it turns out, has exactly the same multiple-realizability as performed cruelty — the architecture is recursive here — and the discriminator is already in hand: the field-flattening test of §6.3, applied now to the resistance-output. Vary the field, remove the audience and the reward, and watch which resistance persists and which evaporates.

And there are the **merely correlated and non-participating**, who carry the cultural-I correlation and carry *no intent whatsoever*. This is the position the emergence-discipline of §6A.1 exists to protect: anthropomorphizing the cultural-I is precisely the error that would convict them of a guilt that was never theirs.

A caution holds this taxonomy together. The five positions are about *attributable agency and production path*, not about the capacity parameters of §§2–4. A genuine resister and a compliant agent may have the very same coupling; what separates them is whether they let their model be thinned. A performative resister and a power-decoupler may share the very same instrumental placement; what separates them is only the field conditions they happen to be acting under. The capacity layer — coupling, margin, placement — and the attribution layer — architect, compliant, genuine resister, performative resister, non-participant — are orthogonal, and they cross. Keeping them orthogonal is what prevents the whole analysis from collapsing back into "good people and bad people," which would undo in a sentence the desert-work of §7.

### 6A.3 What observation can and cannot recover

The performative-resister position generalizes into an epistemic result that the paper has been approaching from several directions. Because genuine and performative resistance are behaviorally identical in an unflattened field, *observable right action is not evidence of an intact model, of recruited routing, or of a constitutive placement.* The inference "the rescuers were the ones who could feel" is not licensed: some rescuers were genuine resisters and some were calculators in a field that rewarded rescue, and conduct alone cannot tell them apart. This is the §6.5 result about reading structure off a series — needing varied conditions and outcome-independent sampling — now stated for moral conduct specifically. It is not nihilism: the genuine resister really is more reliably good, really is field-independent. The claim is only that an *observer* cannot read which is which from the deeds, which is, not incidentally, the exact channel through which reputations are gamed by the instrumentally-placed.

### 6A.4 Re-thickening: when restoring the model re-recruits routing

The atrocity-path repair named above — restore the model, re-thicken the other — leaves open a question the three-repairs table does not settle: does a restored model automatically re-recruit routing, or can a class remain affectively unrouted even after its model is accurate again? Both outcomes occur, and the architecture says what separates them. The repair operates on the modeling channel (T); routing (R) is a distinct channel; so whether re-thickening the model also rebuilds routing turns on *how* the model is re-thickened. A model refilled as information alone — being told, accurately, that the class are full subjects — rebuilds T while leaving R untouched, and yields the residual: an accurate model that is still not felt. This is the converse of the dissociation §8's third prediction turns on — there, restored affect does not confer standing; here, restored standing does not confer affect — and both directions are the same independence of the two channels, read once each way. A model refilled through *lived* re-thickening — sustained osmotic and direct experience of the class as subjects — rebuilds R as well, because that experience just is the repeated affective co-occurrence that osmotically forms a routing disposition (File 3, §8.7); it is the §4 driven thinning run forward rather than back. In most real cases re-thickening is lived rather than merely informational, so routing is usually rebuilt — but it need not be, and the toolkit-only case is a genuine possibility, not a contradiction.

The rebuild has a signature that matters for prediction and for practice alike: *routing strength is latent until a triggering event tests it.* By the formation/activation distinction of §8.7, lived re-thickening builds routing strength as **formation** — accumulating without manifesting, below the threshold at which **activation** fires — and the rebuilt routing produces felt pain and recruited action only when a triggering event arrives *and* strength has crossed that threshold. The pain threshold is therefore never tested by ordinary cooperation; it is tested only by an event that puts the class's preservation in question, and only then does it become visible whether routing was rebuilt at all. (Structurally this is metastable buildup completing at a trigger — the Pattern-B onset-trajectory of *Differentiated Affect*.) The prediction is consequently a *discontinuity* rather than a gradient: re-recruitment shows not as steadily warming conduct during the filling-in, but as a sudden engagement at the first triggering event after the threshold is crossed.

The canonical case makes the structure concrete. Two people, A and B, from groups the culture has thinned against each other, A's the dominant one; they are forced into sustained, often hierarchical, cooperation. Across it, osmotic and direct experience gradually fill in A's degenerate model of B — and, in the typical case, rebuild routing toward B along with it. For a long while none of this is legible at the level of conduct. Then A witnesses B in a conflict, in danger; and the routing that has been quietly rebuilt now fires — B's preservation-disruption registers in A's own configuration, B falls within the scope of the golden rule A already held, and A moves to defend B, where before the threshold was crossed A would have looked away, or watched with no perturbation of frame-coherence at all, because there was nothing there, *for A*, to be perturbed by. The change is not that A acquired a new value; it is that the referent was re-thickened until the value A already held could finally take it up. Re-humanization, then, is necessary but sufficient only when it rebuilds routing past threshold — which lived contact usually accomplishes and instruction alone usually does not, and which, either way, only a test reveals.

### 6A.5 Boundary note

The architecture explains the *general structure* by which ordinary moral agents produce the cold frame at scale, through the cultural-level corruption of their model of a targeted class. It does **not** adjudicate contested present-day cases, assign perpetrator and victim roles in disputed conflicts, or rule on who is thinning whom now. The historical anchors above are used because they are the established explanandum. Any extension to a present, contested case is a matter for evidence and judgment outside this paper's remit; the model supplies the mechanism, not the verdict. And a separate boundary, as load-bearing as the others: *explaining the mechanism is not excusing the deed.* That the atrocity-path agent's architecture was healthy, and that their model arrived pre-thinned, clarifies how the wrong was produced; it does not make the wrong less wrong, and it leaves the compliant answerable for the counter-evidence they declined and the architects answerable for everything. Mechanism is not exculpation.

## 7. Bindingness without desert

A consequence has been accumulating through every section, and it is now stated directly, because it is the paper's meta-ethical core and the place it is most likely to be misread.

The placements that determine an agent's moral character are, with respect to desert, **arbitrary** — not chosen and not earned. Where one's coupling sits was born or driven, never authored. The margin one began with was given. And whether one holds the golden rule constitutively or instrumentally — whether one's morality survives the removal of a watchdog — is itself an unauthored position in the profile, set by the unchosen factors of §6.5. Run the symmetry honestly and it cuts both ways: the arbitrariness that disarms *blame* for a low coupling equally disarms *credit* for a high one. The person whose decency is field-independent did not author the integration that makes it so, any more than the person who curdles under power authored the placement that lets them.

This is not the dissolution of morality, and the distinction that saves it is between *bindingness* and *desert*. A moral constraint can be fully authoritative over an agent without having been authored by them; most real constraints are. The contingency of a constraint's *origin* does not touch its *authority*. So one keeps, intact, "this is right and I am held to it," while giving up "and I deserve credit for being the sort who is held." The two come apart cleanly once they are distinguished, and most confusion in this vicinity comes from fusing them — from inferring, because morality binds, that it must have been chosen, or, because it was not chosen, that it cannot really bind. Neither inference holds.

Three further points follow. The demand "you could simply have chosen to care" is incoherent wherever it is aimed across the real variance in coupling and threshold: it presupposes a uniform resistance of the will's lever that does not exist, and it is coherent only when aimed at someone whose threshold sits where the speaker's does. This is not relativism — the constraints remain real and binding on each agent — but a limit on using one's own placement as the universal yardstick for others' *capacity*.

The most admired moral property in the catalogue, incorruptibility under power, receives the sharpest form of the result. It is real, it is rare, it is behaviorally consequential, and the field-flattening test of §6.3 can detect it. And the *desert* attaching to it is illusory in exactly the way the desert attaching to a high coupling is, because the constitutive placement that produces it was unauthored. The incorruptible person is genuinely more to be relied upon than the instrumental one — and merits no *credit* for the placement that makes them so. Bindingness without desert, stated at its most counterintuitive: the better agent is really better, and deserves nothing for it.

And the result compounds with §6A.3's epistemic limit. Desert was already severed from the placement; observation cannot even recover the placement from conduct. So moral credit-assignment by an outside observer runs on data that cannot distinguish constitutive virtue from instrumental calculation from outright performance. This is not a counsel of cynicism — the genuine article exists and is exactly what it appears to be from the inside — but it is a hard limit on what praise, directed from outside at observed conduct, can actually be tracking.

A final structural floor, stated with its modesty intact. Even pure self-interest does not reach a *standing* decoupling from all others, because no agent exists in a vacuum: one needs the coordinated labor of other subjects, and the fully decoupled agent either free-rides on others' routing or pays the steeper cost of coordinating with subjects it refuses to grant standing. This is a real convergence between decency and the cheapest way to live among others — but it is a floor under the *instrumental* placement specifically, it is field-contingent, and it proves only that routing is the low-effort equilibrium for an embedded needy agent. It is not a derivation of obligation, and it is not the floor under constitutive morality, whose floor is internal and needs no such argument. The convergence is worth naming and worth not overselling.

## 8. Discriminators

The architecture earns its keep only if it predicts what competitors do not, and the predictions must be independent of one another rather than one claim wearing several hats. What follows is the independent core, stated as falsifiable commitments; a set of weaker corollaries and borrowings is noted afterward and deliberately not counted among the core. Each core item still requires measurement-handle-and-exclusion hardening — a specified measurement handle for each variable, and a stated set of excluded alternative explanations — which is not yet done.

The first core prediction is the **independence of modeling and routing**. There exist populations with intact modeling and attenuated routing on the other-class (psychopathy, with intact mentalizing and attenuated distress-response) and, separately, populations that route heavily onto poorly-modeled others (projection-dominant configurations). The two channels dissociate; and as a corollary, behavioral warmth is *not* a valid estimator of the coupling, since kindness can run over an unrouted referent and cruelty over a routed one. This is the load-bearing test, because if the channels do not dissociate, the paper's foundational distinction is false.

The second is that **field-flattening discriminates placement, for both cruelty and virtue**. Remove external accountability and observe golden-rule conduct: constitutively-placed holders maintain it, instrumentally-placed holders revert. Applied to ordinary moral conduct this separates genuine from instrumental morality; applied to resistance under an atrocity it separates genuine from performative resisters. Baseline conduct is identical across placements, so the test *is* the variation of the field — and no account that measures morality by baseline prosocial behavior can make the prediction, nor can any account that treats power-corruption as a faculty breaking rather than a field changing.

The third is that **affect-restoration does not transfer to standing-restoration**. Because routing and modeling are independent, interventions that raise *affect* toward an exogenously-thinned group will not, on their own, restore that group's *standing* — its recognition as full subjects. Measured sympathy can rise while measured attribution-of-full-standing stays flat; repairing standing requires intervention on the *model* specifically. This is the cleanest single test of the foundational distinction, because if warmth and standing move together, the modeling/routing split is wrong.

The fourth is the **non-interchangeability of the three repairs**. Accountability-restoration will do little for atrocity-path agents; re-humanization will do little for psychopaths; and cross-applying a repair to the wrong path will yield null-to-weak effects. No single-mechanism theory of cruelty can make this prediction, and it is the architecture's strongest *applied* claim: misdiagnosis of the path predicts the failure of the intervention.

The fifth is a **specification for reading genuine structure off conduct**, and it is as much a methodological constraint as a prediction. Whether observed good conduct reflects genuine constitutive structure — rather than coincidence, selection, or field-contingency — is not readable from a single outcome, and not even from a long record, unless that record spans *varied conditions* and is *sampled independently of outcome*. The three confounds each leave a signature — the fixed estimate that is right only at its crossings, the pattern that survived because the environment filtered its failures, the field-contingent producer that tracks perfectly until the field varies — and each demands its specific control. The genuine-resister prediction rides on this: intact-model-against-thinning should correlate with wide constitutive routing and independent counter-evidence access, be dissociable from generic courage, and show preserved standing-attribution *across varied conditions and unselected instances*.

The sixth is the **recursive bound on the will**: placement-change requires *both* that the unchosen parameters permit the target placement *and* that the toolkit's self-model certifies the attempt as a live option. The prediction is a dissociation — agents whose capacity already permits a constitutive placement but whose self-model says "I cannot" will not change until the self-model is altered, and interventions on the self-model of the will unlock change only where capacity already permitted it, failing where it did not. Flat libertarian and flat deterministic accounts both miss this joint condition, the one predicting change on will alone, the other predicting no role for the self-model at all. (And the calibration of that self-model is itself an across-instances property, by the fifth result, so this prediction inherits its multi-instance, varied-condition requirement.)

A weaker tier is retained but not counted among the core, and is marked as such honestly: the clarity-not-guilt phenomenology of driven decoupling is a corollary of the no-lie mechanism rather than an independent prediction; the sub-second per-relation reassignment of §5 is a corollary of the per-relation result; the addiction-shaped dynamics of driven decoupling follow from the foundation's osmotic-imprinting/activation machinery (File 3, §8.7) as applied here, so they discriminate the foundation rather than this paper's novel modeling/routing distinction; and the truth-as-affect resistance probe is a low-power test on a rare subpopulation, illustrative of the §3 limit case rather than load-bearing. These are real and they cohere with the architecture, but the ratio of independent discriminators to free parameters should be computed on the core, not padded with corollaries — a discipline the paper imposes on itself precisely to keep the count honest.

## 9. What this paper is not

Several misreadings are close enough to the surface to be worth excluding directly.

It is **not the claim that psychopathy is a choice**. The cold frame's agent-side production is governed by a coupling that is born or driven, distributed across a population, and only sometimes drivable; the one route that involves anything like a conversion is driven by load, not elected.

It is **not a moralization of psychopathy**, and not a theory of good people and bad people. The desert-removal of §7 cuts in both directions, and the capacity and attribution layers are held orthogonal precisely so that the analysis cannot collapse into that binary.

It is **not the claim that incorruptibility is fake or worthless**. It is real, reliable, and detectable; what §7 removes from it is *desert*, not value and not bindingness. "No credit for it" is not "it does not matter."

It is **not proven moral realism**. The observation that, for the truth-as-affect agent, "morals feel right" is inseparable from "morals are endorsed-as-true" is a claim about the architecture of *that* agent's verification channel — that the affective report and the truth-verdict are, for them, the same event — and not a demonstration of mind-independent moral facts.

It is **not libertarian free will, nor hard determinism about character**. The will is real and bounded: it works the slack the unchosen parameters leave, with its reach gated by an unauthored self-model. Free will here is a boundary term, and treating it so is what *preserves* the desert-removal rather than threatening it.

It is **not an adjudication of any contested present-day conflict**, and it is **not an excuse for atrocity**. The architecture supplies the mechanism by which ordinary moral agents are brought to produce the cold frame at scale; it assigns no roles in disputed cases, and explaining how a wrong was produced leaves the wrong wrong and its authors answerable.

> **SKELETON — OP8 (author-gated).** The interaction of endogenous and exogenous thinning. When an exogenously-thinned class is encountered by an agent who is *also* low-coupling or instrumentally-placed, do the effects compound, or does the agent-side state dominate? This bears on why participation rates in atrocity vary across societies — whether the variable is thinning-intensity, the agent-distribution, or their interaction — and it requires the §6A apparatus crossed with the margin structure of §4.5, which is itself pending. Held until §4.5 is ratified.

> **OP9 (resolved — see §6A.4).** Re-thickening dynamics. The atrocity-path repair, "restore the model," re-recruits routing only when the re-thickening is *lived* (osmotic + direct experience), which rebuilds the routing channel, rather than *informational*, which rebuilds the model alone and leaves the routing residual; the sufficiency-versus-necessity question is settled there. The sibling residue — old routing persisting *after* a frame has been revised — is worked as a cross-application case in *Religiously Charged Experience*'s conversion/deconversion prediction.

Beyond these: the precise relationship between the coupling and the existing coordinates needs stating carefully, so that the coupling remains a *relation between two operations* and never drifts into a fourth coordinate. The placement of this paper's phenomena within the *Agency Architecture* family taxonomy is open, and exogenous thinning in particular may belong there as a cultural-level source-compromise distinct from agent-side decoupling. The clinical-population mapping is hypothesis only; the architecture predicts a structure that clinical data could test and does not reclassify any diagnostic category. And the meta-ethical claims of §7 are the highest overreach risk in the paper and should be kept indexed to the verification-architecture that grounds them rather than allowed to float free as a standalone meta-ethics.

## 11. Conclusion

One behavior, several machines. The cold frame — handling another as means or obstacle rather than as a center of experience — is a single recognizable output with at least three distinct productions: an agent whose channels are decoupled, an agent whose instrumental decency has lost its field, and a fully moral agent whose model of some class of others was hollowed out before it reached them. The distinction that does the work is small and entirely within the existing apparatus — between modeling another and routing them — and from it the rest follows: that feeling is intact in psychopathy and only the routing is severed; that the golden rule is a placement whose depth determines whether it survives the loss of a watchdog; that ordinary people produce atrocity by faithfully routing a model that was thinned at the source, and feel not cruel but correct; that the will works only the slack the unchosen leave, and cannot be read off a single deed or an unvaried record; and that the placements deciding whether one is good, and good-without-a-watchdog, are largely unauthored — so that morality binds without anyone deserving it. The architecture's most useful and most uncomfortable yield is the same fact stated twice: that the better agent is genuinely better and is owed nothing for it, and that from the outside, watching the deeds alone, we often cannot tell which agent we are looking at.
