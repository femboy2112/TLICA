## 0. The one-sentence thesis

> The capacity to occupy the instrumental ("black-box") frame toward another I is not a fixed universal trait and not a deficit of affect; it is a *decoupling* between the truth-channel (which models the other as an I-bearing entity) and the affect-routing channel (which delivers the other's modeled φ into one's own ρ as load-bearing), whose baseline is set by a coupling coefficient λ_{T→R} that is *distributed across individuals*: some are positioned past the decoupling threshold from the outset (the cold frame is their baseline, not something they were driven to), some hold a high coupling that only sufficient accumulated load could lower (decoupling as a **driven** state-to-trait conversion), and some sit so far inside the threshold that no realistically encountered load would ever bring them to it. Where an individual sits is arbitrary with respect to desert, while the resulting moral bindingness — for those whose coupling sustains it — is nonetheless authoritative.

**Modal correction (C0, author, sober pass).** Do NOT state the thesis as "decoupling is *a* driven state" full stop. That over-universalizes the driven route. The correct claim is disjunctive over the population:
- **(a) Pre-set / congenital placement.** Some individuals are *born past* (or develop very early past) the threshold. For them the cold frame is not a destination reached under load — it is the standing baseline. Driving language does not apply; there was no drive *to* it.
- **(b) Driven placement.** Some hold a high λ that is plastic and load-sensitive; for them decoupling is the driven state-to-trait conversion developed in §4. This is the route the author's own phenomenology instances.
- **(c) Effectively unreachable placement.** Some sit so far inside the threshold that no load they will realistically encounter would decouple them. For them "you could choose to stop feeling" is not merely incoherent (§7.3) but counterfactually empty — the threshold exists in principle (§4.2) but is not reachable along any live trajectory of their actual life.

The paper's contribution is the *mechanism and the distribution*, not the assertion that everyone arrives by the driven route. "Driven" is route (b), not the universal modal status. (Audit: this replaces the earlier flat "driven state" framing. The driven route remains the richest case — it has the addiction-shaped dynamics of §4.4 — but it is one cell of a three-cell distribution, not the whole claim.)

## 1. The two channels (the core distinction this paper installs)

The paper's single new structural object is the distinction between two channels that *Differentiated Affect* and *Agency Architecture* leave fused by default:

- **Truth-channel (T):** the verification-toolkit operation that establishes the φ-status of the proposition "this other is an I-bearing entity with its own profile P_{m',t}, preservation-ranking Π_{m',t}, and φ-states." T outputs an *accurate model* of the other-I. T is the agency-attribution machinery: running it well = reading the other correctly.

- **Affect-routing channel (R):** the operation by which the other's *modeled* φ-states are taken up as inputs to one's *own* ρ and Π — i.e., the other's preservation-disruption registers as a perturbation in one's own affect-configuration (per the Differentiated Affect affect-criterion). R is what makes the other's pain *land*.

**CLAIM 1.1 (channels are dissociable).** T and R are architecturally distinct operations. T can run at full fidelity while R is attenuated, suppressed, or unrouted. (Audit: this is the load-bearing existence claim. Defended in §2.)

**CLAIM 1.2 (default coupling).** In the modal case, T and R are *coupled*: establishing "this is an I like me" as true (T) automatically recruits R. The coupling is the default, not a necessity. (Audit: distinguishes this paper from any account that treats empathy as either always-on or freely-switchable.)

**CLAIM 1.3 (the coupling is itself a structural variable with a magnitude).** The strength of the T→R coupling varies across individuals and within an individual over time. Name it the **coupling coefficient** (working symbol: λ_{T→R}, m,t-indexed). λ is not chosen; it is a developed/driven parameter. (Audit: this is the variable the whole paper turns on. Do NOT let it drift into a fourth coordinate or a trait. It is a coupling, m,t-indexed, plastic.)

**CLAIM 2.1 (affect is intact in psychopathy).** Psychopathy is not characterized by absence of affect. Euphoria, the pleasure of power, and self-directed affect run normally. The other is modeled as true-entity (T intact, often sharp) while the affect-system does not route that model into ρ. (Audit: this is the reframe. This is *more* dangerous than emptiness, because accurate T makes the other legible as an instrument. (Audit: connects to the cat case — see §5.)

## 3. The exit requires no lie (the mechanism, and the darkest claim)

This is the section that earned goosebumps; treat it with the most suspicion.

**Setup.** Some individuals report an architecture in which **truth registers as affect** — the verification-channel's output of "true" is not separable from a felt quality. Call this **truth-as-affect coupling** (a *specific high-λ regime where T and R share substrate so tightly that T cannot deliver "true" without R firing*). Such individuals report being unable to *directly* lie to themselves.

**CLAIM 3.1 (naive prediction: high-λ individuals are exit-locked).** If T and R are fused (truth-as-affect), the instrumental frame appears unreachable: it would require holding "the other is an I like me" as true (T fires) while withholding R — but R fires *with* T, so the withholding would require T to deliver a falsehood, which the individual cannot perform. (Audit: this was my v1 claim. The author OVERTURNED it. Preserved here because the overturning is the argument — see 3.2.)

**CLAIM 3.2 (the correction: the exit is not a lie, it is referent-thinning).** The exit to the instrumental frame does NOT proceed by lying ("they are not an entity"). It proceeds by **thinning the truth-model of the other** until the referent drops below the threshold at which R has anything to route. At no point is a falsehood asserted. The affect simply ceases to fire because the *referent* has been attenuated, not because the *truth* was denied. (Audit: AUTHOR'S CENTRAL CORRECTION. This is the mechanism by which an individual who *cannot self-deceive* can nonetheless reach the cold frame. The exit is gated, but not by honesty — by referent-richness. Verify this against the Substrate Override "forced coherence" regime: thinning is a perturbation of T's output, not a contradiction of it.)

**CLAIM 3.3 (the exit feels like clarity, not like falsehood).** Because no lie is told, the decoupled state is not introspectively flagged as deception. It presents as clarity / relief / disinhibition from a previously-felt cost. (Audit: structurally identical to the Substrate Override claim that overridden states do not self-present as overridden. This is the cross-paper anchor. The phenomenology of *becoming* colder is not the phenomenology of *deceiving oneself* — that's why it's a trap.)

Those are placement facts about the λ-distribution; this section is about *movement along* it under load.

**CLAIM 4.1 (in the driven case, λ is plastic, not fixed).** For route-(b) individuals, the coupling coefficient is not hardware. It is a developed parameter held at its current value by the *history of loads* met. "I cannot lie to myself" is true *in the current regime*; it describes present coupling strength, not a law of the individual's nature. **CLAIM 4.2 (the driving threshold, and its three population-regions).** A driving force sufficient to lower λ exists *in principle* for each individual, but its location relative to live trajectories differs sharply: (i) for some it is *already behind them* — they began past it (route a); (ii) for some it sits within reach of realistically encountered load — one bad decade can clear it (route b, the drivable middle); (iii) for some it sits so far inside the coupling that no load they will actually meet would reach it (route c, effectively unreachable). Whether a drivable-middle individual reaches it depends on what *happens to* them, not only on what they choose. **CLAIM 4.4 (this is the sibling-relation to addiction).** Substrate Override's dual-layer addiction distinguishes ρ-imprinting from substrate adaptation. (Audit: this is the formal payoff. ### 4.5 Resolution of OP7 — the margin parameter and inverse acquirability (born-low population structure)

**CLAIM 4.5.1 (margin gates placement-type, inversely).** Within the born-low population, distance past the threshold determines which golden-rule placement is *acquirable at all*, and the relation is inverse: **proximity to the threshold permits integration (constitutive placement); distance permits only acquisition (toolkit placement).** Integration requires a routing-baseline (some nonzero R) for the golden rule to bind into the maximally-self-defined I; the closer to threshold, the more baseline survives, the more the golden rule can go constitutive. The further above, the less baseline there is to bind, until only the *acquired black-box* pattern remains available. (Audit: AUTHOR'S RESOLUTION, this pass — and the clean inverse of my first, wrong, formulation. The author corrected "low-margin acquires toolkit / high-margin acquires nothing" to "low-margin **integrates** (constitutive) / high-margin **acquires** black-box (toolkit)." Integration vs. acquisition is the operative verb-distinction.)

- **Low-margin born-low.** Born just past threshold; enough baseline R that the golden rule *integrates* at high ρ. Result: a **constitutive but narrow** golden rule — field-independent in placement (PtCns-enforced, survives field-flattening) but restricted in scope by the low routing-baseline (fewer others, or shallower routing, get integrated). The earlier feel-vs-behave fork dissolves: the answer is neither "learn to behave" (toolkit) nor "raise R" (bidirectional plasticity) but "**integrate existing baseline R**" (constitutive organization of what was already present). ## 5. The per-relation reassignment result (the cat case)

The instrumental/agency frame is NOT a standing stance toward a person. It is **per-relation and reassigns in real time** as the salient I-relation in the field shifts.

**The worked case (stated in full, because the structure is invisible without the sequence).**

*Phase 0 — baseline field.* The agent is absorbed in a self-directed activity (music). The salient relation is agent↔music. Two cats (A and B) are present but peripheral.

*Phase 1 — Cat A as friction.* Cat A solicits attention and is experienced as an *annoying distraction*. Note the encoding: Cat A is not being routed (R off) and is not even an instrument *of* anything — it is friction against the salient agent↔music relation. Low standing, because nothing in the field requires Cat A to have any. This is the universal default (§5.3): the non-salient other is black-boxed not from coldness but from irrelevance.

*Phase 2 — the trigger.* Cat B does something to Cat A (misbehaves toward it). Critically, this does NOT primarily raise Cat A's standing. It makes **Cat B the salient agent** — the thing the agent's ProjectMap now needs to act *on*. The salient relation flips from agent↔music to agent↔(correcting B).

*Phase 3 — the maneuver.* The agent immediately, visibly loves on Cat A. But the affection is not generated *for* Cat A's sake (R toward Cat A stays off). It is an **action projected at Cat B, routed through Cat A's body** as the medium. The display is a *correction of B by demonstrated exclusion*: "the one you mistreated is the one I favor."

*Phase 4 — what the maneuver presupposes.* The maneuver only works if Cat B is modeled as an agent capable of *reading* the display as favoritism-of-A-over-B and being stung by the exclusion. That is full agency-attribution (T high) toward Cat B. So in the same instant: Cat A = unrouted medium (R off), Cat B = fully modeled agent (T high) acted on instrumentally.

**CLAIM 5.1 (frame is per-relation, not per-person, and reassigns on a seconds timescale).** The same other can be routed (R on) and unrouted (R off), and can rise or fall in standing, within seconds — driven not by any change in the agent's stance *toward that other as a person* but by a change in which I-relation is salient in the field. Cat A's standing did not rise when it was wronged; what changed was that *Cat B became the action-target*, and Cat A was conscripted as the instrument of B's correction. (Audit: the load-bearing point is that standing tracks *role-in-the-salient-relation*, not *identity-of-the-other*. The reassignment latency is seconds — see D5. This is the existence proof that frame-occupancy is a state, not a trait.)

**CLAIM 5.2 (the inversion: kindness can run on the unrouted referent, instrumentality on the routed one).** Affection was projected *at* Cat B *through* Cat A's body. The animal shown kindness got the unrouted (instrumental) treatment; the animal corrected got full agency-attribution (T), because the maneuver only works if the target can *read* the display. This inverts the intuitive mapping (kindness↔routing, instrumentality↔de-animation). (Audit: load-bearing. Kindness-as-instrument shows R-off can wear the *behavioral* signature of R-on. Behavior does not reveal λ.)

**CLAIM 5.3 (the cold structure is available to everyone, per-relation, without trait-level decoupling).** Running R-off on a particular referent in a particular relation is universal and does not require low baseline λ. The trait-level claim (§4) is about the *standing baseline*, not about whether the cold frame is ever *locally* occupied. (Audit: prevents over-reading. Everyone black-boxes the non-salient. Psychopathy-as-trait is about baseline λ, not about local occupancy.)

**CLAIM 5.4 (the cold structure proper: full T + instrumental action + no symmetry-recruitment).** The genuinely cold operation is: model the other as a full agent (T high) AND act on them purely instrumentally, where the *target-assignment never recruits the symmetry constraint* (golden-rule back-propagation) even though the agent-model is complete. (Audit: this is colder than de-animation. De-animation [thinning T] at least removes the entity. This keeps the entity in full view and declines to route it. The author flagged this as "the colder structure." It is the limit case of §3.2 with T NOT thinned.)

## 6. The symmetry constraint and the golden-rule projection error

Bridges to Agency Architecture's attribution apparatus.

**CLAIM 6.1 (golden-rule = symmetry-recruitment over R).** The "golden rule" operation is: route the other (R on), then back-propagate one's own Π onto the other as the correct-response signal ("what preservation-ranking would I want here"). (Audit: this is the agency-granting frame from turn 1.)

**CLAIM 6.2 (golden-rule is correct-modulo-projection-error).** Back-propagating one's own Π onto the other is valid only under profile-similarity. The naive-empathy failure mode is *projection error*: assuming the other's Π matches one's own, and getting exploited by others whose actual Π differs. (Audit: turn 1, point 3. This is WHY naive empathy "only hurt." Not too much R — R without profile-discrimination.)

**CLAIM 6.3 (the threat-model corrects the projection, not the routing).** The mature configuration keeps R on but gates back-propagation by *measured* P_{m',t} rather than assumed similarity. Jadedness installs the measurement. (Audit: ties §4.3 to §6.2. The fix for projection error is better T about the other's profile, NOT lower λ.)

**CLAIM 6.4 (two distinct axes, flattened by the aphorism).** "Treat others as agents [golden rule] vs. as black boxes" conflates two independent axes: (a) is T thinned? (de-animation), (b) is R recruited / symmetry back-propagated? An agent can be high-T + R-off (the cold structure, 5.4), or T-thinned (de-animation, 3.2), or high-T + R-on but projection-erroring (naive empathy, 6.2). These are three distinct configurations the folk binary collapses. (Audit: this is the conceptual cleanup the paper offers the moral-psychology literature.)

### 6.5 Resolution of OP3 — the golden rule has two placements, not one object (and the two-object spine survives)

The open question (former OP3) was whether the symmetry move (golden-rule back-propagation) is *part of R* (two-object account) or *a third operation gating R's use* (three-object account). It is neither. The dichotomy was malformed because it asked about the *number of channels* when the operative variable is the *ρ-position at which the golden rule is held*. The T/R two-object spine survives intact; the symmetry move is not a new channel. What varies is where the golden rule sits in the identity-correlation profile P_{m,t}.

**CLAIM 6.5.1 (the golden rule is a content with a ρ-position, not a primitive).** The golden rule — the disposition to back-propagate a (profile-discriminated, per 6.3) symmetry constraint over R — is itself a *content* integrated into the lived-I network at some identity-correlation ρ. It is not a fourth primitive and not a separate faculty. Its architectural significance is set by *where in P_{m,t}* it is held. (Audit: this keeps λ and the T/R spine as the only structural objects; the golden rule is a high- or low-ρ content, handled by existing foundation apparatus.)

**CLAIM 6.5.2 (two placements, observationally identical at baseline, divergent under field-flattening).** The golden rule admits (at least) two architecturally distinct placements:

- **Constitutive placement (high ρ).** The golden rule is integrated into the maximally-self-defined I. Defecting from it is not "abandoning a strategy" — it is a PtCns violation, a perturbation of frame-coherence, a partial self-loss. **Enforcement is internal.** This placement is *field-independent*: removing external accountability does not remove the cost of defection, because the cost was never external.
- **Toolkit placement (instrumental).** The golden rule is held in the verification-toolkit as a learned world-interaction pattern, deployed *because* it is path-of-least-resistance among other Is (the §7.5 coordination argument; "it's hard to fault you when you're genuinely trying to do good"). **Enforcement is external** — it is the field's retaliation/friction/coordination structure. This placement is *field-contingent*: flatten the field (remove the ability of others to fault, retaliate, or withhold coordination) and the least-resistance path no longer routes through the golden rule, so the pattern is dropped.

At baseline (a field with intact resistance) the two placements produce *the same observable behavior* — both holders "do good." They diverge precisely when the field flattens. (Audit: AUTHOR'S RESOLUTION. This is strictly stronger than "the golden rule is a derived least-resistance equilibrium," because the least-resistance story now accounts only for the toolkit placement; the constitutive placement explains the people who stay good with no watchdog. Same apparatus, both populations.)

**CLAIM 6.5.3 (§5.4's cold structure is the toolkit placement under field-flattening — predicted, not anomalous).** The "colder structure" (full T, R available, symmetry not recruited) is exactly what the toolkit placement produces when the field flattens: the agent retains accurate modeling (T) and intact routing-capacity (R available), and simply stops running the golden rule because the incentive that sustained it was removed. The symmetry move was never withheld by an act of will; the cost-gradient that recruited it was deleted. (Audit: this folds §5.4 back into the two-object account as a *consequence* of 6.5.2 rather than as evidence for a third object. Power-decoupling — the CEO, the unaccountable official, the occupier — is the canonical instance: not a broken faculty, a flattened landscape acting on a toolkit-placed golden rule.)

**CLAIM 6.5.4 (the truth-as-affect individual is the constitutive-placement limit).** The §3 truth-as-affect individual ("I cannot directly lie to myself; truth is a feeling") is the high-ρ limit: the golden rule is so deeply constitutive, and so fused with the verification channel, that defection registers as felt self-incoherence. This is *why* such an individual reports being unable to reach the cold frame even under maximal field-flattening (e.g., disinhibition, no external consequence) — the floor under their conduct is internal PtCns enforcement, not external accountability, so flattening the field does not lift it. (Audit: explains the author's own reported case as the constitutive-placement extreme, and predicts the *non*-occurrence of power-decoupling in such individuals. The constitutive holder handed unaccountable power does NOT revert; the toolkit holder does. This is the discriminator — see §8 C-D2.)

**CLAIM 6.5.5 (the projection-gate sits inside the symmetry move at both placements).** Either placement still carries the 6.2/6.3 caveat: bare golden rule (un-gated back-propagation of one's own Π) is path-of-least-resistance *only in a field of sufficiently profile-similar agents*; otherwise projection error invites exploitation. The mature form at *both* placements is golden-rule-gated-by-measured-P_{m',t}, not bare golden rule. The placements differ in *what enforces the disposition* (internal PtCns vs. external field), not in *whether the disposition needs profile-discrimination* (both do). (Audit: this is the correction the author flagged — the least-resistance argument licenses the "→ change behavior" step but not the "bad-for-me → bad-for-them" projection step; profile-gating is required regardless of placement.)

### 6.5.6 Placement is a DERIVED quantity, not a free parameter (the parameter-economy cut, and the bounded-will result)

**CLAIM 6.5.6a (placement is derived, not free — removed from the free-parameter count).** Placement (constitutive vs. toolkit) is NOT an independent knob the theory tunes and NOT a freely-willed choice. It is a *derived quantity*:
$$\text{placement} = f(\lambda,\ \text{margin},\ \text{field-history},\ \text{toolkit-self-model-of-will})$$
λ is unchosen (born/driven; §0, §4); margin is unchosen (§4.5); field-history is external; the toolkit-self-model is the verification-toolkit (Free Will / Agency papers) applied *reflexively* to willpower — pre-existing apparatus, not a new posit. So placement collapses out of the free-parameter set at net cost **−1 parameter, +0 posits**. (Audit: this is the A-cut. It also keeps §7 intact: making placement a *freely-willed* knob would contradict §7.1/§7.6's desert-removal, which depends on these placements being *unauthored*. Deriving placement from unchosen terms is the consistent move, not merely the tidy one.)

**CLAIM 6.5.6b (the will is real but recursively bounded — "constrained but not fixed").** Individual willing contributes to placement, but only at the *margin* λ+margin+field leave open, and its access to that margin is itself gated: **willpower can attempt only what the toolkit's self-model certifies willpower is capable of.** The constraint is reflexive — the will operates within the live-options the toolkit constructs (Free Will paper's live-option construction lemma), and "what the will can reach" is *itself a toolkit content*: a learned, ρ-positioned, unauthored estimate. Placement is therefore constrained (by λ+margin+field) but not fixed (the will works the residual slack), with the slack's *visibility* set by the self-model. (Audit: AUTHOR'S RESOLUTION — "willpower is constrained to what the toolkit sees willpower is capable of." This is the compatibilist (b) reading the rest of the architecture already commits to. It explains the stuck-but-capable case: an agent whose λ+margin+field *permit* constitutive placement but whose self-model never certifies the attempt as a live option will not reach it — the slack exists and is invisible. It explains the author's own case: truth-as-affect (6.5.4) means the toolkit correctly reads "will-the-cold-frame" as not a live option, so the slack toward coldness is empty *for them*. Desert stays removed: the self-model is also unauthored.)

**CLAIM 6.5.6c (calibration of the self-model is an N>1 property — it is ABSENT at a single instance, not merely hidden).** Whether the toolkit's self-model of willpower's reach is *accurate* (tracks actual λ+margin+field reach) is **not a property of any single placement-outcome.** At N=1 there is no calibration content to read — coincidence and genuine-tracking are indistinguishable not because the distinction is hidden but because *it does not yet exist*; calibration is constituted by the pattern across instances. The common-sense framing (accurate vs. inaccurate self-model) only becomes available, and only becomes *true-or-false*, over a series. (Audit: AUTHOR'S CORRECTION — "common sense only holds after multiple instances." Stronger than "single observations underdetermine": the property is emergent on the series, absent from the member. Same epistemic shape as 6A.4c (genuine vs. performative resister) and the §7 desert-inaccessibility — and now stated by its *reason*: the discriminating property is a property of behavior-across-conditions, so single behaviors don't underdetermine it, they don't contain it. THIS IS WHY the recursive-willpower discriminator (C-D6) is definitionally multi-instance.)

**CLAIM 6.5.6d (three failure modes manufacture a correct-looking series without the underlying structure — the broken-clock decomposition).** Even N>1 is insufficient to read calibration/genuine-structure if the series is the wrong series. A long correct-looking record can be manufactured three ways, each requiring a different control:
- **Coincidence-crossing** *(the conspiracy theorist who is occasionally right)* — a fixed estimate that intersects the truth-curve wherever reality happens to meet it; right at the crossings, wrong elsewhere; the hit is not evidence the next call beats chance. *Control: check whether the estimate **moves with** conditions or stays flat.*
- **Selection-on-output** *(the idiosyncratic coworker who wins just enough to keep the job)* — the pattern survives because the environment filters on *output*, not on calibration; the observed series is itself selected by the very output being assessed. *Control: sample instances **independently of outcome**, not the surviving ones.*
- **Field-stability** *(the psychopathic politician doing right because it pays)* — a field-contingent (toolkit) producer tracks *perfectly* across many instances **while the field holds incentive aligned with rightness**; indistinguishable from genuine virtue until conditions vary. *Control: **vary the field** (C-D2 flattening); watch which series breaks.*

(Audit: AUTHOR'S THREE EXAMPLES, each isolating one failure mode. Upgrades the broken-clock guard from metaphor to a **sampling-and-conditions specification**: the genuine-structure discriminator requires multi-instance **AND** varied-conditions **AND** outcome-independent sampling. The politician is the load-bearing case — he is the §6A.4b performative-resister scaled to a career, and shows field-flattening is not optional polish but the *only* axis separating him from the genuine article no matter how long the clean record runs. Ties directly to 6A.4c and §7.)

## 6A. Exogenous referent-thinning: the cold frame in ordinary moral agents (atrocity-path)

*(Numbered §6A this pass to preserve existing cross-references; promote to a full top-level section with renumbering at prose stage. This is plausibly the paper's co-equal second half.)*

**The phenomenon the architecture must explain.** The mass atrocities of the historical record — and contested cases in the present — were not, in the main, committed by psychopaths. The perpetrators were overwhelmingly *ordinary moral agents*: high-λ, constitutive golden rule, intact and wide *within scope*. They loved their families, kept their promises, felt guilt over ordinary wrongs. Yet they produced the cold frame toward an entire referent-class. Any architecture that locates the cold frame solely in agent-side properties (λ, margin, placement) is therefore **incomplete**: it cannot explain the most consequential occurrences of the very thing it theorizes. This section closes that gap. (Historical anchors, to be cited to the scholarly record at literature-anchoring stage, treated as the explanandum, not as rhetoric: the Shoah; chattel slavery and its racial-"science" apparatus; apartheid; the Armenian genocide; and contested present-day cases. The architecture explains the *structure* of how ordinary agents produce the cold frame at scale; it does NOT adjudicate any contested present-day case — see boundary note 6A.6.)

**CLAIM 6A.1 (referent-thinning has two sources — endogenous and exogenous).** §3.2 established that the exit to the cold frame proceeds by *referent-thinning*: thinning the truth-model (T) of the other until R has nothing to route, *without asserting a falsehood*. That section treated thinning as **endogenous** (agent-side: §4 driven, or §6.5 placement). This section adds **exogenous** referent-thinning: a thinning operation applied to a referent-*class* at the level of the cultural-I, inherited by the individual agent as a *corrupted T-input* about that class. The thinning is installed upstream of the individual's routing channel. (Audit: not a new channel and not a new primitive — it is a *source* and *locus* for the existing T-thinning operation of §3.2. The cold frame's output is identical; the production path is different.)

**CLAIM 6A.2 (the atrocity-path: faithful R over a pre-thinned T).** In the exogenous case the agent is NOT running accurate-T-plus-severed-R (that is psychopathy, §2). They are running R *faithfully and fully* over a T that was *thinned at the cultural source*. The routing channel receives a degraded entity and faithfully routes the degraded version — which is to say, routes almost nothing, because there is almost nothing there to route *as the agent's T reports it*. The agent's own architecture is healthy. (Audit: THIS IS THE CORE OF THE SECTION. The danger is precisely that the agent is architecturally normal. The horror is not malfunction; it is correct operation over corrupted input.)

**CLAIM 6A.3 (it presents as clarity, not cruelty — §3.3 inherited).** Because no falsehood is asserted (the thinned T does not register *as* a lie — §3.2/§3.3), the agent does not experience themselves as cruel. They experience themselves as *correct*: "I am not failing to route a person; I am correctly perceiving a non-person / a lesser-person / a threat-object." The dehumanizing cultural materials (vermin, cargo, infestation, "not really like us") are the *thinning instruments* — they install the corrupted T-input across many individual Is. (Audit: this is why ordinary moral people do it and sleep at night. The §3.3 "exit feels like clarity" result, applied to an exogenously-thinned referent, predicts the perpetrator's subjective sense of righteousness rather than guilt. Do not soften this. The mechanism is the absence of felt lie.)

**CLAIM 6A.4 (the cultural-I performs the thinning as a distributed-pattern operation, NOT as an agent).** The thinning operation is carried by the propaganda system, the legal category, the slur-network, the pseudo-scientific apparatus — distributed encoding structures at the cultural-I level. These install a corrupted T-input across a population of individual Is. The cultural-I does NOT "decide," "intend," or "will" anything; it is not anthropomorphized. (Audit: STANDING PROJECT RULE — cultural-I profiles are distributed-pattern constructs, never agents. Most load-bearing here of anywhere in the project, because the temptation to say "the regime wanted" is strongest exactly where the moral stakes are highest. Encode strictly as distributed encoding; attribute intent only to identifiable individual or institutional agents where the record supports it, never to the cultural-I as such.)

**CLAIM 6A.4a (WHY the no-intent rule is load-bearing: emergence distributes attributions downward — the bidirectional locus-of-agency discipline).** The cultural-I is *emergent over* a population of individual Is; it has no existence apart from them. Therefore any predicate attached to the cultural-I does not *remain* at the cultural-I — it **distributes back down** onto every individual I correlated with that culture. This makes the no-intent rule a moral-inference safeguard, not a stylistic preference, and it cuts symmetrically:
- **Up-attribution error → laundered collective guilt.** Attribute *intent* to "the culture" / "the nation" / "the colonizer" and, by the structure of emergence, you have attributed that intent to each culturally-correlated individual — including those who held T intact and did not participate. Anthropomorphizing the cultural-I *launders an unearned intent-attribution onto the uninvolved.* This is the mirror of the mechanism-≠-excuse error.
- **Down-refusal error → mechanism becomes excuse.** Refuse to attribute the wrong *down* to the actual participating individuals and atrocity becomes nobody's fault (§11).
- **Shared root.** Both errors misplace the locus of agency. **The cultural-I thins (pattern, no intent); identifiable individual and institutional Is intend, build, run, comply, or resist (agency, answerable).** Intent lives only at the individual/institutional layer — in both the *doing* and the *judging*. The emergent layer carries pattern, never will. (Audit: AUTHOR'S POINT, this pass — "attribution to the cultural-I leads to attribution to the culturally-correlated individual." This is the deep reason the standing rule exists, stated as a consequence of emergence rather than as a stipulation. Keep it; it converts the rule from "house style" to "load-bearing inference-control.")

**CLAIM 6A.4b (the perpetrator-resolution the no-intent rule generates — FIVE positions, attribution by production path).** Because intent cannot live at the cultural-I, the population under a thinning apparatus is NOT a uniform moral mass. It resolves into distinct, individually-attributable positions. Per author decision (this pass), attribution is split **by production path, not by act alone**: two agents who perform the identical right deed occupy distinct moral positions if their conduct was produced by different structures (one field-independent, one field-contingent). This is the same deed-vs-disposition cut the whole paper enforces, applied to *virtue* as well as wrong.
- **Architects/operators** — built and ran the thinning apparatus; authored the corrupted T-input. Full intent, full answerability.
- **Compliant** — acted on thinned T without resisting available counter-evidence. Answerable for the thinning they permitted (mechanism ≠ excuse, §11).
- **Genuine resisters** — held T intact *against* the thinning (intact/wide constitutive routing refusing the corrupted input). Resist *whether or not observed*; floor is internal PtCns. The righteous-among-nations / refusenik cases. Predicted as the high-λ-intact-T tail of the distribution (C-D5), NOT as anomaly.
- **Performative resisters** — *(split out this pass, author choice (ii))*. Produce the identical resistance-output, but by **toolkit-placement payoff-seeking in an unflattened field**: resistance is the best-payoff move *because it is observed and rewarded* (status, reputation, future coordination). T may be as thinned as the compliant agent's, or simply irrelevant to the calculation; R need not route the victim at all. Reverts under field-flattening — remove the audience/payoff and the resistance evaporates. Attributable position is distinct from the genuine resister precisely because the deed was *contingent*: the victim was not harmed *by them under these conditions*, but would not have been protected by them under flattened ones. (Audit: AUTHOR'S CATCH — "a psychopath would love the opportunity to visibly do the right thing in front of others." This is performed virtue with the same multiple-realizability as performed cruelty; the architecture is recursive here. Discriminator is already in-toolbox: C-D2 field-flattening separates genuine from performative resistance exactly as it separates constitutive from toolkit morality. See §8 C-D2 (resistance-output domain).)
- **Merely correlated, non-participating** — carry the cultural-I *correlation* but carry *no intent*. The row the emergence-discipline (6A.4a) protects; anthropomorphizing the cultural-I is the error that would wrongly convict them.

**CLAIM 6A.4c (observable good conduct underdetermines both desert AND placement — the epistemic symmetry).** Because genuine and performative resistance are behaviorally identical in an unflattened field, *observable right-action is not evidence of intact T, intact routing, or constitutive placement.* This protects the architecture from the naive reading "the rescuers were the empaths" — some were genuine resisters, some were calculators in a field that happened to reward rescue, and the behavior alone cannot distinguish them. It also extends §7's desert work: §7.6 severed *credit* from the (unauthored) placement; this severs *observability* too — an outside observer cannot tell constitutive virtue from toolkit calculation from performance. Desert is both unearned (§7.6) and externally inaccessible (here). (Audit: NOT nihilism — §11 guard holds; the genuine resister is still more reliably good and still field-independent. The claim is only that moral *credit-assignment by observers* runs on data that cannot separate the cases — which is precisely the channel reputation systems get gamed through by the toolkit-placed. Connects the performed-virtue result back to the §7 spine and to C-D1/C-D2.)
(Audit: a real addition generated *by* the author's emergence point, not a restatement. It tightens §6A: "ordinary moral agents do atrocity" was loose; the population resolves into FIVE individually-attributable positions (genuine vs. performative resister split out this pass, author choice (ii)), and the architecture predicts the genuine-resister tail rather than treating it as exception. The deed-vs-disposition cut now applies to *virtue* as well as wrong: identical resistance-deeds can occupy distinct moral positions by production path (field-independent vs. field-contingent). NOTE: positions are about *attributable agency / production path*, not about λ/placement type alone — a genuine resister and a compliant agent may share λ; what differs is whether they let T be thinned. A performative resister and a power-decoupler may share toolkit placement; what differs is the field conditions they're acting under. Keep these orthogonal: {λ, margin, placement} is the *capacity* layer; {architect, compliant, genuine-resister, performative-resister, non-participant} is the *attribution* layer; they cross.)

**CLAIM 6A.5 (the three-source taxonomy of the cold frame — the paper's organizing result).** The same output — *other treated as non-routed instrument-or-obstacle* — has (at least) three structurally distinct production paths, requiring three distinct repairs:

| Path | Agent-side state | T | R | What produces the cold frame | Repair |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Psychopathy** | born/driven low-λ (§4, §4.5) | intact, accurate | severed / never-coupled | agent-side decoupling | barely repairable (re-coupling limited; §4.4 dynamics) |
| **2. Power-decoupling** | high-λ, *toolkit* placement (§6.5.3) | intact | available | field-flattening removes the incentive that ran the toolkit golden rule | **restore the field** (accountability, observability) |
| **3. Atrocity-path** | high-λ, *constitutive*, wide — fully moral (6A.2) | **exogenously thinned** about a referent-class | intact, faithful | corrupted T-input inherited from cultural-I; R routes the degraded entity | **restore T** (re-thicken the referent: contact, counter-narrative, re-humanization) |

(Audit: this table is the section's payoff and probably the paper's organizing spine. Three causes, one output, three non-interchangeable repairs. The practical claim: **conflating the paths makes interventions misfire.** Accountability (path-2 repair) does little for path-3, because the perpetrator already feels accountable to their *own intact morality* — which the thinned T routes around. Re-humanization (path-3 repair) does little for path-1, because path-1's T is not thinned. This is the architecture earning its keep.)

**CLAIM 6A.6 (bridge to the cultural-split paper, with the Hall-sibling constraint).** Exogenous referent-thinning of an out-group is the *limit case* of a divergent threat-map — the out-group's I-status is the content being differentially encoded across cultural-I profiles (the cultural-split paper's core machinery). This generates a sibling to that paper's load-bearing Hall et al. constraint (affective depolarization does not transfer to democratic-norm protection): **re-routing affect toward a thinned group may not transfer to restoring that group's T-status.** Prediction: "humanizing" / sympathy-raising campaigns can produce warmer *affect* toward an out-group without repairing the *thinning* that removed the group's entity-status — yielding sympathy without recognition-of-equal-standing. (Audit: real cross-paper bridge, testable. The affect-axis and the T-status-axis are independent (this paper's whole T/R distinction), so an intervention on one need not move the other. Flag for the cultural-split paper's research ledger.)

**Boundary note 6A.6 (explicit, in-text, like OP4 for clinical claims).** The architecture explains the *general structure* of exogenous thinning — how ordinary moral agents produce the cold frame at scale via culturally-installed T-corruption. It does **not** adjudicate any contested present-day case, assign perpetrator/victim status in disputed conflicts, or rule on who is thinning whom now. The historical anchors are used as the established explanandum; present-day inclusion in any list is the author's, flagged as contested, and is not a claim the *architecture* licenses. The model's job is mechanism, not verdict.

## 7. Bindingness without desert (the meta-ethical result)

**CLAIM 7.1 (placement is arbitrary w.r.t. desert — for BOTH λ and ρ).** Where one's decoupling-threshold sits (the λ baseline) was not chosen and is not earned; the high-λ individual did not author their coupling, nor the low-threshold individual theirs. The same now extends to the golden rule's ρ-placement (§6.5): *whether one's morality is field-independent* — held constitutively (high ρ, survives field-flattening) rather than instrumentally (toolkit, reverts) — is itself an unauthored placement in P_{m,t}. Neither the λ-position nor the ρ-position is credit- or blame-worthy *qua placement*. (Audit: author, turn 6 — "My line in the sand is arbitrary." Run the symmetry: arbitrariness disarms desert in BOTH directions and across BOTH parameters. See 7.6 for the sting.)

**CLAIM 7.2 (bindingness does not require chosen-ness).** A moral constraint can be authoritative over an individual without being authored by them. Most real constraints are unchosen. The contingency of λ's *origin* does not touch the *authority* of the morals that the high-λ regime sustains. (Audit: the saving move. This is what lets the author keep "this is right and I am held to it" while giving up "and I deserve credit for being held." The two come apart. Most accounts fuse them and collapse into either error-theory or libertarian-desert.)

**CLAIM 7.3 ("just choose to care" is incoherent across threshold-variance).** The moral demand "you could have chosen to feel" is only coherent when aimed at an individual whose threshold sits where the speaker's does. Applied across the real variance in λ and in driving-threshold, it presupposes a uniform lever-resistance that does not exist. (Audit: NOT relativism. The morals remain real and binding on each individual; what fails is using one's own threshold as the universal standard for *judging capacity*.)

**CLAIM 7.4 (truth-as-affect yields a desert-free moral realism).** For the truth-as-affect individual, "morals feel right" is — given that truth registers as affect and self-deception is unavailable — as close to "morals are true" as the architecture permits without proof. The feeling is the verification-channel reporting, not decoration on a belief. This grounds a moral realism that routes through the anti-self-deception faculty rather than through theology. (Audit: author, turn 7 — "I dont know if there's a god... but the idea of having morals feels right to me." HANDLE WITH CARE: this is a claim about the *architecture of the author's verification channel*, NOT a proof of moral realism. State it as: in this regime, the affective report and the truth-verdict are the same event, so "feels right" is not separable from "is endorsed-as-true." Do not overclaim to mind-independent moral facts.)

**CLAIM 7.5 (the non-vacuum floor).** Even pure self-interest does not reach standing trait-level decoupling: the agent does not exist in a vacuum and needs the coordinated labor of other Is (roads, institutions, etc.). The fully-decoupled agent either free-rides on others' routing or pays the full coordination cost of dealing with entities it refuses to grant standing — which is more effortful. Morality-as-routing and morality-as-cheapest-coordination-technology converge. (Audit: author, turn 7 — the "I'm lazy, I don't wanna build roads" argument. This is a structural, not sentimental, floor under R. It does NOT prove one ought to route; it shows routing is the low-effort equilibrium for an embedded needy agent. NOTE: §7.5 is the floor under the *toolkit* placement specifically — it is the least-resistance argument, and per §6.5.2 it is field-contingent. It is NOT the floor under the constitutive placement, whose floor is internal PtCns and does not depend on the vacuum-argument at all. Keep the modesty: instrumental convergence, not a derivation of obligation.)

**CLAIM 7.6 (the sting: no credit for field-independent virtue).** §7.1's desert-removal, extended to ρ (§6.5), yields a sharper corollary than the λ-version alone. Not only can one not be *blamed* for a low-λ threshold one did not author; one also cannot take *credit* for being good-without-a-watchdog — for holding the golden rule at constitutive ρ such that one stays good under field-flattening (C-D2). That ρ-placement was developed, not chosen-from-nowhere; the person whose morality survives unaccountable power did not author the integration that makes it survive, any more than the author authored the weld of §4. The most admired moral property in the catalogue — incorruptibility under power — is, on this account, an unauthored placement. (Audit: this is the maximal extension of the §7 spine and the most counterintuitive claim in the paper. It does NOT say incorruptibility is worthless or fake — it is real, load-bearing, and behaviorally consequential (C-D2). It says the *desert* attaching to it is illusory in the same way the desert attaching to a high λ-threshold is. Bindingness without desert (7.2) now reads: the constitutive holder is genuinely held — more reliably held than the toolkit holder — and still merits no credit for the placement that holds them. HANDLE WITH CARE alongside 7.4; this is the claim most likely to be read as moral nihilism, which it is not — see §11.)

## 11. What this paper is NOT (boundary discipline)

- NOT "psychopathy is a choice" (the title-bait version). It is a T/R decoupling whose baseline is distributed across the population: congenital for some (a), load-driven for some (b), effectively unreachable for some (c). Only route (b) involves anything like a driven conversion, and even that is driven by load, not elected.
- NOT a moralization of psychopathy. §7 explicitly removes desert in both directions.
- NOT a claim that empathy is good-because-chosen. §7.2 severs bindingness from chosen-ness.
- NOT a diagnostic instrument. §10 OP4.
- NOT anthropomorphizing any cultural-I or treating λ as an agent. λ is a coupling coefficient.
- NOT proven moral realism. §7.4 is indexed to a verification-architecture, not mind-independent facts.
- NOT libertarian free will, and NOT hard determinism about character. Placement is derived (§6.5.6a) and the will is recursively bounded (§6.5.6b): real, but working only the slack λ+margin+field leave, with access gated by an unauthored self-model. Free will is a boundary term, not a character-setting fiat — and this *preserves* §7's desert-removal rather than threatening it.
- NOT moral nihilism. §7.6 removes *desert* from incorruptibility, not *value* or *bindingness*. The constitutive holder is more reliably held than the toolkit holder (C-D2); the claim is only that the placement which holds them was unauthored. "No credit for it" ≠ "it doesn't matter." Real, load-bearing, behaviorally consequential — and undeserved, like every other unchosen placement.
- NOT an adjudication of any contested present-day conflict (§6A boundary note 6A.6). The architecture explains the *structure* of exogenous thinning; it does not assign perpetrator/victim status in disputed cases or rule on who is thinning whom now. Historical anchors are the established explanandum; present-day inclusions are the author's, flagged contested, and not licensed by the architecture.
- NOT a claim that atrocity-path perpetrators are blameless because their architecture was healthy. §6A explains the *mechanism* (R faithfully routing a culturally-thinned T); it does not exculpate. Mechanism ≠ excuse. The agents who let their T be thinned, and the agents who *built and ran the thinning apparatus*, remain morally answerable; the architecture clarifies *how* the wrong was produced, not *whether* it was wrong. (This boundary is as load-bearing as the nihilism guard — do not let "ordinary moral agents did it" slide into "so it wasn't their fault.")
