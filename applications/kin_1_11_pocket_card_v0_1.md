# KIN-1-11 — Pocket Card
**Kinship · Interdependence · Non-Domination**  
*Carry a conscience, not a chain of command.*

**Purpose:** Protect equal dignity and widen real opportunities for responsible choice, cooperation, learning, repair, and peaceful dissent. This is voluntary, nonviolent civic/ethical practice, not a military organization or spiritual authority system.

## The doctrine in 11 breaths

1. **Dignity:** No disposable people.
2. **Conscience:** Never outsource responsibility for your own acts.
3. **Truth:** Your conviction is not your evidence.
4. **Aid:** Help without buying ownership.
5. **Freedom:** Your agency ends where unjustified domination begins.
6. **Consequences:** Intention does not erase harm.
7. **Burden:** Choose bounded service, never invisible martyrdom.
8. **Conflict:** Oppose harmful conduct, not human worth.
9. **Leadership:** Coordinate without claiming superior dignity.
10. **Correction:** Make disagreement, appeal, and exit safe.
11. **Inheritance:** Leave the next people freer than you found them.

## KIN-SITREP: six questions before acting

| Step | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| **Reality** | What actually happened, and what am I inferring? |
| **Persons** | Who is affected, absent, less powerful, or unheard? |
| **Stakes** | Which right, risk, need, or commitment matters now? |
| **Choices** | What can these people *really* choose? What's the best rival option? |
| **Authorization** | What am I entitled to do under consent, rights, role, safety, and law? |
| **Review** | Who benefited? Who paid? How will I know if I was wrong? |

**Safety:** Respond to immediate threats through appropriate emergency/safeguarding channels; no vigilante action. Respect victims' right not to reconcile.

## Five category errors to catch

- **Dignity ≠ innocence.** Wrongdoing can justify proportionate, lawful restriction without dehumanization.
- **Explanation ≠ authorization.** Understanding motives never creates permission to hurt.
- **Causal connection ≠ jurisdiction.** Being important to you does not make someone yours to direct.
- **Spiritual resonance ≠ proof.** Felt alignment does not verify facts or assign moral worth.
- **Interdependence ≠ captivity.** Sustainable help should preserve genuine refusal and exit.

## The two independent tests of spiritual leadership

**The Iroh test:** Have my bounded efforts widened other people's future options, without concealing what the effort cost?

**The corruption test:** Can the person who disagrees most with me challenge me, get a fair hearing, and leave without retaliation?

**If you cannot answer, you have not earned a verdict.**

*No thrones. No masters. No disposable souls.*
